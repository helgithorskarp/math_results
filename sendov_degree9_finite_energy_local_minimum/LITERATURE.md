## Primary sources

- [Tang--Zhang, Lemma 3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3)
  uses the classical Cheung--Ng reciprocal companion matrix. Section 2
  of this proof derives the characteristic identity directly. The matrix
  representation and spectral functional calculus are credited classical
  tools, not new results.
- [Zhang, Conjecture 1.2 and Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126)
  presents the first-power endpoint as conjectural and proves the
  quadratic case. The current contribution concerns a stronger collapsed
  baseline at small fixed reciprocal energy, not the global endpoint.
- [Tao, Conjecture 19](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
  states the broader stronger-power family and supplies the present
  problem's context. No known ordinary degree-nine Sendov result is
  described as new here.
- [Michael J. Miller, *Unexpected local extrema for the Sendov conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0505424)
  studies local maxima of the maximum, over original roots, of their
  nearest-critical-point distance, including degree-nine examples.
  That objective and constraint differ from the present fixed marked root,
  fixed reciprocal energy, and minimum of the sum of eight reciprocals.
  Its local-extrema terminology is relevant prior-art context, not a
  theorem resolving this local variational claim.

The holomorphic-primitive scalar support, Riesz projector and implicit
function theorem are classical analytic arguments. Section 5 proves the
specific lower support self-contained. No general harmonic-function or
matrix-representation novelty is asserted.

## Completion boundary

Theorems 1--3 are ordinary written author proofs with exact algebra
controls and independent review pending. They permit all original disk
motions locally, but supply no uniform local neighborhood radius. A
global minimizer's asymptotic approach to the one-plus-seven orbit does
not alone put it in these possibly shrinking neighborhoods. Such a
quantitative completeness bridge is a concrete next frontier, not a
claimed consequence of the present proof. The neutral stability curve,
its first correction, effective thresholds and global first power remain
outside the claim.
