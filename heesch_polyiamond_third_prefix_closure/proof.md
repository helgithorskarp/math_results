copies of T, with C3 a subfamily of B, B a subfamily of D and D a subfamily
of E, such that

    P is contained in int(union B),
    union B is contained in int(union D),
    union D is contained in int(union E).

All tile interiors are disjoint. Translations, rotations and reflections
may be arbitrary real Euclidean motions. No disc, connectedness or
preceding-corona contact condition is imposed on B,D,E. The assertion
transports under a common congruence.
