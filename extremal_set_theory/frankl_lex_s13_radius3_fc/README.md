# The radius-three shell around lexicographic `L_13` is FC

This directory proves an exact computer-assisted theorem at the unresolved
`FC(4,9)` frontier of Pulaj and Wood.

## Theorem

Let `L_13` be the first thirteen four-subsets of `[9]` in lexicographic order.
Every four-uniform superfamily of `L_13` on `[9]` with at least sixteen blocks
is Frankl-complete (FC).

Equivalently, if `G` is disjoint from `L_13` and `|G| >= 3`, then
`L_13 union G` is FC.  This improves the earlier deletion-capacity conclusion
from seventeen blocks to sixteen.  It does **not** classify the one- and
two-block shells, prove that every fourteen-block family on `[9]` is FC, or
determine `FC(4,9)`.

## Finite structural reduction

The known equality `FC(4,8)=12` implies that a Non-FC four-uniform family on
`[9]` has at most eleven blocks avoiding any fixed point.  Applied to `L_13`,
this forces every added block in a hypothetical Non-FC extension to contain
`{8,9}` and allows at most three added blocks.  When there are exactly three,
removing `{8,9}` leaves one of the nine edge families

```text
{16,57,67}, {17,56,67}, {26,57,67}, {27,56,67},
{36,57,67}, {37,56,67}, {46,57,67}, {47,56,67},
{56,57,67}.
```

The subgroup generated by `(1 2)`, `(3 4)`, `(6 7)`, and `(8 9)` preserves
`L_13` and has three orbits on this list, represented by

```text
{16,57,67},  {36,57,67},  {56,57,67}.
```

`verify.py` reconstructs the deletion capacities, all nine candidates, and
the three orbits rather than taking the list as input.

## Poonen certificates

For a family `B` of subsets of `[9]`, put

```text
q(B)_i = 2 |{S in B : i in S}| - |B|.
```

Poonen's theorem says that a configuration `A` is FC exactly when a nonzero
nonnegative weight vector `w` exists such that `q(B) dot w >= 0` for every
union-closed `B` stable under union with `A`.  The three representatives above
have the following integral weights (normalization by the coordinate sum is
implicit):

```text
{16,57,67}: (2,2,1,1,2,2,2,2,2)
{36,57,67}: (1,1,1,0,1,1,1,1,1)
{56,57,67}: (2,2,1,1,2,2,2,2,2)
```

The exact SAT instances rule out a violating family for each weight.  There is
one Boolean variable `x_S` for each of the 512 subsets `S` of `[9]`.  The
clauses are precisely

```text
x_S -> x_(S union A)                         for every generator A,
x_S and x_T -> x_(S union T)                 for every S,T,
sum_S (2 w(S) - sum_i w_i) x_S <= -1.        (the negated weight claim)
```

The last pseudo-Boolean inequality is converted to CNF by PySAT/PBLib.  UNSAT
therefore proves the Poonen inequality.  Stability under the sixteen displayed
generators is equivalent to stability under their union-closure, so this is
exactly the quantifier in Poonen's characterization.  Transporting the weights
by the verified subgroup covers all nine candidates.  The deletion reduction
then proves the stated theorem for every larger superfamily by upward
monotonicity of FC.

The deterministic instances contain 111,645 union clauses and 7,680 stability
clauses each.  The two weight types produce respectively 331,942 clauses on
107,099 variables and 219,871 clauses on 50,924 variables after the
pseudo-Boolean encoding.  Their exact instance hashes are recorded in
`EXPECTED_RESULTS.json`.

## Reproduction

Python 3.11 or later is recommended.

```bash
python3 -m venv /tmp/frankl-radius3-venv
/tmp/frankl-radius3-venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
cd math_results/extremal_set_theory/frankl_lex_s13_radius3_fc
/tmp/frankl-radius3-venv/bin/python verify.py
```

`verify.py` uses CaDiCaL 1.9.5 through PySAT and checks all three orbit
representatives.  A one-worker run took about fourteen minutes on the
discovery machine.  It verifies the deterministic CNF hashes against
`EXPECTED_RESULTS.json` and exits nonzero on any SAT answer or mismatch.

The independent command

```bash
/tmp/frankl-radius3-venv/bin/python independent_check.py
```

rebuilds the mathematics as a direct 0-1 model with PySCIPOpt 6.2.1 and SCIP
10.0.2; it does not use the PySAT pseudo-Boolean encoding or the expected CNF
hashes.  It is supplied as a second formulation, not as a proof-trace checker.
In the retained one-worker replay, SCIP proved the three models infeasible in
respectively 11, 35, and 17 branch-and-bound nodes.

## Validation and trust boundary

The retained proof trusts Poonen's characterization, the published equality
`FC(4,8)=12`, the stated deletion reduction, PySAT/PBLib's exact encoding,
CaDiCaL, CPython, and the execution platform.  The independent SCIP replay
uses a direct mixed-integer formulation and a different solver but is still
solver evidence; no DRAT/LRAT trace is retained.  Structural enumeration, symmetry,
weight transport, clause counts, and instance hashes are checked directly by
the public source.  No floating point, random choice, private input, or
unpublished dataset enters the claim.

The weight vectors were found by an iterative Z3/PySAT separation search.
That discovery process is not trusted by the proof: the retained verifier
reconstructs and checks the final certificates from the mathematical
definitions.

## Literature and scope

Pulaj and Wood, *Local Configurations in Union-Closed Families*,
[arXiv:2301.01331v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01331), prove the lower-order
thresholds used here, give `14 <= FC(4,9) <= 21`, formulate the lexicographic
initial-segment conjecture, and state Poonen's characterization.  A targeted
search of that primary source and Discovery Net found no classification of
the three distance-three types above.  This is search-relative novelty
evidence, not a claim of historical priority.
