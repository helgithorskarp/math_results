# Proof and exact scope

## Coordinates and completeness

The coordinate field is generated by `alpha=i sqrt(3)`, `beta=i sqrt(11)`
and `gamma=i sqrt(7)`. Their commuting monomial basis has eight independent
rational coefficients; their squares are -3, -11 and -7. The producer used
this complex basis. The standalone checker instead uses two Cartesian axes
and the positive real radical basis

```
1, sqrt3, sqrt7, sqrt21, sqrt11, sqrt33, sqrt77, sqrt231.
```

For square-free m,n,
`sqrt(m)*sqrt(n)=gcd(m,n)*sqrt(m*n/gcd(m,n)^2)`.
Expanding both coordinate-difference squares decides squared distance exactly.
Equality is coefficient equality; distance one means rational coefficient
96^2 and every other coefficient zero. Independence of the radical basis
justifies both predicates. There is no floating-point arithmetic.

The explicit isometries in README.md produce the three components. Exact
merging proves that they share only O, P and Q, as specified, without a
triple overlap. All 31,125 point pairs are decided. The complete unit-edge
set is exactly the union of the 18, 75 and 977 inherited edges. In particular,
no unexamined cross contact may constrain a colour-permutation argument.

## Every complete B214 word extends

Let c be any proper four-colouring of the entire third component. Put
`a=c(P)` and `b=c(Q)`. The new shared-root colour will be b.

The certificate contains a proper F29 word in which vertices 9 and 12 both
have colour 3. A permutation of four colours mapping 3 to b gives a proper
F29 word agreeing at both O and Q. The certificate contains two proper
Golomb words: one equal on vertices 1 and 4, and one different there. If
`a=b`, map the equal colour to b. Otherwise map its two endpoint colours to
b and a respectively, then extend this injective map to a permutation of
four colours. This gives a proper Golomb word agreeing at O and P.

These three words agree at every identified point. Every physical edge lies
in one of the three components, so their union is proper. Restriction proves
the reverse inclusion of relations. Therefore projection of the complete
whole-graph four-colouring set onto ALL 214 B214 vertices is exactly the
original complete B214 colouring set.

The checker verifies both Golomb templates, the F29 template, and all 16
formal choices of a,b. It does not assert that every formal choice is
realized in B214; it proves an extension rule which works whenever a proper
B214 word has that choice. In particular the proof does not assume or
reprove the negative unequal-pair theorem that motivated selection.

A literal proper whole four-word and a proper word using all five colours
are separately checked. The retained Golomb graph is not three-colourable:
normalize its unit triangle to 0,1,2 and exhaust all `3^7` remaining choices.
Thus the complete physical graph has chromatic number exactly four. The
five-word is an upper-bound witness, not evidence for chromatic number five.

The checker also verifies that the graph is connected and stays connected
on deleting any one vertex. It therefore has no articulation, and, since it
has more than two vertices, no bridge. Its minimum degree is three. This
nonseparability does not prevent universal extension through the two-colour
interface templates.

## The finite finishing gate and its failure

The intended finishing mechanism was an equal-pair spindle, not a proposed
receiver or generic host search. If O and P were equal in EVERY proper
four-colouring of this graph, a second copy rotated about O by
`delta=(7+i sqrt(15))/8` would give a non-four graph: `|delta|=1`, and
`|2-2 delta|=1`, while P and delta*P would both have O's colour. Sharing at
least O would give at most `2*250-1=499` distinct points. Complete merging,
contact reconstruction and a proper five-word would then still be required.

The saved whole four-word has `c(O)=0` and `c(P)=1`. Therefore the premise
fails. Its restriction also prevents any subgraph retaining O,P from forcing
that pair equal. This does NOT prove that the unconstructed two-copy union
is four-colourable: new cross contacts could have independent consequences.
No such union or alternate terminal pair was examined, and this failed gate
does not license a copy, angle, phase, pole or source sweep.
