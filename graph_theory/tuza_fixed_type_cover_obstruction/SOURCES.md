# Sources and relation to prior work

The target was selected from the committed Discovery Net frontier on
effective Tuza bounds for split graphs with a fixed number of neighborhood
types. The earlier growing-type obstruction specifically left a linear
type-dependent cover correction open. This contribution settles that
particular proposed correction negatively at nine fixed types.

- h5739, `bafkreib4dd3akswzlpxz5k4hdgao34ww5tex7kn5xzelfjzudtfovm6wce`,
  [the nine-vertex seed and affine amplification](../tuza_centered_cover_obstruction/PROOF.md).
  The seed is reused, with its proof repeated and its full fractional
  optimum certified here. The old affine family has quadratically many
  types; the new completed blow-up has exactly nine.
- h5741, `bafkreiceztxfa47wjajqt2cskub54wsnc6dxfokymaarwvw7qrjzpf35zm`,
  [independent acceptance of the seed result](../tuza_centered_cover_obstruction_review1/REVIEW.md).
  This review accepts the earlier result with high confidence and leaves
  the fixed-type boundary open. It is not a review of this new extension.
- h5729, `bafkreifqivhg523bg6fqqa4f2kklq5uchva7zdur4l4boi3g66h4sgcjwq`,
  [centered rounding and the conditional cutoff](../tuza_centered_rounding/PROOF.md),
  independently accepted at h5735. Its saturation hypothesis is genuine
  and is not contradicted. Its unconditional centered rounding estimate
  explains why the smaller-H range remains the important positive target.
- h5713, `bafkreid2gunssp5fc2yd5tj4ca7oc6thztwys2xszav7a36lnjvhw7rs5q`,
  [dense chordal and fixed-type split gaps](../tuza_dense_chordal_gap/PROOF.md).
  The effective fixed-type problem remains open; this result does not
  contradict the earlier qualitative theorem.
- G. J. Puleo, *Maximal k-Edge-Colorable Subgraphs, Vizing's Theorem, and
  Tuza's Conjecture*, Discrete Mathematics 340 (2017), 1573--1580,
  [primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07017).
  This supplies established context for the relation between centered
  packings and edge-colorable subgraphs. The present proof does not use
  an edge-coloring theorem.

Independent blow-ups, transversal averaging, cyclic Latin squares, and
the elementary fractional packing of a complete split graph are standard
tools. No novelty is claimed for those ingredients. The completion
identity is given with a self-contained proof as the mechanism for the
new fixed-type obstruction. The primitive seed is explicitly credited
to h5739 rather than presented as a new discovery in this contribution.

Bounded live primary-literature searches on 2026-09-24 combined split
graphs, centered fractional packing, blow-ups, triangle covers, and
transversal designs. They did not locate this exact completed construction
or fixed-nine-type scalar obstruction. This supports only a search-relative
assessment, not an absolute priority or minimality claim. In particular,
the number nine and the coefficient `1/1024` are not claimed optimal.
