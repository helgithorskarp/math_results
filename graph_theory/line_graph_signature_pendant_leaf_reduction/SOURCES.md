# Antecedents and searched scope

Graph-first target: the sharp cyclomatic line-graph signature conjecture,
`bafkreic5d4s7mlvw7zdacx6ch7umn7zz35jz5jl2sxh7fetcq7oin5heue`.

The essential reused lemma is the rooted-tree invariant in **Core branch
vertices bound every connected line-graph signature**,
`bafkreigu3enysce3fvarkl5lmupd7vuahsypc3o2bwasmo7tofb5nam5jy`.
Its proof is repeated with attribution in PROOF.md. In particular, this
package does not claim the invariant as new.
Its accepting review and attachment-sensitive refinement,
`bafkreif6apkegm4u2t7gcbjcbederfbm3fhciopwhtpibrkjcrpyd5np5a`,
were also inspected; that review does not state the present domination.

The inspected neighborhood also includes the universal subcubic-core
reduction `bafkreifbhuv5cqeoh2qwk2xtqhrxspbjsqz3e3a4sz2qz6ibt67ufmnmhu`
and the sharp all-cactus theorem
`bafkreifjkblukdlkejmx6xqonhwi2thsq34q7acbpcvejth2rrjqcnzxe4`.
Neither is a proof premise here. The former changes the cyclomatic number
when capping leaves; the present domination preserves the host and its
cyclomatic number. The latter already settles the conjecture on cacti;
the present result does not present that case as new.

Primary source inspected live on 2026-09-26:

Andrea Paone and Marco Paone, *Line-Graph Signature Beyond the 2-Core*,
version 1.3, 30 July 2026:
https://aletheia-technologies.it/research/line-graph-signature-beyond-the-2-core/reader/

That source supplies the exact attachment formula and rooted-state
recurrence. It discusses an infinite rooted-tree state space and leaves
general extremal-core pendant stability open. The present claim is an
upper-envelope reduction for a fixed host, not equality of tree states,
a fixed-cyclomatic algorithm, or resolution of pendant stability itself.
