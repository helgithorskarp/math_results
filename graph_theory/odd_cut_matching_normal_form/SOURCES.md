# Primary-source boundary

The open problem is the Máčajová--Škoviera conjecture: every finite
bridgeless cubic graph should have two perfect matchings whose intersection
contains no odd edge cut.

Primary sources inspected:

1. J.-L. Fouquet and J.-M. Vanherpe, *Máčajová and Škoviera Conjecture on
   Cubic Graphs*, Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory 30 (2010), 299--306,
   [arXiv:0809.4839](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4839).  This gives the
   conjecture, balanced-matching machinery, small-order and cyclic-connectivity
   consequences, and a stronger result for traceable graphs.
2. G. Mazzuoccolo and J. P. Zerafa, *An equivalent formulation of the
   Fan--Raspaud Conjecture and related problems*, Ars Mathematica
   Contemporanea 18 (2020), 87--103,
   [arXiv:1811.08363](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.08363).  This records the
   implication from Fan--Raspaud to the odd-cut conjecture and distinguishes
   the weaker `S_4` condition.
3. P. Magalhães Júnior and A. K. Silva, *On some perfect matching conjectures
   in infinite, cubic, bridgeless graphs* (2026),
   [arXiv:2607.29511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29511).  This states that the
   finite conjecture remains open and proves finite/infinite transfer results.
4. J.-L. Fouquet and J.-M. Vanherpe, *On Fan Raspaud Conjecture* (2008),
   [arXiv:0809.4821](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4821).  Its balanced
   `M`-matching and good-ring criteria were checked specifically to avoid
   presenting the already known ring construction as new.

Targeted searches of these sources and current indexed literature did not
locate the exact component-parity quotient criterion or its
odd-cycle-transversal alternating-cycle corollary.  This bounded search cannot
establish priority, so no exclusive novelty claim is made.  The contribution
is presented as a proved structural normal form and certificate compression.
