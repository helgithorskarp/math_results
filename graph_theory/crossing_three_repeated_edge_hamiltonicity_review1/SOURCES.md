# Primary sources checked

1. Ken-ichi Kawarabayashi and Kenta Ozeki, “4-connected projective-planar
   graphs are Hamiltonian-connected,” *Proceedings of the Twenty-Fourth
   Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms* (SODA 2013), 378--395,
   <https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611973105.28>.  Revised manuscript:
   <https://tgt.ynu.ac.jp/ozeki/2012KO.pdf>.  Checked Theorems 2, 3 and 5,
   the proof of Theorem 1, and the authors' later direct application of
   Theorem 5 inside a non-null flap.  The longer journal version is
   *J. Combin. Theory Ser. B* 112 (2015), 36--69,
   <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2014.11.006>.

2. Kenta Ozeki and Carol T. Zamfirescu, “Every 4-connected graph with
   crossing number 2 is Hamiltonian,” *SIAM J. Discrete Math.* 32 (2018),
   2783--2794, <https://doi.org/10.1137/17M1138443>.  Manuscript:
   <https://tgt.ynu.ac.jp/ozeki/2017OZ.pdf>.  Checked the main theorem and the
   repeated-crossed-edge argument in Claim 1.

3. Kenta Ozeki, “Hamiltonicity of graphs on surfaces in terms of toughness
   and connectivity,” survey manuscript,
   <https://tgt.ynu.ac.jp/ozeki/2020Ozeki.pdf>.  Checked Section 6.2, which
   records the conjecture for 4-connected graphs of crossing number at most
   five and states that the general conjecture remains open.

4. D. P. Sanders, “On paths in planar graphs,” *J. Graph Theory* 24 (1997),
   341--345,
   <https://doi.org/10.1002/(SICI)1097-0118(199704)24:4%3C341::AID-JGT6%3E3.0.CO;2-O>.
   The form used here is restated as Theorem 3 in source 1 and as Theorem 4 in
   source 2.

Live bounded searches were performed on 2026-09-21 for later primary work on
4-connected crossing-number-three Hamiltonicity and for the exact relative
edge-addition statement.  No exact match or settlement was found.  That is
search-relative evidence only, not a priority claim.
