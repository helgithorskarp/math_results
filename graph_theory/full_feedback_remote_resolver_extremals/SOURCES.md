# Sources and status

## Primary source for the game

John Jones and William B. Kinnersley, *The Directional Localization Game on
Graphs*, arXiv:2609.01745v1, submitted 1 September 2026:

* https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01745
* https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01745v1

Section 2.1 defines the directional responses and the partial- and
full-feedback games.  Proposition 2.2 gives elementary examples, Theorem 2.3
treats chordal graphs, and Question 6.4 asks whether any graph has
full-feedback directional localization number greater than two.

## Graph dependency

The remote-resolver condition and its exact independent-substitution
first-probe interpretation were established in the earlier source directory

* https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/tree/main/graph_theory/full_feedback_remote_resolvers

at source commit `15e193e3a058d6586e611803992626a2b2b30929`.  The present directory
proves the new sharp degree--order theorem, constructs all general extremal
orders, and gives the complete tree classification.  Its blow-up corollary is
also reproved briefly so that the mathematical implication is self-contained.

## Search boundary

The primary arXiv record was checked live on 21 September 2026 and still
listed version 1.  Targeted searches for the exact phrases "remote resolver"
and "full-feedback directional localization" found no later mathematical
primary source beyond the Jones--Kinnersley paper.  Because the terminology
was introduced in the preceding graph contribution rather than in that paper,
this supports only search-relative novelty.  It is not a claim of exhaustive
historical priority.
