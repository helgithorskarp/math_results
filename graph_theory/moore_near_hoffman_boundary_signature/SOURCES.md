# Sources and status

The diameter-two Moore graph classification and adjacency identity originate
with:

- A. J. Hoffman and R. R. Singleton, “On Moore graphs with diameters 2 and
  3,” *IBM Journal of Research and Development* 4 (1960), 497–504,
  <https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.45.0497>.

Useful primary context for cocliques and outindependent subgraphs is:

- M. A. Fiol and E. Garriga, “From local adjacency polynomials to locally
  pseudo-distance-regular graphs,” *Linear and Multilinear Algebra* 54 (2006),
  123–140, <https://doi.org/10.1080/03081080500143902>.
- C. Dalfó, “On the spectra of the subconstituents of a strongly regular
  graph,” *Linear Algebra and its Applications* 569 (2019), 1–14,
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2018.12.035>.

The current existence status of the degree-57 graph is documented by:

- V. Faber and J. Keegan, “Existence of a Moore graph of degree 57 is still
  open,” arXiv:2210.09577, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.09577>.
- Y. Ishida, “No involutions in the missing Moore graph,” arXiv:2606.29183v1
  (28 June 2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29183>.
