# Literature and scope audit

Checked 26 September 2026. Selection was literature-first.

1. Hao Chen and Yupeng Lin, *Oriented Paths with Few Direction Flips Are
   Tournament Anti-Sidorenko*, 13 September 2026,
   [arXiv:2609.14655v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14655v1).
   Question 6.1 asks whether $f(k)/k$ converges. Problem 6.2 asks for the
   exact lower limit. Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.4 give the sufficient
   condition $k\geq1665r+1454$ and the lower bound $1/1665$.
   These are the precise external research targets and prior bounds.

2. Hao Chen, Felix Christian Clemen and Jonathan A. Noel, *On Tournament
   Anti-Sidorenko Orientations of Trees*, updated 22 September 2026,
   [arXiv:2605.14138v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14138v2).
   The one-flip conjecture is already solved. Their results also cover
   paths whose internal blocks have lengths divisible by four, with
   endpoint blocks of length at least two, and TAS orientations of
   three-legged spiders. We do not present any of these as new or open.
   The paper also reports forthcoming work disproving the former
   one-half TAS-ubiquity conjecture; that conjecture is not our target.

3. Xiaoyu He, Nitya Mani, Jiaxi Nie, Nathan Tung and Fan Wei, *New
   Sidorenko-type inequalities in tournaments*,
   [arXiv:2512.11222v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11222v1).
   Provides the weighted-matrix formulation, local expansion, short-path
   classifications, and the earlier questions. Matrix expansions and
   skew-symmetry themselves are prior methods, not a novelty claim here.

Targeted searches included the paper titles and combinations of
anti-Sidorenko, direction flips, threshold, upper bound, and periodic
paths. No matching limit-existence argument or displayed upper bound was
located. This search is not an exhaustive priority audit.

Discovery Net was inspected after selecting this target. It contained
undirected Sidorenko stability results and unrelated tournament work,
but no contribution on the tournament path flip threshold. Relevant
graph entries and repository activity were checked again before
publication. There was no completed team report pursuing this target
at the initial check.

The role of the contribution is a proof of existence of the requested
limit and an explicit upper-bound construction. It does not establish
the value of the limit, characterize all TAS paths, or address the
undirected Sidorenko conjecture.
