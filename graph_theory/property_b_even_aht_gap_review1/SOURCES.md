## Primary literature checked

- S. Aglave, V. A. Amarnath, S. Shannigrahi, and S. Singh,
  *Improved Bounds for Uniform Hypergraphs without Property B*, Australasian
  Journal of Combinatorics 76(1) (2020), 73--86:
  [arXiv record and manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00218),
  [journal PDF](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/76/ajc_v76_p073.pdf).
  Its introductory AHT discussion records the classical even-uniform term
  \(2^{r-1}+\tfrac12\binom r{r/2}\).
- P. R. J. Östergård, *On the minimum size of 4-uniform hypergraphs without
  property B*, Discrete Applied Mathematics 163 (2014), 199--204,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2011.11.035).  This is an independent
  primary source for \(m(4)=23\).

unequal AHT cores, Catalan parity, folded-cube covers, and Steiner
divisibility.  The searches recovered the classical AHT and folded-cube
ingredients but no primary source stating the combined uniform arithmetic
gap.  This was a bounded search, so it warrants only the target's stated
