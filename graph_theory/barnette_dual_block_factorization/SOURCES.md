# Sources and status boundary

Checked 2026-09-22.

1. Behrooz Bagheri Gh, Tomas Feder, Herbert Fleischner, and Carlos Subi,
   *On Finding Hamiltonian Cycles in Barnette Graphs*, Australasian Journal
   of Combinatorics 87 (2023), 1--20.
   https://fi.episciences.org/10485/pdf

   This gives the dual language of non-separating A-trails and spanning
   trees of faces.  Its disk/weak-dual mechanism is the main prior framework
   behind the face-boundary bijection used here.

2. Muhammad Jawaherul Alam, Michael A. Bekos, Vida Dujmovic, Martin
   Gronemann, Michael Kaufmann, and Sergey Pupyrev, *On Dispersable Book
   Embeddings*, Theoretical Computer Science 861 (2021), 1--22.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2021.01.035

   This is the origin of the relevant face-colored dual-tree construction.

3. Michael A. Bekos, Michael Kaufmann, and Maximilian Pfister,
   *Approximating Barnette's Conjecture*, LIPIcs GD 2025, Article 6.
   https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.GD.2025.6

   Section 2 states the dual-tree traversal and its edge-selection
   properties.  The present theorem does not require that algorithm for its
   proof, but the construction motivates fixing one edge-color matching.

4. Jan Florek, *A Sufficient Condition for Cubic 3-Connected Plane
   Bipartite Graphs to Be Hamiltonian*, Journal of Graph Theory 110 (2025),
   272--282.
   https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23270

   This is relevant neighboring work giving broad face-incidence sufficient
   conditions and lower bounds on numbers of Hamilton cycles.  It does not,
   in the inspected statement, give the cactus block product for cycles
   containing one specified color matching.

5. Tobias Schnieders, *Barnette Graphs with Faces up to Size 8 Are
   Hamiltonian* (2025).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03531

   This records a strong current Hamiltonicity class and the continuing open
   status of the unrestricted conjecture.

The Discovery Net predecessor
`bafkreicx2wgyreczjhqrxq36efvaieeeggytmtjohvg3q55iy7mkfs253e`
characterizes which prescribed cycles are recovered by the published
dual-tree construction: exactly those containing an edge-color class.  The
present result counts that entire image by an exact block factorization for
every bicolored dual, then evaluates every factor in closed form under a
cactus hypothesis, including a zero criterion and a direct linear
construction.

Targeted searches for Barnette bicolored-dual cacti, cactus counts of
Hamilton cycles, fixed color-matchings, induced dual trees, and exact product
formulas found the sources above but no statement matching this theorem.
Hamiltonicity conditions requiring degree-four vertices on bicolored cycles
are related and can overlap the positive cases here; existence alone is not
claimed as the new content.  The potentially new content is the exact
matching-specific boundary bijection, general block product, and explicit
cactus specialization.  This is a search-relative status statement, not
proof of historical priority.
