# Exact physical closure of the C0 higher-incidence star

Let omega=(1+i sqrt(3))/2, T={0,1,omega}, and
A5(z)=T+zT+z^2 T+z^3 T+z^4 T. Equal complex coordinates are identified and **every** pair at Euclidean distance one is an edge. This is a physical point graph, not merely the abstract Cartesian product on 243 labels.

Write z=x+i sqrt(3)y, with x,y real. The factor of sqrt(3) is essential throughout. The coordinate pair (u,v) in the code means u+i sqrt(3)v, with squared norm u^2+3v^2.

## The finite class and theorem

The event curves C_j are the lexicographically sorted primitive integer polynomials regenerated by the h4105 architecture source. Its zero-based curve C_0 is exactly

    |-z + (1-omega)z^2 - z^3|^2 - 1 = 0.

The equality with the curve inventory is checked from its Eisenstein coefficient row `[(0,0),(-1,0),(1,-1),(-1,0),(0,0)]`.

Let B be the explicit 76-element partner list in `pairs.json`: each row is [0,j]. It equals, entry by entry, all rows containing curve 0 in the `remaining_six` list of the pinned h4195 residual. There are 68 degree-six partners and 8 degree-eight partners. Define the **C0 higher-incidence star** as the parameter set

    S = union over j in B of {x+i sqrt(3)y : (x,y) in R^2, C_0(x,y)=C_j(x,y)=0}.

The words “higher-incidence” identify the inherited residual mode; they do not assert that a root has six active curves.

**Exact computer-assisted theorem.** S has exactly 251 distinct complex parameters, represented by 77 irreducible real algebraic components. Every A5(z), z in S, is exactly three-chromatic. More precisely:

| Parameters | Physical vertices | All unit edges | Active event curves |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 240 | 243 | 279 | 2 |
| 7 | 243 | 351 | 2 |
| 2, namely omega and conjugate(omega) | 27 | 63 | 811 |
| 2, namely i and -i | 84 | 312 | 288 |

In particular, within S a third active event curve forces z to be one of the four listed unit-circle parameters. All other parameters are collision-free and lie on exactly their two source curves. No admissible injective parameter requiring six active curves remains in this class.

This is a theorem about the explicit set S. Neither a global orbit count nor completeness of a global counterexample cover is required. It does not assert that every point of C0 is three-colourable, close all of A5, or improve the smallest known five-chromatic construction.

## Complete algebraic coverage by two methods

The producer uses a lexicographic Groebner basis over Q with variable order y>x. Its x-eliminant is factored into primitive irreducible polynomials q. An invertible linear relation a(x)y+b(x)=0 gives y=-b/a in Q[x]/(q). The producer treats each exceptional rational x fiber separately by specializing the original equations and factoring their common y-polynomial. It fails on any unsupported nonrational exceptional fiber. No such unsupported case occurs here.

The verifier does **not** call this decomposition. For each pair it computes the nonzero resultant Res_y(C0,Cj) over Q[x]. Every common affine zero projects to a zero of this resultant; possible roots introduced by loss of leading degree are retained for testing rather than assumed to be actual intersections.

For each irreducible resultant factor q with a real root, the verifier applies polynomial Euclid to the original two equations in K[Y], where K=Q[x]/(q). Arithmetic in K is exact rational polynomial arithmetic modulo q. The common polynomial is made monic.

* If its degree is zero, that projected factor has no affine common root.
* For deg(q)>1, every surviving fiber in this class is linear, Y-h(x). Thus the common roots are exactly (alpha,h(alpha)) for the real roots alpha of q.
* For a rational x=x0, the gcd specializes to a polynomial over Q in Y; factoring it gives every real y value. This handles fibers such as x=0 and x=1/2 without dividing by a vanishing coefficient.
* An identically zero fiber, nonlinear fiber over a nonrational field, or zero resultant makes the verifier fail rather than omit a branch.

Each output point is substituted into both original equations. Real roots are counted by exact rational real-root counting, not numerical approximation. The full pair-by-pair component lists from the resultant method must equal those in the Groebner certificate.

A nonzero q with no real root contributes no physical parameter. Multiplicities do not duplicate points; the claim concerns the reduced set of real intersections.

## Why the 251 parameters are distinct

A nonrational ordinary component has x=s, primitive irreducible minimal polynomial q(s), and y=h(s) reduced modulo q. Two different primitive irreducible q have disjoint x-root sets. For the same q, different reduced h cannot agree at one of its roots, because that would make a nonzero polynomial of degree less than deg(q) divisible by q.

A nonrational vertical component has rational x=x0, y=s, and primitive irreducible minimal polynomial q(s). These cannot overlap ordinary components of nonrational x, and their distinct x0 or irreducible y-polynomials distinguish their point sets. Rational points are normalized to q=s, with both x and y constants. Thus equality of these canonical component encodings is precisely the possible overlap between pair decompositions. Deduplication leaves 77 encodings, with 251 real embeddings in total; each real pair (x,y) gives a different complex parameter z.

The two exceptional nonrational embeddings have q=3s^2-1, x=0, y=s, hence z=+i or -i. The two rational points are (x,y)=(1/2,+1/2) and (1/2,-1/2), hence omega and conjugate(omega).

The unit-circle intersection has a short independent explanation. For |z|=1,

    |-z+(1-omega)z^2-z^3|
      = |(1-omega)-(z+z^(-1))|
      = |1/2-2x-i sqrt(3)/2|.

Its norm is one exactly when (1/2-2x)^2=1/4, so x=0 or 1/2. Therefore the entire C0/unit-circle intersection is precisely the four parameters above. The control script also verifies the polynomial remainder C0 mod (x^2+3y^2-1)=2x(2x-1).

## Physical realization and complete unit edges

For a certified real component, fix any real embedding of K=Q[s]/(q). The code represents complex coordinates by (u,v) in K^2, interpreted as u+i sqrt(3)v. This is an injective representation at the chosen embedding: real and imaginary parts vanish separately. Irreducibility of q implies that equality of elements of K is equivalent to equality at any one real embedding. Consequently the collision partition and unit-edge graph are the same at every real embedding of a component.

`physical.py` starts with the three digits (0,0), (1,0), (1/2,1/2), multiplies by successive powers of (x,y), and enumerates all 3^5 words in lexicographic order. Equal pairs of field elements are merged by first occurrence. For every pair of resulting distinct physical points it tests

    (u1-u2)^2 + 3(v1-v2)^2 = 1 in K.

It enumerates all physical point pairs. No floating threshold, assumed edge owner, or unmerged label graph enters this distance check.

The producer obtains edges from the h4105 event-owner table, mapping its endpoints through the exact collision quotient. The verifier's definition-level physical edges must match the complete canonical edge hash and count of the producer, for every component. This is a check using a different edge construction. Both routes share the coordinate generator and exact arithmetic libraries; this is author validation, not an independent external review.

## Chromatic decision

For each component the producer seeks one of three colours at every physical vertex. Its SAT encoding has an at-least-one clause and pairwise at-most-one clauses at each vertex, and forbids equal colours across every event-derived physical edge. It pins the universal triangle corresponding to the labels of 0,1,omega to colours 0,1,2; any proper colouring can be globally permuted to satisfy this pinning.

All calls return satisfying assignments. The certificate retains every three-colour word. The verifier imports no SAT solver and checks the word on every directly constructed physical unit edge. Thus chi<=3 is witnessed, with no dependence on an UNSAT verdict. The points 0,1,omega are distinct and pairwise at distance one in every A5(z), and the verifier checks this triangle after merging, so chi>=3. Hence chi=3 for every parameter in S, including collisions.

## Asymmetry and dependency boundary

The parameter symmetries are R(x,y)=((-x-3y)/2,(x-y)/2) and C(x,y)=(x,-y). The verifier expands the six images of C0 exactly. For every listed partner, none of the five nonidentity images equals C0 or that partner. Any symmetry stabilizing the unordered pair would have to send C0 to one of them. Each named pair therefore has trivial D3 stabilizer, independently of the inherited stabilizer metadata.
