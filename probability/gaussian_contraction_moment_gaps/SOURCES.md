# Source and novelty audit

Checked 26 September 2026. The human-assigned problem is Conjecture 1.1
of the first source, in dimension three for bounded probability inputs.

1. Aishwarya--Li, [Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the
   Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
   [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07041).
   We use the elementary Gaussian replica formula, equation (61), and
   the pairwise-distortion integration mechanism in (63)--(64).
   We do not claim those identities, continuous-contraction monotonicity,
   or individual power comparisons as new. The theorem here compares
   different replica orders by the online variance identity, finds the
   optimal ratio, and gives the resulting exact two-power cone and
   additional exponential-energy comparisons.

2. Aishwarya--Li, [The Kneser--Poulsen phenomena for entropy](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.03664v3),
   IMRN 2025(12), rnaf140. Theorem 1.5 supplies qualitative Gaussian
   Renyi comparisons. Theorem 1.6 and the proof in Section 3 treat
   continuous contractions and lifted paths. These results do not state
   the cross-order gap bound or its sharp two-power coefficient cone.

3. Team B's other lanes develop the general hinge/Hankel criterion,
   a rank-five geometric lift, and obstructions to inferring majorisation
   from entropy/pressure comparisons alone. Those independent mechanisms
   are not claimed by this contribution and are not proof dependencies.
   The rank/degree search screening and unpublished numerical experiments
   remain research context, not ingredients of the uniform proof.

Targeted searches combined Gaussian convolution, contraction, internal
energy, moment differences, relative moment gaps, and two powers. The two
primary manuscripts above and committed graph neighborhoods were inspected.
No matching sharp relative-gap classification was located. This is a
bounded search-relative novelty assessment, not a historical priority claim.

The online variance update and Abel summation are classical identities;
the contribution is their application to the contractive Gaussian replica
gap, with optimal constants and precisely scoped energy consequences.
