# Sources, ownership and trust

The sole problem source is Aishwarya--Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
arXiv:2609.07041v2. Gaussian replica integration, the pressure identity,
continuous-contraction monotonicity and the auxiliary two-dimensional
Gaussian cancellation are credited prior ingredients. No historical
priority is claimed for projection stability or the assembled guard.

The new claim is the uniform sign condition on a marginal directional
variance relative to the square of mean pair loss, with its exact
finite-input spectral certificate. This is a quantitative neighborhood
of an existing positive boundary, covering arbitrary laws and positive
loss. It is not a new R5 motion, a classified atom family or a new
Kneser--Poulsen consequence.

The Euclidean Kirszbraun extension theorem is the classical existence
input when the original map is specified only on its support. The source
problem gives a global map, in which case no extension is needed. The
Gaussian translation bound is proved by integrating a directional
derivative; hinge L1 stability is immediate from the positive-part map.

The target-thin control uses the credited simplex-flap coordinates with
the R2 damping factor63/64, followed by a linear target flattening. The
source-thin and rare-outlier controls use the familiar contraction
`(x1,x2,x3) -> (|x1|,|x2|,|x1+x2|)/4`. They test distinct algorithm
branches and the L2 rather than uniform-distance hypothesis. They are
not claimed as new positive geometries or counterexamples.
