# Sources, dependencies, and bounded novelty claim

Primary papers were checked live on27 September2026. The sole target is
[Aishwarya--Li, Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the
Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture, arXiv2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2).
Its unrestricted dimension-three full majorisation question remains open
here. A bounded positive measure can be normalized to probability; no
claim for signed measures is intended.

[Gorbovickis, Strict Kneser--Poulsen conjecture for large radii,
arXiv1006.0531v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1006.0531v2), Theorem1.5, supplies
strict point-hull mean-width increase under a noncongruent **finite**
expansion. Reversing the finite contraction gives the orientation used
here. That theorem is not silently extended to arbitrary infinite compact
supports. Cap positivity for a fixed compact support, and the elementary
finite-net/continuity arguments, are proved directly in the manuscript.

## What is added and what is checked

The added step is the uniform cap/width reserve and its exact rational
consumer for arbitrary bounded strict-width contractions, including
diffuse laws and Lip=1 maps. It closes the entire spherical ray and hence
all Gaussian thresholds above a single quantified cutoff. It includes
finite rational certificate existence in this sector once genuine
support and mass information is available. It is not the finite eventual
theorem, the strict-map theorem, or another equivalence of test classes.

The source pin manifest [INPUTS.json](INPUTS.json) records exact versions
and byte hashes of principal dependencies and contextual comparisons.
The exact audit tests arithmetic and certificate premises only. It does
not formalize the continuum proof, establish independent acceptance, or
prove historical novelty. No large dataset or hidden numerical premise
is needed. The all-variance target, global defect cap, and campaign
handoff gate are unchanged.
