Conjecture 1.1, checked live on 27 September 2026. We address its bounded-law

## Related prior optimization and team mechanisms

Researcher 3's [common-set and prior-localization proof](../gaussian_prior_localization/PROOF.md),
source `541d4b7de3d73b41444e5350378a8ecc43d914ea`, graph
`bafkreiam5rzibygouwnzj7aokt23ld4twuarsvrpr4ffdxvokehvtl7gd4`
(6122), already obtains equality of component Gaussian acceptance
potentials on the support of a minimizing law, by minimax and prior
optimality. We do not claim that complementary-slackness mechanism as
new. Its optimization fixes a source test set and can have a uniquely
diffuse optimizer. Here a strict failure first chooses a finite support;
we then find a *transverse first heat contact*, ordered for every prior
on that support, with simultaneous source/target top sets. No universal
atom bound, exact atomic optimizer for that earlier minimax problem, or
finite localization of a diffuse equality law is asserted.

Researcher 5's [fixed-atom reduction](../gaussian_majorisation_global_criterion/ANCHOR_REDUCTION.md)
requires arbitrary rare packets. Our changed prior is not assumed to have
a dominant atom. The same lane's [eighth-beta proof](../gaussian_averaged_eighth_beta/PROOF.md),
source `18fd5b8a6cf732357196f3cb6e413ddf9b2c7a35`, graph
`bafkreibv2gicfcp4n4pghj6y3jb5gnmubc42zjwi7w35zpndlrqbxs4svq`
(6315), was read before publication. It proves an averaged replica sign
and was still an author proof pending review at this inspection. It is
not used as a premise here. Our observable keeps the actual contraction
and the two contact top sets; it makes no claim of pointwise conditional
kernel positivity or of a radius-preserving endpoint coupling.

Researcher 8's accepted [seven-factor proof](../gaussian_seven_factor_kernel/PROOF.md)
and the later [small-radius defect bound](../gaussian_majorisation_high_noise_window/SMALL_RADIUS_DEFECT.md)
are separate moment/error controls. No positive error budget is set to
zero here, and no closed beta frontier is reopened. Researcher 4's
[straight-path pair obstruction](../gaussian_straight_path_pair_obstruction/PROOF.md),
graph `bafkreigr7q3hjdrmclmjdckwhfczkbkzfvebc7utennz5uaxvopx5fsotq`
(6295), explains why this packet retains full indicators and their common
law instead of asking every pair action to be positive. The new Brownian
energy sign has not been proved from any of these results.

## Classical probability and claim boundary

The Brownian conditional-expectation construction and Ito covariance
identity are classical. For a directly checked exposition of
M_s=P_(1-s)D(B_s) and its stochastic differential, see
[Chihao Zhang's course, Lecture 10, “Evolution of M_t”](https://chihaozhang.com/teaching/AA2025/lectures/lec10/lec10.html)
(instructed by Zhang, scribed by Rui Huang and Yuchen He), checked live
on 27 September 2026. We apply the identities to bounded Borel data by
Gaussian smoothing for s<1 and L2 convergence at the endpoint; the proof
spells out that extension. No differential-subordination theorem, adapted
image coupling, or comparison of arbitrary diffusion matrices is invoked.
The probability bounds follow from the displayed Gaussian moments,
Cauchy--Schwarz, and the elementary chi-square exponential moment.

The substantive combination is prior-stationary transverse contact
selection plus exact component balance and a quantitatively detectable,
bounded-region stochastic witness to any adverse flux. The generic
optimality, coarea and martingale ingredients are not new. No historical
priority claim or independent acceptance is made. The exact checker only
audits finite algebra and constants. The analytic proof and its use of
the accepted contact theorem remain the external trust boundary.
