# One admissible adverse certificate

The new usable target is a strictly negative **joint superlevel volume**
for two rigid components. Their Gaussian variances may differ. A certified
violation here is sufficient for an actual common-variance Gaussian
majorisation counterexample on finitely many barycentres in the two convex
hulls. This is stronger than merely obstructing an attempted proof.

For finite component profiles with their Gaussian peak factors removed,
it suffices to provide positive certified a,b,eta_A,eta_B,delta such that

```
volume({F_A > a exp(eta_A)} intersect {F_B > b exp(eta_B)})
    >= volume({G_A > a} intersect {G_B > b}) + delta.
```

The proof supplies finite posterior grids, unchanged ball radii, exact
barycentric contraction, and a common-variance Gaussian hinge gap strictly
less than `-h delta/2`. The final variance may be chosen by (12).

The original weighted measure need not itself violate a hinge. No such
claim follows from a joint-level datum. Conversely, a strict datum cannot
be dismissed as only a stronger, unrelated functional condition: the
finite conversion proves its relevance to the full conjecture.

No adverse datum currently accompanies this packet. Its eight-site fixture
checks the identities only. Floating negative volumes, equality of separate
grid counts, and a finite threshold census do not meet the premise above.
This packet does not implement or replace a volume certifier.

Both component maps must be rigid. Existing adverse interaction examples
with contracting internal shapes do not meet this premise and do not
become counterexamples through the present transfer.

The terminal Gaussian variance conversion uses exponential priors selected
from a certified ball-volume violation. It does not reopen the completed
fixed-prior finite-atomic low-noise exclusion. The mathematical exploration
target remains the finite-variance joint contact, including unequal scales.

The fixed-domain equivalence quantifies over **all laws and all finite
centre lists on both convex hulls**. A positive computation for just the
original eight or twenty-four sites cannot discharge that obligation.
