**Mechanism to reuse:** choose a tail i of outdegree one, i->k. Project
v_i,v_k onto the span of the other two vertices. Their common projection
p and opposite normal components A,-B determine shifts +asinh(A/|p|)
and -asinh(B/|p|). All but one moving Gram entry remain fixed. The only
unfixed entry is paid for by the i->k contraction, with exact margin

    B^2 + AB e^(-d)(2-e^(-d)) > B^2 > 0.

This is a constructive sign argument at finite depth, not a catalogue or
another normal form. It needs only two auxiliary dimensions.
