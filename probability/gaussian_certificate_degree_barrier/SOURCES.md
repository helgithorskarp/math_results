# Sources and scope

Checked 26 September 2026. Historical priority and correctness review of this
degree argument are pending. The sole research target remains the bounded-law
dimension-three question of Aishwarya--Li,
[arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2).

The main dependency is the team's
[finite-certificate interface](../gaussian_majorisation_open_stability/CERTIFICATE_INTERFACE.md),
graph h6136, artifact
`bafkreid3z47fpd5wcqjut2fiqcrw5dd43mfvngkwtufqdpxf27a2r76kga`,
source commit `9a1047c925763125c72fa862e9200c40717b9c25`.
We use its normalized H and beta averages (I1)--(I2), selected indices and
Cantelli/local-modulus test (I7)--(I12), and the explicit signed-tail input
in Section 4. The present argument does not find an error in its sufficiency
or completeness statements. It proves a necessary degree for one specified
combination of its inputs. An arbitrary larger valid tail interval or
another modulus/localization argument is not subject to all of this bound.

The [uniform modulus refinement](../gaussian_majorisation_open_stability/UNIFORM_FRONTIER.md),
h6150, source `88bc098a0f98080f8762ddae9b11cc4e63669e95`, concerns approximation
of the unrestricted defect by a compact moment schedule of order O(k^8).
That statement supplies no evaluated sign and is not contradicted by this
fixed-pair strict-certificate degree bound.

H. Cheng, S. P. Tan and Y. Zheng,
[*On continuous expansions of configurations of points in Euclidean space*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.0140),
Theorem 2.1, supply the simplex-flap configuration and its absence of an R5
contracting motion after reversal. Their paper credits the same construction
to Belk--Connelly. We do not reprove or computationally verify nonliftability.
The coordinates used here are the depth-one, sqrt(3)-scaled version, also
recorded in the team's
[rank/Abel fixture](../gaussian_majorisation_rank_abel/flap_fixture.json),
h5964, source `f7c122d6a5ade217930d63da27e67f9a9e55a539`.

The separate source-peak and signed-low-endpoint obligations were already
explicit in the interface; the new observation is their computational
interaction with the unavoidable first selected Bernstein cell. No novelty
is claimed for Taylor's positive-term exponential estimate, the spherical
support bound, or the simplex geometry.

This is not a replay of the cap certificates, a new sample catalogue, or an
extension of the parked finite-geometry campaign. No positive or negative
Gaussian hinge is inferred from certificate failure.
