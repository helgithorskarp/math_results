# Exact finite-frontier handoff

For any supplied finite rational contraction, run `certificate.py INPUT.json`.
`CERTIFIED` signs all Gaussian hinges, at every prior and variance, and both
arbitrary-radius ball-volume comparisons. The result is useful before any
quadrature or moment enumeration. The supplied checker directly validates
the positive chain without importing the graph producer.

The component classifier is complete only for chains that switch each
label once between its supplied endpoints, using displacement-plane and
same-anchor primitives. It is not a complete positivity test, frame search,
or membership test for the full R6 geometric portfolio.

If an adverse hinge input were found, its component chain has an adverse
failing step. For positive weights, the ordered loss and hinge gap telescope.
Some such step preserves or improves the negative gap divided by D/s.
Coordinates are selected from the original endpoint lists; all labels,
weights, the variance, threshold and an enclosing ball remain available.
The extracted step may have tight pairs and collisions. No claim of strict
pair preservation or of a Gaussian integral evaluation is made.

For the accepted rooted indecomposable test class:

1. All cases on at most seven distinct source labels are positive.
2. A remaining adverse case must move at least four labels, have one
   strongly connected component on its movers, and have displacement rank3
   with augmented anchor rank4.
3. Four rows witness the last rank condition exactly; their inconsistency
   is not an adverse hinge. Arbitrarily large such components remain open.

The accepted R5-motion-obstruction cluster remains closed and unchanged.
None of it is replayed or used as a new result here. No new claim about
screw barriers, cap/flap variants, or internal review is part of this packet.
