parallel lines, ruled maps, cylindrical contractions and Schur complements,
and compared the explicit classical lifting results above. It located no
matching statement of this class or rational-clock identity. This is only
search-relative evidence. The Alexander (1985) publisher endpoints returned
HTTP 403; the earlier full-text access gap remains. No absence claim is based
on that inaccessible source or on irrelevant keyword-search results.

The defensible claim is an author geometric theorem with a broad
all-variance/all-radius consequence and explicit separation from two direct
team criteria, **pending independent correctness and priority assessment**.
The inequality transfers, short-map extension, affine product cases and
ordinary leapfrog are prior results. The new mechanism is the simultaneous
five-dimensional interpolation for an arbitrary short planar profile with
a genuinely mixed fiber coordinate.

The program uses only exact fractions and a small sparse polynomial ring.

bounds are finite controls. The continuum theorem, hinge representation,
external comparison theorems and volume-continuity passage remain written
proofs. There is no exhaustive search, quadrature, randomized evidence,
solver, unpublished certificate or claimed independent verification.
