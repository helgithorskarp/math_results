# Attribution and review boundary

The single problem source is Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture,
arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2). It proves the full
comparison in dimensions at most two and a restricted pressure-class
comparison in dimension three. This review does not extend either theorem
or settle the remaining dimension-three sign.

The [bounded-law strict-homothety formula](../gaussian_majorisation_open_stability/PROOF.md),

claimed to be independent of every prior result. The
[strict finite-witness reduction](../gaussian_majorisation_rank_abel/PROOF.md)

explicit Gaussian translation budget; Euclidean Kirszbraun extension
remains a classical external theorem.

Leon Simon's author-hosted
[Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory](https://web.stanford.edu/class/math285/ts-gmt.pdf),
Chapter 2, Theorem 1.4, supplies the standard almost-everywhere
differentiability of Lipschitz maps. The scalar critical-image argument is
written explicitly in REVIEW.md and requires no assumption that the
minimizing envelope is C1. The source's use of the one-dimensional area
formula is valid; the cover proof is an alternative verification of that
step, with no claim of a new measure-theoretic result. Other standard inputs
are Gaussian differentiation, null level sets of nonconstant real analytic
functions, the classical volume bound for Lipschitz maps, elementary
concentration/rearrangement, and dominated convergence.

The current [analytic handoff](../gaussian_majorisation_global_criterion/INTERFACES.md)
and [uniform moment interface](../gaussian_majorisation_open_stability/UNIFORM_FRONTIER.md)

individual exact comparison does not certify the contact covariance sign.
The auxiliary unbounded input of the contact reduction has no finite
support radius for direct use in the latter interface.

This is ordinary mathematical review within the campaign. No computer

dataset, human peer review or new Gaussian sign certificate is evidence for
the verdict. Source/hash checks are reproducibility checks only. The
remaining inequality is the exact unweighted posterior-covariance comparison
at globally ordered contacts; it is not implied here by entropy or MMSE.
