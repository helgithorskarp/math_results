# Sources and scope

The sole problem source is Aishwarya--Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
arXiv:2609.07041v2, checked live on 27 September 2026. Its full
dimension-three majorisation question remains open.

The reference geometry, coordinate boxes, angular charts, exact scalar
primitives and equal-prior endpoint architecture are R2's
[deep-flap certificate](../gaussian_deep_flap_cell/PROOF.md), original6341,
mathematical source `9a3465863e32c53d9594f202a0bcc28fb58578f8`.
Its [first review](../gaussian_deep_flap_cell_review_frontier/REVIEW.md),
accepted6358, independently reproduced the radial cover. Its
[second review](../gaussian_deep_flap_cell_review2/REVIEW.md), accepted6368,
additionally recomputed the complete middle histogram with a different
orbit construction and scalar enclosures. Neither review accepts the
new weight and diffuse-component extension in this directory.

The nonsmooth second-order spatial quadrature is the accepted
[direct hinge oracle](../gaussian_prior_localization/DIRECT_HINGE.md),
original6228, accepted6271. Its one-hinge bound and one-sided omitted
mass bounds justify the coefficient-weighted error in the new proof.
The [paired-cubature frontier](../gaussian_prior_localization/CUBATURE_FRONTIER.md),
original6212, accepted6218, supplies the unchanged rational family.
INPUTS.json pins the exact consumed code, proof and review files.

R3's [earlier full-prior cell](../gaussian_frontier_prior_cell/PROOF.md),
original6335, accepted6347, used a common point-target reference. The
present target is extended and depends on the prior. Standard convex
supporting planes, evaluated through reflected priors, give a finite
upper bound for this dependence. No historical priority is claimed for
convexity, subgradients, label averaging, or Gaussian peak estimates.
The substantive result is the completed sign certificate with these
new quantifiers, rather than an abstract reformulation alone.

The new radial calculation uses the prior-uniform lower source envelope
and upper target envelope in PROOF.md (9). The previous equal-prior
radial bounds cannot simply be reused: their coarse grid fails for these
envelopes. The refined full cover is therefore recomputed. The far-tail
geometry survives because the new mass floor still has logarithmic cost
below3. The middle calculation averages over label orbits, preserving
asymmetric weighted vertices, and accounts for all three auxiliary
mixtures with their correct signs.

The [loss-proportional cubature](../gaussian_prior_localization/LOSS_CUBATURE.md)
published at original6364 now has
[independent acceptance6380](../gaussian_loss_cubature_review2/REVIEW.md).
Its approximation theorem is not a premise of this cell certificate. R8's
dominant-atom window6349 and R4's exposed-edge tail6351 also are not
premises. In particular, the known incompatibility of their published
sufficient endpoint schedules is not bypassed by assuming overlap.

All seven other researchers' bounded new completed reports and relevant
graph contributions were inspected. Closed beta obligations, the
convex-core positive class, and the new eight-site motion obstruction
are preserved. This result says nothing about all variances, the
nonliftability of the damped cell, an unrestricted sign, or a new
Kneser--Poulsen consequence. The global accepted cap remains7/50.
