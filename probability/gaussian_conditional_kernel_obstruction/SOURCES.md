# Sources and exact scope

- Aishwarya--Li, *Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the
  Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture*, [arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
  is the sole problem source. The full dimension-three conjecture remains
  open. The positive folding control here is an elementary isometric-mixture
  case, not a new majorisation or Kneser--Poulsen class.
- R2's [pair-conditioned beta reduction](../gaussian_beta_pair_conditioning/PROOF.md),
  mathematical source `a649ce1267fac02c0e11972a988e545ffab0db79`, supplies
  the conditional kernel and the need to average against pair losses.
  Its [two scoped accepting team reviews](../gaussian_beta_pair_conditioning/REVIEW_STATUS.md)
  are preserved. That theorem signs every order q<=6.
- R8's [seven-factor kernel proof](../gaussian_seven_factor_kernel/PROOF.md),
  source `f5bbd92be43517c18a6958acf900ddd67bac62f8`, now signs q=7 at
  every base index, with explicit PSD certificates. R5's
  [accepting analytic and exact review](../gaussian_seven_factor_review_r5/REVIEW.md),
  source `c93cddbbe5e3943acead1d964d8de2cce6a419de`, independently checks
  the new recentering and Gram argument and discloses authorship of the
  earlier projection premise. Thus the first general unsigned entry is
  b_(8,0). Its finite-base Gram analysis motivated checking
  whether pointwise positivity could hold at every order. It is neither
  replayed nor contradicted by this obstruction.
- R5's [projection source](../gaussian_beta_projection/PROOF.md) and later
  analytic campaign notes distinguish an instantaneous kernel sign from
  an endpoint sign. Those notes also flagged the gap between central gamma
  existence and a six-dimensional noncentral configuration with five
  effective Gaussian degrees of freedom. The present paper makes a finite
  negative-square certificate and the actual-kernel implication explicit;
  no private note is a premise or required input.
- The earlier [admissible instantaneous-lift obstruction](../gaussian_majorisation_local_lift_obstruction/PROOF.md),
  graph height 5980, already gives a negative transformed Hankel determinant
  at an interior lift time for an actual contraction. It is stronger in
  retaining replica averaging in that example. We do not claim the general
  failure of pointwise positivity as new. The present contribution is the
  exact paired-rank classification, a seven-site algebraic moment certificate,
  and failure on every rank-six paired geometry. The different notions of
  averaging and the nonconstructive order bound are kept explicit.
- Scott--Sokal, *Complete monotonicity for inverse powers of some
  combinatorially defined polynomials*, Theorem 2.2 in the
  [authors' expanded manuscript](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/scott/Papers/compmono.pdf),
  states the Bernstein--Hausdorff--Widder--Choquet representation used to
  pass from the negative moment matrix to failure of complete monotonicity.
  Our application is the positive orthant and its nonnegative dual cone.
  The classical theorem and Gaussian square completion are external
  analytic premises. No novelty is claimed for either method.
- R2's [endpoint-scatter obstruction](../gaussian_beta_endpoint_scatter_obstruction/PROOF.md),
  source `89fa448312a98df2543f8c2c483b8a72e9ec4473`, excludes a positive
  inverse-variance Laplace representation of an actual b_(7,0) expression.
  Here the variables are replica weights of a conditional factor instead.
  Here all-order pointwise conditional positivity is classified exactly
  by paired affine rank. Neither obstruction supplies a
  negative endpoint beta or refutes the unrestricted conjecture.
  A separate [accepting review](../gaussian_endpoint_scatter_review2/REVIEW.md)
  is committed at graph height 6242; its checker was not replayed here.

The [accepted global defect bound](../gaussian_uniform_defect_bound/PROOF.md)
and R3's [paired-cubature frontier](../gaussian_prior_localization/CUBATURE_FRONTIER.md)
remain the unrestricted context. R3's [direct hinge enclosures](../gaussian_prior_localization/DIRECT_HINGE.md)
can evaluate actual endpoints with rigorous error; they are not needed in
this algebraic obstruction and were not replayed. No constant is optimized.

The exact certificate is self-contained. Historical priority is unassessed;
the public contribution is the explicit finite witness and its precise
consequence for the current conditional-certificate route.
