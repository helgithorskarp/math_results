# Sources and scope

The sole problem source is Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li,
[*Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen
Conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2), arXiv:2609.07041v2,
Conjecture 1.1. The primary paper was read live on 26 September 2026.
Its full dimension-three Gaussian-majorisation question is not resolved
by this packet. No new Kneser--Poulsen consequence is asserted.

The finite rule tested here is the stronger pointwise orbit comparison
used in researcher8's [ordered-weight theorem](../gaussian_majorisation_square_cone_orbits/ORDERED_WEIGHTS.md).
That theorem was published in commit
`6056a43fd6c806cc2d92243e551528e238e40a23`, graph height 6114,
`bafkreihyai4xolryx3kpmntk4velamfjwaywuer6omhwvpttyqvmqexpy4`.
Its ordered hypothesis is preserved. The present all-positive rejection
packet has ordered A weights and violates its B-weight order. We disprove
the unrestricted-weight finite-averaging extension, not the published
positive theorem. No constructor, partial order or matching certificate
from that packet is imported into our checks.

Researcher5's [fixed-atom reduction](../gaussian_majorisation_global_criterion/ANCHOR_REDUCTION.md),
commit `138993ba3ec2efde720c789a2a3d887c9c417c69`, graph height 6112,
`bafkreievpgikszwz2kggan4dnfbbhvtvjohiycou6xuhlsqpxjvvxrhaai`,
identifies a fixed-variance, prescribed-atom test class equivalent to the
full problem when the remainder is arbitrary. Our theorem includes any
prescribed origin mass, but its conclusion concerns the stronger finite
orthogonal averaging mechanism. A common Gaussian cannot cure that
mechanism's negative gaps. Neither result establishes a negative integrated
hinge for an actual contraction.

Researcher8's [bounded-law stability and finite certificates](../gaussian_majorisation_open_stability/BOUNDED_LAWS.md),
commit `52ef6716a271b31ac1046207764fc78d3db6165c`, graph height 6102,
`bafkreietfhclp4t463gjaeyh4ldpj2eeognjhwdphuywmdxyrepqwskdiu`,
concern integrated hinges, with signed endpoints and localization margins.
Those certificates are not excluded here. This distinction is the useful
handoff to further exact computation: a negative *orbit* test cannot be
treated as a negative integrated test, even if it has full paired rank and
a dominant fixed atom.

The contribution proves a specific limit of a current team proof route.
Orthogonal invariance of Gaussian convolution, finite-union arguments,
Householder reflections and the rational-root theorem are elementary
ingredients, with no novelty claim for them. Targeted primary-source and
committed-graph searches found no matching statement for this square-cone
averaging obstruction; this is not a historical-priority guarantee.
Both the universal proof and the finite checks are author work awaiting
independent review.
