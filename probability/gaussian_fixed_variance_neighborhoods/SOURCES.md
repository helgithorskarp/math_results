# Sources, dependencies and scope

The human-named problem source is Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li,
[*Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen
Conjecture*, arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
13 September 2026. Conjecture 1.1 asks for all convex internal energies;
Theorem 1.4 supplies continuous-contraction monotonicity. The present
unrestricted R3 target remains open. Primary source refreshed on
27 September 2026. A targeted arXiv search did not supply an earlier
fixed-variance neighborhood theorem in this form; that is not a priority
claim. Unrelated smoothing-distance papers are not used as evidence.

The proof's core is self-contained: bounded Gaussian differentiation,
posterior covariance, strict homothety, directional exponential moments,
and L1 perturbation control. Homothety monotonicity and these Gaussian
calculus facts are not claimed as new. The useful bridge is their
quantitative all-threshold join with both endpoints perturbed.

The latest R3 [support-cap localization](../gaussian_support_cap_localization/PROOF.md)
and R4 [rigid-block certificate](../gaussian_rigid_block_certificate/PROOF.md)
were inspected before publication. They address, respectively, an explicit
eventual-noise cutoff and an all-variance geometric motion class. Neither
is used to prove this transfer, and neither is extended by changing a
constant here. R7's [screw primitive obstruction](../gaussian_screw_primitive_obstruction/PROOF.md)
closes a norm-preserving/R5 finite-chain route; no claim is made that the
present neighborhood hypotheses cover that screw pair.

The theorem requires both a reference Gaussian comparison and a
directional exponential-moment comparison (unless Z=cX, when both are
automatic). A support cover supplies displacements, not those reference
signs. Moment matching alone supplies none of these uniform tail inputs.
The source full-covariance floor and the strict homothety reserve are
genuine extra hypotheses. The positive-loss floor deteriorates as that
reserve tends to zero. No global zero-defect or unrestricted cover is
claimed.
