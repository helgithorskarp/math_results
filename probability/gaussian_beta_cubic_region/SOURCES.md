# Sources, dependencies and claim boundary

The problem is Aishwarya--Li, *Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and
the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture*,
[arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2), Conjecture1.1.
The Gaussian product/replica identity and real-power comparison are credited
prior work. This packet does not claim to discover the majorisation/hinge
equivalence, Gaussian interpolation, the Gamma integral, Appell polynomials,
or a general moment representation theorem.

Campaign dependencies, preserved at their existing scopes:

- [Global criterion](../gaussian_majorisation_global_criterion/PROOF.md),
  original6088: beta and hinge normalization, complete endpoint criterion.
  This packet proves signs for only an unbounded proper subset of its tests.
- [Retained replica interaction](../gaussian_replica_interaction/PROOF.md),
  original6362: common R6 Gaussian-mixture lift. Its integer identity is
  extended directly to real orders in Section6 here, with the normalization
  rederived rather than inferred from hypothetical fractional replicas.
- [Seven-factor theorem](../gaussian_seven_factor_kernel/PROOF.md),
  original6244, independently accepted: every base at orders at most seven.
  The present sufficient region includes unbounded orders, but has a large
  base requirement. It does not supersede those stronger low-order results.
- [Complete row twelve](../gaussian_complete_beta_row_eleven/MULTILEVEL.md),
  original6476/review6484: all rows through12; that review also accepts the
  full retained interaction inequalities. No finite-row certificate is
  rerun or extended here.
- [Scalar boundary](../gaussian_replica_scalar_boundary/PROOF.md),
  original6540, author proof awaiting review at the entry snapshot: abstract
  scalar countermodels exclude a universal fixed positive linear aperture
  from the listed scalar premises. They are not Gaussian counterexamples.
  The present proof uses actual Gaussian location-mixture structure and
  establishes a sublinear region, so there is no contradiction.

The accepted conditional rank-six obstruction6267 remains binding. The
new lemma averages the common Gaussian field and the remaining iid law;
it does not sign arbitrary fixed replica configurations. Likewise the
earlier local Hankel obstruction does not prohibit this restricted
high-base derivative region. No unrestricted local or integrated Hankel
positivity is asserted.

The new author claim is the Gaussian localization and one-crossing estimate
in Sections3--5 and its all-order cubic-region consequence. The proof is
self-contained apart from the ordinary definitions and standard elementary
Gaussian integration, which are also calculated explicitly. Exact controls
are internal evidence, not an independent correctness review.

Primary-source searches on 27 September2026 for Gaussian-mixture Mellin
complete monotonicity and high-order Gaussian beta comparison produced no
identified antecedent for this precise estimate. This bounded search is
not a priority proof. Results about Gaussian **scale** mixtures or
complete monotonicity in a noise parameter were not imported as premises
for this location-mixture, real-power-order statement.

Accepted eventual-variance results, positive geometric classes and
fixed-variance stability theorems have different quantifiers. This packet
does not claim a new geometric class, smaller-noise comparison, or any
Kneser--Poulsen conclusion. Full bounded-law R3 majorisation stays open.
