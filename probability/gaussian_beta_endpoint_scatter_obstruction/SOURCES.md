Completely monotone functions and positive Laplace representations are
classical; see Schilling--Song--Vondracek, *Bernstein Functions: Theory and
Applications*, Chapter 1. The proof here needs only the elementary necessary
alternating-derivative condition, and verifies its failure directly by
Gamma moments and Chebyshev's inequality. It does not need the converse
Bernstein theorem or an inverse-Laplace uniqueness theorem.

degree is optimized or used as a premise here. No historical priority claim is made for this
