# Sources and precise dependencies

The sole problem source is G. Aishwarya and D. Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture,
arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2).
The unresolved target is the full bounded-law three-dimensional
majorisation statement. No priority claim is made beyond the sources
searched in this campaign.

The following are durable team artifacts, not claims of external peer
review. Graph identifiers are the immutable artifact references; source
links use the authorized repository's reader paths.

- The [high-noise coarea proof](../gaussian_majorisation_high_noise_window/PROOF.md),
  original6008, `bafkreia7jrgymt6rfik4g5kktjinnt5a2dlvpe4y75kwmemx563nssqh7q`,
  supplies the exact lifted moment/coarea/Abel identity, strong convexity,
  and high-threshold sign window. The present proof extends its estimates
  to absolute first and second level derivatives. It does not assume its
  quantitative interior Theorem B or spherical Theorem C.
- The [independent small-radius review](../gaussian_small_radius_defect_review_frontier/REVIEW.md),
  review6301, `bafkreidb3jx7u4uxhnydsjllidgofa72hzgsstellnwkh4lrm4veeb6v7e`,
  accepts the needed high-noise Theorem A and its coarea normalization and
  modal boundary, as well as the exponential defect. It does not review
  the new derivative bounds in this packet.
- The [small-radius defect](../gaussian_majorisation_high_noise_window/SMALL_RADIUS_DEFECT.md),
  original6279, `bafkreicadnrjyx7f6nhq6ftzlthcsop7xuof7uq7zccaar7z26gdr63p3e`,
  source `debfb7ae35895c71f36d5d89ac4efb372ae7435b`, supplies only
  `E(epsilon)` in (28). The new loss-scaled term is additional.
- The [global moment criterion](../gaussian_majorisation_global_criterion/PROOF.md),
  original6088, `bafkreihp3see622wt3h2fxowhnp6cj4jtz5t53dxp5bmerz7aj5tfo2eeq`,
  supplies the complete beta tests, the Bernstein--Durrmeyer polynomial,
  and its elementary kernel variance estimate. Those constructions and
  equivalences are credited there. The new input is the error proportional
  to the actual loss and its resulting uniformity near zero loss.
- The [signed finite endpoints](../gaussian_prior_localization/SIGNED_ENDPOINTS.md),
  original6287, `bafkreidzywknhi3khr65enmniumcplrqltn7r6igpo3degnh6unhijkii4`,
  are a possible source of the separate endpoint premises. The
  [relative hinge oracle](../gaussian_prior_localization/RELATIVE_HINGE.md),
  original6258, `bafkreigrupfl3ygeobrisweseaoxdunqlnlxyph7gzlmfxkiqob3k6m65q`,
  can supply validated sample enclosures for the log-grid test. Neither
  dependency is silently assumed for arbitrary bounded laws; sign,
  normalization, variance and radius hypotheses must match.

The [seven-factor result](../gaussian_seven_factor_kernel/PROOF.md),
original6244, `bafkreiesoq6imyvs2qs7shkij5lygxmvmb5smgmz4bb3t2wt543faefise`,
is closed and independently accepted. It is context, not a premise of
the new estimates, and this work does not pursue the next beta obligation.

At the prepublication refresh, the
[explicit frontier-cell certificate](../gaussian_frontier_middle_cell/PROOF.md),
original6311, `bafkreif25s5jph7vfopsnlkpodlal5ng56tfzsyomy62pktnhhqwhytchm`,
already supplied actual all-threshold signs on its strict rational cell.
Its variance-one source radius does not satisfy this packet's high-noise
condition; its geometric endpoints and direct quadrature are separate.
Our reusable input is the loss-scaled modulus for cells that do satisfy
that condition, especially when the loss floor is small. We do not claim
to improve or recertify that specific cell.

Also at that refresh, the
[averaged eighth-beta proof](../gaussian_averaged_eighth_beta/PROOF.md),
original6315, `bafkreibv2gicfcp4n4pghj6y3jb5gnmubc42zjwi7w35zpndlrqbxs4svq`,
had signed the next obligation as an author result. It is not a premise
of (24)--(27); this packet makes no new beta-sign claim.

The exact Python checker is a small supporting artifact. Its rational
checks do not replace the analytic proof, and no externally generated
certificate, private ledger, large dataset or numerical hinge observation
is needed to reproduce it.
