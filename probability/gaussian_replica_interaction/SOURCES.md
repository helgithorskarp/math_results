# Sources and claim boundaries

## Primary problem source

Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li, *Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies,
and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture*, arXiv:2609.07041v2:
[official text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2).
Conjecture1.1 is the full majorisation target; Theorem1.3 gives the known
dimension-dependent pressure classes. Equation(61) is the Gaussian product
identity used in the replica normalization. The retained-interaction
inequality and the two beta certificates in this packet are our arguments,
not claims attributed to that paper.

## Binding obstruction and scope

The accepted [conditional-kernel obstruction](../gaussian_conditional_kernel_obstruction/PROOF.md)
and [instantaneous Hankel obstruction](../gaussian_majorisation_local_lift_obstruction/PROOF.md)
exclude stronger pointwise approaches. The new proof first averages the
interaction under a common tilted iid law; it never makes every individual
cross term positive. Its nonlinear moment comparison is compatible with
an adverse instantaneous Hankel determinant.

The new b_(12,0) certificate uses the improved exponent in a way the old
exponent does not validate: the displayed old-exponent scalar lower bound
is negative at r=4/5. This is a scoped comparison of two explicit proof
premises, not a claim of impossibility for all earlier methods. The rational
constants are convenient and not asserted optimal. There is no complete
later beta strip, universal quartic sign or full majorisation theorem.

The [global defect bound](../gaussian_uniform_defect_bound/PROOF.md) is
unchanged. The [prior-stationary contact](../gaussian_prior_stationary_contacts/PROOF.md)
still lacks its nonlocal flux sign. The team's
[full-prior finite cell](../gaussian_frontier_prior_cell/PROOF.md),
[deep-flap cell](../gaussian_deep_flap_cell/PROOF.md), and
[loss-normalized functional estimates](../gaussian_loss_normalized_hinges/PROOF.md)
are complementary progress; none is used to infer a missing sign in this
argument. The [convex-core class](../gaussian_radial_contractions/CONVEX_CORES.md)
has independent correctness acceptance, with priority unresolved. It is
geometric work distinct from this unrestricted replica mechanism.

## Evidence and attribution boundary

The elementary conditional Jensen argument in Section2 is an author proof,
not an appeal to an unverified external inequality. The finite scalar
premises are verified entirely with Python integers and Fraction arithmetic;
universal integration and Jensen are unformalized written mathematics.
An exploratory SciPy1.14.1/HiGHS calculation suggested minorants before their
rational coefficients were fixed. That floating search is not proof evidence
and is not needed for reproduction. No numerical search output is published.

The bounded graph/source refresh found no earlier instance of the displayed
interaction inequality or the two exact beta bounds in this campaign.
The kernel/Jensen principle itself is classical. No historical priority claim
or independent acceptance of the new packet is asserted.
