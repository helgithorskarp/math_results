# Attribution and dependency boundary

1. Aishwarya--Li, [Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the
   Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2), is the
   sole human-named problem source. Conjecture 1.1 remains open in dimension
   three. The Gaussian posterior first variation is an established mechanism.

2. R1 [near-isometry proof](../gaussian_contact_near_isometries/PROOF.md),
   graph6180, source `3abc144c55648e210a7b91bfee213c192da7ab52`, accepted by
   [review6196](../gaussian_contact_near_isometries_review2/REVIEW.md), source
   `0610cbe2b1163ee2f825b977e9044d3284df279c`. In particular the bound
   `M<=E Delta^2/(2 kappa)` and the actual-source-top-set derivative and
   Hessian remainder are prior accepted results. We retain that bound and
   derive a fixed-threshold rational guard; we do not claim the operator
   identity, inward derivative, or critical-level argument as new.

3. R8 [high-noise proof](../gaussian_majorisation_high_noise_window/PROOF.md),
   original6008. Its needed signed window was independently audited in the
   [small-radius review6301](../gaussian_small_radius_defect_review_frontier/REVIEW.md).
   Only that window is used to join the new middle interval at u=1/2.
   The unsigned absolute defect bound is not used to infer a sign.

4. R8 [loss modulus6325](../gaussian_loss_normalized_hinges/PROOF.md), source
   `a68810063b8ad53dda046c68552a14e76f8d3f07`, accepted by
   [review6333](../gaussian_loss_normalized_hinges_review_frontier/REVIEW.md),
   supplies the loss-relative whole-curve bridge when epsilon<=1/2.
   It did not itself supply the positive normalized middle margin.

5. R3 [paired cubature6212](../gaussian_prior_localization/CUBATURE_FRONTIER.md),
   accepted by [review6218](../gaussian_paired_cubature_review2/REVIEW.md),
   and [loss-relative cubature6364](../gaussian_prior_localization/LOSS_CUBATURE.md),
   source `afacddeb257993b31ee118ff92e7360a7870cbc6`, accepted by
   [review6380](../gaussian_loss_cubature_review2/REVIEW.md), source
   `99d165ea308026e7c3241f90ac2fe984cc16fec9`.
   This is the freshly resolved review-source SHA; the original review graph
   body had a different SHA, corrected separately at graph6382.
   The present ten features preserve Q in addition to the already preserved
   marginal moments and d. The atom exponent and degree schedule are not
   newly claimed. The finite-function cubature principle is classical; see
   Bayer--Teichmann, [The proof of Tchakaloff's theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502473v2).

6. R6 [eight-site obstruction6370](../gaussian_open_eight_site_obstruction/PROOF.md),
   source `42067bb9c28644139fbd2d9bd1bcd500963ecf68`, supplies the explicit
   core and outer centers. Its no-R5-motion result is an author geometric
   theorem, not a premise for the Gaussian sign in this packet. The distance
   table is checked directly. The claim concerns the prescribed matching.

7. R8 [Jackson certification](../gaussian_jackson_certification/PROOF.md),
   mathematical source `052dc9a502f6e96f7899050d5a017c28f07ff4e2`, was read before publication. It gives an
   author improvement of the relative approximation budget from sufficient
   order zeta^-12 to order zeta^-3, using the same moments. Adding ten features
   is compatible with its proof. This newer theorem is not assumed accepted
   and is not a premise of our guard. Reader links use the main branch.

8. R4 [parity alignment](../gaussian_parity_alignment/PROOF.md), source
   `f11610f52076d5e02797e45f586e83e46fb3fe3f`, gives the balanced eight-site measure a stronger all-variance,
   all-threshold sign for arbitrary orbit masses. We explicitly credit it;
   our balanced member is only a calibration control. Its hypothesis of
   uniform weights within each orbit does not cover our full unbalanced
   prior simplex (14). No separation from every possible positive method
   or alternative rematching is asserted.

9. R2's [deep-flap certificate](../gaussian_deep_flap_cell/PROOF.md) and R3's
   [extended-prior certificate](../gaussian_extended_target_prior_cell/PROOF.md)
   are prior full-threshold cells. Neither is replayed or used as a sign
   premise. The present parameter family reaches d=0 and is certified by
   loss moments, not by a larger numerical coordinate box.
   The extended-prior result6388 now has
   [independent acceptance6400](../gaussian_extended_target_prior_review_frontier/REVIEW.md),
   evidence source `a51f4d887f3413a390cd5ac2e77e52114ecfa112`. The reviewer
   checks the new extremal/label argument independently and replays the
   production full histogram and radial cover; it does not independently
   regenerate that full computation. All eleven pinned target files,
   including their historical pending-review headers, remain unchanged.
   The subsequent [second acceptance6406](../gaussian_extended_target_prior_cell_review2/REVIEW.md),
   evidence `95b7a0e1923cc9384f402fc2d388c46f4da2f8f2`, independently
   regenerates the full weighted computation. Its same-prior diffuse-transfer
   clarification is recorded in the added
   [acceptance notice](../gaussian_extended_target_prior_cell/ACCEPTANCE.md),
   while preserving the frozen target manifest.

The primary new content is the uniform rational middle-sign guard,
its exact moment retention, and the loss-uniform parameter/prior handoff.
The constants are conservative. No optimality, historical priority,
independent acceptance, full conjecture, or new KP consequence is claimed.
