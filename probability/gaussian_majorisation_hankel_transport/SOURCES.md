# Sources and scope

Checked on 2026-09-26. The single research problem is the dimension-three
case of Aishwarya--Li's majorisation conjecture.

1. Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li, *Gaussian Convolution, Internal
   Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture*, arXiv:2609.07041v2,
   13 September 2026.
   [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2).
   Conjecture 1.1 is the target. Theorem 1.3 supplies pressure-class
   comparisons in higher dimensions; it does not supply every convex
   energy in dimension three. Equation (61) is the Gaussian replica
   identity, equation (66) the continuous-contraction pressure identity,
   and equation (92) the standard lift. Theorem 5.1 gives the geometric
   implication **after** the requisite energy inequalities are established.
   Those known ingredients are rederived or explicitly credited here.

2. Daniel Azagra, Erwan Le Gruyer and Carlos Mudarra, *Kirszbraun's theorem
   via an explicit formula*, arXiv:1810.10288v3.
   [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10288).
   Used only for the classical same-constant Euclidean extension theorem:
   a finite labelled contraction extends to a global 1-Lipschitz map.

3. Daniel Jonathan and Martin B. Plenio, *Entanglement-assisted local
   manipulation of pure quantum states*, Physical Review Letters 83,
   3566--3569 (1999), arXiv:quant-ph/9905071v2.
   [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9905071).
   The standard four-entry catalytic-majorisation pair is used solely
   as an exact negative control for the validator. Its rational Hankel
   witness is checked by both moments and direct piecewise hinge
   integration. It is not Gaussian contraction data.

Classical polynomial approximation and positivity of moment matrices are
not claimed as new. Theorem 1 is their exact application to the normalized
hinge gap, with a finite Gaussian certificate format and a complete
rational-data reduction. Theorem 3 is the explicit obstruction to all
fixed orthogonal versions of a proposed coupling. Targeted searches for
Gaussian majorisation with Hankel/moment criteria found no matching
specialized reduction; this is a bounded search assessment, not a priority
claim.
