# Gaussian and ball-volume comparisons for twisted meridian maps

An author proof permits spatially varying azimuth rotations together with
arbitrary coupled meridian contractions satisfying an explicit uniform
budget. A delayed phase schedule supplies one contracting R5 motion for the
whole class. This gives Gaussian majorisation at every variance for arbitrary
bounded laws, and Kneser--Poulsen inequalities for any number of centers
with independent ball radii.

For meridian Lipschitz constant g<1, transverse factor at most q<1,
cylindrical radius at most R and phase Lipschitz constant L, it suffices that

    (RL)^2 <= 8(1-g^2)(1-sqrt(q))/(q(1+sqrt(q))).

See [the proof](PROOF.md) for the motion, an additional exact pairwise
criterion, degeneracies, and a folding helical example outside the direct
all-frame scalar and strong-coordinate criteria. [Sources and scope](SOURCES.md)
credit the meridian precursor, the classical transfers and R4's obstruction.
No rotational symmetry of the probability law or center list is assumed.

This is a complete author proof awaiting independent correctness and
historical-priority review. It does not solve unrestricted majorisation or
assert exclusion from all compositions of earlier classes.
