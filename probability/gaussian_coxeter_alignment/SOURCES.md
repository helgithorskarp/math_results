## Classical inputs

- G. Aishwarya and D. Li, [Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the
  Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
  arXiv:2609.07041v2. Conjecture 1.1 is the headline target. Theorem 1.4
  supplies the continuous-contraction Gaussian comparison used after
  alignment. The exponential small-noise union transfer is credited
  prior methodology. Neither the general transfer nor the arbitrary
  contraction conjecture is claimed as proved here.
- K. Stempak, [Finite reflection groups and symmetric extensions of
  Laplacian](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.03045), arXiv:2012.03045,
  Corollary 1.2 and the Dirichlet-kernel positivity discussion on page 3.
  Taking Omega=R^d and heat time s/2 identifies the alternating covariance-s
  Gaussian sum with the positive chamber Dirichlet kernel. This is an
  explicit classical premise of the general W theorem. For B3 the proof
  also supplies the classical sinh-determinant/generalized-Vandermonde
  argument directly.
- K. Bezdek and R. Connelly, [Pushing disks apart](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0108098),
  Theorem 1. Its original-dimension special case supplies both arbitrary-radius
  ball-volume comparisons for the final continuous radial motion. The
  preceding alignment comparison is proved separately in this packet.
- D. Azagra, E. Le Gruyer and C. Mudarra,
  [Kirszbraun's theorem via an explicit formula](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10288).
  The usual equal-Lipschitz-constant extension permits the finite support
  maps to be viewed as global contractions. No extension oracle is used.
- A. R. Francis and H. P. Wynn, [Subgroup majorization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.2707).
  Its order is the convex-hull order of group orbits of vectors. This
  related algebraic setting was inspected; the present order instead
  compares the complete distributions of convolved density values.
  This is a scope distinction, not a priority exclusion theorem.
