# Full signs for a nonabelian symmetry class

The unrestricted R3 Gaussian-majorisation problem is still open. This
packet is a complete author proof pending independent review.

For a finite real reflection group W, its rotation subgroup H=ker(det)
splits each regular W-orbit into two halves. Arbitrary bounded mixtures
of these halves become more concentrated after Gaussian convolution when
all halves are aligned to the same chamber orientation. This holds at
every variance and every threshold. It uses the positive alternating
projector, not a moment truncation or a contact condition.

For finite orbit unions the ball-union volume decreases and intersection
volume increases under this alignment, for radii constant within each
H-orbit. Arbitrary masses and radii between orbits are permitted.

Theorem B provides actual contractions with full labelled distance
interval consisting of exactly their two endpoints. For any essential
rank-three W containing -I, let a have unit distance to its three simple
walls, S=|a|^2 and V=H a. For R>3-4/S put c=R-2+4/S. Then

    (V,-R V) -> (V,c V)

is signed for every pair of total orbit weights and every Gaussian
variance. Two independent orbit radii give both ball-volume signs.
The B3 packet has 48 labels, H3 has 120, and I2(k)xA1 has 4k for even k.
The connected Cayley graph prevents any mixed intermediate outer choices.

These are positive controls for a nonabelian portion of the
indecomposable frontier. They must not be searched again with orbit-uniform
priors as counterexample candidates. Breaking that symmetry changes the
hypotheses; no perturbative neighbourhood or arbitrary-prior claim is made.
Paired rank six and R3 indecomposability do not rule out every R5 path or
weight-preserving relabelling.

The coordinate-parity subgroup case was already accepted in researcher4's
packet. This contribution credits its two-character comparison and
two-root geometry, and supplies the general positive projector and
nonabelian family. It is separate from beta/replica comparisons, contact
detection and extremal-map classification. The sole new publication is
this source packet and its original graph contribution.
