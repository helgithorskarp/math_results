# Sources, dependencies and scope

The sole problem source is Aishwarya--Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2),
arXiv:2609.07041v2. The full bounded-law dimension-three question remains
open. The Gaussian replica product formula is classical; its reuse here
is not a new integration theorem.

The [complete Hankel criterion](../gaussian_majorisation_hankel_transport/PROOF.md),
original `bafkreids462diitdof5ijilzcq2xqwftk2bzjr5t2gihnxk327uezbndbm`,
supplies the normalized moments and the rational curvature-square
interpretation of any failed hinge comparison. The present proof writes
out its finite-order estimates; it does not reimplement the general
finite-atomic certification oracle.

The earlier [low-noise degree barrier](../gaussian_low_noise_degree_barrier/PROOF.md),
original `bafkreiad73hqpuryomtsdkzoumtarg3csbpi2d5jld6smpeyvbe5nzh2ly`,
requires positive loss at a nearest target pair. Its normalizing-matrix
argument is reused and credited. The present theorem removes that
qualitative restriction and also treats every target collision pattern.
Its sufficient cutoff need not improve the earlier cutoff on their
common domain. The earlier public packet and its independent-review
status are preserved. This is a generalization of the eventual
finite-order conclusion, not a correction or an all-hinge upgrade.

The [paired-rank result](../gaussian_majorisation_rank_abel/PROOF.md)
motivates checking rank-six fixtures. The principal fixture here is a
genuinely non-orthocentric tetrahedron with three deep reflected normal-ray
sites and unequal priors. All its nearest target pairs are tight. No
claim places it outside every known full positive class.

The geometric lane's [open eight-site obstruction](../gaussian_open_eight_site_obstruction/PROOF.md)
provides a second benchmark, used at its unshrunk template with binary
weights. Its path obstruction is a separate author result pending review,
not a logical premise of the new sign theorem. The exact endpoint
distances and rank are checked here directly. This does not repeat its
path proof or reopen the closed orthocentric flap assignment.

The collision estimate uses the classical Legendre recurrence and
orthogonality; see NIST DLMF [Section 18.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.3)
and [Section 18.9](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.9). The coefficient bound and
the resulting explicit matrix margin are derived in the proof.
Concavity of a negative squared norm, the parallelogram identity,
diagonal dominance, Gaussian merger limits and these orthogonal-polynomial
identities are credited as classical tools.

Targeted primary-literature, repository and committed-graph searches did
not identify the same all-finite degree-exclusion statement. That is not
a historical-priority guarantee. The contribution is the quantitative
control of all active replica minima, together with a separate collision
margin, yielding a sign for every finite Hankel order below an explicit
noise cutoff.

The statement is distinct from the analytic lane's unrestricted quartic
and replica inequalities, the geometric lane's all-variance map classes,
the measure lane's cubature error bounds, and the exact lane's full-hinge
cells. It does not infer their conclusions from bounded-degree positivity.
No raw search output, floating quadrature, interval oracle, or omitted
large certificate is a proof input. Exact finite controls corroborate the
written proof; they are not independent mathematical review.
