# Sources, attribution, and bounded novelty audit

## Primary problem

Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li, *Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies,
and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture*,
[arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2), 13 September 2026.
Checked live on 26 September 2026. Conjecture 1.1 is the sole problem source:
Gaussian convolution should preserve the majorisation comparison induced
by a 1-Lipschitz image. Theorem 1.2 proves the full planar comparison;
Theorem 1.3 gives dimension-dependent pressure classes in higher dimension.
Our finite measures are bounded inputs to the open R3 full comparison.

The hinge characterization of majorisation is classical: all convex
energies with value zero at zero are generated by hinges and a linear
term, with appropriate limiting/integrability conventions. The two masses
are equal, so linear terms cancel. We prove the hinge inequalities directly.
Kirszbraun's extension theorem is used only to extend the specified finite
contraction to a global Euclidean 1-Lipschitz map. The Gaussian-tail,
log-mixture Hessian, and coarea tools are classical; the particular estimates
and their assembly are supplied in full in PROOF.md.

dependencies identified below. Older obstruction results supply provenance

## What is and is not claimed

Targeted primary-source searches for small-noise Gaussian majorisation,
union-volume limits, and contraction comparisons did not locate this
uniform paired-layer criterion. That bounded search is not a priority proof.
The contribution is a complete all-threshold small-variance localization
argument and its completion, using the credited team results, to one
all-variance spatial neighborhood of asymmetric undamped geometries.
The latter radius is existential. Individual analytic ingredients and
finite-subcover compactness are classical.

The equal-radius union comparison used here follows an elementary folding
and relabeling identity, already recognized in the team's scope discussion.
It is not claimed as a new Kneser--Poulsen case. Gaussian majorisation on
these restricted weight and geometric classes does not supply the all-law
hypotheses needed to infer other ball comparisons automatically.
No private floating-point search, finite positive moment table, or apparent
negative quadrature value is used as a mathematical premise. Independent
review of this proof remains pending.
