# What this result changes

The target remains unrestricted dimension-three Gaussian-convolution
majorisation. This author proof does not settle every variance.

The new step is the **sign** of the actual spherical log-MGF gap, not
another sufficient criterion. Formula (15) in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) writes
it as an integral of pair-distance losses times positive posterior products.
Formula (17) extends this to every bounded measure. It rules out negative
spherical-gap witnesses and positive-parameter zeros at positive mean loss,
assuming acceptance of this manuscript.

The previous endpoint contribution at height 6032, independently accepted
at 6048, left precisely this middle spherical-parameter sign open for
arbitrary finite contractions. Theorem A supplies it. Its coercive finite
tail then gives **all thresholds simultaneously at every sufficiently
large variance for every finite contraction**, without a martingale,
small-loss, covariance-floor, or geometric-motion premise.

The arbitrary-radius convex-ball-hull mean-width inequality in Theorem C
also signs the weighted-width question used by the earlier spherical-tail
package. The earlier Gumbel equivalence and endpoint reduction are credited
dependencies, not new claims of this contribution.
