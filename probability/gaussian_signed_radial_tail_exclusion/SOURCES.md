# Sources and claim boundary

The sole problem source is the human-named dimension-three question in
[Aishwarya--Li, Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen
Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2). The full question is not
settled here. Source and graph status were inspected on 27 September 2026.

The four-point convex comparison is an elementary application of Jensen's
inequality and the classical Rademacher coefficient contraction principle.
No new general contraction principle is claimed. The source supplies the
complete four-row barycentric proof, so no external empirical-process
theorem, normalization, or constant is needed as a premise.

The deduction particular to this note is the separate exchangeability of
the iid scalar pair and iid directional pair, which yields the two
contracted coefficients A-B and A+B. Oddness is needed for the sum. This
then signs every directional Laplace product and every spherical tail test
for independent-radius laws under signed radial maps. We did not locate
this precise application in the targeted source search; that is not a
historical priority guarantee.

Relevant team sources:

- [Spherical tail transfer](../gaussian_majorisation_spherical_tail/PROOF.md),
  original `bafkreiakfdyplrgs5zveoa2ocaggbrq4ptyrkfdq5efhatguxxntp7u7pi`,
  gives the route from an actual negative spherical gap to a Gaussian
  counterexample. Its published author argument supplies the interpretation
  of our exclusion; our convex-order and Laplace inequalities do not depend
  on that transfer's correctness. Positive spherical gaps do not imply
  full Gaussian majorisation.
- [Nonnegative radial contractions](../gaussian_radial_contractions/PROOF.md),
  source `78c08178239c5dabd91e4c46cfddd7ffb2e440f9`, proves full Gaussian
  and arbitrary-radius ball comparison for nonnegative profiles. Signed
  profiles are explicitly outside that theorem. The present result is
  stronger in neither conclusion nor overall scope: it permits signed
  profiles but assumes independent radii and directions and only excludes
  the spherical-tail route. It does not extend the prior full comparison.
- Its later [convex-core extension](../gaussian_radial_contractions/CONVEX_CORES.md),
  source `15808c71e5da96669aefdc8736bc721f8e5c6521`, retains nonnegative
  normal-distance profiles. Its reflected-projection collar reaches only
  twice the parallel-body radius. Our radius-four to signed-radius-minus-two
  fold lies beyond that collar about a singleton core. No claim is made
  excluding every other representation by earlier positive classes.
- [Paired rank](../gaussian_majorisation_rank_abel/PROOF.md) explains why
  the seven-site fixture is relevant: its paired rank is exactly six.
  No further sign conclusion is inferred from this rank.
- [Closed orthocentric flap comparison](../gaussian_flap_selector_motion/PROOF.md)
  is preserved. The new signed-radial domain and four-point probabilistic
  argument do not reopen its selector assignment or motion proof.

The counterexample lane owns the present search boundary. It uses no
finite-atomic hinge certification code and does not duplicate researcher 2's
oracle work. The exact checker uses only integers and Fraction. No private
search dump, ledger, credential, or numerical quadrature is a proof input.
The negative-independence control concerns a directional Laplace product;
it is explicitly not a Gaussian or spherical-average counterexample.
