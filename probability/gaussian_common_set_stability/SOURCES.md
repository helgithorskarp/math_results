# Sources, dependencies and claim boundary

The sole problem source is Gautam Aishwarya and Dongbin Li,
[Gaussian Convolution, Internal Energies, and the Kneser--Poulsen Conjecture,
arXiv:2609.07041v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07041v2). Its full Gaussian
majorisation question in dimension three remains open in this packet.
The result here is a quantitative functional interface for its common-set
formulation, with an explicit residual obligation for actual source sets.

The positivity of the Gaussian second-energy difference is classical and
also follows from the elementary product identity (16). More generally,
Aishwarya--Li's [The Kneser--Poulsen Phenomena for Entropy,
IMRN 2025, rnaf140](https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2025/12/rnaf140/8160080)
proves the Gaussian Renyi comparisons under contractions. This note does
not claim a new second-energy inequality. It quantifies its transfer to
specific common-set tests and keeps the additional profile-error term.

The singleton-reference ball transfer is already in Aishwarya, Alam, Li,
Myroshnychenko and Zatarain-Vera,
[Entropic exercises around the Kneser--Poulsen conjecture,
arXiv:2210.12842v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.12842), Theorem 2.4.
Radial monotonicity, reflection pairing, the Gaussian derivative bounds,
the Taylor improvement for a function with bounded Hessian, and fixed-level
hinge estimates are standard ingredients. They are proved or attributed
here, not asserted as separate new discoveries. Targeted primary-literature
searches were made on 26 September 2026; no historical priority claim is
based on those searches.

The decisive team dependency is researcher 5's
[isometric-reference theorem](../gaussian_isometric_reference/PROOF.md),
commit `3a70618cd4611e258dcd275bdf45a139fd44e459`. Its reference transfer,
exact zero-slack characterization and positive transverse coefficient are
credited inputs. Section 2 recalls their proofs to expose precisely what
is used. Its full zero-minimizer classification is not reproved or claimed
here. This packet adds the quantitative translation gain, an all-weight
coercive estimate, and the explicit source-top-set error. Both packets are
author mathematics awaiting independent review. Publication is not acceptance.

Recent cube-set reductions, broad cap classes and matrix-path regularity
boundaries were inspected as team context. They are neither premises nor
subjects of this proof. No deformation theorem, cube localization, new
geometric class or Kneser--Poulsen result is asserted.

The proof is analytic. No Gaussian coefficient has been numerically
certified, no unknown prior has been tested, and no program or formal system
is claimed to establish the theorem. The explicit coefficient floors exist
for a fixed finite configuration, but their effective enclosures are inputs
for the finite-atomic producer. There is no uniform floor when geometry,
reference support, variance or volume degenerates. The source-shell bound
is o(epsilon), with no universal power at a critical level.

The finite producer can use the result immediately as an input contract:
a lower bound on pair-distance loss or the convex translation model, minus
a certified source-set remainder, is a signed profile certificate. Whether
such certificates cover the unrestricted target remains unproved. This is
the exact bridge supplied, rather than an entropy-to-majorisation equivalence.
