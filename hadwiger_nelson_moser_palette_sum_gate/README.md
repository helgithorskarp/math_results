This package rejects one exact multi-forcing composition: the point-sum of
the published eleven-point Moser relation source M and sixteen-point
opposed-palette source P, in their displayed coordinate frames.

The 176 labelled sums collision-merge to **174 distinct plane points**.
Their complete strict unit-distance graph has **578 edges** and the supplied
proper four-colouring. All 27 constituent relations are simultaneously
satisfied. The attempted composition therefore fails the non-four gate and
is retired. It is not a record candidate, a stronger full-interface theorem,
or a classification of other sums, phases, source choices or architectures.

The whole object was selected before computation, with raw cap 11*16=176.
It contains 16 translated copies of M and 11 translated copies of P, sharing
points throughout the address matrix. This supplies a finite co-realization
without guessing equality constraints between physically distinct points.
It uses both established forcing sources and is distinct from attaching one
local palette driver, the retired Parts master, and the E457 connector lane.
The first ordinary four-colour query was positive; no sustained search or
phase/copy-count expansion followed.

The input relations are:

- M has four independent terminals A,B,C,D and requires
  `colour(A)!=colour(B) or colour(C)!=colour(D)`. Its complete graph has
  11 points and 19 edges. [Source](../hadwiger_nelson_moser_four_terminal_relation/README.md),
  [independent review](../hadwiger_nelson_moser_four_terminal_relation_review1/README.md).
- P has eight marked independent terminals and forbids 104 of 2,795 canonical
  four-colour terminal patterns. Its complete graph has 16 points and 25
  edges. [Source](../hadwiger_nelson_opposed_palette_cells/README.md),
  [independent review](../hadwiger_nelson_opposed_palette_cells_review1/README.md).

This package consumes those sources, without re-reviewing their negative
relation proofs. The negative construction conclusion needs only the
literal full colouring below; restricting it to each embedded source gives
compatible extendible terminal assignments regardless of the imported proof.

Coordinates and complete graph

Use the positive radicals generated by 3,5,7,11. A 32-integer tuple is
`(sum a_mask*sqrt(rad_mask), sum b_mask*sqrt(rad_mask))/24`, where
`rad_mask` is the product of the primes whose bits are present. These 16
radicals are linearly independent over the rationals, so tuple equality is
physical equality. All points are in the real field
`Q(sqrt(3),sqrt(5),sqrt(7),sqrt(11))` in each coordinate.

The verifier reconstructs M from
`rho=(1+i*sqrt(3))/2`, `t=(5+i*sqrt(11))/6` and the published eleven-point
formula. It reconstructs P directly from its five-cycle and equilateral
triangle formulas. The optional producer instead reads the source rows in
`inputs.json`; the source-file hashes are recorded there. Each sum is exact.
Sorted coefficient tuples define the final vertex numbering.

The only repeated addresses `(M index,P index)` are
`(0,1)=(1,0)` and `(2,1)=(3,0)`. Every translated source remains injective.
The 27 copies contribute 574 distinct inherited unit edges. Exhausting all
15,051 unordered physical pairs finds four additional edges:
`55--74`, `59--80`, `90--112`, `97--116`. These contacts are included in the
colour check. No proximity tolerance, omitted contact or imposed nonunit
edge is involved. The displayed support also contains points outside the
previously four-coloured source field `Q(i*sqrt(3),i*sqrt(11))`.

Certificate and scope

`certificate.json` gives a 174-character proper four-colouring and canonical
point/edge hashes. `verify.py` checks every physical edge and the 27 full
source restrictions. All sixteen M terminal words normalize to `0120`;
all eleven P terminal words normalize to `00111111`. These particular
simultaneous words prove satisfiability; they are not an enumeration of the
whole composite terminal relation. The graph contains a Moser spindle and
therefore is exactly four-chromatic, by the usual two-diamond argument.

Discovery used Kissat 4.0.4 once, on an ordinary exactly-one four-colour CNF
with four edge-inequality clauses per physical edge, no pins and no symmetry
breaking. It returned SAT in under 0.01 seconds. Replay trusts only the
decoded word, not the solver. No UNSAT trace is asserted or needed.
The five-colour candidate gate was not reached.

Reproduce from this directory with Python 3.11 and its standard library:

```sh
python3 -B verify.py
python3 -O -B verify.py
python3 -B controls.py
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
```

Both verification modes print `expected.json`. The controls compare every
point tuple, all 15,051 complete norm coefficient vectors and every edge
between the producer's squarefree-gcd multiplication and the verifier's
bit-mask formula reconstruction. They also reject five damaged certificates.
These are author-side checks, not an independent review or formal proof.
`validation.json` records versions, commands, hashes and runtimes.

For optional graph/CNF regeneration, choose a new output directory:

```sh
python3 -B build.py --out /tmp/hn-moser-palette-sum
kissat --time=30 /tmp/hn-moser-palette-sum/full4.cnf
```

Generated graphs, CNFs, logs and caches are not publication inputs. The
