# Verification and reproducibility

`reproduce.py` compiles two distinct native programs, regenerates the full canonical table, checks that table under every invertible map, recomputes the cycle-index certificate in Python, runs small exhaustive physical controls, and independently reads every Ramsey clause in four full43 formulas. All comparisons raise explicit exceptions and remain active with Python optimization enabled.

## Census checks

The production generator completed in 27.1784 seconds and the independent complete checker in 5.6222 seconds with GCC 12.2.0, `-std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -O3 -mpopcnt`. The producing child peak was 134,036 KiB. Times are observations on the campaign host, not required certificates or predicted completion times.

The checker tests 220,933,440 representative-map pairs, verifies all stabilizers and orbit sizes, and checks both Burnside's orbit count and the raw profile mass. Its independently generated group has 20,160 distinct maps. All 12 cycle types are also regenerated by `burnside.py`. The canonical table SHA256 is:

    bd1161b4261eb1ee3f8bc2cd0104a062c858bf38d596cf725acbfb195b158bac

Complete rank-two weights 4/5 and rank-three weights 9/10 controls were run in ordinary and AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer builds. Their tables matched byte for byte. A sanitized rank-four lookup-table control checked 262,144 transformations against direct maps, weights and ranks. Fresh reproduction additionally rejects six corrupted small certificates: missing last representative, duplicate representative, and falsely zero orbit size, at each of ranks two and three.

To repeat the sanitized controls in a private directory, compile both native sources with `-O1 -g -fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-omit-frame-pointer`, and run these parameter sets:

```text
cover 2 4 5 small2.tsv
check_cover 2 4 5 small2.tsv
cover 3 9 10 small3.tsv
check_cover 3 9 10 small3.tsv
cover 4 19 20 unused.tsv --selftest
```

## Physical interface checks

`audit.py` compares the implementation with direct definitions rather than trusting solver exits. It checks:

* All 3,200 small factor-label cases on sides 3+3 and 3+4 over F2², including unsorted columns, against direct span, rank and cap conditions.
* All 83,136 primary internal-edge assignments passing those base conditions against literal five-set checks; 78,984 are good small graphs.
* Four known-sector guard controls and 1,384 mutations of equivalence-gate outputs.
* Sixteen full43 changes of basis and vertex permutations, transporting and comparing all 14,448 physical edge coordinates.
* All 443 internal decisions in each of the four formula categories, for 1,772 single-coordinate checks.
* A known good42 fixture by all 850,668 literal five-sets, and seven negative target/transcript controls. The 42 fixture and the saved 23-vertex projection cannot pass the target-size boundary.

For one representative in **each** of the four categories, the audit writes the actual DIMACS file, reads it back, independently reconstructs each physical literal by closed-form vertex/pair indexing, and compares every Ramsey clause with its five-set. It also checks the clause's truth against an independently constructed dense 43-vertex graph. In total it checks 7,592,730 emitted Ramsey clauses. All four deterministic full43 fixtures contain forbidden five-sets and are deliberate negative controls, not candidate constructions.

Exact CNF hashes, clause-length histograms and counts are in `EXPECTED_AUDIT.json`. Four complete representative formulas, rather than all 10,959 formulas, are materialized for this audit. The uniform physical encoding and the proof establish coverage for every table entry. The representative tests support that reasoning and do not constitute a solver decision for the whole family.

## Trust boundary

This is an ordinary exact integer computation and a mathematical proof, not a proof-assistant formalization. Trust includes the documented previous graph-theoretic theorems and full-support exclusion, standard finite-field linear algebra, the source translations and host compiler/runtime. The census is independently checked by a different group enumeration and by Burnside's lemma. The graph verifier imports no producer code and examines actual edges. No probabilistic estimate, heuristic feasibility claim or incomplete solver trace is used as a certificate.

The public table and expected JSON are compact evidence. Binaries, large CNFs and exploratory run logs are not published; all required verification data regenerate from source. `PUBLIC_REPLAY.json` records successful fresh ordinary and optimized-Python runs. No target SAT/UNSAT result or new Ramsey bound follows from those replay statuses.

The fresh ordinary and optimized-Python replays took 256.329885 and 185.757424 seconds, respectively; both produced audit SHA256 `4ce493a63cf754fc7329d8236820d8cb53e442089b403e5ffa638fb098c44c29`. Peak Python RSS was 120,948 / 122,368 KiB. Five standalone interface commands also passed; the documented all-blue-internal example is deliberately rejected with 77,288 blue five-sets.
