## 1. Polynomial sector

Let p be a complex degree-nine polynomial with all zeros in the closed
unit disk and critical multiset H six times, L1 and L2, allowing
coincidence. Fix a marked zero a. A critical point at a contributes
infinity. Otherwise assume

\[
 {a-L_1\over a-L_2}\in\mathbb R_{>0},\qquad
 {2\over|a-H|}\ge {1\over|a-L_1|}+{1\over|a-L_2|}.       \tag{1}
\]

The first condition says the two light points lie on a common ray
from a. It permits unequal distances, a nonreal heavy point and

\[
 S_1(a)={6\over|a-H|}+{1\over|a-L_1|}+{1\over|a-L_2|}
 \ge8.                                                   \tag{2}
\]

The inequality is strict for |a|<1. Equality is exactly
p(z)=C(z^9-a^9), C nonzero and |a|=1, within this sector.

noncollinear light points. The general three-phase critical6+1+1
inequality remains unproved here.

The new step is a quantitative light-radius splitting principle.

\[

\[
