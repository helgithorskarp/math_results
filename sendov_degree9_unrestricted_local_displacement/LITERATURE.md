and identifies exponent one as its strongest endpoint. Theorem 1.3 proves

reports the all-degree ordinary Sendov proof and states the stronger
family as Conjecture 19. The present work preserves this stronger local
stability frontier rather than reopening the historical degree-nine seed.
The historical [Meng manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235) and
[explicit large-degree threshold paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256)
were refreshed for context; a later low-degree summary is not a refutation
of the former manuscript.

The matrix compression, secular identity and Riesz projection calculus
are classical. [Tang--Zhang's matrix framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3)
provides predecessor polynomial context. No new general spectral theorem
is claimed. The new calculation uses an analytic upper support for a
specific previously derived angular functional, explicit norm estimates,
and the normal cone of the maximum-slope constraint.

Bounded live searches for Sendov angular local maxima, displacement
stability and first-power stability found no primary duplicate of the
precise eight-coordinate local chart and loss theorem. This is only
search-relative novelty, not a historical-priority determination.
