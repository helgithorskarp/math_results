adapted from Jeremy Tan Jie Rui's Shibuya repository:

- repository: <https://github.com/Parcly-Taxel/Shibuya>

certified here.

## Team evidence boundary

An earlier R3 intake extracted the 21-vertex/38-edge EI21 abstract graph from a
high-precision numerical seed and checked its four-chromaticity and binary
pair neutrality.  It deliberately did not exactify the physical graph and did
not run a contact or host sweep.  This package does not present that intake as
a new theorem.  Its new object is a different parameter value defined by the
extra physical unit contact `(0,14)`, isolated by a square 38-variable exact
system, followed by the one declared commutative self-sum continuation.

The reviewed 343-point S343 lens class and the reviewed flexible-fish
difference body were banked before this work and are not used here.  No Parts
receiver, E457 connector, R2 conditional-core completion, or R3 realization
lane is invoked.
