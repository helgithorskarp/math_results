  concerns the quadratic case. The present small-energy theorem does

  Ordinary Sendov's reported resolution is a distinct statement.
- [Tang--Zhang, arXiv:2508.10341v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3),
  Lemma 3.4, records the classical Cheung--Ng matrix representation.
  Our reciprocal characteristic `9R(q)-qR'(q)` also follows directly
  by differentiating the translated original polynomial.
- [Sharma--Bhandari, *Skewness, kurtosis and Newton's inequality*, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.2896v1),
  Lemma 1 and Theorem 1, supplies classical moment context. The
  [current arXiv metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.2896) marks v2
  withdrawn for personal reasons. The campaign angular audit gives
  its own real-rooted polynomial proof, including degenerate cases,
  of every scalar moment inequality used here; no unverified assertion
  is inferred from the withdrawn version.

All four primary texts and the withdrawal metadata were inspected.
These sources supply background and credited inequalities, not a
