# Independent review of the nine-branch tail selector

## Verdict

Accepted with high confidence inside the stated computational and catalog
trust boundaries.  This reviews h3869, *R(5,5): canonical 12-vertex residual
selectors preserve nine complete physical branches*, at substantive source
commit `b827c28fbc9edcaf0a01bdfd7639f4b92e2f7f12`.

The selector formula is satisfiable if and only if, conditional on completeness
of McKay's Ramsey(3,5,12) catalog, one of the nine h3863 branches
`gc1-rR-s1-tT` with `R=5,6,7` and `T=0,1,2` contains a good43.  This is an
intermediate physical-family encoding theorem.  It does not cover the other
30 h3863 branches, construct a good43, exclude a branch, or prove
`R(5,5) >= 44`.

## Mathematical audit

In each source branch the final 12 vertices have no red triangle and, for a
good43, no blue K5.  Catalog completeness therefore supplies an isomorphism
to one of the 12 representatives.  Applying that isomorphism to the entire
tail transports all 372 tail-to-core edges, which remain free variables.  It
preserves the physical good43 conditions and the larger red-K4-free closure
union.

Conversely, a selected catalog graph can be split successively into three blue
triangles: at residual orders 12, 9, and 6, `R(3,3) <= 6` and the absence of a
red triangle force one.  The last triple has zero, one, or two red edges, so it
reconstructs one of the three `t` branches.  Relabeling within the triples
restores their prescribed shapes and root-signature orders.  This proves an
existential equivalence under relabeling, not a bijection or graph census.

The restricted core normalization is also sound without importing the
unreviewed h3859 lemma.  The active whole-block comparisons are exactly the
adjacent nonroot blocks of each equal-color class for `r=5,6,7`; all constrained
unions consist of whole blocks.  Prefix variables define lexicographic order
exactly, so every orbit has an auxiliary extension while tied keys may retain
multiple representatives.  No symmetry denominator is asserted.

## Independent finite audit

`independent_check.py` uses only the Python standard library and imports no
target module.  It independently:

- verifies the 15-entry target and 29-entry reviewed-parent manifests;
- parses all 12 graph6 records and checks red-triangle and blue-K5 exclusion;
- enumerates ordered repackings, obtaining final-red-edge totals
  `(43536, 52278, 26814, 0)` and at least one repacking for every catalog graph;
- reconstructs the 792 physical edge variables, two block-color variables,
  12 selectors, 227 shared predicates, and 150 prefix variables;
- exhausts 4,096 selector assignments, 5,448 predicate-definition cases,
  37,752 tail membership cases, every local prefix truth assignment, and all
  256 projected four-bit comparator inputs; and
- processes all `C(43,5)=962,598` physical five-subsets in both colors and all
  conditional closure four-subsets.

```bash

python3 -O -B ramsey_r55_tail12_selector_review1/independent_check.py .
```

## Trust boundary

The accepted h3863 parent is imported rather than reopened.  The numerical
covering direction additionally imports Brendan McKay's claim that the linked
12 records are all Ramsey(3,5,12) graphs; the local file identity and every
record's defining forbidden subgraphs are independently checked.  Catalog
completeness is unnecessary for checking a satisfying physical graph.

The historical CaDiCaL receipt remains `UNKNOWN`.  Its parser controls and CNF
identity reproduce, but the omitted solver binary, stdout, witness, and
266 MB partial DRAT stream were not independently recovered.  They are not a
certificate and play no role in this verdict.  Remaining trust includes
CPython arbitrary-precision and file semantics, SHA-256, the independent
transcription, and ordinary hardware.  No correction is required.
