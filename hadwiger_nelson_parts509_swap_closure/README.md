# One-point swap closure of the Parts 509-vertex graph

## Result and scope

Let `G` be the strict unit-distance graph on Jaan Parts's 509 algebraic points
(vertex set `V`, 2,442 edges, `chi(G) = 5`, 5-vertex-critical; see
`../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_criticality`).  For a point `q` of the plane and a
vertex `u`, write `G - u + q` for the strict unit-distance graph on
`(V \ {u}) ∪ {q}`.

The evidence in this directory establishes, with exact arithmetic throughout:

1. **Completion points.**  Exactly 1,158 points of the plane outside `V` are at
   unit distance from at least three Parts vertices, of which 697 are at unit
   distance from at least four.  Their neighbour counts are

   ```text
   3:461  4:322  5:180  6:119  7:29  8:27  9:16  10:4
   ```

   and every such point lies in `Q(sqrt3, sqrt5, sqrt11)^2`.

2. **Swap closure.**  For every point `q` of the plane not in `V` and every
   vertex `u`, the graph `G - u + q` is 4-colourable, **except** for exactly
   eleven pairs `(q, u)`, listed in `swap_drat_reports.json` and in the
   `swaps` field of `swap_certificate.json`.  For those eleven pairs
   `G - u + q` is not 4-colourable (DRAT-checked), so each is a further
   509-vertex 5-chromatic unit-distance graph, and by item 3 each is
   5-vertex-critical.

3. **No 508 by "delete two, add one".**  Each of the eleven swap points has a
   single swap vertex.  Therefore for every point `q` of the plane and every
   pair `{u, v}` of Parts vertices, the strict unit-distance graph on
   `(V \ {u, v}) ∪ {q}` is 4-colourable.  No 5-chromatic unit-distance graph on
   508 vertices arises from the Parts graph by deleting two vertices and adding
   one point of the plane.

Item 3 generalises the earlier four-point search in
`../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_degree10_replacements` from four explicit points to
all points of the plane.  Parts (2020, Section 5.1) reports that replacing a
pair of vertices by one new vertex "as a rule" fails to leave the local minimum;
this directory turns that heuristic observation into a certified statement for
the 509-vertex record graph.  Nothing here improves the bounds
`5 <= chi(R^2) <= 7` or the 509-vertex record.

## Reduction to a finite exact computation

- A point at unit distance from three distinct points of `K^2`,
  `K = Q(sqrt3, sqrt5, sqrt11)`, is their circumcentre (three points of a
  circle are never collinear), hence lies in `K^2`.  So every point with at
  least three unit neighbours in `V` is a `K`-rational intersection point of two
  unit circles about Parts vertices.
- For `p1 != p2` in `K^2` with `d^2 = |p1 - p2|^2`, the unit circles about them
  meet in `K^2` exactly when `rho^2 = (4 - d^2)/(4 d^2)` is a square in `K`, in
  which case the points are `m ± rho (-dy, dx)` with `m` the midpoint.
  `kfield.field_sqrt` decides squareness exactly by recursion down the tower
  `Q ⊂ Q(sqrt3) ⊂ Q(sqrt3, sqrt5) ⊂ K` (a root `s + t sqrt p` of `a + b sqrt p`
  satisfies `s^2 + p t^2 = a`, `2 s t = b`, so `s^2 = (a ± r)/2` with
  `r^2 = a^2 - p b^2`; every candidate is tested and a returned root is
  confirmed by exact squaring).  Negative elements are never squares because
  all embeddings of `K` are real.
- A `K`-point with `m >= 2` unit neighbours is generated by all `C(m, 2)` pairs
  of them; keying the generated points by exact coordinates recovers each
  neighbour set as the union of its generating pairs.  As a built-in check, all
  509 Parts vertices are regenerated with exactly their 2,442-edge
  neighbourhoods, and every point with at least three neighbours is rescanned
  exactly against all 509 vertices.
- If `|N(q) \ {u}| <= 3` the graph `G - u + q` is 4-colourable trivially from
  the certified 4-colouring of `G - u`.  Otherwise `q` is one of the 697
  points with at least four neighbours, and a proper 4-colouring of `G - u` in
  which `N(q) \ {u}` uses at most three colours extends to `q`.  Restricting
  such a colouring to `V \ {u, v}` still leaves `N(q) \ {u, v}` short of a
  colour, which gives item 3.

No floating-point number enters the enumeration or the verification; floats
appear only as a convenience column in `completion_points.json`.

## Certificate

`swap_certificate.json` lists, for every vertex `u`, a family of proper
4-colourings of `G - u` (1,190 rows in total, at most eight per vertex) packed
as in the sibling certificates: 508 retained vertices, four 2-bit colours per
byte.  Together with the 509 base deletion colourings of
`../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_criticality/certificate.json`, they cover all
`697 * 509 = 354,773` instances except the eleven declared swaps:

```text
covered_by_base_deletion_rows = 343361
covered_by_family_rows        = 11401
declared_swaps                = 11
max_swaps_per_point           = 1
```

`swap_drat_reports.json` records, for each declared swap, the plain
4-colouring CNF of `G - u + q` (2,040 variables, 10,280–10,288 clauses, three
triangle pins), the CNF hash, the DRAT proof size and hash, and the
`drat-trim` core statistics.  All eleven proofs returned `s VERIFIED`.  The
CNFs and proofs (57–99 MB in total) stay under `/scratch`; the CNFs are
regenerated deterministically by `certify_swaps.py`.

## Verification

Solver-free primary verifier (CPython 3.11, SymPy only for parsing the
published coordinate expressions through the sibling `parts509.py`).  Keep
environments under `/scratch`:

```bash
python3 -m venv /scratch/parts509-swap-venv
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python swap_certificate.py verify \
  completion_points.json swap_certificate.json
```

This re-enumerates all completion points exactly (about six minutes with eight
processes), compares them with the committed list, decodes every colouring,
checks 2,894,629 retained-edge inequalities, and replays the coverage.  Expected
summary:

```text
all_checks=true
q3_points=1158  q4_points=697  swap_instances=354773
covered_by_base_deletion_rows=343361  covered_by_family_rows=11401
declared_swaps=11  max_swaps_per_point=1
```

Independent checker, importing none of the primary code (own coordinate
parsing into SymPy's `AlgebraicField`, all 129,286 unit tests redone in that
representation, completion points re-derived by double-precision screening of
circle intersections followed by exact circumcentre confirmation and exact
rescans, own row decoder):

```bash
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python independent_check.py \
  completion_points.json swap_certificate.json
```

It must report `all_checks: true` with the same counts.  The numerical screen
only proposes candidate triples; every listed point is confirmed exactly, and
the comparison with the primary enumeration is on the exact family of
neighbour sets.

## Regeneration

```bash
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python enumerate_completion_points.py
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python swap_search.py --solver cadical195
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python swap_certificate.py build \
  swap_results completion_points.json /scratch/swap_certificate_new.json
/scratch/parts509-swap-venv/bin/python certify_swaps.py \
  --swaps swaps.json --outdir /scratch/parts509-swap-drat \
  --cadical /path/to/cadical --drat-trim /path/to/drat-trim
```

The swap search uses CaDiCaL 1.9.5 through PySAT with one incremental solver
per deleted vertex and selector literals per completion point; every model is
decoded and checked before it is stored, and colourings are shared greedily
across points.  The run took 1,201 solver calls.  Regenerated certificates may
contain different valid colourings and therefore different hashes.

## Trust boundary

- Exact geometry trusts the published coordinate input, CPython integers and
  `fractions.Fraction`, SymPy 1.14.0 parsing and denesting of the coordinate
  expressions (through the sibling `parts509.py`), and the small tower-field
  implementation in `kfield.py`.  The independent checker replaces the last
  two by SymPy's `AlgebraicField` arithmetic.
- The 4-colourability side (items 1–3 apart from the eleven exceptions) trusts
  only the solver-free checkers; SAT solvers were used to find witnesses.
- The non-4-colourability of the eleven swap graphs trusts the recorded CNF
  encoding, CaDiCaL sc2021 (Debian package 1.5.3-2), and the C implementation
  of `drat-trim` at commit `2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985`.  It
  also uses `chi(G - u) = 4` from the sibling certificate to conclude that the
  swap graphs are vertex-critical.
- `chi(G) = 5` itself is the separately reviewed sibling result.
- No proof-assistant formalisation was performed.
