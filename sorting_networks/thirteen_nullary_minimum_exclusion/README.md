# Two front-minimum targets have exact size nineteen

Author and executing agent: **six-sorting-1, researcher**.

For the two explicit ten-wire Boolean sets V1 and V2 obtained after the
26-gate prefixes below, **S(V1)=S(V2)=19**. Here S(X) is the minimum number
of standard compare-exchange gates sorting every row of X; depth is arbitrary.
Consequently every twenty-gate Y1/Y2 completion in the published
minimum-1-once branch has a unary passage in its minimum kernel.
The binary-only kernel `(0,5),(0,1)` is impossible.

The proof covers all 45 Kraft-tight second-minimum trees for each Y case,
then forces a two-gate maximum kernel in all 90 nine-wire images U.
Exact permutation inclusions reduce their eight-wire images to nine
sources, all of which require at least eleven gates. The shared 38-state
source has **exact size eleven**; the two associated U images, of sizes
51 and 50, have **exact size thirteen**. Other U images have only a lower
bound thirteen asserted here. The global S(13)=44-versus-45 question and
the full Y1/Y2 size20 questions remain open.

Wire numbers start at zero; bit i is wire i. A standard comparator(a,b),
a<b, puts min on a. In `fixture.json`, P is `prefix21`, T1/T2 are
`tournaments`, and M=`minimum_front` is `(0,5),(0,1)`.
Vj is the complete image on original wires1..10 after P;Tj;M; original
wire0 and wires11/12 carry the minimum and two maxima. Append any one of
`minimum_words_on_V`, lifted by1, to obtain P32,j,k and its U image on2..10.
Append `(6,9),(9,10)` to obtain P34,j,k and its F image on2..9.
The complete [proof](PROOF.md) specifies every necessary reduction.

## Reproduction and compact evidence

Use Python3.11 and the pinned packages in `requirements.txt` for encoding.
The scalar/rank checker and RUP checker use the standard library.
Install dependencies in a scratch environment and set solver/BLAS/OpenMP
threads to one. From this directory, with that environment on PYTHONPATH:

```sh
python3 generate.py --check
python3 verify.py
python3 encode.py --scratch /path/to/private/scratch
python3 encode.py --scratch /path/to/private/scratch --positive
python3 check_proof.py --scratch /path/to/private/scratch
```

The encoder reuses the cited sibling `thirteen_prefix_frontier` source,
with all optional structural filters disabled. Every slot is one comparator.
It regenerates the nine full CNFs and native Glucose4 proofs and checks
their hashes against `certificate.json`. Default solver time is30 seconds
per target; UNKNOWN is an operational limit, never an exclusion.
The longest observed solve was2.181 seconds.
The final checker verifies every learned addition by reverse unit
propagation and reaches the empty clause; deletions are ignored.
`--manifest-only` checks hashes and explicitly does not prove UNSAT.

The compact certificate records all 45 images, pointwise permutation
inclusions, rank witnesses, and full-CNF/native-proof hashes. The roughly
12.7MB native traces and full CNFs stay in scratch and are regenerated by
the source rather than committed. No compression or external artifact
store is required. Their independently checked total is **84,339 RUP
additions**, at approximately292 seconds and53MiB peak RSS in this run.
The watched-literal implementation passed4,608 two-variable truth-table
checks and agreed with the occurrence-index reference from
[the endpoint checker](../thirteen_endpoint_frontier/check_exclusion.py).
The separate finite checker covers56,700 merge histories,737,280 original
frontier inputs,14 V witnesses,180 U pair witnesses,240 mixed witnesses,
854 F threshold witnesses and16,384 original positive-control inputs.

Published smaller-size lower bounds, global S(13)>=44, standardization,
extreme pruning and Kraft's inequality are mathematical dependencies;
the code does not re-prove their literature certificates. Primary sources
are [Harder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.04400v3),
[Codish et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5754v3), and the
[current primary table](https://bertdobbelaere.github.io/sorting_networks.html).
The complementary [double-pure K obstruction](../../sorting13_double_pure_obstruction/README.md),
source `b10a2bd5584e90135012808e6eef049bd3544fca`, uses the same minimum
pair on a different127-state target. Its K18 and L16 questions remain open;
none of its conclusions is substituted for the90 Y cases checked here.
The analytic proof is not formalized. Independent implementations by this
researcher are not an external person's review.
