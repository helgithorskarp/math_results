# Primary sources checked

Checked 21 September 2026.

1. Michał Adamaszek, *Clique complexes and graph powers*, Israel Journal
   of Mathematics **196** (2013), 295--319.
   [arXiv:1104.0433v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.0433),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-012-0166-1).
   The source of Section 5 was inspected.  Its Theorem 5.2 states
   `Cl(L(G)) ~= Cl(G)^(2)` for a connected nondiscrete graph.  Theorem 5.1
   concerns the different total-graph construction.

2. Shuchita Goyal, Samir Shukla and Anurag Singh, *Topology of Clique
   Complexes of Line Graphs*, Art of Discrete and Applied Mathematics
   **5**(2) (2022), P2.06.
   [arXiv:2009.12130v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12130),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.26493/2590-9770.1434.bf4).
   The arXiv source was inspected.  Lemma 3.2 gives the same two-skeleton
   equivalence and supplies an independent modern statement of the imported
   theorem.

3. Yair Caro, Josef Lauri and Christina Zarb, *Index of Parameters of
   Iterated Line Graphs*, Discrete Mathematics **345**(8) (2022), 112920.
   [arXiv:2105.02496v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02496),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2022.112920).
   The source of Section 4.3 and Theorem C was inspected.  It proves the
   maximum-degree index-three result for prolific graphs and identifies the
   type-A subdivided claws and sufficiently subdivided `S_(2,2)` family in
   its extremal analysis.

4. Erich Prisner, *Homology of the line graph and of related graph-valued
   functions*, Archiv der Mathematik **56**(4) (1991), 400--404.
   [publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01198228).
   The bibliographic record was verified, but full text was not accessible.
   The review therefore makes no historical-priority claim for homology
   consequences of the splitting.

The target's literature boundary is responsible: the one-step homotopy
equivalence and maximum-degree growth are prior results, while the audited
new package is the explicit iterated splitting and its asphericity
consequences.  Absence of an identical theorem from this bounded search is
not evidence of global priority.
