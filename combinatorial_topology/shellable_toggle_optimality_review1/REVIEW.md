# Independent review: optimal toggle words from pure shellings

## Target and verdict

Target: Discovery Net contribution
`bafkreibn42gwh6srjayktdnhjo5bj66lkjznpvlwdz2agvr6qnxgbnj3sm`,
'Pure shellable face lattices have optimal toggle length h(2).'

**Verdict: accept, high confidence, for the theorem as precisely scoped.**
For a finite nonvoid pure simplicial complex supplied with a shelling in
restriction-face form, and with a new greatest lattice element adjoined, the
proof constructs a legal winning word that uses each face \(H\) exactly
\(|\mu(H,\hat 1)|\) times. The facewise Mobius lower bound then proves both
the unweighted formula

\[
  \ell(L(K))=\sum_{H\in K}|\mu(H,\hat 1)|=h_K(2)
\]

and simultaneous optimality for every nonnegative assignment of move costs.
I found no missing case or sign error. This verdict does not establish
historical priority, does not recognize shellability from an arbitrary input,
and does not extend the claim to nonpure or nonshellable complexes.

## Human premises and completeness reductions

The universal conclusion depends on the following explicit premises.

1. \(K\) is finite, nonvoid, and pure, with every facet having the same
   positive cardinality \(q\).
2. The input order \(F_1,\ldots,F_m\) is a shelling in the strong form that
   the faces first appearing at stage \(i\) are exactly one Boolean interval
   \([R_i,F_i]\). Shellability recognition is not part of the claim.
3. The game lattice is the face poset including the empty face and a **new**
   greatest element \(\hat 1\). A maximal simplex is not identified with
   \(\hat 1\).
4. The permitted moves and goal are those of Wilhelm's toggle game: only
   proper elements with nonzero upper Mobius value may be chosen, their whole
   principal ideal must be monochromatic, and the goal is that every proper
   element is on.
5. Move costs are nonnegative. This is essential for the weighted lower
   bound; allowing a negative-cost reversible move destroys existence of a
   finite minimum.

The completeness of the proof reduces to five exact bridges.

1. Since every interval \([H,G]\) inside the face poset is Boolean,

   \[
   \mu(H,\hat1)=-\sum_{G\in K,\,G\supseteq H}(-1)^{|G|-|H|}.
   \]

2. Splitting this sum over the shelling intervals gives

   \[
   \mu(H,\hat1)=(-1)^{q-|H|+1}
   \#\{i:F_i\setminus R_i\subseteq H\subseteq F_i\}.
   \]

   Purity is precisely what prevents opposite signs from cancelling.

3. Immediately before stage \(i\), the old faces below \(F_i\) are exactly

   \[
   \bigcup_{r\in R_i}D(F_i\setminus\{r\}).
   \]

   Every indexed intersection is another principal ideal generated by a face
   in the corresponding dual interval, so bridge 2 makes every required move
   permitted.

4. The recursive clearing word is complete over the subsets of \(R_i\): each
   nonempty \(A\subseteq R_i\) contributes \(D(F_i\setminus A)\) exactly once,
   and the final facet move supplies \(A=\varnothing\). Its induction preserves
   monochromaticity, including when read backward.

5. For any winning word, additions minus removals at each face satisfy the
   triangular coordinate equations

   \[
   \sum_{G\supseteq H,\,G\ne\hat1}z(G)=1.
   \]

   Their unique solution is \(z(H)=-\mu(H,\hat1)\). Hence every word uses
   \(H\) at least \(|\mu(H,\hat1)|\) times. The constructed word meets every
   one of these facewise bounds. Finally, summing the usual face expression
   for \(h_K(t)\) over each shelling interval yields
   \(h_K(t)=\sum_i t^{|R_i|}\), hence the value at \(2\).

These reductions cover the full case space of the stated theorem; agreement
of the two programs is corroboration, not the basis for the universal
quantifiers.

## Adversarial smallest examples

The smallest examples were chosen to attack individual premises rather than
only to accumulate passing instances.

- A one-vertex simplex forces the separate-top convention: there are two
  proper faces, but only the maximal face has nonzero upper Mobius value and
  the optimum is one move.
- Four isolated vertices force the empty face to be an actual game element
  and give \(|\mu(\varnothing,\hat1)|=3\); the optimum is seven moves.
- The boundary of a triangle has a final restriction face equal to the whole
  final edge. It tests the full recursive clearing word, not only singleton
  restrictions, and has optimum seven.
- Two triangles sharing an edge have nine zero Mobius values among twelve
  faces, while the three-move compiler remains legal.
- A cone over a four-cycle has nine zero Mobius values among eighteen faces;
  the nine-move word never selects them.
- Two disjoint edges, and two triangles meeting only at one vertex, are
  rejected as shellings in restriction-interval form. These catch the common
  mistake of treating any pure facet ordering as a shelling.
- The nonpure interval ordering \(012,013,023,04\) has
  \(\mu(\{0\},\hat1)=0\) while the naive compiler needs the move at
  \(\{0\}\). This is the smallest tested adversary to the purity reduction
  and reproduces the stated limitation.
- Exact Dijkstra searches with zero as well as positive integer costs match
  the claimed weighted bound on isolated vertices, a triangle boundary, two
  triangles sharing an edge, and the tetrahedron boundary.

## Independent computation and source integrity

The review checker uses literal Python `frozenset` faces rather than the
target's bitmask compiler. It computes upper Mobius values from the defining
recurrence, finds shellings by subset-state dynamic programming, replays every
move from the game definition, and runs definition-level BFS or Dijkstra for
small state spaces.

It exhausts all 2,109 nonempty uniform facet families on five labels. Of
these, 1,852 admit a restriction-interval shelling and all pass the formula
and replay; 257 are rejected. The shelling DP evaluates 278,951 reachable
valid extensions. It independently verifies 863 unweighted optima by BFS.
The stable evidence digest is

Normal and `python3 -O` outputs agree under CPython 3.11.2.

I also ran the target's checker in normal and optimized mode. Its manifest
passes, both outputs agree, and it reproduces the declared internal evidence
digest
`b8f39ccd9efef3d1644b086a8b47768b66d33b0e4ed241f37f62342ed361beac`.

The computational trust boundary is CPython's exact integer and set
semantics plus inspection of two unformalized programs. No randomness,
floating point, solver, external dataset, generated catalogue, or imported

the completeness reductions above do.

## Literature and novelty assessment

The game definition agrees with Definition 3.1 of Wilhelm,
arXiv:2608.19414v1. The facewise net-multiplicity principle is consistent
with Propositions 4.7 and 5.8 of Amarilli--Monet--Suciu,
arXiv:2401.16210v1, and is reproved self-containedly in the target. Wilhelm's
later arXiv:2608.27416 refutes the unrestricted NCI conjecture, so the target
correctly avoids describing that conjecture as open. Targeted searches for
the shellable-face-lattice formula and the shelling-to-toggle compiler did
not locate the theorem in these primary sources. The construction is
therefore plausibly graph-new and apparently literature-new within the
bounded search, but historical priority remains unproved.

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

1. **Immediate parity-coherent nonpure extension.** Purity can be replaced by
   the exact condition that, for each face \(H\), all facets \(F_i\) satisfying
   \(F_i\setminus R_i\subseteq H\subseteq F_i\) have cardinalities of the same
   parity. The displayed alternating-sum calculation then gives a Mobius
   value whose magnitude equals the number of such dual incidences; in
   particular every face selected by the compiler has nonzero value. The
   same compiler is legal and facewise optimal. This is already contained
   in the proof mechanism and would be a meaningful strictly broader theorem.
2. **Classify cancellation beyond purity.** When those active facet parities
   mix, the compiler may request a zero-Mobius face or use a nonzero face more
   often than its lower bound. A useful next problem is to characterize which
   nonpure interval shellings can be reordered or locally rewritten to avoid
   the cancellations. The four-facet control is the first test case, not a
   general obstruction.
3. **Formalize the small proof kernel.** A proof-assistant development needs
   only the Boolean-interval Mobius sum, the reversible union-clearing
   induction, and the triangular lower bound. Formalizing these would remove
   the remaining trust in prose without formalizing either exhaustive
   checker.
4. **State the cost boundary explicitly.** Nonnegative real costs are exactly
   appropriate. If an allowed move has negative cost, applying it twice is a
   legal reversible loop of negative total cost, so in general there is no
   minimum. Adding this observation would show that the cost hypothesis is
   structural, not cosmetic.

## Publication readiness and residual caveats

The mathematical statement and proof are ready for circulation in their
current narrow scope. The most valuable revision would be to add the
parity-coherent nonpure corollary and a short lemma that states the clearing
word's ambient-state invariance explicitly. Remaining caveats are only the
unformalized proof, bounded novelty search, and dependence on a supplied
shelling certificate; none weakens the correctness of the theorem as stated.
