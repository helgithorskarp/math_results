# Flag completions of equivelar tori: a homotopy trichotomy

Let T be a finite simplicial torus triangulation with degree six at every
vertex, and suppose its graph is K4-free. Its flag completion has exactly
one of these forms:

| Missing triangles | Completion, up to homotopy |
| --- | --- |
| None | The original torus |
| n/3 disjoint parallel triangles partitioning the vertices | S¹ wedged with n/3 copies of S² |
| Nine, on nine vertices; graph K3,3,3 | Eight copies of S² wedged together |

Consequently the completion is aspherical exactly when T was already flag.
The key obstruction is local: a nonfacial triangle must pass straight through
each vertex, because a turn through two link edges would produce a K4.
The classical triangular-lattice representation then reduces the question
to the periods of length three in the three edge directions.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) contains the all-size proof, including the disk-attachment
homotopy argument and the limited Whitehead consequence.
[REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) distinguishes the classical representation,
the known nine-vertex example, and the present structural statement.
No historical-priority claim or general bounded-degree classification is made.

## Reproduce the exact checks

Python 3.11 or later; standard library only. From this directory:

```sh
python3 verify.py
```

The first output line must be `PASS: exact checks match expected.json`, followed
by the compact JSON in [expected.json](expected.json). Expected counts:

- 3,014 HNF parameter tuples `(a,b,c)`, for the lattice generated by
  `(a,0),(b,c)`, where `a,c>0`, `0≤b<a`, and `a*c≤60`.
- 519 fail the six-distinct-neighbors simplicial-surface prerequisite.
- 2,495 valid degree-six torus triangulations; 317 have K4 and are excluded.
- 2,178 satisfy the theorem: 2,024 torus cases, 153 one-axis cases, and one
  three-axis case. These are lattice parameter tuples, **not isomorphism
  classes of graphs or triangulations**.
- Every applicable tuple passes exact equality of its enumerated missing
  triangles with the short-axis orbits, and an independent F2 boundary-rank
  computation of the predicted Betti numbers.
- Five fixtures, including twisted quotients and every homotopy type, also
  pass an integral H1 presentation reduction using unimodular unit pivots.
  Their residual matrices vanish, certifying the displayed integral homology
  without a torsion inference from finite-field ranks.
- Seven- and eight-vertex torus controls contain K4 and have clique numbers
  seven and four. They test the essential dimension hypothesis. A `[2]`
  matrix control verifies that the integer routine retains a nonunit residual.

The deterministic detailed-row digest is

## Trust boundary and limits

The verifier separately constructs surface faces and graph cliques, validates
connectedness, orientability and six-cycle surface links, then computes
homology. All arithmetic is integer or F2. The written geometric proof,
standard CW quotient facts and its interpretation of disk attachments remain
unformalized. A finite homology check alone would not establish a homotopy
type, asphericity, or an all-size theorem.

The code has no solver dependency and no external mathematical data.
No downloaded paper, raw fixture dump, private graph state, or environment is
part of this contribution. `MANIFEST.json` hashes the public source and compact
evidence (excluding the manifest itself).

The named target is complete. A generic extension to other surfaces, a larger
parameter table, or an unrestricted degree-six census is not a continuation
of this result.
