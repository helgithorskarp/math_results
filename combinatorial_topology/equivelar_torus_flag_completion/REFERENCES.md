# Prior work and scope of the literature check

Primary sources checked on 2026-09-20:

- Amos Altshuler, *Construction and enumeration of regular maps on the torus*,
  Discrete Mathematics 4 (1973), 201–217.
  [Publisher record and abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X73800020),
  DOI 10.1016/S0012-365X(73)80002-0.
  The triangular-lattice quotient construction is classical. This record
  describes a construction of all maps of type {3,6}; the full 1973 text was
  not needed as a proof dependency here.
- Bojan Mohar and Jesús Salas, *On the non-ergodicity of the
  Swendsen–Wang–Kotecký algorithm on the kagomé lattice*, Journal of Statistical
  Mechanics (2010), P05016, DOI 10.1088/1742-5468/2010/05/P05016.
  [Author-hosted full paper](https://www.sfu.ca/~mohar/Reprints/Inprint/BM10_JSM-P05016_Salas_SwendsenWangKotecky.pdf).
  Theorem 2.2 explicitly represents every degree-six torus triangulation as
  a triangular grid with opposite sides identified with a possible shift.
  Our proof gives the short flat-metric justification of that representation.
- F. Larrión, M. A. Pizaña, and R. Villarroel-Flores,
  *Equivariant collapses and the homotopy type of iterated clique graphs*,
  Discrete Mathematics 308 (2008), 3199–3207.
  [Publisher full text](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2007.06.021).
  Theorem 4.5 concerns finite orientable graphs whose induced neighborhoods
  are C6 and the homotopy of their iterated clique graphs. Such graphs are
  already flag surface triangulations. That hypothesis excludes the extra
  neighbor chords caused by the missing triangles treated here. Also,
  the clique-graph operator in that paper differs from flag completion.
- Hongxuan Yan and Luoyi Sun, *When Single-Scale Betti Counts Are Not Enough:
  Ring Statistics for Structured Network Populations*, Mathematics 14
  (2026), 2520, DOI 10.3390/math14142520.
  [Publisher full PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/mathematics/mathematics-14-02520/article_deploy/mathematics-14-02520.pdf).
  Section 3.3 already records that the 3×3 triangular toroidal grid has flag
  Betti vector (1,0,8), and that the K7 torus triangulation has contractible
  flag completion. We therefore do not present either example as new.
- Armindo Costa, Michael Farber, and Danijela Horak,
  *Fundamental groups of clique complexes of random graphs*, Transactions of
  the London Mathematical Society 2 (2015), 1–32.
  [Publisher paper](https://academic.oup.com/tlms/article/2/1/1/1898345).
  Its sparse two-complex results use a strict density threshold; the torus
  triangulations here have e=3v and sit at equality. This motivates a separate
  geometric analysis, rather than treating this as an incremental degree
  bound. We use no theorem from that paper in our proof.

Targeted searches combined “flag completion”, “equivelar”, “6-regular”,
“triangulated torus”, “clique complex”, “homotopy”, and “parallel triangles”.
The sources checked did not give the present complete trichotomy for K4-free
completions with missing triangles. This is a bounded literature check, not
an assertion of bibliographic completeness or historical priority. The
contribution is the explicit straight-triangle obstruction and its all-size
homotopy consequence, assembled from elementary topology and the classical
lattice representation. The general Whitehead conjecture remains outside
its scope.
