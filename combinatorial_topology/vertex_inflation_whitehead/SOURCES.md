# Sources and scope

Primary sources inspected on 2026-09-21.

1. Anders Björner, Michelle Wachs and Volkmar Welker, **Poset fiber
   theorems**, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 357
   (2005), 1877--1899. [Author manuscript](https://www.math.miami.edu/~wachs/papers/fibers.pdf),
   section 6A, Theorem 6.2 and Corollary 6.3. The vertex inflation
   construction and absolute wedge decomposition are known. In dimension
   two their formula already gives an absolute asphericity criterion:
   the extra summands are graphs and 2-spheres, and the three local
   conditions in this note are precisely the absence of extra spheres.
   We do not claim that criterion as an independent new discovery.

2. Michelle Wachs, **Poset Topology: Tools and Applications**,
   [author notes](https://www.math.miami.edu/~wachs/papers/toolnotes.pdf),
   section 5.5, Theorem 5.5.2. This gives the variable-multiplicity
   inflation formula and attributes it to Björner--Wachs--Welker.

3. Anton Ayzenberg and Nadya Khoroshavkina, **Homotopy types of finite
   étale spaces and generalized inflations**, 9 February 2026,
   [arXiv:2602.08694v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08694v1).
   Theorems 1.2 and 5.2 extend the fiber-theorem approach to suitable
   generalized flabby inflations. Those broader constructions are not
   covered by our relative-collapse theorem. The inspected paper treats
   absolute homotopy types and does not state the arbitrary-subcomplex
   inheritance result proved here.

4. Andrey Mikhovich, **Rational and p-adic analogues of J. H. C.
   Whitehead's conjecture**, Izvestiya: Mathematics 89 (2025), 274--318,
   [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.00281), introduction.
   It distinguishes the open integral Whitehead conjecture from the
   analogues it establishes. We make no claim about those analogues.

5. Sam P. Fisher and Yash Lodha, **A note on normal generation and the
   first l^2-Betti number**, 26 August 2026,
   [arXiv:2608.25988v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25988v1), introduction.
   This recent primary source discusses a route to a special Whitehead
   case through the Osin--Thom inequality. Its counterexamples are not
   finitely generated; it leaves the finitely generated version open.
   It does not provide a counterexample to Whitehead. Our inflation
   argument has no dependency on this group-theoretic route.

The claimed increment is specifically an explicit collapse **relative to
the canonical original complex**, together with a proof that its
triangle/free-edge sequence restricts to every subcomplex. An absolute
homotopy splitting by itself does not transfer a statement about arbitrary
subcomplexes. The resulting inheritance theorem closes the possibility
of obtaining a new Whitehead counterexample by vertex inflation of a
base already satisfying the subcomplex property.

The proof is elementary and self-contained apart from standard facts
about elementary collapses, spherical fundamental cycles and adjoining
edges to CW complexes, which are explained in the proof. It does not
require the general poset fiber theorem. Targeted searches for vertex
inflation/duplication with relative collapse, asphericity and Whitehead
inheritance found no identical statement. This is a bounded search,
not a historical-priority claim. The result should be assessed as a
modest structural refinement of known inflation topology.

There is no theorem here about arbitrary finite étale sheaves, higher
dimensions, non-simplicial cell inflations, or arbitrary aspherical
two-complexes. The computation does not certify the asphericity of its
input bases. No proof-assistant formalization or independent peer review
is asserted.
