# Primary sources and search boundary

Sources were checked on 2026-09-21.

1. Anders Björner, Michelle Wachs and Volkmar Welker, **Poset fiber
   theorems**, Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 357 (2005),
   1877--1899, [author manuscript](https://www.math.miami.edu/~wachs/papers/fibers.pdf).
   Section 6 defines variable-multiplicity vertex inflation.  Theorem 6.2 gives
   the homotopy wedge decomposition by suspended links, and Corollary 6.3 gives
   the corresponding homology decomposition.  This supports the target's
   attribution of the absolute inflation topology.
2. Michelle Wachs, **Poset Topology: Tools and Applications**, section 5.5,
   [author notes](https://www.math.miami.edu/~wachs/papers/toolnotes.pdf).
   Theorem 5.5.2 restates the variable-multiplicity inflation formula and gives
   a useful independent presentation of its scope.
3. Anton Ayzenberg and Nadya Khoroshavkina, **Homotopy types of finite étale
   spaces and generalized inflations**, arXiv:2602.08694v1,
   [primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08694v1).  Construction 4.4 is
   the same classical vertex inflation; Theorem 5.2 extends the absolute wedge
   decomposition to inhabited flabby generalized inflations.  The paper does
   not state the target's relative elementary collapse or arbitrary-subcomplex
   inheritance.
4. Andrey Mikhovich, **Rational and p-adic analogs of J. H. C. Whitehead's
   conjecture**, Izvestiya: Mathematics 89 (2025), 274--318,
   [arXiv:2105.00281v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.00281v2).  This distinguishes
   the rational and pro-p analogues proved there from the integral conjecture.
5. Sam P. Fisher and Yash Lodha, **A note on normal generation and the first
   l2-Betti number**, arXiv:2608.25988v1,
   [primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25988v1).  The introduction states
   the Whitehead asphericity conjecture and discusses a special group-theoretic
   route.  Its counterexamples are not finitely generated, and it leaves the
   finitely generated replacement open.  This is status context only and is
   not used in the reviewed proof.

Bounded searches combined the phrases `vertex inflation`, `inflated simplicial
complex`, `relative collapse`, `elementary collapse`, `arbitrary subcomplex`,
`aspherical`, and `Whitehead conjecture`.  They recovered the classical
absolute formula and the 2026 generalized-inflation paper, but no identical
relative-collapse or Whitehead-inheritance theorem.  Novelty is therefore
search-relative and terminology-sensitive.
