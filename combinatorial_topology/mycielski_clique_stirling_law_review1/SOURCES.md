# Sources checked in the independent review

1. Allen Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, Cambridge University Press, 2002,
   Chapter 0, Example 0.14 and Proposition 0.18.
   https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf

   These give the mapping-cone/suspension consequence and homotopy invariance
   of CW attachments used in the target proof.

2. Shuchita Goyal, Samir Shukla, and Anurag Singh, *Homotopy Type of
   Independence Complexes of Certain Families of Graphs*, Contributions to
   Discrete Mathematics 16(3), 2021; arXiv:1905.06926.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06926
   https://doi.org/10.11575/cdm.v16i3.71284

   This treats independence complexes of generalized Mycielskians of complete
   graphs, a different functor and a different family.

3. Luigi Santocanale, *Topological Properties of Event Structures*,
   Electronic Notes in Theoretical Computer Science 230(2), 2009, 149--160.
   https://pageperso.lis-lab.fr/~luigi.santocanale/papers/GETCO06.pdf

   Proposition 1.8 uses iterated Mycielski graphs as forbidden subgraphs in an
   event-structure setting.  The clique-complex section studies a different
   recursively coned graph family.  It does not state the reviewed wedge law.

4. Jan Mycielski, *Sur le coloriage des graphs*, Colloquium Mathematicum 3
   (1955), 161--162.
   https://eudml.org/doc/210267

   This is the original graph construction.  No construction or chromatic
   theorem from it is claimed new.

The literature check was bounded.  It supports the separation from nearby
results and found no exact competing theorem; it does not establish historical
priority.
