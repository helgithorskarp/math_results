# Primary literature and scope of the advance

Sources inspected live on 2026-09-20.

1. Antoine Amarilli, Mikaël Monet, and Dan Suciu, *The Non-Cancelling
   Intersections Conjecture* (2024),
   [arXiv:2401.16210](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16210),
   [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.16210).
   Propositions 4.7 and 5.8 already force the net Möbius multiplicities;
   this is the credited source of our universal lower bound. Section 7
   explicitly distinguishes the left-linear and sign-consistent
   strengthenings. Its proposed two-covering-zero criterion is different
   from pure shellability. Our cone-over-four-cycle example has four
   minimal covering zeroes at the empty face. The paper's original general
   conjecture has since been refuted; it must not be described as open.

2. Hermann Wilhelm, *The Non-Cancelling-Intersections Conjecture Fails for
   Left-Linear Trees* (2026),
   [arXiv:2608.19414](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19414),
   [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19414).
   Definition 3.1 gives the exact game used here. Section 3.1 connects
   legal words to left-linear dot-algebra trees, and Section 9 poses the
   small-obstruction question. The present theorem supplies a positive
   structural class, not a resolution of that minimum-order question.

3. Hermann Wilhelm, *Refutation of the Non-Cancelling-Intersections
   Conjecture* (2026),
   [arXiv:2608.27416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27416),
   [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27416).
   The later paper refutes even unrestricted witnessing trees and still
   motivates smaller explicit obstructions. Its existence theorem is
   context, not a premise in the shelling proof.

4. William Q. Erickson and Jan Kretschmann, *The Sum of Width-one Tensors*,
   Enumerative Combinatorics and Applications 4:1 (2024), article S2R7,
   [primary journal PDF](https://ecajournal.haifa.ac.il/Volume2024/ECA2024_S2A7.pdf).
   Section 4.1, equation (7), records the classical restriction-face
   expression for a pure shellable complex's h-polynomial. We use exactly
   that convention and also derive it directly. Shellability and this
   h-polynomial identity are established background, not new claims.

5. Michael S. Yang, *A Join-Tree Sufficient Condition for the
   Non-Cancelling Intersections Conjecture* (2026),
   [publisher's statement](https://ijctjournal.org/join-tree-sufficient-condition-non-cancelling-intersections-conjecture/).
   The publisher states a sufficient condition requiring a join tree on
   maximal non-cancelling intersections and non-cancelling separators.
   This was checked as nearby prior art, not used as a proof dependency.
   The cone-over-four-cycle example has maximal allowed ideals indexed by
   its four triangular facets; its rim vertices force a cycle in any
   proposed running-intersection tree. Thus that named condition does not
   cover all instances of the present theorem. We do not claim a converse
   comparison or an independent review of the paper.

The apparent new content is the legal shelling-to-word construction attaining
the known Möbius lower bound face by face, hence the exact h(2) length and
simultaneous optimality for all nonnegative move costs. The broad class can
have arbitrarily many zero Möbius values. The cancellation calculation is
elementary and may have other formulations in specialist literature.

Targeted searches for shellability, face lattices, Möbius toggle games,
non-cancelling intersections, and shortest sign-consistent words found no
matching theorem in the inspected sources. This is a bounded search-relative
novelty assessment, not a claim of exclusive priority or exhaustive coverage.
The exact proof does not depend on novelty or on any unverified external
mathematical theorem.
