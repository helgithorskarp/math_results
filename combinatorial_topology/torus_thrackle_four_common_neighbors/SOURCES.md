# Primary sources and priority boundary

Checked 2026-09-20.

1. G. Cairns and Y. Nikolayevsky, **Bounds for Generalized Thrackles**,
   *Discrete & Computational Geometry* 23 (2000), 191–206.
   [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/PL00009495),
   [full primary PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/PL00009495.pdf).
   Lemma 5(a), p. 203, is the four-cycle homology obstruction used here.
   We credit the upper bound to its immediate application, rather than
   claiming a new obstruction.

2. C. Hernández-Vélez, J. Kynčl, and G. Salazar,
   **Thrackles on nonplanar surfaces**,
   [arXiv:2506.11808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11808),
   [full primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11808).
   Corollary 16 gives the general bound
   $\operatorname{tg}(K_{2,n})\le\lceil(n-1)/2\rceil$.
   Section 5.4 recalls the homology obstruction and studies the asymptotic
   genus question. Specializing the cited upper bound to $n=4$ gives two;
   the construction here gives one.
   The fetched HTML identifies itself as v1, submitted June 13, 2025,
   while its internal date reads August 24, 2026. We do not infer a
   separately numbered revision from that date.

We did not find an explicit $K_{2,4}$ torus thrackle in the two full
primary sources above or in targeted searches combining the graph's
notation with “thrackle” and “torus.” That is a bounded literature
check, not an exhaustive priority determination. In particular, we do
not claim this construction is the first historically.

Searches also located G. Cairns, M. McIntyre, and Y. Nikolayevsky,
*The thrackle conjecture for K5 and K3,3* (2004),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/342/06129).
Indexed manuscript excerpts recall the four-cycle lemma and, in
Section 6, exclude $K_{2,4}$ in the plane. A complete manuscript
download timed out during this audit. We do not
count that paper as fully inspected or rely on its toroidal claims.

The asserted advance is the explicit attaining rotation scheme and
the resulting sharp common-neighbor threshold, with all construction
data supplied here. No conjecture about unrestricted thrackle genera
is declared resolved.
