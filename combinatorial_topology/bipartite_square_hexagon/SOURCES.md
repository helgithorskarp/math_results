# Sources and scope of the proposed increment

Primary texts checked on 2026-09-22. Bounded searches did not locate the
stated ambient six-cycle survival or exact asphericity criterion for all
`C4`-free bipartite incidence graphs. This is a search-relative novelty
statement, not certified historical priority.

1. **Michał Adamaszek, _Clique complexes and graph powers_, Israel J.
   Math. 196 (2013), 295--319.**
   [Primary manuscript, arXiv:1104.0433v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.0433).
   Proposition 2.2 gives collapse for girth at least `3r+1`; its `r=2`
   case already proves our no-six-cycle direction. The introduction
   explicitly identifies the square of a six-cycle with an octahedral
   sphere. Theorem 3.1 gives a closed-neighborhood clique-cover criterion,
   and Theorem 3.2 gives sufficient forbidden-subgraph conditions.
   Theorem 5.1 gives the total-graph sphere splitting: when one part of
   our incidence graph consists of subdivided graph edges, that case is
   already known. The proposed addition concerns survival under arbitrary
   larger `C4`-free bipartite incidence structure, with its integral cycle
   detector. No new high-girth collapse or total-graph theorem is claimed.

2. **Kazuhiro Kawamura, _A variant of the bipartite relation theorem and
   its application to clique graphs_, Contributions to Discrete
   Mathematics 6(2) (2011), 91--100.**
   [Journal primary PDF](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/download/62059/46707/177343).
   Observation 2.1 decomposes the square complex into its two half-squares
   and the Dowker bridge. Theorem 2.5 proves the bridge collapses onto
   either Dowker complex. Proposition 3.2 and Theorem 3.3 compare relative
   homology and homological equivalences. These general constructions,
   including the acyclic matching reproduced in our proof, are prior art.
   Our `C4`-free specialization identifies the bridge with the original
   graph up to collapse and makes the connecting map an explicit lattice
   of six-cycles represented by sphere maps. Mayer--Vietoris itself is
   classical and carries no novelty claim.

3. **Francisco Larrión, Miguel A. Pizaña, Rafael Villarroel-Flores,
   _The fundamental group of the clique graph_, European J. Combin.
   30 (2009), 288--294.**
   [Author manuscript](https://xamanek.izt.uam.mx/map/papers/Fundame06w.pdf).
   Theorem 3.1 proves that the clique complex of any connected bipartite
   graph's square and its two half-squares have isomorphic fundamental
   groups. We credit this general result. Our normal-closure formula
   identifies its relators for `C4`-free incidence graphs; the new spherical
   claim is a higher-homotopy assertion. The linked PDF's actual title
   and theorem were checked; search metadata may display a different title.

The Discovery Net source was the Whitehead asphericity neighborhood and
its graph-operation results. Neither that conjecture nor any arbitrary
subcomplex-inheritance assertion is proved here. Existing line-graph,
total-graph fixed-space, inflation, and Mycielski results remain separate.
No formal proof-assistant certificate or independent review is claimed.
