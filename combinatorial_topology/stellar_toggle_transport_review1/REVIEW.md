## Human premises and completeness reductions

My verdict depends on the following complete list of human proof premises.

1. **Lattice convention.**  The empty face is a game element, the empty game
   state is different from it, and a new greatest element is adjoined even
   when the complex is already a simplex.  The top is never a move.  With
   these conventions the displayed upper Möbius formula is correct.

2. **Signed-multiplicity lower bound.**  If `z(H)` is additions minus
   removals at `H`, final parity gives
   `sum_(H superset X) z(H)=1` over actual faces.  Triangular Möbius inversion
   gives `z(H)=-mu(H)`.  Consequently every winning word uses `H` at least
   `|mu(H)|` times, including the empty face when its value is nonzero.

3. **Exact stellar face partition.**  Freshness of `p` makes every new
   `p`-face uniquely `p union A union B`, where `A` is a proper subset of
   `sigma` and `B` lies in its link.  Every surviving old face omits `sigma`.
   These are all faces of the stellar subdivision and there is no overlap
   ambiguity in the carrier map.

4. **Möbius transport.**  Alternating Boolean coface sums show that surviving
   faces retain their upper Möbius values.  For a new face the coface sum
   factors into the proper-subset sum over `sigma` and the old link coface
   sum, giving
   `mu'(p union A union B)=(-1)^(|sigma|-|A|-1)mu(sigma union B)`.
   This covers zero values as well as both signs.

5. **Local macro completeness.**  For nonempty `C`, the facets
   `C union (sigma-{v_i})` shell `2^C * boundary(2^sigma)`.  At stage `i`,
   the already-on part is exactly the union of the ideals generated by
   deleting one earlier `v_j`.  The recursive clearing word uses every
   indexed intersection once; these generators are precisely the dual
   interval of that stage.  Across stages the intervals partition all
   `C union A` with `A` proper in `sigma`.  Thus the macro is legal, fills the
   fiber, and uses each of its `2^s-1` generators once.  Its reversal legally
   clears an all-on fiber.

6. **Global nonvanishing of local moves.**  In an affected old move
   `H=sigma union B`, every local generator has carrier `H`.  The transport
   identity makes its global Möbius value a sign times `mu(H)`, which is
   nonzero because the old move was legal.  The local word needs no agreement
   between this sign and its temporary add/remove direction.

7. **The two carrier-preimage cases are exhaustive.**  If `H` omits
   `sigma`, no `p`-face can have carrier below `H`, so the inverse image of
   `D(H)` is exactly `D(H)`.  If `H=sigma union B`, the inverse image is
   exactly `2^({p} union B) * boundary(2^sigma)`.  There is no third case.

8. **No action leaks outside a carrier fiber.**  Every generator in the local
   block is itself a face of that fiber, so its full principal ideal in the
   subdivided complex is contained in the fiber.  Colors outside the fiber
   therefore cannot affect legality inside it.

9. **Carrier synchronization handles arbitrary words.**  At a compiled-block
   endpoint, every new face has its carrier's old color.  Old legality makes
   the relevant preimage monochromatic.  The unchanged move or local macro
   flips exactly that preimage and restores synchronization.  Reversing the
   macro handles old removal moves, so backtracking and nonoptimal winning
   inputs are genuinely covered.

10. **Optimal multiplicities and costs.**  A surviving face is used exactly
    as often as its old copy.  A new fiber generator is used once for every
    occurrence of its unique carrier and never elsewhere.  On a facewise
    optimal input this is exactly its transported Möbius magnitude.  The
    universal facewise lower bound proves both shortest length and optimality
    for every nonnegative face-dependent cost assignment.

11. **Iteration and singleton faces.**  Each compiled output is again a legal
    winning word, so the argument iterates with fresh vertices.  When
    `|sigma|=1`, the fiber has one generator and the operation is exactly a
    relabeling; the formulas specialize without an exceptional case.

12. **Barycentric completeness.**  Stellar subdivision of all original faces
    of size at least two in decreasing size produces precisely the complex of
    strict chains of nonempty original faces.  This remains true for nonpure
    complexes; omitting singleton subdivisions merely retains their old
    labels.

13. **Barycentric Möbius and count formulas.**  A nonempty chain ending at
    `H` has transported value `(-1)^(|H|-r)mu(H)`.  Chains of length `r`
    ending at `H` correspond bijectively to ordered partitions of `H`, so
    summing magnitudes gives the ordered-Bell formula.  The empty-face value
    is unchanged.

14. **Surface application.**  In a closed triangulated surface the Möbius
    values at triangles, edges, vertices and the empty face are respectively
    `-1,1,-1,chi-1`.  Hence the projective-plane and torus seeds have
    facewise bounds `31` and `43`, and all their forward stellar refinements
    have optimum `2E+1`.  Stellar subdivision preserves the closed surface
    and its Euler characteristic.

15. **Beyond-shellability claim.**  The displayed seed words really are
    facewise optimal.  The projective plane has its mod-2 fundamental
    2-cycle, and the torus has negative empty-face Möbius value, excluding the
    pure-shellable mechanism.  The claim does not extend from forward stellar
    refinements to arbitrary PL-equivalent triangulations because stellar
    equivalence also uses inverse moves.

16. **Literature and scope boundary.**  The signed lower bound and sign/
    left-linear strengthenings are prior work; stellar subdivisions and their
    relation to barycentric subdivision are classical.  The new assertion is
    the carrier-synchronized transport of legal words with exact
    multiplicities.  Bounded searches found no matching primary theorem, but
    this is search-relative evidence and not a priority claim.

These premises cover the universal quantifiers.  Agreement of implementations
below is corroboration, not a substitute for any item.

## Independent adversarial computation

`audit_independent.py` imports no target code.  It generates complexes as
deduplicated closures of all facet selections, computes Möbius values by
alternating coface sums, and searches for local macros by dynamic programming
over all subsets of one-use generators.  This is algorithmically independent
of the target's recursive clearing compiler.

The exact audit covers all 167 complexes on four available labels, including
ghost labels, nonpure complexes, simplexes, and the complex containing only
the empty face.  It checks:

- 1,177 `(K,sigma)` stellar subdivisions and 12,976 transported Möbius
  values;
- 2,679 affected and 8,279 unaffected carrier-preimage identities;
- both the addition and removal blocks for all 1,849 affected faces with
  nonzero old Möbius value;
- 830 affected zero-Möbius faces, ensuring they are not silently compiled;
- 811 new faces whose Möbius sign is opposite to their carrier's sign;
- the full barycentric construction and chain Möbius formula on all 167
  complexes, covering 3,386 barycentric faces; and
- six malformed-input or illegal-word rejection controls.

The local search independently finds one-use macros for `|sigma|=1,2,3,4`.
For three common-simplex sizes the number of legal orders is respectively
`1, 2, 24, 17472`; its independence from the common-simplex size adversarially
checks the join factor.  The smallest cases include the singleton relabeling,
the three-move subdivided-edge macro, zero-filled simplex Möbius tables,
nonpure edge-plus-isolated-vertex complexes, and both directions of every
legal block.

The three published surface certificates are replayed from the game
definition.  Their word lengths equal the independent facewise bounds
`15,31,43`.  A separate subset dynamic program finds a shelling for the sphere
but no shelling for the projective-plane or torus triangulations.  Surface
edge incidences, cyclic vertex links, connectivity, Euler characteristic and
orientability are checked independently.

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

The carrier argument can be extracted as an abstract fiber-macro lemma.  A
surjective carrier map between two finite toggle games transports words when
every principal-ideal preimage has a reversible legal macro confined to that
fiber and every macro generator has nonzero Möbius value whenever its carrier
does.  Stating this lemma would isolate the reusable mechanism and invite
applications to other combinatorial subdivisions.

The local macros deserve a small structural classification.  The independent
counts `1,2,24,17472` through `|sigma|=4` suggest studying the poset of legal
one-use orders and explaining why the count is independent of the common
simplex.  This is not needed for existence but could give simpler compilers or
canonical normal forms.

The proof could also state an output-sensitive runtime bound for compilation
and formalize the short kernel consisting of the two Möbius identities, the
clearing induction, and the carrier invariant.  Finally, characterizing when
a winning word descends across a stellar weld is the precise next question
toward any two-way or PL-invariance statement; the current theorem correctly
does not assume such a converse.
