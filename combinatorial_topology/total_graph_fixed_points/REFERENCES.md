# Prior work and scope

Sources inspected on 2026-09-21, before publication.

1. Michał Adamaszek, *Clique complexes and graph powers*,
   [arXiv:1104.0433](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.0433),
   [full text, Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/1104.0433).

   Theorem 5.1 already gives the ordinary homotopy decomposition of the
   total-graph clique complex into the original clique complex with one
   2-sphere for each triangle. Lemma 5.3 classifies maximal faces. Its
   proof removes the edge-only triangles, collapses the remaining core,
   and observes that the attaching maps are null-homotopic. We use this
   core and credit that structure. The ordinary decomposition, the
   classification, and the broad attachment mechanism are not new here.

   This note makes the deformation explicit and invariant under every
   graph automorphism. It keeps each triangle's stabilizer action,
   obtains reduced-permutation representation spheres, and extracts
   the homotopy type of every subgroup fixed space. In particular a
   transitive triangle adds an isolated fixed point, not a fixed
   2-sphere. The integral orientation module is a consequence of this
   symmetry-sensitive refinement.

2. F. Larrión, M. A. Pizaña, R. Villarroel-Flores,
   *Equivariant Collapses and the Homotopy Type of Iterated Clique Graphs*,
   [author manuscript](https://xamanek.izt.uam.mx/map/papers/iteratedhtype21w.pdf).

   This is prior context for equivariant graph-topological methods.
   Its operator K(G) is the graph of maximal cliques, not the total
   graph T(G). The abstract and initial equivariant-collapse criteria
   were inspected. The present direct coordinate deformation does not
   claim to introduce equivariant collapsing as a method, and does
   not depend on the manuscript's clockwork-graph classification.

## Novelty and trust boundary

A bounded live search for total-graph clique-complex fixed spaces,
equivariant total graphs, triangle homology actions, and total-graph
representation spheres did not locate this exact equivariant statement.
That is not an exhaustive priority investigation. The result should be
evaluated as an explicit structural refinement of a known theorem,
not as a new ordinary wedge theorem or a major conjecture resolution.

The attachment step uses elementary equivariant homotopy invariance
along invariant disk-boundary cofibrations; the proof describes its
collar implementation. No claim is based on equating geometric fixed
spaces with invariant homology or with the complex on fixed vertices.

The computations are finite corroboration by the same researcher.
They do not replace the proof or independent mathematical review.
