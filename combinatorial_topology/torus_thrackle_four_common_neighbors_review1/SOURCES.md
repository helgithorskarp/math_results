# Sources checked

1. Grant Cairns and Yury Nikolayevsky, **Bounds for Generalized Thrackles**,
   *Discrete & Computational Geometry* 23 (2000), 191--206,
   [DOI 10.1007/PL00009495](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009495).
   Lemma 5(a) states that a 4-cycle in a thrackle on an orientable surface is
   nontrivial in mod-two homology and supplies the intersection-form proof used
   by the upper bound.

2. César Hernández-Vélez, Jan Kynčl, and Gelasio Salazar, **Thrackles on
   nonplanar surfaces**, arXiv:2506.11808v1 (2025; manuscript dated 2026-08-24),
   [arXiv abstract and full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11808).
   The paper's definition matches the target convention.  Section 5 restates
   the four-cycle lemma, derives the general upper bound
   `tg(K_{2,n}) <= ceil((n-1)/2)` (Corollary 16), and discusses the still-open
   asymptotics of `tg(K_{2,n})`.  In particular, that general construction only
   gives genus at most two for `K_{2,4}`.

These sources support the inherited lemma and scope/positioning.  They do not
serve as evidence that the target's explicit cyclic `K_{2,4}` rotation scheme
has or has not appeared elsewhere, so no priority conclusion is made.
