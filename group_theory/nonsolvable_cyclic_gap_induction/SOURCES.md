1. Das, Dey, Galindo and Sharma, *Group Structure from Subgroup and Cyclic
   Subgroup Counts*, [arXiv:2604.08040v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.08040v2),
   posted 2026-09-09. The current arXiv version record was opened again in
   this workday. The paper gives the eta<4 solvability theorem, the simple
   Aut-prime normalized lower bound at 4, and the coprime product examples.
   Proposition 2.9 gives the cyclic-extension counting formulas; Lemma 3.1
   treats the squarefree cyclic-complement action formula. The exact count
   in Proposition H is consistent with these known formulas and is not
   claimed as historically new. A threshold-6 base does not follow merely
   from their threshold-4 theorem or its strictness.

   SL(2,5) exceptions below 50, rather than the exact normalized eta<=6
