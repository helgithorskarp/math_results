## Reproduction and results

From this repository root, using CPython 3.11.2 and its standard library:

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 group_theory/nonsolvable_cyclic_gap_induction/verify_base_bounds.py --compare-evidence group_theory/nonsolvable_cyclic_count_base/evidence.json --compare-catalogue group_theory/nonsolvable_cyclic_count_base/catalogue.json > /tmp/atlas-base-bounds.json
```

The comparison command expects Nova's evidence file in the joint source
layout; before joint publication, point those arguments to the transferred
files whose hashes are recorded in the check report. The deterministic output
matches BASE_BOUNDS.json. Without the comparison arguments the same
bounds/counts are produced without the comparison metadata. The complete run
took about 0.2 seconds of wall time in the checker environment, using one process.
All arithmetic is integer or Fraction arithmetic; no solver or class-table
library is read. The script fails if a row lacks a justified bound, a group
closure has the wrong order, a permutation power count disagrees with cycle
order, or literal cyclic sets disagree with generator multiplicities.

The declared catalogue interface has 53 simple groups of order at most
724052. It is matched against the primary simple-by-order listing, retaining
Outer automorphism order for every row. The exceptional aliases are
PSL(2,7)=L3(2), PSp(4,3)=U4(2), and PSL(2,4)=PSL(2,5)=A5,
PSL(2,9)=A6. The last three presentations are not additional groups.

37 rows satisfy |S|>36*4^omega(Aut S), so Lucchini's square-root lower bound
excludes them. The remaining exclusions use 11 PSL2 torus bounds, the Sz(8)
torus bound, and literal counts for A6, A7 and M11. A5 has count32 and remains
the allowed simple base. Literal S5 has count67>48, excluding the proper
A5 almost-simple overgroup. No intermediate groups for another simple socle
need enumeration once c(S)>6*2^omega(Aut S) is established.

## Different counting mechanisms

For PSL(2,q), q=p^f and d=gcd(2,q-1), the standard subgroup partition has
q+1 elementary abelian Sylow p-subgroups with trivial pairwise intersections,
and two classes of cyclic tori, of orders (q-1)/d and (q+1)/d. Their normalizers
have twice their orders, giving q(q+1)/2 and q(q-1)/2 distinct tori. These full
tori alone give c(S)>=q^2+1. Counting all cyclic subgroups in the partition gives

    c(S)=1+(q^2-1)/(p-1)
          +q(q+1)/2*(tau((q-1)/d)-1)
          +q(q-1)/2*(tau((q+1)/d)-1).

The script also reconstructs the complete element-order histogram from this
partition and checks that its total is |S|. This is a geometric subgroup
count, rather than Nova's conjugacy-class computation. Breuer's primary
CTblLib examples, section4.6 Lemma1, record the partition and normalizers:
[primary source](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Thomas.Breuer/ctbllib/doc2/chap4_mj.html).
The prior repository PSL2 note uses this same classical structure; no new
priority is asserted for it.

For Sz(8), the classical partition has 65 Sylow2 subgroups, each with seven
involutions and 56 elements of order4. The odd cyclic tori have orders7,13,5
and normalizers of orders 14,52,20. They have 2080,560,1456 conjugates. Hence
c(S)>=8^4+1=4097, and counting the full partition gives c(S)=6372. The complete
order histogram is reconstructed separately. Downs--Jones, sections5.2--5.4,
give the requisite structures and disjointness:
[author paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.5215).

A5,A6,A7 and S5 are enumerated as even/all permutations on their natural
points. M11 is generated by the standard ATLAS natural permutations
a=(2,10)(4,11)(5,7)(8,9), b=(1,4,3,8)(2,5,6,9); closure has 7920 elements.
Each cyclic subgroup is the literal set of powers of a permutation.
The resulting counts are 32,167,947,67,2576, respectively. Identifying these
M11 generators imports the [primary ATLAS page](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M11/).
It does not require trusting GAP's conjugacy-class output.

and outer orders are checked against the [primary CTblLib table](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Thomas.Breuer/ctbllib/ctbltoc/views/simplebyorder.html)
and [official GAP reference, AllSmallNonabelianSimpleGroups](https://gap-system.github.io/gap/doc/ref/chap39_mj.html).
Their declared complete scope is an imported CFSG-dependent result, not a
consequence of the integer code. Nova's family-order and exceptional-alias
audit, socle reduction, and self-contained measure/order-bound argument are
accepted at the exact scope recorded in the full check report. The checker is
an internal check, not external peer review or formalization.
