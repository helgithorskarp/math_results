# Sources and literature boundary

## Imported theorem

- Nicole Berline and Michèle Vergne, *Local Euler--Maclaurin formula for
  polytopes*, Moscow Mathematical Journal 7 (2007), 355--386;
  [arXiv:math/0507256](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507256).
  The proof imports their local cone identity, lattice-translation
  invariance, local Euler--Maclaurin formula, and affine-denominator period
  bound.  Those statements are not reproved here.

## Ehrhart periods

- Matthias Beck, Steven V. Sam, and Kevin M. Woods, *Maximal periods of
  (Ehrhart) quasi-polynomials*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory A 115
  (2008), 517--525; [arXiv:math/0702242](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0702242).
  In particular, the second-leading coefficient has its maximal expected
  period.  Our `g=1` corollary is consistent with this established result.

- Tyrrell B. McAllister and Kevin M. Woods, *The minimum period of the
  Ehrhart quasi-polynomial of a rational polytope*, Journal of Combinatorial
  Theory A 109 (2005), 345--352;
  [arXiv:math/0310255](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0310255).  This is a
  primary source for period collapse and denominator-versus-period
  questions.

- Kevin M. Woods, *Computing the period of an Ehrhart quasi-polynomial*,
  Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 12 (2005), R34;
  [arXiv:math/0411207](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0411207).  This gives the
  algorithmic period context via rational generating functions.

## Fourier--Dedekind context

- Matthias Beck, Ricardo Diaz, and Sinai Robins, *The Frobenius problem,
  rational polytopes, and Fourier--Dedekind sums*, Journal of Number Theory
  96 (2002), 1--21;
  [arXiv:math/0204035](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204035).  Finite root-of-
  unity sums in explicit Ehrhart formulas are classical.  The character
  filter in the present proof is elementary; we do not claim to introduce
  Fourier--Dedekind methods.

## What bounded searches did and did not establish

Searches on 2026-09-21 covered combinations of “Ehrhart”, “prime index”,
“local Euler--Maclaurin”, “Fourier character”, “Fourier--Dedekind”, “period
collapse”, and “maximal period”, and followed the primary sources above.
They located general period theorems and explicit Fourier--Dedekind
formulas, but not the face-local normalized-character formula (4), its
character-coherent noncancellation criterion, or the full-support proof
from minimal bad-face codimension.

This is only a bounded literature boundary.  The mathematical claims rest
on the proof, not on an assertion of historical priority.  Equivalent
formulations may exist under different terminology.
