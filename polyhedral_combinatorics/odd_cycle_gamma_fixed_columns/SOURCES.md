## Direct computational/proof dependency

The finite coefficient identity and determinant recurrence used here were
proved and checked in the adjacent repository artifact:

- [Odd-cycle graph polytopes: a spectral-to-gamma bridge](../odd_cycle_width_one_gamma/)

The current artifact adds the fixed-column annihilator, the spectral ordering,
the two-term Perron asymptotic, and the resulting eventual-positivity theorem.
