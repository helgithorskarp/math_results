## Primary literature checked

- Illya Ivanov, *Illuminating Primitive Polytopes*, arXiv:2607.08944v1:
  [abstract and version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08944),
  [version 1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08944v1), and
  [arXiv API metadata](https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2607.08944).
  The abstract and Section 1.3 give the facet-omission definition;
  Conjecture 6 gives the vertex bound and affine-cube equality clause.
  The same source separates this conjecture from its theorem on illumination.
- Francisco Santos, *A counterexample to the Hirsch conjecture*,
  arXiv:1006.2814:
  [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2814).  Section 2.1 discusses the
  classical one-point suspension dual to the facet-wedge construction.  The
  reviewed counterexample does not depend on that source because it proves
  its affine-box lifting rule directly.
