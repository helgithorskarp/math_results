## Current primary status

Teng Zhang's [quadratic Tang--Zhang paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126),
Conjecture 1.2 and Theorem 1.3, was rechecked live on 2026-10-01. It states
the reciprocal-distance power family and proves the quadratic case;
first power remains the strongest conjectural endpoint there.
[Tao's account](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
reports the resolution of ordinary Sendov and discusses the stronger family.
The historical assignment seeds are Zhang's
[explicit large-degree theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256) and
Meng's [degree-nine Sendov preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235).
They concern ordinary Sendov and are not proof inputs for this local
angular theorem. No full first-power solution is claimed here.

## New mechanism and unresolved obligations

The reusable full-compression bound Psi>=N/D for the basis
(I-C^2),C(I-C^2),C^2(I-C^2) is proved here whenever D>0. It is exact on
the credited face for 0<=u<1. The concrete novelty sought is the
complete positive-coefficient proof on S<=1/100, u in [1/16,1/9], including
independent triple splitting and all collision boundaries.

Two 90-second floating-point cover proposals for a larger ordered
saturated-triple region remained incomplete. They are heuristic work,
not proof input, a certificate, or evidence of nonexistence. Expensive
cover searches were paused without increasing resources. Whole six-level
and unrestricted displacement classification, effective original-root
cutoffs and the full complex first-power endpoint remain unproved here.
Bounded current source/graph and primary-literature checks are used to
avoid duplicating known work, not to certify exhaustive historical priority.
