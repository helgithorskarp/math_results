## Primary literature

- Teng Zhang, *Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang–Zhang
  inequality*, September 2026, [primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126).
  Conjecture 1.2 states the complex reciprocal-distance family and identifies
  first power as its strongest endpoint. Theorem 1.3 proves the quadratic
  case. This paper does not supply the present original-root fixed-energy
  minimizer classification.
- Terence Tao, *A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture*,
  [author account](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/).
  Conjecture 19 states the Tang–Zhang reciprocal family. Ordinary Sendov and
  the stronger first-power endpoint are distinct claims.
- Tang–Zhang, *Sharp Schoenberg type inequalities and the de Bruin–Sharma
  problem*, [primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3),
  Lemma 3.4: classical Cheung–Ng matrix representation. Inversion after
  translation yields the reciprocal matrix used here; that matrix is
  established machinery, not a new result.
- Michael J. Miller, *Unexpected local extrema for the Sendov conjecture*,
  [primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0505424).
  Its objective is nearest-critical-point distance. Local extrema for
  that different objective do not classify this fixed-energy sum. The
  abstract is context only, not a theorem used in this proof.
