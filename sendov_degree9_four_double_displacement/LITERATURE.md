## Primary status

[Zhang's quadratic Tang--Zhang paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126)
states the reciprocal-power family as Conjecture 1.2 and identifies
exponent one as its strongest endpoint. Theorem 1.3 proves the quadratic
case and Corollary 1.4 covers higher powers. Current live verification
preserves the degree-nine complex exponent-one assignment.
[Tao's primary exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
reports ordinary Sendov and states the stronger family as Conjecture 19.
The historical seeds
[Meng 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235) and
[the explicit large-degree threshold paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256)
are context, not new results being claimed here.

[Tang--Zhang's matrix framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3)
provides polynomial compression context. Secular residues, companion
traces, Newton identities, Descartes' rule and Bernstein positivity are
classical. The present specific equal-pair invariant identity, global
restoration, cohort optimizer and phase-class consequence were not found
in bounded current primary searches or the relevant committed campaign
neighborhood. This is search-relative novelty, not historical priority.
