# Sequential generated-anchor preservation theorem

## Exact scope

Fix `zeta=exp(2*pi*i/5)`, `phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2`, and
`q=1/abs(1-zeta)`. Put `K=Q(zeta)` and

```
A = Z_(3)[zeta],
Z_(3) = {m/n in Q : 3 does not divide n}.
```

Let B be the 16-point Parts carrier, whose physical coordinates are

```
q * (5*zeta^4 + sum(e_j*(zeta^j-zeta^4))),
e in {0,1}^4.
```

Start with the whole fixed B. At each of finitely many steps add a whole
similar copy of this original B, of absolute scale `phi` or `1/phi`, with
arbitrary orientation, allowing reflections. Require at least two distinct
points of the new copy to coincide with points already present. The anchors
may be generated by different earlier copies and need not belong to B.
All coincidences are physical equalities. Let G be the complete strict
unit-distance graph on the collision-merged union.

**Theorem.** Every such G has chromatic number exactly three. In particular,
no such network, or any subset of its points, can provide a five-chromatic
strict unit-distance graph with at most 508 points.

The arbitrary number of steps is treated by induction. No new network or
copy-placement census is used in this proof. The theorem permits incidental
contacts and cycles in the final contact graph; it requires only the stated
two-anchor construction order.

## 1. The residue ring is a field

The unique power basis `1,zeta,zeta^2,zeta^3` identifies

```
A/3A = F_3[T]/(T^4+T^3+T^2+T+1).
```

The polynomial is irreducible modulo three. One direct finite proof is that
it has no root in F_3 and none of the nine monic quadratic polynomials divides
it. These are all possible factors of degree at most two; a reducible quartic
would have such a factor. The checker gives every remainder explicitly.

Equivalently, a root is a primitive fifth root of unity, and the smallest d
with `5 | (3^d-1)` is d=4. The residue field therefore has 81 elements.

Complex conjugation sends zeta to zeta^4, preserves A and 3A, and descends to
an automorphism of this field. In fact it is the ninth-power Frobenius map,
since `3^2=4 mod 5`. Thus a nonzero residue and its conjugate have nonzero
product.

## 2. Norm integrality forces coefficient integrality

**Lemma.** For `u in K`, if `u*conjugate(u) in A`, then `u in A`.

The case u=0 is immediate. Suppose u is not in A. Choose the least positive
integer k for which `v=3^k*u` belongs to A. Such k exists by clearing the
powers of three from the denominators of u's four rational coefficients.
Minimality gives `v notin 3A`; otherwise k-1 would suffice.

But

```
v*conjugate(v) = 3^(2*k) * u*conjugate(u) in 3A.
```

Modulo 3, the left side is the product of two nonzero elements of the field
A/3A. It cannot vanish. This contradiction proves the lemma.

This is an exact denominator-clearing argument. It does not infer closure
from a finite sample of similarities, and it needs no unstated assumption
that generated anchor differences are invertible in A. They need only be
nonzero in the field K.

## 3. Every allowed attachment preserves qA

Assume all previously inserted physical points lie in qA. Write a direct
similarity of the unscaled source as `z -> u*z+t`, or a reflected one as
`z -> u*conjugate(z)+t`.

Choose two distinct anchors, `q*x` and `q*y`, already present, and their
source preimages `q*a` and `q*b`. Here `x,y in A`, `a,b in Z[zeta]`, and
`a != b`. In the direct case

```
u = (x-y)/(a-b) in K;
```

in the reflected case replace the denominator by its conjugate. The common
physical factor q cancels because q is real and positive. In either case

```
u*conjugate(u) in {phi^2, phi^-2}.
```

Both squared scales lie in A: `phi=-zeta^2-zeta^3` and
`phi^-1=phi-1`, so both are algebraic integers with integral power-basis
coefficients. By the lemma, u belongs to A. Then
`t=x-u*a` or `t=x-u*conjugate(a)` also belongs to A.
Every point of the new whole copy consequently lies in qA.

The starting B lies in qA. Induction proves preservation for every finite
sequential two-anchor network. Further coincidences do not affect this
argument, and further unit contacts will be covered by the same global
colouring below.

## 4. Apply the reviewed three-colouring

The independent review of the frozen 462-point network proved that the
complete strict unit graph on qA is three-colourable. We restate its short
algebraic certificate to make the present corollary reproducible without
rerunning the reviewer's graph reconstruction.

For `z=a0+a1*zeta+a2*zeta^2+a3*zeta^3`, define

```
p = a0*a1+a1*a2+a2*a3,
h = a0*a2+a0*a3+a1*a3,
r = 2*sum(a_i^2)-p-h,
s = p-h.
```

The norm identity is `2*z*conjugate(z)=r+s*sqrt(5)`. Since
`|1-zeta|^2=(5-sqrt(5))/2`, a physical unit difference has r=5 and s=-1.
Reduce all coefficients modulo three; this is valid because they are in
Z_(3). Among the 81 possible rows, exactly ten have `(r,s)=(2,2)` modulo
three. On every such row the linear form

```
L(a0,a1,a2,a3) = a1+2*a2+a3 mod 3
```

is nonzero. Therefore `c(q*z)=L(z)` is a proper three-colouring of the
complete unit graph on qA, including every incidental physical unit pair.
The ten rows and their nonzero values appear in `EXPECTED.json` and in the
reviewed source. This colouring assigns a single value to each actual point,
so collision merging poses no additional compatibility condition.

The source contains the unit five-cycle with one-based labels
`6-3-1-2-5-6`. Its five distinct coefficient rows and all five unit distances
are checked directly. Every G contains B, hence is not bipartite. Together
with the three-colouring this proves `chi(G)=3`.

## 5. Consequence and limits

The generated-anchor direction left open by the fixed-base overlay theorem
is closed for every network satisfying this sequential two-anchor rule,
regardless of copy count, overlap pattern or final order. It cannot cross the
sub-509 record boundary. Subsets inherit the three-colouring but may lose the
five-cycle, so their chromatic number is only asserted to be at most three.

The proof does not classify arbitrary plane constructions, one-anchor
placements, other sources, arbitrary auxiliary points, or simultaneous
assemblies that have no valid two-anchor insertion order from B. It does not
assert a global Hadwiger--Nelson bound or classify all points of qK. Those
unaddressed scopes are not proposed as next experiments here.

The new step is the norm-integrality lemma and its application to the
permitted construction operation. The three-colouring of qA and the exact
three-chromaticity of the original 462-point graph belong to the cited
independent review. This corollary is author-side and has not received an
independent review of its own. No historical priority claim is made.
