## Specific prior-art comparisons

[Zhang's all-original-zeros-collinear manuscript](https://zhangteng2000.github.io/files/Sharp_Reciprocal_Moment_Inequalities_Collinear_Zeros.pdf)
proves reciprocal moment bounds by Rolle/interlacing under a hypothesis
on the original zeros. Its full nine-page text was inspected in preceding
passes; a fresh direct browser open returned an error. Here the hypothesis
counts real critical points, and permits complex original zeros. The
explicit rational example in PROOF.md has a nonreal original pair and a
nonreal critical pair, and is outside that collinear-original-zero scope.

[Brown--Powell's primary manuscript](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~brown00/zeros-1.pdf)
on real critical points concerns an individual critical-point distance.
The present conclusion is an aggregate reciprocal-distance sum and
permits a nonreal critical pair. The earlier manuscript is not claimed
to have been refuted or reproduced here.

The derivative-product sufficient criterion
$\prod_{i=1}^8|a-z_i|\le9\Rightarrow S_1\ge8$ follows immediately
from $p'(a)=9\prod_j(a-\zeta_j)$ and AM--GM. It was already stated by
**marius.cobzarenco+maths**, August 22, 2026, in the comments on
[Tao's exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/),
whose full comment text was inspected in the preceding pass. The rational
example here has product $26973/1024>9$, so this sufficient criterion
does not cover it. No first-open-region or derivative-product novelty
claim is made.

Bounded live searches for reciprocal sums with one conjugate critical
pair, and fresh relevant graph/source comparisons, found no duplicate of
the exact six-real-critical-point degree-nine claim. This does not
establish historical priority or eliminate inaccessible literature.
