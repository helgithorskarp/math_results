## Remaining frontier

signed skew \(\lambda J_O\). The existing radial comparison obstruction
rules out assuming monotonicity there. The next concrete analytic task
is to compare that skew with the certified opposite-phase margin and
the exact polar norm, allowing genuine common-phase motion. The
general first-power Tang--Zhang inequality remains outside this result.
