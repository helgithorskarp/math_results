integers and rational numbers. Complete source is supplied, rather than
large coefficient arrays. Norm and substitution algorithms are compared
entrywise, and every tensor is inverted. These are author cross-checks.
The surrounding geometry, Bernstein positivity and strictness, cited
mean/minimum lemmas, classical identities and polynomial interpretation
remain unformalized written mathematics.

Other critical multiplicity patterns, a general first-power proof,
quantitative interior gaps and bridges to original-root stability remain
open here. The new result advances one full restricted polynomial class;
it is not an enumeration of all disk-root polynomials.
