# Provenance and scope

The exact input is the reviewed source package
[`hadwiger_nelson_fish_flex_contact_source_loss`](../hadwiger_nelson_fish_flex_contact_source_loss/README.md),
including its refinement at commit
`a0fa3ac2b53ab0d538ec376a5fbe011accd41665`.  The imported geometry
certificate has SHA-256

```text
e079c2d86f0b3574e6e24d9fc18a9abc2d8b7d7fe111ba011d5978875d5fc732.
```

That source credits the 23-vertex graph to Hochberg and O'Donnell and the
flexible parameterization to Parcly Taxel's Shibuya implementation.  This
package makes no novelty claim for the fish, its flex, difference sets, or
conservative interval colouring.  Its new exact statement is confined to the
one isolated self-contact realization and the complete ordered difference
body defined in the README.

The earlier
[`hadwiger_nelson_fish_selfsum_fourcolour_stop`](../hadwiger_nelson_fish_selfsum_fourcolour_stop/README.md)
uses the different symmetric fish root and the commutative sum `{p_i+p_j:i<=j}`.
It neither contains nor decides the present flexible-contact difference body.
The present result does not classify another flex root or any nearby operation.
