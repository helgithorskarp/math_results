# Verdict and limitations

## Verdict

**ACCEPT_WITH_STRICT_FIXED_SOURCE_AND_CYCLIC_FRAME_LIMITATION.**

The independent evidence proves:

1. The pinned EI17 source has one root in the declared rational box, its
   complete unit graph is the declared 31-edge graph, and its chromatic number
   is four.
2. The declared enumeration contains all `31*2*2 = 124` labelled frames of
   the stated same-edge cyclic architecture.
3. Every frame has exactly 48 physical points after precisely the three anchor
   identifications.
4. Every actual unit edge is included in the conservative upper graph.
5. The upper graphs have 93 edges in 116 frames and 96 edges in eight frames.
6. Every complete source four-colouring extends through every upper graph.
7. Therefore every actual complete physical unit graph has chromatic number

No material error was found in the scoped stopping theorem.

## Binding limitations

- The eight 96-edge objects are conservative upper graphs.  The proof does not
  establish which, if any, of their three extra possible pairs are actual unit
  contacts.  Do not quote 96 as an exact physical edge count.
- The 124 frames use the same chosen ordered/reflected source-edge placement on
  all three sides under the fixed cyclic map.  Independent edge choices,
  relative phases, and other copy counts are not classified.
- Only the pinned root of one EI17 realization is covered.
- The frames have vertex connectivity two.  Their exactly three two-cuts are
  the anchor pairs, so the construction remains an edge-based cyclic 2-sum.
- All accepted chromatic claims concern complete four-colouring extension.
  No five-chromatic graph, relation loss, or sub-509 record candidate results.

## Assessment

The stopping theorem is useful because it excludes an entire finite family at
the semantically correct full-input level, rather than checking one terminal
projection.  The independent implementation doubles the labelled source
checks and reproduces the complete geometry stream.  Its value is nevertheless
negative and architecture-specific; it must not be promoted into an exclusion
for arbitrary EI17 assemblies.
