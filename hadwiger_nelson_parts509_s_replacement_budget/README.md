# Replacement budget of the small part of the Parts 509 graph inside its sealed completion pool

## Result and scope

Let `G = L ∪ S` be Parts's 509-vertex 5-chromatic unit-distance graph with the
decomposition of `../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_interface_lemma` (`L`: the 374
points free of `sqrt5`, indices 0..373; `S`: the 135 points involving `sqrt5`,
indices 374..508; 19-vertex interface `I_L`, 30 cross edges).  Let `Q5` be the
168 completion points of `G` (points at unit distance from at least three
vertices of `G`, `../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_swap_closure/completion_points.json`)
whose coordinates involve `sqrt5`, and `U := S ∪ Q5` (303 points, ambient
indices as in `../hadwiger_nelson_parts509_pair_closure/ambient_w3_edges.json`).
The pool is *sealed*: every unit-distance edge from `L` to `U` ends in `I_L`
(checked by both verifiers), so replacing `S` by any `X ⊆ U` leaves `L` and its
interface untouched.

**Theorem A (solver-free, exact).**  If `X ⊆ U` and `L ∪ X` is not
4-colourable, then `|X| ≥ 112`.  Equivalently: keeping `L` fixed, no choice of
at most 111 points of the sealed pool gives a 5-chromatic graph.  (Parts's `S`
has 135 points; the record would need `|X| ≤ 134`.)

**Theorem B (S-only budget).**  Let

    h_S := min { |Y| : Y ⊆ S and L ∪ Y ∪ Q5 is not 4-colourable }.

Then `74 ≤ h_S` (solver-free), `90 ≤ h_S` (solver-trusted, exact minimum
hitting set of the certified family, RC2/HiGHS) and `h_S ≤ 98` (DRAT-checked:
an explicit `Y*` of 98 vertices of `S` makes `L ∪ Y* ∪ Q5`, 640 vertices and
2,979 edges, non-4-colourable).  Consequently every 5-chromatic `L ∪ X` with
`X ⊆ U` keeps at least 90 (solver-trusted) vertices of `S`, and with *all* of
`Q5` added at most 45 and at least 37 vertices of `S` can be dropped.

**Forced structure (solver-free).**  All 30 interface vertices of `S` are
singleton killing sets (each is indispensable even with all of `Q5` present),
and 125 pairs `{a, b}` of non-interface vertices of `S` (on 78 vertices) are
*irreplaceable pairs*: `L ∪ (S \ {a,b}) ∪ Q5` is 4-colourable, so every
5-chromatic `L ∪ X`, `X ⊆ U`, keeps `a` or `b`.

**What this constrains.**  Combined with the committed closures (deleting 2, 3
or 4 vertices of `G` and adding 1, 2 or 3 points never gives a 5-chromatic
graph), a 5-chromatic graph `L ∪ (S \ R) ∪ A` with `A ⊆ Q5` and `|A| < |R|`
would need `5 ≤ |R| ≤ 45` and `|R| − 23 ≤ |A| ≤ |R| − 1` (the `45` and the
`90`-based statements are solver-trusted; `|R| − |A| ≤ 23` is solver-free).
The exact value of `min |X|` over the pool (Parts's `S` gives 135) is left
open: the implicit-hitting-set relaxation stalls with an LP value near 111 and
a HiGHS dual bound of 116, so the gap 116..134 is not closed here.

This does not improve the 509-vertex record or the bounds `5 ≤ χ(R²) ≤ 7`.  It
says nothing about modifications of `L`, about completion points free of
`sqrt5` (they touch `L` outside the interface), or about points outside the
level-1 completion pool.

## Method

Call `D ⊆ U` a *killing set* if `L ∪ (U \ D)` is 4-colourable (an *S-killing
set* if moreover `D ⊆ S`, i.e. `L ∪ (S \ D) ∪ Q5` is 4-colourable).  A set `X`
with `L ∪ X` not 4-colourable must meet every killing set, and conversely a set
meeting every *inclusion-minimal* killing set has this property, so the
minimum size of such an `X` is the minimum hitting set of the family of minimal
killing sets.  Killing sets were generated by implicit hitting set dualisation
(`ihs_sonly2.py`; `sgadget.py` holds the SAT machinery): alternately compute
an exact minimum hitting set of the family found so far (RC2 core-guided MaxSAT
over the 135 variables of `S`; HiGHS MILP over the 303 variables of `U`), then,
if it is not valid, grow it to a valid set by disjoint minimal killing sets
found through the 20 interface classes of `L` (one CaDiCaL call per class,
minimised by deletion, recorded with its witness colouring).  The general-pool
search stalled (LP value ≈ 111, HiGHS dual 116 after 1,800 s) and the S-only
search's incremental bound stalled at 89 for eleven consecutive rounds (the exact
recomputation on the final family gives 90), so the results are
reported as bounds.  Greedy repeated-sweep minimisation of `S` with all of `Q5`
present (`minimize_pool.py`) supplied the upper bound `Y*`.

## Certificates and verification

`general_pool_dual_certificate.json` (Theorem A): 200 killing sets `D ⊆ U`,
each with an interface class index and a proper 4-colouring of `U \ D`
(concatenated with the class's witness colouring of `L` from the interface
lemma this is an explicit proper 4-colouring of `L ∪ (U \ D)`), and a rational
weight `y_D > 0`.  Weak LP duality: since `Σ_{D∋v} y_D ≤ 1` for every `v ∈ U`,
every `X` meeting all `D` satisfies `|X| ≥ Σ_D y_D |D ∩ X| ≥ Σ_D y_D =
267577/2408 = 111.12`, hence `|X| ≥ 112`.  `verify_general_dual.py` rebuilds
the pool, replays every colouring against the exact ambient edge list and
re-derives the inequality with exact rationals (4 s).

`certificate.json` (Theorem B): the 3,575 S-killing sets found (2,852
inclusion-minimal; sizes 1..27) with witness colourings; a rational
weak-duality certificate over 74 of them (bound 74); the recorded exact minimum
hitting set 90 of the family (RC2, 3,273 s on the final family); and the upper-bound set `Y*` with the
SHA-256 of its pinned 4-colouring CNF and of the DRAT proof.  `verify_sonly.py`
replays all witnesses, checks the rational inequality, lists the forced
vertices and irreplaceable pairs, rebuilds the CNF byte-for-byte and (with
`--drat`) runs drat-trim on the proof; `--rc2`, `--milp`, `--cbc` recompute the
minimum hitting set of the minimal family with independent exact solvers.

```text
python3 verify_general_dual.py general_pool_dual_certificate.json
python3 verify_sonly.py certificate.json                                  # solver-free part, ~2 min
python3 verify_sonly.py certificate.json --drat upper.drat --drat-trim /path/to/drat-trim
python3 verify_sonly.py certificate.json --rc2                            # exact minimum hitting set (3,273 s on this host)
python3 drat_upper.py Y.json outdir                                       # regenerate the DRAT proof (CaDiCaL + drat-trim)
```

## Trust boundary

- Geometry: the 1,667 ambient points and the 11,074 unit-distance pairs among
  them were computed exactly in `Q(sqrt3, sqrt5, sqrt11)` in the committed swap
  and pair closures; the lower bounds rely on that list containing *every*
  unit pair among the points (a missing edge could invalidate a witness), the
  upper bound only on every listed pair being a genuine unit pair.
- Theorem A and the bounds 74, 30 forced vertices and 125 pairs are solver-free
  (explicit colourings and exact rational arithmetic in the checkers).
- The bound 90 is the optimum of a MaxSAT/MILP instance and rests on the
  solvers' optimality answers (RC2 with the CaDiCaL backend; HiGHS agrees on
  earlier snapshots; recomputable with the verifier).  The HiGHS dual bound 116
  for the general pool is likewise solver-trusted and is not part of any
  certificate.
- The upper bound 98 is a DRAT-checked UNSAT proof (proof regenerable, hash recorded).
- The interface lemma (20 classes) is used only to accelerate the search; the
  certificates replay its 20 witness colourings of `L` explicitly.
