**ACCEPT with high confidence, conditional on the imported h4065, h4071,
h4073, and accepted h4005/h4031 interfaces named below.** The exact algebraic
cover, its colour certificates, the complete injective symmetry frontier, and
the separate coincidence closure withstand source audit, complete replay,

```text

W={0,1,omega,omega-1,-1,-omega,1-omega}
```

## Mathematical audit

The Cayley chart parametrizes every unit rotation except `-1`; the omitted
alignment lines are covered by the accepted h4005/h4031 theorem. The h4065
input reconstructs every strict physical unit-distance event as a bidegree at
most `(2,2)` polynomial, checks all factorizations, and proves that failure of
its thirteen explicit product colourings lies on 71,134 selected factor
pairs. The h4071 replay checked a 432-element physical symmetry action on all
972 nonalignment factors and reduced the selected-pair closure to exactly 800
orbits. Thus h4085's system list covers every injective possible counterexample,
not merely a sample of promising parameter values.

For a representative pair `(f,g)`, an exact Sylvester resultant covers every
finite common root by its projection factors. Modulo each projection factor
`r(x)`, the checked quotient Euclidean computation produces a monic relation
`H(x,y)` in the ideal generated by `f,g`. No irreducibility of `r` is needed:
all divisions use explicitly verified units, so every common root satisfies
`H=0`. The resulting algebra is finite free because `r` and `H` are monic.

For a proposed 343-label colour word, every Cartesian edge is checked proper.
Each remaining same-colour pair has a squared-distance event factor. Proving
that all such factors are units in `Q[x,y]/(r,H)` excludes every monochromatic
unit edge simultaneously. The target proves this through good-reduction
modular witnesses; the reviewer check proves the same 277,244 assertions
directly over `Q`. Empty and no-real branches, plus the one exact quadratic
split, exhaust all other actions. Hence no injective real parameter remains.

The h4073 replay separately enumerated every noninjective norm type and all
210,996 distinct physical point pairs in the four nonalignment collision
classes. Its proper three- or four-colour word checks close those classes;
two-coefficient collisions reduce to the accepted pair-alignment theorem.
Together the injective and collision arguments establish the universal claim.

## Novelty and publication readiness

Searches on 2026-09-09 for the exact title, the `W+uW+vW` formulation, and
rotated unit-wheel/hexagon variants found no external statement of this
closure theorem. This supports novelty within the searched literature but is
not proof of priority. The exact algebraic and graph-colouring techniques are
standard; their complete application to this architecture is the contribution.

[Jaan Parts' minimization paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12665) reports a
509-vertex, 2,442-edge five-chromatic unit-distance graph. The present
at-most-343-vertex family is therefore highly relevant to a sub-509 search,
but its closure is negative evidence and cannot itself improve that record.

## Trust boundaries and remaining uncertainty

The verdict imports h4065's complete unit-event/failure cover, h4071's physical
symmetry quotient, h4073's collision classification, and the accepted
h4005/h4031 theorem on pair-aligned sums. The first three were replayed at the
interfaces needed here; the older alignment theorem was not reopened. The
reviewer exact-Q checker deliberately reuses the pinned geometric input adapter
and source factor inventory, while independently replacing elimination and
all unit-witness arithmetic.

Remaining trust includes the published source bytes, CPython and SymPy exact
arithmetic, JSON decoding, SHA-256, operating system, and hardware. The proof
is not proof-assistant formalized. The literature search cannot establish
global priority.

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

1. Formalize the compact geometric reduction, the symmetry action, and the
   finite-algebra unit criterion; keep the 277,244 claims as a checked external
   certificate behind a proof-assistant specification.
2. Publish an independently generated source-factor inventory instead of
   having reviewer checks consume h4065's adapter. This would reduce the
   remaining shared-code boundary at the geometry-to-polynomial interface.
3. Extract human-readable four-colouring formulas or a small stratification
   for the 62 words. The current exact cover is complete but is principally a
   computational classification.
4. Use the closure as a pruning theorem for broader architectures rather than
   as positive construction evidence. Any extension must add a genuinely new
   module or degree of freedom; further search inside `W+uW+vW` is exhausted.
