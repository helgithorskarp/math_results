# Sources

Primary sources used to define the external problem and computational
frontier:

1. W. P. Orrick, B. Solomon, R. Dowdeswell, and W. D. Smith,
   [*New lower bounds for the maximal determinant
   problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0304410).  This paper publishes the
   order-23 record matrix and determinant used here.
2. W. P. Orrick, [*The maximal {-1,1}-determinant of order
   15*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401179).  This gives the candidate-Gram
   definitions, normalization, exhaustive generation strategy, and
   decomposition framework for odd orders.
3. R. P. Brent, W. P. Orrick, J.-A. Osborn, and P. Zimmermann,
   [*Maximal determinants and saturated D-optimal designs of orders 19 and
   37*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.4160).  This sharpens the candidate-Gram
   pruning method and documents the current exact-search architecture.
4. W. P. Orrick, [*On the enumeration of some D-optimal
   designs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511141).  Its survey of known
   classifications reports at least 14 inequivalent order-23 matrices
   attaining the current determinant record.  This bounds the scope of the
   present computation, which is centered at one specified record matrix.
