* Collier Gaiser, **Restricted generalized Schur numbers**, arXiv:2608.08789v1,
  submitted 2026-08-09. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08789),
  [full primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08789v1).
  Theorem 1.1 gives the same value for fixed distinctness and sufficiently
  many summands. Section 3 supplies the lower coloring used here. Section 5
  supplies the original majority-prefix strategy and several special-case
  identities. Open Question 6.4 asks for the smallest stabilization threshold.
  Our theorem gives a uniform linear upper bound; it does not answer the
  optimal-threshold question in full.
