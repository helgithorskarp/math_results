# Sources and attribution

Elsholtz, Führer, Füredi, Kovács, Pach, Simon, and Velich,
*Maximal line-free sets in F_p^n*, Periodica Mathematica Hungarica90
(2025),7–21:
[author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.03382v2),
[published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10998-024-00617-x).
The introduction attributes the exact planar bound to Jamison and to
Brouwer–Schrijver. Theorem1.5 proves r_5(F_5^3)<74; Figure4 gives70.
Those results, rather than an assertion that73 remains the campaign's
best upper bound, supply the primary background.

Benedek Kovács, *A superlinear improvement on line-free sets in F_p^3*,
[arXiv2605.23437v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23437v1), May2026,
improves an asymptotic lower bound as p grows. It does not settle p=5.

Earlier campaign sources:

- [Upper bound72](../../affine_line_free_f5_3/upper_bound72.md).
- [Four-case low-plane reduction for72](../../affine_line_free_f5_3/low_planes72/README.md).

The second source has an integer feasible control for an aggregate
incidence model. The present certificate concerns a different relaxation:
all geometric planes, full planar subset polytopes, and common one-point
marginals, with an actual maximum grid fixed and a parallel small plane.
It does not contradict either earlier contribution or rely on the72 bound
for certificate validity.
