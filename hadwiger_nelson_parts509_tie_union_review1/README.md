# Independent review: Parts-509 tie-union minimum-order theorem

## Verdict

**ACCEPT, scoped to the stated finite pools.**  The committed finding
`bafkreic7cfo5ehr4iaxipfpf3nrgdp37qziieayr3du3xgracun2olfegu` correctly proves that each of
the three specified strict unit-distance graphs has no 5-chromatic subgraph on at most 508
vertices.  This is an intermediate exclusion theorem, not a sub-509 five-chromatic
unit-distance graph and not a proof that no such graph exists in the plane.

Reviewed source:

- `hadwiger_nelson_parts509_tie_union_minimum` on the repository's `main` branch;
- source commit `1eb1d8b5736055e8cef4f467cc8fa158ceaccd5d`;
- three committed JSON certificates with SHA-256 digests pinned in `verify_exact.py`.

## Independent derivation of the reduction

Let the finite ambient graph have vertex set `F disjoint-union R`, where every `u` in `F`
has a certified proper 4-colouring of the ambient graph with `u` deleted.  Every
5-chromatic subgraph must contain every member of `F`.  If `D` is a certified killing set,
the ambient graph minus `D` is 4-colourable.  Consequently the free part `X` of any
5-chromatic subgraph must meet every certified killing set.

The previously committed delete/add closures imply that a 5-chromatic subgraph on at most
508 vertices must use at least four vertices outside the original Parts graph.  All pool
vertices are free, so its `X` is a hitting set containing at least four pool selectors.
For the three pools the certificate proves

| Pool | `|F|` | free vertices | minimum constrained hitting set | `509-|F|` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P25 | 483 | 51 | 26 | 26 |
| P44 | 475 | 78 | 34 | 34 |
| level 2 | 475 | 173 | 34 | 34 |

Thus a putative subgraph of order at most 508 would need a hitting set of size at most one
less than the certified minimum.  This is impossible.  The original 509-vertex graph is
present in each ambient graph, so the minimum order is exactly 509.

## Checks performed

1. The contribution's verifier was run on all three certificates.  It reproduced exact
   edge counts 2,620 / 2,754 / 3,119, accepted every forced-vertex and killing-set witness,
   and independently recomputed constrained optima 26 / 34 / 34 with both RC2 and HiGHS.

2. `verify_exact.py` is a standard-library-only implementation independent of the
   contribution.  It checks all 209,628 pairs of the largest 648-vertex graph directly in
   `Q(sqrt(3),sqrt(5),sqrt(11))`, with no floating-point prefilter.  Restriction gives the
   two smaller edge sets.  It checks coordinate uniqueness and nesting, replays all 2,329
   proper-colouring witnesses, extracts the inclusion-minimal killing sets, and independently
   writes the three pseudo-Boolean decision instances.

3. The independently reconstructed OPB files have exactly the published hashes.  RoundingSat
   commit `d4edbf7908a9bb951fd181940919e0f3ac7ab1ee` with `--lp=0` regenerated all three
   cutting-planes proofs byte-for-byte.  VeriPB 3.0.2 reported
   `s VERIFIED UNSATISFIABLE` for each.  The proof hashes and sizes are:

   - P25: `49ffe96e4cb50bcdf603595c7231745a80609746cf9521cfac110de9931d54a6`,
     4,532 bytes;
   - P44: `39b13eabd38950e21b85fdd07d4d6514513f5445e5888ef31ada2f0ca4108690`,
     262,815 bytes;
   - level 2: `a7642baa7edc8a1dea3fea5526cee9cf208ebf3e0341776a03bc09c0b6c32869`,
     122,264,591 bytes.

   Proof logs are reproducible bulk artifacts and are deliberately not committed here.

4. `rebuild_level2.py` independently recovers the pool definitions from committed inputs.
   It finds the stated 11 swap, 60 pair, and 79 triple-tie records and exactly recovers P25
   and P44 (25 and 44 points).  It then enumerates every K-rational circle intersection
   generated by a P44 anchor and a vertex of `V union P44`, without unpublished scratch
   inputs or multiprocessing.  Of 599 new points, exactly 141 have at least three base
   neighbours (131 have three; 10 have four).  An exact all-pairs graph build followed by
   iterative deletion of degree-at-most-three vertices removes 46 points and leaves exactly
   the 648 certificate vertices.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 hadwiger_nelson_parts509_tie_union_review1/verify_exact.py \
  --write-opb /tmp/tie-union-opb
python3 hadwiger_nelson_parts509_tie_union_review1/rebuild_level2.py
```

To reproduce the proof checks after building the pinned RoundingSat commit and VeriPB 3.0.2:

```bash
for pool in P25 P44 L2; do
  roundingsat --lp=0 --proof-log="/tmp/tie-union-opb/${pool}.pbp" \
    "/tmp/tie-union-opb/${pool}.opb"
  veripb "/tmp/tie-union-opb/${pool}.opb" "/tmp/tie-union-opb/${pool}.pbp"
done
```

The proof files should have the hashes above.  `EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt` records the review run.

## Scope and remaining trust boundaries

- The theorem concerns three explicit finite pools.  It says nothing about points outside
  them and does not change the global Hadwiger--Nelson bounds.
- The necessary condition "at least four pool points" imports the previously committed
  criticality, swap, pair, and triple delete/add closures; those dependencies were not
  re-proved in this review.
- This review checked that the 79 triple records are labelled UNSAT with recorded successful
  DRAT checks and used them to reconstruct P25, but did not regenerate their 79 separate
  colouring proof logs.  Their being genuine ties affects the interpretation of P25 as the
  pool of 150 known ties, not the independently verified fixed-pool minimum-order argument.
- Exact field operations, certificate parsing, witness replay, pool regeneration, RoundingSat,
  and VeriPB remain in the computational trust base.  The two review scripts use distinct
  code from the contribution; the large cutting-planes proof is independently checked rather
  than accepted from solver status.
