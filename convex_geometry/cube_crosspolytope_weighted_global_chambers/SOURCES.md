## Primary literature checked

- Z. Xu, *Multivariate Splines and Polytopes*, Journal of Approximation
  Theory 163 (2011), 377--387; arXiv:0806.1127,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.1127.  This is the principal general reference
  for multivariate truncated powers, box splines, and polytope-volume
  formulas.  It does not state the coordinate-tail replacement formula or
  the specialized chamber set (18).
- L. Pournin, *Shallow sections of the hypercube*, Israel Journal of
  Mathematics 255 (2023), 685--704; arXiv:2104.08484,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08484.  This treats extremal shallow sections of
  the cube, not the cube--crosspolytope Minkowski family.
- H. Koenig, *Non-central sections of the simplex, the cross-polytope and the
  cube*, arXiv:2002.10743, https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10743.  This treats the
  three individual bodies rather than their Minkowski sum and does not give
  a weighted all-radius chamber formula.

Targeted searches for weighted/noncentral sections, box-spline chamber
formulas, multivariate truncated-power partial fractions, and arbitrary
sections of cube--crosspolytope sums found the general framework and
individual-body literature above, but no matching formula.  The novelty
assessment is bounded and search-relative, not a priority claim.

## Trust boundary

The proof uses a finite coordinate-measure decomposition, bilateral Laplace
inversion of a rational function, partial fractions, and beta integrals.  The
checker uses CPython and exact `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic.  Its polygon
engine is independent finite-dimensional corroboration, not a theorem
premise.  No floating-point computation, solver, external dataset, or hidden
certificate is used.
