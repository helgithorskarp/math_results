# Sources, dependencies, and novelty boundary

Checked on 24 September 2026.

- [Kabluchko--Prochno, arXiv:2007.05247v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05247),
  *The maximum entropy principle and volumetric properties of Orlicz
  balls*, supplies general maximum-entropy and leading-volume antecedents.
  It does not state this flat-potential critical boundary layer.
- [Fruehwirth--Prochno, arXiv:2407.15579v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15579),
  *Sharp concentration phenomena in high-dimensional Orlicz balls*, uses
  first-order Edgeworth analysis for concentration and intersection
  questions.  Its setting and scaling do not give the finite-tail Bessel
  crossover here.
- [König--Rudelson, arXiv:1908.09358](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.09358),
  *On the volume of non-central sections of a cube*, proves dimension-free
  bounds for affine cube sections.  Cube sections are the boundary reference
  in the present theorem, but its critical Minkowski thickening is different.
- [König, arXiv:2002.10743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10743),
  *Non-central sections of the simplex, the cross-polytope and the cube*,
  studies extremal affine sections of the three individual bodies, not the
  $N^{-2}$ cube--crosspolytope transition.
- The direct Discovery Net source is contribution
  `bafkreigdfmtmgrkcmqk7cv3dvoldjoqjqvokzy3pgxn2pv3zu4taoboshe`,
  *Full all-order asymptotics for every interior affine cube-crosspolytope
  section*, committed at height 5697.  Its public source is the
  [fixed-interior theorem](../affine_cube_crosspolytope_sections/PROOF.md).
- The exact finite-section engine used for corroboration is derived in the
  [affine stratum formula](../affine_cube_crosspolytope_sections/EXACT_SECTION.md).

Targeted live searches combined “cube crosspolytope Minkowski sum,”
“noncentral section,” “flat Orlicz potential,” “boundary layer,” “Bessel
crossover,” and “thin neighborhood.”  They found the individual-body and
general Orlicz antecedents above, but no primary source stating the critical
$N^{-2}$ scale, the Bessel multiplier, or its explicit first correction.
This is a bounded, search-relative novelty statement, not certification of
historical priority.
