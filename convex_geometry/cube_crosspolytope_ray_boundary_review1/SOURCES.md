# Literature scope checked for this review

Checked live on 24 September 2026.  These are primary sources or primary
preprints relevant to the target's scope.

- Z. Kabluchko and J. Prochno,
  [*The maximum entropy principle and volumetric properties of Orlicz
  balls*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05247), studies precise asymptotic
  volumes and phase transitions for Orlicz balls.  Its stated scope is
  general asymptotic volume, not the target's finite-dimensional affine
  section identity.
- H. König,
  [*Non-central sections of the simplex, the cross-polytope and the
  cube*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10743), studies extremal non-central
  sections of the three individual bodies.  It does not state a section
  formula for their Minkowski sum.
- H. König and M. Rudelson,
  [*On the volume of non-central sections of a
  cube*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.09358), proves dimension-independent
  bounds for affine cube sections, rather than exact boundary-layer volumes
  for the cube--crosspolytope sum.
- R. Liu and T. Tkocz,
  [*A note on the extremal noncentral sections of the
  cross-polytope*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.06993), treats extremal
  non-central sections of the cross-polytope itself.
- K. Fukuda and C. Weibel,
  [*f-Vectors of Minkowski Additions of Convex
  Polytopes*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-007-1310-2), supplies
  combinatorial background for Minkowski sums, not the claimed section
  volumes or Bessel boundary layer.

Targeted searches combined the phrases “cube cross-polytope Minkowski sum,”
“affine hyperplane section,” “ray boundary,” “exact scaling,” and “Bessel.”
They found the individual-body and general-volume literature above, but no
primary source stating the offset-independent polynomial `Q_N`, its strict
slack-two range, or its Bessel expansion.  This supports only a bounded
search-relative novelty assessment; it cannot establish historical priority.
