# Sources and scope

Checked 22 September 2026.

1. The preceding [sharp simplex-envelope proof](../sharp_simplex_orthant_projections/PROOF.md),
   source commit b103e916f6efca491e2119e1bc9c2879cbb4e4f7,
   proves the finite unconditional realization of the envelope and
   the simplex optimum16/3. The present argument is self-contained
   as an envelope calculation; this previous theorem supplies its
   interpretation as a sharp projection ratio.

2. Matthieu Fradelizi, Auttawich Manui, Mark Meyer and Cheikh Saliou
   Ndiaye, [arXiv:2607.03582v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03582v1),
   Conjecture3, is the original unconditional orthant-projection
   conjecture, refuted by the preceding
   [counterexample package](../unconditional_orthant_projection_counterexample).
   Its factor4 in the plane is not an open target of this note.
   The narrower ball/zonoid questions are not settled here.

3. Stefano Campi and Paolo Gronchi, *Volume inequalities for sets
   associated with convex bodies*,
   [author manuscript, Section3](https://www3.diism.unisi.it/~campi/files/lavori/cinaproc.pdf).
   This describes the classical Rogers–Shephard shadow-volume
   convexity theorem, parallel chord movements, and the planar
   triangle-reduction method for functionals satisfying the required
   convexity hypothesis. We disprove that hypothesis for the
   optimized orthant envelope, not the classical theorem.

4. Daniel A. Klain, *On the equality conditions of the Brunn–Minkowski
   theorem*, [arXiv:1005.1409](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1409).
   Only classical Brunn–Minkowski concavity is needed to promote
   the explicitly proved local maximum to the global maximum.

The exact kite optimization, its local quadratic formula, and the
optimized shadow-convexity obstruction were not found in targeted
searches by the defining envelope, orthant projections, the candidate
constant, and parallel chord movements. This is a bounded literature
check, not a historical-priority guarantee.

The global maximum over all planar positive-image shapes remains
unresolved by this work. Its purpose is a rigorous obstruction to a
specific plausible proof route, with an exact family calculation
that can be reused when testing alternative routes.
