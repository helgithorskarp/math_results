# Sources and precise scope

Checked on 22 September 2026.

1. Matthieu Fradelizi, Auttawich Manui, Mark Meyer and Cheikh Saliou
   Ndiaye, *L_p-Rogers–Shephard type inequalities for L_p-zonoids and
   symmetric bodies*, [arXiv:2607.03582v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03582v1).
   Conjecture 3 proposes the factor $2^n$ for arbitrary unconditional
   bodies. The source's definition assumes coordinate sign invariance;
   permutation invariance is a separate condition.
   The [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03582) still lists v1.

2. The earlier Discovery Net
   [orthant counterexample package](../unconditional_orthant_projection_counterexample)
   gives the four-dimensional planar ratio $61/15>4$ and the complete
   classification of dimension pairs in which the original inequality
   is universally true. Its source commit is
   82ac40483e0df6ba76daaf3782440737bb9303df.
   The present work was selected from that graph neighborhood and
   determines a new sharp extremal problem with a simplex positive image.
   It does not claim to refute the original conjecture for the first time.

3. Daniel A. Klain, *On the equality conditions of the Brunn–Minkowski
   theorem*, [arXiv:1005.1409](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1409),
   [Theorem 1.1 in the primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1005.1409).
   The classical inequality and its homothety equality criterion are
   used for strict concavity and central-section maximality.
   All bodies in those applications have positive volume in the
   relevant subspace.

4. [NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §5.11](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11),
   supplies the classical Stirling expansion. The uniform factorial
   estimate used here and the elementary largest-term argument are
   explained in the proof.

The new mathematical content is the exact projection envelope and
its finite unconditional realization, the sharp simplex formula,
strict placement concavity and classification, and the exponential
growth rate. The finite dimension-three value is separately certified
by complete rational polyhedral calculations.

Targeted searches for the orthant/simplex projection problem, the
envelope's defining relation, and the constants did not locate an
earlier matching theorem. This is a bounded literature check, not
an exhaustive priority claim. Brunn–Minkowski, ordinary polyhedral
elimination, volume dissection and Stirling's formula are classical
inputs, not asserted as new.

The constant is optimized over lifts in all finite ambient dimensions.
It is not claimed to be sharp for arbitrary positive-image shapes or
at a prescribed ambient dimension. No asymptotic polynomial prefactor
is asserted. The source's narrower ball/zonoid conjectures remain open
within the scope of this contribution.
