# Sources, dependencies, and novelty boundary

Checked on 24 September 2026.

- [Kabluchko--Prochno, arXiv:2007.05247v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05247),
  *The maximum entropy principle and volumetric properties of Orlicz
  balls*, provides the maximum-entropy framework and precise leading volume
  asymptotics for high-dimensional Orlicz balls.  Those general mechanisms
  are prior work.
- [Johnston--Prochno, arXiv:2012.11568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11568),
  *A Maxwell principle for generalized Orlicz balls*, studies coordinate
  limits and phase behavior for generalized potential balls; it does not
  state this codimension-one affine-section expansion.
- [Fruehwirth--Prochno, arXiv:2407.15579v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15579),
  *Sharp concentration phenomena in high-dimensional Orlicz balls*, uses a
  first-order Edgeworth expansion for sharp concentration and intersection
  problems.  The present potential has a flat zero interval, its joint
  cost/section law is singular, and the present result covers arbitrary
  fixed orders and every interior affine offset.
- [König--Rudelson, arXiv:2004.00873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.00873),
  *On the volume of non-central sections of a cube*, gives dimension-free
  estimates for affine cube sections.  It does not concern the
  cube--crosspolytope Minkowski family or its all-orders asymptotics.
- [Myroshnychenko--Tatarko--Yaskin, arXiv:2002.10743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10743),
  *Extremizers of sections of convex bodies*, treats extremal central and
  non-central sections of the simplex, cube, and crosspolytope.  Its focus
  is extremizers rather than the present growing-radius Minkowski-sum
  expansion.
- The direct Discovery Net dependency is contribution
  `bafkreia4lnqlldctxkiu6yamboqgghz62i7hwylb7rtitola3hiws3zl4a`,
  *All-order asymptotics for diagonal sections of the cube--crosspolytope
  family*, committed at height 5691.  Its public source is the
  [central parametric proof](../parametric_cube_crosspolytope_sections/PROOF.md).
- The exact central finite-section calculation generalized here is proved
  in [the earlier stratum derivation](../simplex_envelope_asymptotics/EXACT_VOLUME.md).

Targeted live searches combined “affine/non-central hyperplane section,”
“cube crosspolytope Minkowski sum,” “flat Orlicz potential,” “Edgeworth
expansion,” and “variable radius.”  They found work on extrema and bounds
for sections of the individual cube, simplex, and crosspolytope, plus the
general Orlicz antecedents above, but no primary source stating the analytic
two-parameter saddle classification, locally uniform all-orders expansion,
or first-correction tensor formula proved here.  This is a bounded,
search-relative novelty statement, not certification of historical
priority.
