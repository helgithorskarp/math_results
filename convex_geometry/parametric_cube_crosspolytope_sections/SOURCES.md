# Sources, dependencies, and novelty boundary

Checked on 24 September 2026:

- [Kabluchko--Prochno, arXiv:2007.05247v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05247),
  *The maximum entropy principle and volumetric properties of Orlicz
  balls*, establishes Gibbs/maximum-entropy methods and precise leading
  volume asymptotics for high-dimensional Orlicz balls. Those mechanisms
  are prior work.
- [Johnston--Prochno, arXiv:2012.11568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11568),
  *A Maxwell principle for generalized Orlicz balls*, treats coordinate
  limits and phase behavior for generalized potential balls. It does not
  give the present codimension-one diagonal-section expansion.
- [Fruehwirth--Prochno, arXiv:2407.15579v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15579),
  *Sharp concentration phenomena in high-dimensional Orlicz balls*, uses a
  first-order Edgeworth expansion for sharp concentration and intersection
  questions. Its stated Orlicz setting requires a potential vanishing only
  at the origin and concerns full-dimensional ball probabilities. The
  present potential \((|x|-1)_+\) has a flat zero interval, its one-step
  tilted law is singular in the joint section/cost variables, and the
  object is a codimension-one section.
- The direct Discovery Net source is the campaign contribution
  `bafkreid56cnyjlphywmhit7iuv3yfesmhmwrkakni4scms7jt3pvkwklei`,
  *Full asymptotic expansion and second correction for sharp simplex
  projection constants*, committed at height 5681. Its public source is
  [the single-radius proof](../simplex_envelope_edgeworth_correction/ALL_ORDERS.md).
- The exact finite-section formula used for corroboration is proved in
  [EXACT_VOLUME.md](../simplex_envelope_asymptotics/EXACT_VOLUME.md).

Targeted live searches combined “cube crosspolytope Minkowski sum,”
“central/diagonal hyperplane section,” “flat Orlicz potential,” “Edgeworth
expansion,” and “variable radius.” They found the general antecedents above
but no primary source stating the locally parameter-uniform full expansion
for these sections or the rational correction (7). This is a bounded,
search-relative novelty statement, not certification of historical priority.
