# Sources, prior results, and scope

Checked on 22 September 2026.

The target is **Conjecture 3** in Matthieu Fradelizi, Auttawich Manui,
Mark Meyer and Cheikh Saliou Ndiaye,
*L_p-Rogers–Shephard type inequalities for L_p-zonoids and symmetric
bodies*, arXiv:2607.03582v1, dated 3 July 2026:
[primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03582v1),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03582).
The version record still lists v1 at this check.

The relevant source distinctions are:

| Source item | Relevance |
| --- | --- |
| Conjecture 3 | Arbitrary bodies invariant under coordinate sign changes; this is the claim refuted here. |
| Propositions 3 and 4 | The hyperplane and line positive cases, respectively; these are prior results. |
| Conjectures 1 and 2 | Asymmetric $L_p$-zonoids and $\ell_q$-ball projections; these narrower claims are not refuted here. |
| Proposition 6 | An averaged statement with additional assumptions, including permutation symmetry; not the present target. |

The new evidence is the exact box example, its constant-positive-image
family, the resulting negative cases in the dimension classification,
the product amplification, and the explicit smooth comparison.
Elementary proofs of the known positive cases are included for
completeness, not asserted as new.
