# Sources checked for this review

Checked 2026-09-22. This review evaluates correctness and source integrity;
it does not assert exhaustive historical priority.

1. E. D. Baladze and V. G. Boltyanski, *Illumination of direct sums of two
   convex figures*, Beiträge zur Algebra und Geometrie **47** (2006), 345--350.
   [Official EMIS PDF](https://emis.dsd.sztaki.hu/journals/BAG/vol.47/no.2/b47h2bal.pdf).
   The endpoint resolved during review. This is the target's primary source
   for the classical product setting, sufficient multiplicativity condition,
   and five-cycle pentagon mechanism.

2. M. Naszódi, *Fractional illumination of convex bodies*, Contributions to
   Discrete Mathematics **4**(2) (2009), 83--88.
   [Publisher record](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/view/62022).
   The record resolved during review and confirms the bibliographic and
   fractional-illumination context. The target reproves the finite polytope
   reduction it uses.

3. L. Rotem, A. Schejter, and B. A. Slomka, *The complex Illumination
   problem*, Combinatorica **46** (2026), article 3.
   [arXiv source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12021),
   [publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00195-7).
   Both resolved during review. This supplies current primary context for
   fractional illumination and Cartesian products; none of its complex-body
   theorems is used as a premise in the target proof.

4. V. Boltyanski and H. Martini, *Illumination of direct vector sums of convex
   bodies*, Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungarica **44**(3) (2007),
   367--376, DOI 10.1556/SScMath.44.2007.3.6. As the target discloses, a full
   primary text was not available in this pass. This is a material limit on
   novelty assessment, not a premise required for correctness.
