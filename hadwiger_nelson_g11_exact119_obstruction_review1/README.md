# Independent review of the `G_11` exact-119-image obstruction

Verdict: **ACCEPT at high confidence**, with the exact scope below.  No
edge-preserving map

```text
G_11 = Cay(F_11^2,{d : d_1^2+d_2^2=1}) -> R^2
```

has exactly 119 distinct images.  Together with the 121- and 120-image
exclusions, every such plane map has at most 118 images.  Existence at 118 or
below is not claimed.

The reviewed source is
[`hadwiger_nelson_g11_exact119_obstruction`](../hadwiger_nelson_g11_exact119_obstruction/README.md)
at commit `8a96fd3545bf35d6578cee34df69db2a4205cd68`.  Its Discovery contribution
`bafkreibhcjohovpwzfhslgatgywpqhdih52x4nlpxrio4tmajingjs6cvq` was pending,
not committed, when this review was performed, so no target relation is
asserted here.

## Scope and Hadwiger–Nelson relevance

`G_11` is an **abstract** 121-vertex, 726-edge graph of chromatic number five.
The accepted theorem is a realization obstruction for this fixed source.  It
does not produce a plane unit-distance graph, coordinates, or a record
candidate; it does not concern arbitrary 119-point unit-distance graphs,
edge-deleted subgraphs, or other abstract sources.

Parts's 509-vertex, 2,442-edge five-chromatic plane unit-distance graph remains
the published unrestricted record
([arXiv:2010.12665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12665)).  Haugland's August 2026
paper still calls 509 current; its 2,131-vertex construction addresses the
Moser-spindle-free restricted problem
([arXiv:2608.04542v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04542v4)).

## Mathematical audit

A map with 119 images has collision deficit two.  Because endpoints of a
source edge cannot coincide, every fibre is independent, and the nonsingleton
fibres have exactly one of the shapes `3` or `2+2`.  Choose a pair in one such
fibre and translate one member to zero.  The form `x^2+y^2` is anisotropic over
`F_11`, so a nonzero nonedge difference has norm in `{2,...,10}`.  Multiplying
in `F_121=F_11[i]` by a norm-one element proves transitivity on each nonzero
norm shell; direct enumeration independently finds 24 orthogonal matrices and
one 12-vector orbit per shell.

After the first pair is normalized, every valid third member of the triple or
every valid second nonedge pair of singleton classes is enumerated.  This
covers 864 triple events and 56,871 two-pair events.  The 57,735 events are a
coverage enumeration with harmless duplicates, not an isomorphism-class
count.  This establishes completeness for all fibre partitions of deficit
two.

For cyclically ordered distinct points `a,b,c,d` joined by four unit edges,
`b,d` are the two intersections of equal-radius circles centred at `a,c`.
Their common midpoint gives the signed rhombus equation

```text
p_a - p_b + p_c - p_d = 0.
```

Every such integer row annihilates constants.  On an `n`-vertex quotient its
real rank is therefore at most `n-1`.  Rank `n-1` over any finite prime field
exhibits an integer minor nonzero modulo that prime, hence a nonzero real
minor.  Both Cartesian coordinate vectors would then be constant, contrary to
a unit edge in an injective quotient drawing.

The target verifies maximal ranks over `F_2`.  Its producer, verifier,
alternate-basis audit, controls, finite-abelian predecessor, and one-collision
predecessor all replayed successfully; both certificates regenerated byte for
byte.

## Independent signed `F_3` verification

[`independent_mod3.py`](independent_mod3.py) imports no target, producer, or
predecessor code.  It pins the reviewed source bytes, reconstructs `G_11` from
the norm definition, enumerates every quotient and every needed four-cycle,
and performs packed Gaussian elimination on the **signed** rhombus rows over
`F_3`.  Thus it changes the coefficient field, sign representation, basis, and
fallback set from the target calculation.

The complete source four-cycle matrix has rank 120 over `F_3`, independently
excluding an injective 121-image drawing.  The nine one-collision quotient
matrices have rank 119, independently excluding all 120-image maps.  Finally,
all 57,735 deficit-two events have rank 118.  The independent basis needs 776
complete-cycle fallbacks containing 1,565,898 cycles, compared with the
target's 777 fallbacks and 1,567,802 equations.  The difference is expected
from the new field and basis and gives useful non-replay evidence.

The packed ternary elimination was checked against ordinary definition-level
Gaussian elimination on all 21,304 matrices through three rows and columns.
Normal and optimized runs produced byte-identical results and CNFs in 61.603
and 62.241 seconds.

## Independent abstract chromatic certificate

The review separately checks that the source is abstractly five-chromatic.
The published proper five-colouring passes all 726 edges.  The independent
checker emits a symmetry-free CNF with exactly one of four colours per vertex:
484 variables, 3,751 clauses, and SHA-256
`4ad85413d9b179993b82cd78d965afdc30418b0683941de9a0bdab379e05bb3e`.

Kissat 4.0.4 and CaDiCaL 1.9.5 both returned UNSAT.  Their respective
86,998-byte and 188,139-byte DRAT traces have SHA-256
`f5e936c88766036648fb2e91e2277be8e7def7c6b2bcd260ae3068b3080c47f2`
and `4e7f1ffd5e9fbe6fbde22c29521a0c864d7fecc8296f9d653f7b0a23c5f1cb6e`;
`drat-trim` returned `s VERIFIED` for both.  Abstract chromaticity and physical
realizability are therefore checked separately and are not conflated.

## Reproduce

From the repository root with CPython 3.11 or later:

```sh
mkdir -p /scratch/g11-exact119-review
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -B \
  hadwiger_nelson_g11_exact119_obstruction_review1/independent_mod3.py \
  --cnf-out /scratch/g11-exact119-review/g11-four.cnf \
  | diff -u \
      hadwiger_nelson_g11_exact119_obstruction_review1/EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt -
(cd hadwiger_nelson_g11_exact119_obstruction_review1 && \
  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS)

kissat --seed=260914 -f /scratch/g11-exact119-review/g11-four.cnf \
  /scratch/g11-exact119-review/kissat.drat
drat-trim /scratch/g11-exact119-review/g11-four.cnf \
  /scratch/g11-exact119-review/kissat.drat

cadical --seed=260914 /scratch/g11-exact119-review/g11-four.cnf \
  /scratch/g11-exact119-review/cadical.drat
drat-trim /scratch/g11-exact119-review/g11-four.cnf \
  /scratch/g11-exact119-review/cadical.drat
```

## Limits and trust boundary

The review trusts the written fibre-partition, finite-field action, Euclidean
rhombus, and rank-lift arguments; pinned bytes; CPython integer/JSON/SHA-256
semantics; ordinary hardware; and `drat-trim` for the ancillary chromatic
proofs.  The rank theorem itself uses no floating point, randomness, SAT
answer, numerical tolerance, or unpublished certificate.  No proof-assistant
formalization is supplied.

I found no missing fibre shape, failed orbit reduction, quotient loop, omitted
normalized event, invalid four-cycle, deficient rank, chromatic-certificate
error, or hidden physical-realization assumption.  Acceptance is warranted
for this fixed-source exclusion and its at-most-118 corollary, with the absence
of any actual plane construction kept explicit.
