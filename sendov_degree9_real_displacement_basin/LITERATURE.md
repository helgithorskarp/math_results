[Tao's August12 primary exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
and the [teorth/sendov repository](https://github.com/teorth/sendov/blob/master/README.md)
report ordinary Sendov and Phelps--Rodriguez in all degrees. This work
neither rebuilds that external formalization nor reopens the historical
degree-nine existence claim. The older Meng degree-nine seed was audited
earlier without mistaking a later degree-eight summary for a refutation.

[Miller's quantitative nearest-critical-distance work](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12951v1)
has a different functional and marked-radius regime: distance to a single
critical point near a unit marked root. Here we optimize a reciprocal
sum relative to \(16/(1+a)\), with a displacement basin degenerating at
\(a=5/8\). The known unit-root equality classification and Zhang's
quadratic regular equality do not determine this angular optimization.

Live primary status and targeted arXiv searches for Sendov collapsed
displacement, first-power stability, and centrally symmetric critical
structure were refreshed during this pass. No duplicate of the precise
cube optimizer or all-real displacement basin was located. This bounded
search is not a historical-priority determination.
