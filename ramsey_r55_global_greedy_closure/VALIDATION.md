# Validation and trust boundary

The exact public sources passed both ordinary Python3.11.2 and optimized
`python3 -O` replays. No assertion controls proof acceptance. The ordinary
full public audit took 87.686629097 seconds; optimized mode took
88.303516534 seconds. Both checked the same seven saved complete formulas.
`PUBLIC_REPLAY.json` pins the corresponding interface audit hash.

The source copy is checked against the private production source. Every
h3835 dependency is hashed against its fixed source manifest on each replay.
No solver is invoked by these tests.

## Finite residual census

All 334 catalog entries are independently parsed and inspected for every
red triangle and independent five-set. A remaining-subset recurrence counts
ordered independent triple partitions. A separate vertex-to-bin recursion
counts the same objects by assigning individual vertices to named bins.
Each entry and each final-shape count agrees: 167,046 ordered partitions.
The six or two possible final-triple bijections are independently enumerated.
No graph automorphism quotient is computed or assumed.

There are 2,889 first/middle/last checks of catalog embedding codes and their
inverses, with 103,590 physical edge comparisons against the independent
catalog representation. Physical duplicates between codes are allowed.
Catalog completeness is imported from McKay's author page, not regenerated.
The global greedy proof and complete CNF coverage do not depend on that
catalog import; the catalog carrier and its numerical bound do.

## Global physical interface

Every branch count is independently recomputed using the remaining disjoint
matrix factors. All 39 branches, both residual unions, and all 106,263 added
clauses are checked literally. Of those clauses, 103,941 meet at least three
blocks. Thirty-five branches receive extra clauses; four do not.

The carrier checks 117 global index boundaries and 105,651 physical edges.
Thirty-nine deliberately non-target full43 fixtures verify the greedy
normalizer and its 35,217 vertex-transport edge checks. They are structural
controls, not certificates of good43 existence. The interface audit has 75
negative controls, in addition to six catalog-checker controls. The public
replay checks four malformed or physically invalid SAT claims through the
final target decoder. `verify_registry.py` verifies all task records and all
39 disjoint covering-code intervals.

Seven complete physical formulas were generated and separately reconstructed
from vertex pairs and literal forbidden subsets. This compares every one of
9,858,581 clauses, including 21,414 extra closure clauses. The generated files
total 441,175,531 bytes and remain outside the repository. Each formula retains
the h3835 treatment of all 962,598 five-subsets. `FULL_AUDIT.json` gives exact
hashes, sizes and branch records, including the smallest carrier task.

The representative branches are (5,1,0), (5,4,3), (6,2,1), (6,3,2),
(7,1,2), (7,4,0) and (5,1,2). The complete source parameterizes all 39
branches; all their extra clauses and dimensions are audited. The pre-existing
full-target generator itself remains the independently accepted h3835 source.

Two development integration mistakes were repaired before source freezing:
the carrier CLI initially called the parent's nonexistent `verify` name
instead of `count`, and an in-memory integer-key statistics dictionary was
normalized to the JSON string-key representation for replay comparison.
Neither affected the mathematical family or any counted value. All public
replays and the final target-decoder controls passed after these repairs.

## Scope

No SAT or UNSAT decision was attempted. No good43, branch exclusion,
Ramsey-bound improvement or historical novelty is claimed. The computation
is not a proof-assistant formalization. The new strengthening awaits external
review. The h3835/h3845 dependency is accepted; the recently shared h3859
sorting result is contextual and is not included in the generated formulas.
The failed good19 bridge, its saved witness, invalidated h3687 verifier, and
parked neighborhood and occupancy routes are not premises.
