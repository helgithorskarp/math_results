## Primary endpoint and comparison

[Zhang, September 16, 2026, Conjecture 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126)
states the reciprocal-power family conjecturally for exponent at least
one. Its exponent-one endpoint is strongest; Theorem 1.3 proves the
quadratic case, with regular unit binomials as the equality case, and
Corollary 1.4 gives higher exponents. The degree-nine general complex
first-power endpoint is the assigned unresolved target.
[Tao's August 12 exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
reports an all-degree ordinary Sendov and Phelps--Rodriguez proof and
states Tang--Zhang as Conjecture 19. The
[Lean repository](https://github.com/teorth/sendov/blob/master/README.md)
is a primary formalization report. This pass did not rebuild or
independently audit the full ordinary-Sendov proof.

The historical [2017 degree-nine claim](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235)
and [2026 threshold seed](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256) were previously
audited for a status discrepancy: a later statement of only the low-degree
range through eight is not a refutation of the 2017 argument. The newer
primary ordinary-Sendov report supersedes that historical target in the
current authorized brief. The present result is a local strengthening
frontier and makes no acceptance or falsity verdict on the 2017 claim.

For a nearby classical comparison,
[Brown, On the Ilieff--Sendov conjecture, 1988](https://msp.org/pjm/1988/135-2/pjm-v135-n2-p02-s.pdf)
proves ordinary Sendov for any degree with at most four distinct roots.
That is a nearest-critical-distance assertion for the full polynomial.
Our multiplicity condition is on the eight angular slopes near collapse,
allows independent inward depths and hence more distinct original roots,
and optimizes a different reciprocal-sum displacement coefficient.
Even the boundary four-block profile has four other-root values plus
the marked root. This classical theorem is not the new basin claim.

The matrix compression and secular residue mechanism are classical.
The reciprocal companion formulation appears in
[Tang--Zhang, Lemma 3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341v3).
The new exact calculation concerns the cubic active trace for the whole
asymmetric 3+3+1+1 angular class. Neither polynomial differentiation,
matrix compression, the secular norm identity nor Bernstein positivity
is claimed as a new general method.
