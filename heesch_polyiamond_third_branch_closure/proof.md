## Tile, conventions and claim

[prototype input](../heesch_polyiamond_deficit_review1/input.json).
Axial coordinates use the unit basis at sixty degrees. Centroid coordinates
in the certificates are multiplied by three. The earlier sources credit the
Mann-family construction; that known finite-five example is not claimed anew.
The numerical catalogue of 59 placements with 21 retained is not itself a
proof of the 38 excluded interior-pair lemmas.

A packing is a finite family of distinct whole congruent closed copies of T214
with disjoint interiors. Write U(F) for its union. A strict-surround chain is
nested F0,...,FH with U(Fj) contained in int U(Fj+1). Real translations,
rotations and reflections are allowed. The negatives permit arbitrary
topology and impose no new-copy contact condition. Ordinary coronas also
require new copies to touch the preceding layer; closed point contact suffices.
Positive fixtures below have disc cumulative unions, hence meet both the
Hc and final-outer-holes Hh conventions.
