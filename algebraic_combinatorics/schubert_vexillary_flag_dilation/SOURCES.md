# Sources and status check

Searches were last run on 2026-09-21.

## Primary mathematical sources

- Morales, Pak, and Panova, *Asymptotics of principal evaluations of
  Schubert polynomials for layered permutations*, Conjecture 4.1:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04341>.
- Worley, *On the combinatorics of tableaux -- A notebook of open
  problems*, Problem 14 (the all-`k` extension):
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25446>.
- Nadeau and Tewari, *The permutahedral variety, mixed Eulerian numbers, and
  principal specializations of Schubert polynomials*, Section 7 for the
  canonical shape/flag description of vexillary permutations and the
  flagged-tableau specialization:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12194>.
- Knutson, Miller, and Yong, *Groebner geometry of vertex decompositions and
  of flagged tableaux*, for vexillary Schubert/Grothendieck polynomials and
  flagged tableaux: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502144>.
- Proctor and Willis, *Row bounds needed to justifiably express flagged Schur
  functions with Gessel--Viennot determinants*, for the flagged determinant:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.01182>.
- McDowell, *Flagged Schur polynomial duality via a lattice path bijection*,
  for a modern path treatment of flagged determinants:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04957>.

## Bounded novelty and status search

The committed Discovery Net neighborhood around the Morales--Pak--Panova
problem was checked through indexed height 5405.  It contained all-`k`
theorems for Grassmannian, inverse-Grassmannian, and parabolic-product
classes, but no vexillary closure/flag-dilation reduction.

Targeted searches included the exact phrases and combinations
`vexillary identity inflation`, `2143 identity block inflation`, `vexillary
Kronecker identity permutation`, `flagged Schur dilation inequality`, and
`vexillary Lehmer flag dilation`, as well as the cited papers and the public
source repository.  They found the classical ingredients above but no direct
statement of the combined reduction in this directory.  This is a
search-relative observation, not a claim of historical priority.

The general Morales--Pak--Panova `k=2` conjecture remains open in the cited
2026 notebook.  This contribution does not claim to prove it for vexillary
permutations; it identifies the precise flagged-tableau inequality needed for
that class.
