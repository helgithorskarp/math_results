# Sources and literature boundary

## Primary sources

1. Alejandro H. Morales, Igor Pak, and Greta Panova,
   *Asymptotics of principal evaluations of Schubert polynomials for
   layered permutations*, arXiv:1805.04341v1, especially (1.1) and
   Conjecture 4.1.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04341>

2. Eric Marberg and Brendan Pawlowski,
   *Principal specializations of Schubert polynomials in classical types*,
   Algebraic Combinatorics 4 (2021), 273--287, arXiv:2002.00303.  This
   records Macdonald's type-A weighted reduced-word identity in the
   principal-specialization setting.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00303>

3. Hugh Thomas and Nathan Williams (organizers),
   *On the combinatorics of tableaux--A notebook of open problems*,
   arXiv:2509.25446v3, Problem 14 for the all-\(k\) formulation.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25446v3>

4. Yiming Chen, Neil J. Y. Fan, Rui Xiong, and Ming Yao,
   *Equivariant Schubert Calculus for Inverse Grassmannian Permutations*,
   arXiv:2607.16797.  This is a current primary source confirming the named
   inverse-Grassmannian class; its multiplication results do not address
   identity inflation or the inequality proved here.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16797>

## Search boundary

On 2026-09-20 we searched the current arXiv/web index, the committed
Discovery Net graph through indexed height 5295, and the authorized source
repository for combinations of

- `inverse-Grassmannian`, `inverse Grassmannian`;
- `identity inflation`, `w tensor 1_k`, and permutation-matrix Kronecker
  product;
- principal specialization, Schubert polynomial, inversion, and
  Macdonald's formula.

The search found the classical ingredients and current work on products of
inverse-Grassmannian Schubert classes, but no statement combining inversion
symmetry with identity inflation to obtain this all-\(k\) class or its
equality criterion.  This is search-relative novelty evidence, not a claim
of historical priority.
