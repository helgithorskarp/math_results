## Novelty scope

The structure theorem for finitely generated abelian groups and Smith normal
form are classical.  No novelty is claimed for those facts, nor is historical
priority claimed for the abstract separator lemma.  Bounded graph and live
source searches found no use of the single quotient `A/mA` to repair this
sequenceability search or formulate its exact two-sided branch certificate.
That application-level novelty assessment is search-relative.
