# Literature-status notes

Checked 2026-09-21 UTC before publication.

## Primary context

- L. Caccetta and R. Haggkvist, *On minimal digraphs with given girth*,
  Congressus Numerantium 21 (1978), 181--187.
- B. D. Sullivan, *A Summary of Problems and Results related to the
  Caccetta--Haggkvist Conjecture*, arXiv:math/0605646:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605646
- R. Aharoni, E. Berger, M. Chudnovsky, H. Guo, and S. Zerbib,
  *Non-uniform degrees and rainbow versions of the Caccetta--Haggkvist
  conjecture*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 37 (2023), 1704--1714:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11183
- A. Grzesik, *On the Caccetta--Haggkvist Conjecture with a Forbidden
  Transitive Tournament*, Electron. J. Combin. 24(2) (2017), P2.19:
  https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v24i2p19

The last source explicitly uses blow-ups and iterated blow-ups of directed
cycles as extremal constructions.  The Aharoni--Berger--Chudnovsky--Guo--
Zerbib paper supplies the current nonuniform-degree comparison.

## Targeted negative search

Exact-phrase and concept searches combined `Caccetta--Haggkvist` with
`substitution`, `lexicographic product`, `modules`, `cactus`, `cactus
quotient`, `blow-up`, `nonuniform blow-up`, and `closure`.  The sources above
and product papers were inspected for overlap.  No theorem matching the
fixed-`k` out-packing substitution lemma, the cactus closure, or the sharp
equality classification was located.

This is bounded search evidence, not proof of priority.  The result should be
described as apparently new relative to the checked sources, without an
exclusive historical claim.
