# Dependencies and scope

The new exactly-one-centre-edge argument is self-contained apart from
elementary unit-circle geometry and the 251-case parity-allocation lemma.
The following durable results cover degenerate boundary cases and context:

  [source](../hadwiger_nelson_dominating_unit_path/README.md), Discovery Net

  `bafkreicj6dz3yq4yfla3nlhcx2ah4gskbe63mk4ayl7mzfep7ppu2jnv3a`.

The h3977--h3993 episode is a methodological warning, not a premise: an
abstract five-chromatic graph can fail plane unit-distance realizability. In
this contribution the only abstract unsatisfiable phase systems are tested
against exact centre and intersection geometry before any conclusion is
drawn; all are unrealizable.

The current published construction record remains Parts' 509-vertex graph:
[Parts, arXiv:2010.12665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12665). Haugland's August
2026 paper also identifies 509 as the smallest known unrestricted example:
[arXiv:2608.04542](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04542). The present theorem does
not change that record. Both primary pages were refreshed on 2026-09-13.

A targeted live search for strict unit-distance colouring theorems formulated
through domination found work on unit-*disk* network domination and perfect
domination in lattice graphs, but no matching theorem for a dominating triple
containing an exact unit edge. Those topics use different adjacency or ambient
families. This is only a collision check; no literature-priority claim is made.
