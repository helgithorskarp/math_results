# Complete fresh-triangle insertion gate and a larger joint candidate

Let `S` be the published nine-move 509-point seed, `V` the original Parts
point set, and `C(V)` the set of all points with at least two unit neighbours
in `V`. The completed target-order gate proves:

> For every unit equilateral triangle `A` disjoint from `S`, such that each
> vertex of `A` has at least two unit neighbours in `S` and at least one
> vertex lies outside `C(V)`, and for every four-element `D` contained in
> `S`, the strict graph on `(S minus D) union A` is four-colourable.

This classifies the stated multipoint mutation family over all planar
placements and excludes every 508-point candidate in it. It requires the
two-neighbour condition and a triangle that leaves the original two-circle
catalogue. It does not classify arbitrary triangle additions or arbitrary
subgraphs of the larger joint host below. The full proof is in
[TRIANGLE_GATE.md](TRIANGLE_GATE.md).

The standard-library verifier regenerates the entire geometry and checks
the compact positive-colouring certificate:

```sh
python3 hadwiger_nelson_cyclic_batch_probe/gate.py --output /tmp/hn-triangle-gate
```

Expected: `all_checks:true`, `maximum_declared_deletions:3`,
`gate_four_colourable:true`. The census has 126 exact field-valued fresh
triangles and 1468 conservative non-field triangle candidates. Every true
triangle in the specified family is covered. There are 300 additional
checked seed-deletion colourings beyond the sibling's 509 rows. This proof
needs no SAT solver, floating-point decision or imported completion census.

The probe produced a **543-vertex, 2587-edge, 5-vertex-critical** planar
unit-distance graph. It replaces six vertices of the
[nine-move seed](../hadwiger_nelson_neutral_mutation_candidate/README.md)
with forty points generated by unit-circle intersections. Twelve of the
forty points lie outside the original Parts field-valued completion
catalogue. The result retains 494 original Parts points.

This is an uncompetitive construction checkpoint: it is larger than the
509-vertex starting graph and does not meet the target of at most 508.
It records that coupled cyclic additions can change the critical support,
but the observed replacement cost is forty points for six. No priority,
isomorphism classification, or improvement to the known vertex count is claimed.

The comparison baseline is Parts's [509-vertex construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12665),
still identified as the record in Haugland's
[August 2026 manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04542v4).
Both primary sources were checked on 7 September 2026.

## Reconstructing the candidate

The seed labels are the canonical `0,...,508` labels produced by the sibling
nine-move verifier. Delete seed vertices

```text
113, 114, 346, 349, 366, 367.
```

Their earlier source labels are `113,114,349,354,372,373`.
Keep the remaining 503 seed vertices in increasing order and append the
forty points specified by `circle_templates` in `construction.json`.

A template `[a,b,j]` uses the two unit circles centred at seed vertices
`a,b`. If `d=b-a` denotes the displacement vector, the intersections are

```text
(a+b)/2 +/- sqrt(1/|d|^2 - 1/4) perpendicular(d).
```

Compute both intersections exactly in
`K=Q(sqrt(3),sqrt(5),sqrt(11))`, remove duplicates, and order them
lexicographically by their rational coefficient vectors. Take entry `j`.
Circle centres are taken from the full original seed, even if their vertices
are subsequently deleted. The forty templates, six deletions, input hash,
five-colouring and regression hashes occupy approximately 3 KB.

Every coordinate of the resulting graph is an integer coefficient vector
divided by 288 in the basis

```text
1, sqrt(3), sqrt(5), sqrt(15), sqrt(11), sqrt(33), sqrt(55), sqrt(165).
```

All 543 points are distinct. Independently of the intersection formula,
integer monomial squares test every one of the 147,153 unordered pairs and
recover exactly 2587 unit edges. The supplied five-colouring is checked on
all of them.

## Lower bound and vertex-criticality

The canonical four-colour CNF has 2172 variables and 14,152 clauses. It has
one at-least-one and six at-most-one clauses per vertex, four incompatibility
clauses per edge, and three distinct-colour pins on the actual triangle
`(0,147,150)`. Any proper four-colouring can be permuted to satisfy those
pins, so the encoding preserves satisfiability.

A Kissat 4.0.4 refutation was verified with `drat-trim`. All 543 single-vertex
deletions have explicit four-colourings, checked against the exact graph.
The public command below then independently regenerated the same refutation
and a second complete set of deletion colourings and checked both. Thus the
graph has chromatic number exactly five and is vertex-critical. Further
vertex deletion from this particular graph cannot reach the target.

```text
CNF SHA256

DRAT SHA256 (5970404 bytes)
490b164d7c5f6914439451f46c7e7a6c2d93f13a8bb187e057ef859272bd2a3e
Original packed deletion words SHA256 (73848 bytes)
cab775ba4be62ead6a0e82f6bb298f9b706ecacc98ac915c7142be306e6055dc
Publicly regenerated deletion words SHA256
d5ecbb6463af7d969f0d0cf57418045e59f36b4f7f030602198083d2aaa9d4fc
```

The two sets of colour words differ and both pass direct verification.
Regenerated witnesses or proofs need not have these hashes. Expanded graphs,
colour words, traces and logs remain outside Git; the source regenerates them.
Recorded hashes alone do not prove non-four-colourability or criticality.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, Python 3.11+ and the standard library suffice for
exact reconstruction, the five-colouring check and CNF generation:

```sh
python3 hadwiger_nelson_cyclic_batch_probe/reproduce.py \
  --output /tmp/hn-cyclic
```

This invocation explicitly reports `DRAT_verified:false` and
`deletion_words_checked:0`. For complete certificate regeneration, install
`requirements-search.txt` in a separate virtual environment and supply the
Kissat and `drat-trim` executables:

```sh
python3 hadwiger_nelson_cyclic_batch_probe/reproduce.py \
  --output /tmp/hn-cyclic \
  --kissat /path/to/kissat --drat-trim /path/to/drat-trim \
  --generate-deletions
```

Expected output includes `vertices:543`, `edges:2587`,
`DRAT_verified:true`, and `deletion_words_checked:543`.
Use `--proof /path/to/graph.drat` instead of `--kissat` to replay an existing
trace; use `--deletions /path/to/deletion_words.bin` instead of
`--generate-deletions` to replay existing colour words. The producer's default
conflict budget is 2,000,000 per deletion. An unresolved or non-SAT case fails
the run rather than claiming criticality.

The recorded environment used CPython 3.11.2 for replay, CPython 3.12.14 with
PySAT 1.9.dev15 / CaDiCaL 1.9.5 for fresh witnesses, Kissat commit
`8af8e56f174b778aef3aa45af9f739b2a5f492c2`, and `drat-trim` commit
`2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985`.
Trust is in the exact coordinate and CNF bridges, Python arithmetic, the
hash-bound sibling coordinate inputs and the C DRAT checker. SAT models are
checked directly. No earlier chromaticity or closure theorem is needed as a
premise for this graph, and no external review or formalization is claimed.

## How the probe reached its stopping point

The private construction search began with the certified nine-move seed.
It generated all field-valued circle centres with at least two seed
neighbours by combining the old completion catalogue with circle pairs
involving a moved point. There were 104 coordinates outside the old 4372-point
field-valued catalogue and 126 unit triangles involving them.

The union of those triangles used 260 added points. Adjoining them to the
seed gave a 769-point, 3560-edge graph. One deterministic deletion pass,
with degree-below-four peeling, produced the graph certified here. It is
**not** a proof that every subgraph of the 769-point host needs 543 vertices,
or that the host has no 508-vertex candidate.

Separately, the initial single-triangle gate found no four-delete/three-add
improvement among those 126 triangles: a checked colouring library reduced
the decision to six triangles and 343 direct 508-point checks, all positive.
An integer interval screen for fresh triangles outside `K` gave 1468
conservative triangle candidates; the same library covered each after every
single seed deletion. These searches concern triangles whose new vertices
each have at least two seed neighbours. Their full transcripts are retained
in the research checkpoint. The compact certificate refinement and complete
geometry regeneration are now public in `gate.py` and `gate_geometry.py`.
The separate `reproduce.py` command certifies the explicit 543-point graph.

The probe reaches its boundary with a complete target-order triangle gate
and a larger critical graph from the joint construction. The triangle gate and vertex-criticality of
the 543-point graph do not exclude other batches, other subgraphs of the
769-point host, or other geometric mechanisms. The campaign's 508-vertex
target remains open.
