# A complete orbit-repair obstruction for the spherical 372-vertex graph

Let `G` be the labelled 372-vertex, 1,710-edge graph in `source.edges`. Let `Gamma`
be the group generated by the three vertex permutations in `generators.json`.
It is a group of 60 graph automorphisms, discovered from the rotations of the
source's icosahedral construction. The permutations, not a numerical geometric
interpretation, define the group used in this theorem.

**Exact computer-assisted theorem.** If a spanning edge subgraph `H` of `G` is
`Gamma`-invariant and admits a map of its vertices to the Euclidean plane taking
every edge to length one, then `H` is four-colourable. The map need not be
injective and need not realize the abstract automorphisms as plane isometries.
Moreover, any such map requires deletion of at least **390 source edges**.

Equivalently, **none of the 536,870,912 selections of complete edge orbits can
retain a non-four-colourable graph that has a plane unit-edge realization**.
This closes a candidate-producing conversion family: its source already has
fewer than 508 vertices, but deleting whole edge orbits cannot retain a
five-chromatic obstruction and make it realizable in the plane.

The conclusion concerns the specified edge graph `H`. We do **not** classify
extra unit distances created in a new drawing, or graphs obtained by identifying
vertices and then adding edges. A colouring of `H` need not descend through an
arbitrary identification. Repairs without this group invariance and constructions
with added edges or vertices are also outside. No smaller five-chromatic plane
graph is found; no optimal repair to an actual plane drawing is claimed.

## Why seven wheels settle the whole family

The group has 29 edge orbits: one of size 30 and 28 of size 60. A plane unit-edge
graph cannot contain an odd wheel: all neighbours of its hub lie on a unit circle,
and a unit step between neighbours changes polar angle by `+pi/3` or `-pi/3`.
An odd number of these steps cannot close modulo `2*pi`. This remains true when
nonadjacent labels coincide. Thus every vertex neighbourhood is bipartite.

The source has 444 five-wheels. Under complete edge-orbit selection their
obstructions reduce to seven minimal forbidden masks:

| Forbidden orbit set (zero-based orbit labels) | Required deletion |
|---|---|
| `{7}` | Orbit 7 must be absent |
| `{0,5}` | At least one of these orbits |
| `{11,15}` | At least one of these orbits |
| `{10,21}` | At least one of these orbits |
| `{9,22}` | At least one of these orbits |
| `{8,26}` | At least one of these orbits |
| `{12,27}` | At least one of these orbits |

Orbit labels are canonical: each orbit is sorted by source edge index, and
orbits are ordered by their least edge index. The checker reconstructs them.
The seven orbit sets are disjoint. The other 16 orbits are free at this gate.
A maximal selection avoiding the wheels takes all free orbits and exactly one
orbit from each pair. There are precisely **64 maximal selections**, each with
1,320 edges. All are certified four-colourable. Every wheel-free selection is
contained in one of them and inherits a colouring. Every other selection contains
one of the seven literal forbidden wheels and admits no plane unit-edge map.

The number of selections avoiding all source odd wheels is
`2^16 * 3^6 = 47,775,744`; there are `2^29 = 536,870,912` selections in total.
The mandatory losses are the 30-edge orbit and one 60-edge orbit from each of
six pairs, hence at least `30 + 6*60 = 390` edges. Avoiding these obstructions is
necessary, and is not asserted to suffice for a plane realization.

This is also a reusable repair test: a finite list of geometrically forbidden
subgraphs becomes a monotone constraint on orbit selectors. Positive colourings
of all maximal allowed selectors exclude every non-four-colourable realizable
member, without enumerating all drawings or all Boolean selectors individually.

## Reproduce

Python 3.11 or later, standard library only:

```sh
python3 verify.py --check-expected
python3 controls.py
python3 -O controls.py
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
```

The verifier imports no producer code or solver. It checks the graph input hash,
the exact permutation group, all edge orbits, the complete local cycle census,
the seven literal wheel witnesses, maximal-selector coverage and all
**84,480** colour inequalities in the 64 words. Full expected output is in
`expected.json`; measured validation is in `validation.json`.

The certificate has seven wheel witnesses and 64 records `{mask,word}`. Masks
select whole edge orbits. Each word has exactly 372 characters from `0123`, in
source vertex order. Colouring checks include every selected source edge; isolated
vertices are retained and coloured as well. The certificate file SHA256 is
`bd952f051b4a37fde09ab6e9d15e9f9045dea5cdaa7edb13a8d153469876480d`.

Optional word regeneration uses `python-sat==1.9.dev15` and CaDiCaL 1.9.5, tested
with Python 3.12.14:

```sh
python generate.py --out out
cmp certificate.json out/certificate.json
```

The producer enumerates edge orbits by generator traversal and five-cycles by
explicit paths, then incrementally constructs maximal selectors. For each it
uses four Boolean colour indicators per vertex, at least one true per vertex,
and excludes a common indicator at both ends of every selected edge. Vertex zero
is pinned to colour zero; selecting the first true indicator decodes a colouring.
Each query has a 300,000-conflict cap. All 64 are SAT; the largest used 2,935
conflicts. The published words are checked directly, so no SAT soundness or search
completeness assumption enters the theorem.

## Independent checks and trust boundary

The verifier constructs the entire 60-element permutation group and obtains an
edge orbit from its images under **every** group element. This differs from the
producer's generator traversal. It obtains local cycles by connected components
of the neighbourhood graphs, each of maximum degree two, independently of the
producer's path enumeration. All cycles have length five. It confirms that the
13 distinct wheel masks have exactly the seven stated minimal elements.

Coverage uses the seven disjoint factors. Independently, controls exhaust all
8,192 assignments to the 13 constrained selectors and confirm that exactly 729
are admitted, each covered by a certified maximal word. The 16 unconstrained
selectors can always be extended to one. Generic maximal-support enumeration is
also compared to brute force on 278 small hypergraphs, including unsatisfiable
empty-clause cases. Further controls check a dihedral group and its edge orbits,
2,728 odd angle walks, a four-coloured but geometrically forbidden five-wheel,
and 15 malformed or corrupted inputs. Normal and optimized controls agree.

The proof trusts the explicit finite graph and permutation data, Python integer,
set, JSON and SHA256 operations, the elementary odd-wheel lemma, and direct
positive colour checks. It needs no numerical coordinates, polynomial roots,
spherical realization certificate, source UNSAT claim, large trace, or external
binary. This is author-run validation, not independent peer review.

## Source and campaign scope

The source is the graph reported in Section 4 of
[Voronov–Neopryatnaya–Dergachev, arXiv:2106.11824v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.11824v4).
The authors give a five-chromatic graph on a sphere. Its adjacency is taken from
[their interactive visualization data](https://github.com/vsvor/vsvor.github.io/blob/main/372.dg).
The source file hash, canonical edge hash and upstream commit are in
`provenance.json`. Reproduction is self-contained: the final theorem is about the
included exact edge list and does not depend on the correctness of the source's
geometric or chromatic assertions. Numerical coordinates were used only to
suggest permutations, all of which are verified exactly as graph automorphisms.

The plane comparison is the 509-vertex example of
[Parts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12665), also cited as the record by
[Haugland](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04542v4). Primary sources were checked on
2026-09-07. No priority claim is made for the orbit-repair test.

The teammate's Haugland metric-ball exclusion and Parts realization classification
are separate context, not premises. Earlier E477, mixed-sum and EI17 work is
preserved unchanged. A preliminary full 120-element-symmetry check is subsumed
by this 60-element-group theorem. One exploratory order-five repair with 1,535
edges was four-colourable; it does not close that weaker-symmetry family and is
not used here. This pass ends at the complete stated gate, without starting a
subgroup, repair-budget or vertex-addition ladder.
