# No automorphism of a Ramsey(5,5,43) graph has exactly two vertex cycles

Let `G` be a graph on 43 vertices with neither a clique nor an independent set
of size five.  No nonidentity color-preserving automorphism of `G` can have
exactly two cycles on the vertex set.

The proof combines the degree window forced by `R(4,5)=25` with complete
exact classifications of the only three cycle types not eliminated by that
window.  The three computational minima are

```text
cycle type 19+24: 1059 monochromatic K_5s
cycle type 20+23: 1009 monochromatic K_5s
cycle type 21+22:  928 monochromatic K_5s.
```

Thus all three families are very far from the zero-clique target.  This is a
structural obstruction, not a 43-vertex construction and not an improvement
to the known lower bound for `R(5,5)`.

## Degree reduction to three cases

Write the two cycle lengths of an automorphism as

```text
a <= b,  a+b=43.
```

Every vertex of `G` has degree between 18 and 24: its neighborhood is a
`(4,5)`-Ramsey graph and its nonneighborhood gives the color-complementary
bound, so `R(4,5)=25` applies in both colors.

Because `gcd(a,b)=gcd(a,43)=1`, the cyclic action generated by the
automorphism is transitive on the `a*b` edges between its two vertex cycles.
Consequently either all cross edges are red or all are blue.  For a vertex in
the `a`-cycle:

- if the cross edges are red, its degree is at least `b=43-a`; and
- if the cross edges are blue, its degree is at most `a-1`.

When `a<=18`, these bounds are respectively at least 25 and at most 17, both
impossible.  Hence only

```text
19+24, 20+23, 21+22
```

remain.

## The 22-variable exact classification

For either surviving type, the edge orbits are particularly simple:

```text
floor(a/2) internal distance orbits in the a-cycle,
floor(b/2) internal distance orbits in the b-cycle,
one cross-edge orbit.
```

Their total is always 22.  A color-invariant graph is therefore one of
`2^22 = 4,194,304` edge-orbit colorings.

For each of the `C(43,5)=962,598` five-sets, record the mask of edge-orbit
variables among its ten edges.  If `w(M)` counts five-sets with mask `M` and

```text
F(S) = sum_{M subset S} w(M),
```

then for a red orbit set `S` its monochromatic objective is exactly

```text
q(S) = F(S) + F(complement(S)).
```

`enumerate_two_cycles.cpp` constructs orbit indices directly from the two
cyclic distances, forms every five-set mask, applies the 22-variable subset
zeta transform, and evaluates all `2^22` colorings.  It obtains:

| cycle type | distinct masks | minimum `q` | minimizing colorings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `19+24` | 1,779 | 1,059 | 18 |
| `20+23` | 1,687 | 1,009 | 22 |
| `21+22` | 1,666 | 928 | 60 |

In every minimizer all monochromatic five-sets have the same color.  The red
edge-orbit counts and red/blue clique-count splits are preserved in
`EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt`.

## Independent direct verification

`verify_direct.cpp` uses neither the distance formulas nor a zeta transform.
For every unordered edge it explicitly follows the simultaneous vertex
permutation through its full orbit, selects the least edge representative,
and rebuilds the 22 orbit variables.  It then stores only the distinct
weighted five-set masks.

The verifier scans all `2^21` color-complement-normalized assignments for
each cycle type directly against that weighted mask list.  Once a partial
positive sum exceeds the best complete objective already found, it stops
that assignment; nonnegativity makes this pruning exact.  Color complements
are restored in every reported count.  The verifier reproduces the production
output byte-for-byte, including all three minima and minimizing-coloring
counts.  This is a different orbit construction, objective algorithm, and
data representation from the production enumerator.

## Reproduction

Requirements: GCC 12.2 or another conforming C++20 compiler.  There are no
third-party dependencies, random choices, floating-point operations, solver
calls, or external inputs.

```bash
g++ -O3 -std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic \
  enumerate_two_cycles.cpp -o enumerate_two_cycles
g++ -O3 -std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic \
  verify_direct.cpp -o verify_direct

./enumerate_two_cycles | cmp - EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt
./verify_direct | cmp - EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt
```

On the research host the production enumerator took 1.62 seconds and the
independent direct verifier took 6.07 seconds.  Both complete programs also
reproduced `EXPECTED_OUTPUT.txt` under AddressSanitizer and
UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer.  Fixed-width counters are safe: a zeta entry is
at most 962,598, the number of five-sets; aggregate counts use 64 bits.

## Scope and literature

The proof excludes exactly the two-cycle automorphism types.  Automorphisms
with three or more cycles need different orbit structures; the separate
circulant classification handles the one-cycle type `43`.  The result is an
exact pruning theorem for structured construction and isomorph-free search.

The sole imported input to the degree reduction is McKay and Radziszowski's
[*R(4,5)=25*](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.3190190304), also available from the
[authors' site](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/papers/r45.pdf).  Exoo's
[*A lower bound for R(5,5)*](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.3190130113) and Ge,
Jayasooriya, Qiu, Sun, and Yuan's
[*Study of Exoo's lower bound for Ramsey number R(5,5)*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12630)
provide the structured-construction context.  Neither the inspected primary
sources nor the refreshed Discovery Net graph stated this complete two-cycle
classification.  Novelty is asserted only relative to those sources and the
graph, not as a universal priority claim.
