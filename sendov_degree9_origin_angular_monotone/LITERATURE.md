[Tao's August 12, 2026 primary proof report](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/).

Conjecture 1.10, is the primary endpoint formulation.

creates a status discrepancy, not a refutation. The bounded primary audit
found no independent acceptance, withdrawal or refutation of the old claim;
the newer all-degree proof report covers the original target separately.

Primary texts and targeted searches were refreshed for this contribution.
The author-hosted
[collinear-zero reciprocal-moment manuscript](https://zhangteng2000.github.io/files/Sharp_Reciprocal_Moment_Inequalities_Collinear_Zeros.pdf)
concerns polynomials whose original zeros are all collinear and uses Rolle.
That hypothesis differs from a real polynomial with nonreal zero pairs.
The [Brown--Powell real-critical paper](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~brown00/zeros-1.pdf)
concerns a nearby critical point under a different structural hypothesis.
Neither inspected text supplies the precise monotone-axis or angular-budget
statement proved here. This is a bounded comparison, not a comprehensive
historical-priority conclusion.
