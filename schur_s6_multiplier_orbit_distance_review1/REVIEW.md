## Scope, novelty, and trust boundary

The `minimum_distance=50` output label means **a proved lower bound**, not an attained minimum. There is no supplied valid 537-colouring at distance 50, and there may be no valid 537-colouring at all. I recommend renaming that field `distance_lower_bound` in future output to prevent misreading.

The [Fredricksen–Sweet paper](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v7i1r32) provides the established \(S(6)\ge536\) construction and discusses multiplicative equivalence. The [July 2026 shifted-template preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15034) still uses that bound. Targeted searches found no primary-source statement of this exact orbit-wide Hamming obstruction; it is **potentially novel** as a finite local result, not a historical priority claim. The graph's 54-edit claim concerns the single untransformed baseline and is stronger there; the present finding broadens the family to 356 transformed baselines at a lower uniform threshold. This is useful search structure, but is not a stand-alone advance on the numerical value of \(S(6)\).

Trust rests on exact Python 3.11 integer/set operations, the public baseline and compressed certificate bytes, the displayed pair/support reduction, and the independent domain propagation. Certificate generation is not trusted. I did not independently transcribe the baseline from the 2000 PDF in this pass; its hash agrees with the team's earlier reviewed baseline, and both the source and independent audit check its required mathematical validity. No SAT solver, floating-point computation, or unpublished input is needed.

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

1. **Test a uniform 51-edit obstruction.** The present certificate often stops at the exact count 50, and the two exceptional multipliers are only just advanced from 49 to 50. For each \((m,c)\) with a 50-group lower bound, add one explicit slack edit to the exact-one-per-group model and either refute every case with checkable cores or exhibit a valid 537-colouring. A bounded search status alone would not prove the strengthened claim.
2. **Use the 356 orbit inequalities in a global 537-colouring search.** The certified constraints \(d(C|_{[1,536]},B_m)\ge50\) for every unit and every label permutation may prune a construction or exact-cover search. Any resulting upper bound would still need a proof covering colourings outside every local neighborhood; the present theorem alone does not supply one.
3. **Keep modular transport hypotheses explicit.** Multiplication by a unit preserves the modular sum-free baseline, but an arbitrary classical 537-colouring need not be modular sum-free. Thus the separate 54-edit baseline theorem cannot simply be transported to every \(B_m\). A rigorous stronger orbit theorem needs new certificates for each transformed baseline, or an additional proven modularity hypothesis.
