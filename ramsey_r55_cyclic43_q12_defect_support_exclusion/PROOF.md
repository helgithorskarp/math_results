# Proof and finite certificate

## The selected graph and family

Number the 903 edges of `K43` lexicographically.  The Cyclic(43) seed colors
edge `{u,v}` red when its cyclic length belongs to

```text
{1,2,7,10,12,13,14,16,18,20,21}.
```

A persisted representative lists the edge indices toggled from this seed.
The input file is pinned by SHA256.  Both census programs directly reconstruct
all 238 graphs and verify that each has exactly twelve monochromatic `K5`s.
The primary program enumerates all 962,598 five-subsets.  The independent
program instead recursively enumerates red and blue cliques from adjacency
bitsets.  They agree entry by entry on red/blue defect counts and on the union
of defect edges.

Choose the representative minimizing the number of edges in that union,
breaking ties by persisted source index.  The exact histogram is recorded in
`SUPPORT_CENSUS.tsv`; the minimum is 60, attained thirteen times, and the
selected index is 51.  Its toggle list is

```text
200,273,308,497,525,672,693,798,812,837,893,897,900,902.
```

It has twelve red defects and no blue defect.  Let `S` be the union of their
edges, so `|S|=60`.  The family consists of all colorings agreeing with source
51 on the 843 edges outside `S`, with the edges of `S` arbitrary.  Hence it has
exactly `2^60 = 1,152,921,504,606,846,976` assignments.

## Complete formula reduction

Give the edges of `S` variables `x1,...,x60`, where true means red.  For each
physical five-set, substitute every fixed edge into the two standard Ramsey
clauses:

- the disjunction of negated edge variables forbids an all-red `K5`;
- the disjunction of positive edge variables forbids an all-blue `K5`.

A clause is satisfied and removed if a fixed edge already has the opposite
color.  Deduplication leaves 600 nonempty clauses on 60 variables, with exact
width histogram stored in `family.json`.  `family.cnf` is therefore sound and
complete for good43 graphs inside this family; no degree or symmetry
condition is added.

## The 61-clause physical core

For every free edge `e` in `S`, `certificate.json` gives a physical five-set
`W_e` with these properties:

1. `e` is one of its ten pairs;
2. its other nine pairs lie outside `S`; and
3. those nine fixed pairs are blue in source 51.

If `e` were blue, `W_e` would be a blue `K5`.  Thus every good coloring in the
family would have to color all 60 free edges red.  These are exactly the 60
positive unit clauses of `core.cnf`.

The certificate also gives the red five-set `{1,2,3,23,24}`.  All ten of its
pairs belong to `S`; once the units are propagated they are all red.  Its
all-red prohibition is the last clause of `core.cnf`, and all ten literals
are false.  Unit propagation therefore refutes the family.

The Python checker reconstructs the selected graph from the pinned input,
recounts all five-sets, rebuilds `S` and the complete 600-clause formula, and
checks each of the 60 physical blue witnesses and the red conflict witness
literally.  It also parses the core and performs the displayed unit
propagation.  This is a direct finite proof independent of CaDiCaL or any
other solver.

## Reproducibility and trust boundary

The primary and independent C++20 censuses are compiled with strict warnings
as errors.  Release builds use `-O3 -DNDEBUG`; checking builds use address and
undefined-behavior sanitizers on the full 238-source workload.  Python runs
under both normal and assertion-disabled modes.  The replay compares every
generated byte to the compact published artifacts and rejects deliberate
changes to one census row and one physical witness.

Trusted components are the inherited published 238-state input, SHA256,
C++/Python integer and bit semantics, compiler/runtime correctness, and the
elementary Ramsey-clause interpretation above.  The completeness of the
238-state list is needed only for the minimum-support selection statement,
not for the exclusion of the explicitly identified source-51 family.

No good43 graph is produced.  The theorem does not exclude arbitrary repairs
that also change an edge outside `S`, and it does not exclude the analogous
families of the other representatives.
