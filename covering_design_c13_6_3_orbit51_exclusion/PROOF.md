# Proof and certificate boundary

## 1. Normal form

Assume a 20-block `(13,6,3)` cover has profile `(12,9^12)`, and let `h` be
the degree-12 point. The link of every low point is a nine-block `(12,5,2)`
cover. The imported exact link classification says that its point degrees
are one of

```text
(4^9,3^3), (5,4^7,3^4), (5^2,4^5,3^5), (5^3,4^3,3^6).
```

In particular every link degree is at most five. The 12 blocks through `h`
have 60 low-point incidences. Every low point occurs at most five times in
them, so all 12 occur exactly five times. Each low point consequently occurs
four times in the eight blocks away from `h`.

Suppose three blocks through `h` share a low triple and have disjoint residual
pairs. Relabel them as

```text
h01234, h01256, h01278.
```

Write `Q={0,1,2}`, `S={3,...,8}`, and `R={9,10,11}`. Removing `h` and the
three fixed rows leaves nine 5-rows `Y`; the away family gives eight 6-rows
`Z`. Their column sums are

```text
Y: (2^3,4^6,5^3),    Z: (4^12).
```

For each low pair, its total codegree is at least three because one block
containing the pair covers only four of its eleven incident triples. It is at
most five by the link theorem. Since `h` already has link degree five, the
same link classification also shows that each low point has at most two low
neighbors of codegree five.

These facts, through-pair coverage, and low-triple coverage are precisely the
constraints generated by `generate_dual_cnf.py`.

## 2. Seven `Q`-free row types

The three `Q` columns have total sum six across nine rows. Hence at least one
row avoids `Q`. A `Q`-free 5-row chooses `c` points of `R` and five minus `c`
points from three matched pairs. If `d` matching edges are selected twice
and `s` are selected once, then `2d+s+c=5`. This gives seven types:

```text
c0:21, c1:12, c1:20, c2:03, c2:11, c3:02, c3:10.
```

The wreath-product symmetry of the matching and `S3(R)` is transitive on each
type. The audit enumerates all `C(9,5)=126` rows and obtains orbit sizes
`6,36,9,24,36,12,3`. Designate one such row and sort all remaining rows with
the same fixed incidence data. This is sound symmetry breaking because block
rows are unordered.

## 3. Dual Venn invariant

For a pair in `Q`, the three fixed rows cover the third points `h`, the other
point of `Q`, and all six points of `S`, but none of `R`. At least one further
block is therefore required. Total codegree is four or five. Subtracting
the fixed contribution gives an edge-label triangle with labels one or two;
up to `S3(Q)` it is `111`, `211`, `221`, or `222`.

Let `T_i` be the two rows of `Y` containing `i in Q`. Three labelled
2-subsets are classified up to row permutation by

```text
(a,b,c,t) = (|T0 cap T1|, |T0 cap T2|, |T1 cap T2|,
             |T0 cap T1 cap T2|).
```

The Venn cell counts are

```text
t, a-t, b-t, c-t,
2-a-b+t, 2-a-c+t, 2-b-c+t,
8 - |T0 union T1 union T2|.
```

Thus `(a,b,c,t)` determines the row-permutation orbit. If the residual
codegree labels are `(e01,e02,e12)`, the away columns `U_i` have size four and
forced pair intersections `eij-|Ti cap Tj|`. Replacing `2` by `4` in the
seven nonempty-cell formulas and requiring all eight cells to be nonnegative
leaves four joint orbits for `221` and seven for `222`. The generator derives
these types rather than accepting a hand-written list; the audit proves that
the manifest contains every resulting case.

## 4. CNF equivalence

Primary variables are the 108 entries of `Y` and 96 entries of `Z`.

- one-hot saturated-count automata enforce every row sum, column sum,
  codegree interval, codegree-five flag, and exact normalized intersection;
- Tseitin variables are equivalent to the relevant pair or triple
  conjunctions in a row;
- an `OR` of through-pair conjunctions covers every triple containing `h` not
  already covered by a fixed row;
- an `OR` of through/away triple conjunctions covers every low triple not
  already covered by a fixed row;
- strict lexicographic row ordering only removes permutations among rows with
  identical fixed `Q`-membership data;
- remaining rows are forbidden to duplicate each other or a fixed row.

The last condition loses no 20-block cover: a duplicate could be deleted,
giving a 19-block cover, contrary to the imported lower bound 20.

The primitive audit exhausts truth tables for conjunctions, saturated counts,
and lex comparators. The written clauses are equivalences or exact automaton
transitions, so projecting a satisfying assignment to its 204 primary
variables gives exactly a normalized cover extension, and every such
extension supplies the intended auxiliary states.

## 5. Certificate result

There are 14 `111/211` instances and 77 feasible joint-Venn `221/222`
instances. CaDiCaL 3.0.1 returns UNSAT on all 91. `drat-trim` independently
accepts every binary DRAT trace. `EXPECTED.json` pins each generated CNF and
proof separately; the aggregate generated sizes are 113,188,593 and
569,850,222 bytes.

The 91-way split is complete by Sections 2 and 3, so no normalized extension
exists. Relabeling proves the stated exclusion of the support-six `3K2`
heavy-triple type.

## 6. Trust and scope

The theorem trusts the normal-form argument, the imported optimal-link degree
classification and lower bound `C(13,6,3)>=20`, the Python generator/runtime,
CaDiCaL, `drat-trim`, their builds and hardware, and inspection of the compact
source. DRAT checking removes trust in CaDiCaL's UNSAT reasoning but not in
the mathematical encoding or checker implementation.

This theorem excludes one orbit inside one degree profile. It neither
excludes orbit 52 nor the full `(12,9^12)` profile and therefore does not
change the recorded global interval `20 <= C(13,6,3) <= 21`.
