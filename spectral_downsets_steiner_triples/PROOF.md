# The claim and its matrix

Write STS(v) for a family T of triples of a v-point set in which every pair lies
in exactly one triple. Let D be the downset generated by T. If v>=3, Spectral
Chvátal Conjecture H holds for D, with a rational certificate.

More generally, suppose v>=9 and T_1,...,T_m are pairwise block-disjoint STS(v)
on the same point set, with m>=1. Their union generates

\[
D=\{\emptyset\}\cup\binom{[v]}1\cup\binom{[v]}2
  \cup T_1\cup\cdots\cup T_m.
\]

Every point has the same star size. With b=v(v-1)/6 and r=(v-1)/2, put

\[
N=1+v+\binom v2+mb,\qquad s=v+mr.
\]

We construct Q with Q1=N1, Q>=0, and
Q(A,B)=s*1_{A=B} whenever A intersects B. Then

\[
M=\frac{Q-sI}{N-s}
\]

is a real symmetric rational matrix with M1=1, M(A,B)=0 on intersecting pairs,
and (N-s)M+sI=Q>=0. Its weighted Hoffman bound is at most s. An s-element star
is intersecting, so this bound is tight. The empty set is retained, with its
loop; its diagonal entry is unrestricted by the support rule.

Partition D without the empty set into the singleton layer, the pair layer,
and the m separate triple systems. Entries between different layers are zero.
For nonempty sets in one layer, define:

| Layer | Q(A,A) | Q(A,B), for distinct disjoint A,B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Singletons | s | s |
| Pairs | s | s/(v-3) |
| Triples in the same T_j | s | 2s/(v-7) |

All other entries within a layer are zero. For a nonempty k-set A set

\[
Q(\emptyset,A)=Q(A,\emptyset)=q_k=N-\frac{sv}{k}.
\]

Finally set

\[
Q(\emptyset,\emptyset)
=\frac1s\left(q_1^2+(v-1)q_2^2+mrq_3^2\right).
\]

Negative entries at the empty vertex are allowed: Conjecture H requires real
weights, not entrywise nonnegative weights.

# Incidence proof for v>=9

The local ingredients below are standard spectral identities, derived here to
make the construction self-contained. The spectral bound for Steiner block
graphs is already known; see the discussion of the Delsarte bound in
[Adriaensen et al., Section 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26607#S1).

For each layer X_l of size n_l, construct a PSD matrix C_l whose diagonal is a
constant t_l and whose row sum is n_l, with zero entries between distinct
intersecting sets. We use t_1=1, t_2=v-1, and t_3=r separately for each T_j.
Their sum is s.

For the singleton layer C_1=J_v, which has diagonal 1 and row sum v.

For pairs, let P be the pair-by-point incidence matrix and let A_2 be the
disjointness adjacency matrix. Direct counting gives

\[
P^TP=(v-2)I+J_v,\qquad A_2=J+I-PP^T.
\]

Define

\[
C_2=(v-1)I+\frac{v-1}{v-3}A_2
=\frac{v-1}{v-3}\big((v-2)I-PP^T+J\big).
\]

It has diagonal v-1 and row sum n_2=binom(v,2). The block space is the orthogonal
sum of span(1), P(1^perp), and ker(P^T). On these spaces C_2 has eigenvalues
n_2, 0, and (v-1)(v-2)/(v-3), respectively. Thus it is PSD with rank n_2-v+1.
This decomposition follows from P^TP above and P1=2*1; it makes no assumption
about a selected Steiner system.

For one triple system, let B be its block-by-point incidence matrix and A_3
its disjointness adjacency matrix. Distinct blocks intersect in zero or one
point, hence

\[
B^TB=(r-1)I+J_v,\qquad A_3=J+2I-BB^T.
\]

Every block meets exactly 3(r-1) other blocks, so the disjointness degree is
b-3r+2=(v-3)(v-7)/6. Define

\[
C_3=rI+\frac{v-1}{v-7}A_3
=\frac{v-1}{v-7}\left(\frac{v-3}{2}I-BB^T+J\right).
\]

Its diagonal is r and its row sum is b. Because v>=9, the prefactor is
positive and B^TB is positive definite. The orthogonal decomposition into
span(1), B(1^perp), and ker(B^T) gives eigenvalues b, 0, and
(v-1)(v-3)/(2(v-7)). Consequently C_3 is PSD of rank b-v+1. This applies to
every STS(v), without a transitivity, resolvability, or isomorphism assumption.

Here is the elementary lift that assembles these layers. Put

\[
K=\bigoplus_l\frac{s}{t_l}C_l,\quad
R_l=\frac{sn_l}{t_l},\quad q_l=N-R_l,\quad
e|_{X_l}=\frac{q_l}{R_l}\mathbf1.
\]

Then K>=0 and Ke is the vector that equals q_l on each layer. The full matrix is

\[
Q=\begin{pmatrix}e^TKe&e^TK\\Ke&K\end{pmatrix}
  =\begin{pmatrix}e^T\\I\end{pmatrix}K\begin{pmatrix}e&I\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.
\]

Its nonempty rows sum to R_l+q_l=N. Since sum(t_l)=s and sum(n_l)=N-1,

\[
e^TKe=\sum_l\frac{t_l}{s}(N-R_l)^2
=N^2-2N(N-1)+\sum_l n_lR_l,
\]

which, after adding sum_l n_l(N-R_l), gives N for the empty row as well.
For our layers n_l/t_l=v/k, so this congruence gives exactly the stated formula.
The identity block in the congruence shows that rank(Q)=rank(K), namely

\[
\operatorname{rank}Q=\binom v2-v+2+m(b-v+1).
\]

This proves the entire infinite v>=9 family analytically. There is no numerical
or exhaustive-computation premise in that part of the proof. Infinitely many
examples already come from affine lines in F_3^d for d>=2: each pair determines
one three-point line.

# The Fano exception and small orders

The layered triple formula has a zero denominator at v=7 and is not used there.
Label the seven points by the nonzero vectors of F_2^3, encoded as integers
1,...,7. Its seven blocks are {a,b,a xor b}. The generated downset has N=36 and
s=10.

The following table specifies every entry of its Q. Symmetry is implicit.
For distinct intersecting nonempty sets the entry is zero; all nonempty diagonal
entries are 10. For disjoint nonempty pairs use the table.

| Entry or disjoint pair type | Value |
|---|---:|
| Empty, empty | 22 |
| Empty, singleton | -5 |
| Empty, pair | 2 |
| Empty, block | 1 |
| Singleton, singleton | 2 |
| Singleton, pair whose union is a block | 1 |
| Singleton, pair whose union is not a block | 1/2 |
| Singleton, block | 5/2 |
| Pair, pair whose union has block complement | 4 |
| Pair, pair whose union has nonblock complement | 1 |
| Pair, block | 5/2 |

There are no disjoint pairs of blocks. The exact verifier checks every entry's
support, every row sum 36, and every maximum-star equality Q*1_star=10*1.
It then proves PSD by rational symmetric Schur elimination, giving 29 positive
pivots and an identically zero remaining 7-by-7 matrix. Each positive pivot d
replaces A by A_rest-A_rest,p*A_p,rest/d, a congruence to diag(d, new_A).
An all-zero residual is PSD. The verifier rejects a residual with zero diagonal
and nonzero off-diagonal entries. Thus this is a finite exact certificate,
not floating eigenvalue evidence.

To cover **all** STS(7), the verifier enumerates exact covers of the 21 pairs
by the 35 possible triples. At every node it branches over every triple that
contains the first uncovered pair and uses no covered pair. A completion has
exactly one such triple, so inductively it appears in one branch. Every terminal
cover is an STS(7). Independently, all 7! point permutations of the Fano blocks
are generated. The two sets agree entry by entry and contain 30 labelled
systems. Every enumerated system therefore receives the displayed matrix by
permutation congruence. The checker also checks every relabelled matrix's
definition-level conditions. No downset census or unverified data is imported.

For v=3 the unique system consists of the full three-point block, whose downset
is the Boolean cube. The complement-permutation matrix M=P_complement has row
sum 1, disjoint support, and Q=4(I+P_complement)>=0. At orders 4,5,6,8 a Steiner
triple system cannot exist: counting pairs through a point requires v odd, and
the total block count v(v-1)/6 must be integral. Thus the analytic family and
these two finite cases cover every STS(v) with v>=3.

# Scope, provenance, and further frontier

The source establishes the specified class, including the seven-point finite
exception. It makes no assertion for an arbitrary rank-three downset or for a
triple family lacking the stated Steiner decomposition. It does not settle
Conjecture H globally or Conjecture I.

The original target is [Ellis–Filmus–Friedgut, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v1#S4).
Its friendly-vertex relation to Lovasz theta and its numerical experiments are
prior context, not new claims here. The known uniform and design spectra are
being used to construct explicit matrices on the full downsets.
An exact affine GL(3,2) reduction and a numerical rational-grid search discovered
the Fano weights; neither search completeness nor numerical positivity is needed
by the proof. The retained certificate is checked over Q from the definitions.

The main analytic argument is unformalized written mathematics. The Fano
positivity and seven-point classification rely on the small exact Python
verifier, its reduction/branch arguments above, and ordinary interpreter
correctness. All published fixtures are generated from compact definitions.

Next frontier: remove or replace the Steiner-decomposition requirement for
regular triple families, especially small balanced 2-(v,3,lambda) designs that
cannot be split into STS(v). The current proof does not imply that relaxation.
