## 1. Quantified results

independent vertices. It contains the empty set, all r+t singletons,
the ell=binom(r,2) center edges, and the rt center-leaf spokes. For
integers r>=2,t>=2, put

```
N=1+r+t+ell+rt,       s=r+t,       rho=s/(N-s)<1.
```

The r center stars have size s; every leaf star has size r+1<s.

**Theorem.** There is an explicit rational symmetric matrix M on D_(r,t),
with row sums one, zero entries on intersecting pairs, and

```
-rho I <= M <= I,       M[A,B]>=0 whenever A!=B,
rank L=N-r,            rank(NI-L)=N-1,
L=(N-s)M+sI.
```

The lower eigenvalue -rho has multiplicity r and the upper eigenvalue
one is simple. The rank N-r is maximal among **all real H certificates**,
including ones without a cap or a sign condition. The only maximum
intersecting families are the r center stars. The allowed empty loop is
retained and need not be nonnegative.

## 2. The centered core for all r>=3,t>=2

Use types A_i for center singletons, E_ab for center edges, c_j for leaf
singletons and X_ij for spokes. Set d=r+t-1. Every core diagonal is d,
and every distinct intersecting entry is -1. The remaining entries are:

| Disjoint pair | Entry |
| --- | ---: |
| A_i,A_k | alpha |
| A_i,c_j | p |
| c_j,c_k | beta |
| A_i,E_ab with i outside {a,b} | q |
| c_j,E_ab | u |
| A_i,X_kj with i!=k | v |
| disjoint center edges | eta |
| E_ab,X_ij with i outside {a,b} | w |
| c_j,X_ik with j!=k | h |
| X_ij,X_kl with i!=k,j!=l | z |

These exhaust every distinct disjoint nonempty pair.

For t>=r-1 choose

```
alpha=q=eta=0,       p=-(r-1)/t,
beta=-1+r(r-3)(t-r+1)/(2t(t-1)),
u=(t-r+3)/t,         v=1/t,       w=2/t,
h=(r-2)(r-1-t)/(t(t-1)),
z=(2t-2r+3)/(t(t-1)).                                (1)
```

For 2<=t<r-1 (so r>=4), put

```
delta=t(t-1)/(r-1+t(t-1)),
q=eta=2(r-1-t)/((r-1)(r-2)),
alpha=-1+delta,      p=beta=-1,
u=w=2/(r-1),        v=2/(r-1)-delta/t,
h=0,               z=delta/(t(t-1)).                 (2)
```

All denominators are nonzero. Every off-diagonal entry is at least -1.
In (1), beta>=-1, -1<=h<=0, p>=-1 and the other displayed entries are
nonnegative. For h, use r-2<=t-1 and t-r+1<=t. In (2), 0<delta<1,
alpha>-1, v>-1/t>=-1/2 and q,eta,u,w,z are positive.

Here are direct identities that establish both star kernels and
centering, without a spectral assumption. Entries outside a given center
star sum to zero exactly when

```
alpha-1+(r-2)q+t*v=0,
q-2+(r-3)eta+t*w=0,
p-1+(r-1)u+(t-1)h=0,
v-2+(r-2)w+(t-1)z=0.                                (3)
```

The within-star row sum is d-(s-1)=0. Substitution of either (1) or (2)
proves (3) for every parameter in its stated regime. Four further identities
are

```
t*p=-(r-1)+binom(r-1,2)q,
t*u=d-2(r-2)+binom(r-2,2)eta,
d+(t-1)beta=ell*u,
-1+(t-1)h=-(r-1)+binom(r-1,2)w.                      (4)
```

Equation (4), on the four row types, says that the core kills the vector
sum_j e_(c_j)-sum_(ab) e_(E_ab). Summing all r star kernels says it kills
sum_i e_(A_i)+2sum_(ab)e_(E_ab)+sum_(ij)e_(X_ij). Their sum is the
nonempty all-one vector. Therefore C1=0. Equations (3)--(4) also explain
the Gram factorizations used next.

## 3. Complete incidence decomposition and PSD

Let B be the r by ell zero-one center-edge incidence matrix. Direct
counting gives

```
BB^T=(r-2)I_r+J_r.
```

For r>=3 it has rank r. Its edge coordinate space is the orthogonal sum
of constants, the (r-1)-dimensional image B^T(1_r perpendicular), and
ker B of dimension ell-r=r(r-3)/2. Split the center and leaf natural
spaces into constants and their perpendicular spaces. The spoke space
is their tensor product. The following five invariant summands are
orthogonal and exhaust all m=N-1 nonempty coordinates.

1. **Center-edge incidence kernel**, dimension ell-r. The core acts by
   d+2+eta>0. Indeed the center-edge block is
   (d+2+eta)I-(1+eta)B^TB+eta J; all its couplings to other levels vanish
   on ker B. This summand is absent at r=3.

2. **Centered centers and centered leaves in the spoke level**, dimension
   (r-1)(t-1). The scalar is d+2+z>0. The spoke block is
   (d+2+z)I-(1+z)(I_r tensor J_t+J_r tensor I_t)+z(J_r tensor J_t).

3. **Centered leaves**, with their singleton and center-constant spoke
   levels. There are t-1 orthogonal copies of

   ```
   H=[[d-beta,-sqrt(r)(1+h)],
      [-sqrt(r)(1+h),t+1-(r-1)z]].                    (5)
   ```

   In (1), write gamma=beta+1>=0. Since
   gamma<=r(r-3)/(2t)<=r(r-3)/(2(r-1))<=(r-2)/2,
   the first diagonal is at least t+(r+2)/2. Also 0<=z<=2/t and
   t>=r-1 imply the second is at least t-1>0. The squared cross entry
   is at most r. Hence

   ```
   det H >=(t+(r+2)/2)(t-1)-r
          >=r(3r-8)/2>0.
   ```

   In (2), the first diagonal is r+t and
   (r-1)z=(r-1)/(r-1+t(t-1))<1, so the second is greater than t.
   The squared cross entry is r, and det H>r(t-1)+t^2>0.
   Thus (5) is positive definite in both regimes.

4. **Centered centers and constant leaves**, using their center-singleton,
   incidence-image edge and spoke levels. There are r-1 copies of

   ```
   T=[[d-alpha, -sqrt(r-2)(1+q), -sqrt(t)(1+v)],
      [-sqrt(r-2)(1+q), t+3-(r-3)eta, -sqrt((r-2)t)(1+w)],
      [-sqrt(t)(1+v), -sqrt((r-2)t)(1+w), r+1-(t-1)z]].
   ```

   The standard center-star kernel has coefficients (1,sqrt(r-2),sqrt(t)).
   Therefore T=R K R^T, with

   ```
   K=[[d-alpha,-sqrt(r-2)(1+q)],
      [-sqrt(r-2)(1+q),t+3-(r-3)eta]],
   R=[[1,0],[0,1],[-1/sqrt(t),-sqrt((r-2)/t)]].        (6)
   ```

   This equality follows from (3), or multiplication. In (1), det K is
   (r+t-1)(t+3)-(r-2)>0. In (2), the first diagonal exceeds r+t-1>=r+1
   and the second exceeds t+1>=3. The squared cross entry is at most
   (r-2)(1+2/(r-2))^2=r+2+4/(r-2)<=r+4. Thus det K>2r-1>0.
   Both diagonals are positive; (6) has rank two in each regime.

5. **All level constants**, dimension four, normalized by the sizes
   r,ell,t,rt. The block is R_0 K_0 R_0^T, where

   ```
   K_0=[[d+(r-1)alpha,t*p*sqrt(2/(r-1))],
        [t*p*sqrt(2/(r-1)),t*u]],
   R_0=[[1,0],[0,1],[0,sqrt(ell/t)],
        [-1/sqrt(t),-2sqrt(ell/(rt))]].               (7)
   ```

   The two Gram relations in (3)--(4) give this factorization. In (1),
   K_0 has diagonal r+t-1,t-r+3 and squared cross entry 2(r-1).
   Writing a=t-r+1>=0 gives
   det K_0=a^2+2ra+2(r-1)>0. In (2), its diagonal is
   t+(r-1)delta,2t/(r-1), with squared cross entry 2t^2/(r-1).
   Consequently det K_0=2t*delta>0. Thus (7) has rank two.

For the stated normalizations, a unit center coefficient vector x with
sum x_i=0 gives an edge vector B^Tx/sqrt(r-2), whose norm is one.
This specifies the edge-image basis used in (6); (7) uses normalized sums
on each level. The dimensions are

```
(ell-r)+(r-1)(t-1)+2(t-1)+3(r-1)+4
   =r+ell+t+rt=m.
```

All summands are PSD; their nullities are zero, zero, zero, r-1 and two.
Thus rank C=m-r-1=N-r-2 and

```
ker C=span{1_m, indicators of the r center stars}.     (8)
```

These r+1 vectors are independent: a leaf singleton first forces the
coefficient of 1_m to be zero, then each center singleton forces its own
star coefficient to be zero. The lift

```
R_empty=[-1_m^T;I_m],  L=J_N+R_empty C R_empty^T,
M=(L-sI)/(N-s)
```

is the previously published H construction in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).
It has rank rank C+1=N-r-1. Since C1=0 and C_off>=-1,

```
U=NI_m-J_m-C
 =I_m+sum_(A<B)(1+C[A,B])(e_A-e_B)(e_A-e_B)^T >= I_m. (9)
```

The cap identity is NI_N-L=R_empty U R_empty^T. Thus the centered matrix
already has the cap, with a simple upper endpoint. Its nonempty disjoint
weights are (1+C[A,B])/(N-s)>=0 and its empty off-diagonal weights are
1/(N-s)>0. The maximum centered rank is N-r-1, because every centered
H core has all r star kernels and the additional 1_m kernel.

## 4. Explicit maximal-rank repair for r>=3

Label the centers 0,...,r-1 and leaves r,...,r+t-1 in Z/sZ. Give singleton
i color 2i mod s and pair {i,j} color i+j mod s. Distinct incident pairs
have distinct colors, and no pair at i has color 2i. This is a proper
partition into s disjoint classes; every center star uses all s colors.

Let q_c be the class sizes. If all sizes coincide, recolor the singleton
of leaf r to the first color unused at that leaf. Its star has r+1<s
members, so a color is available. The old color is used there and differs
from the new color; recoloring changes two formerly equal class sizes by
one each. Otherwise leave the partition alone. In every case it is proper
and its size vector is not constant. No search or edge-coloring oracle is
needed. The code implements precisely this bounded deterministic rule.

Define C_P[A,B]=s*1_(same color)-1. This is PSD, being s times the Gram
matrix of the s standard simplex vectors. It kills each center-star
indicator, since that star uses every color once. With m=N-1,

```
1^T C_P 1=s*sum_c q_c^2-m^2>0.                       (10)
```

Strictness follows from the nonconstant size vector and Cauchy--Schwarz.
Let b=max(0,s*max_c q_c-N), epsilon=1/[2(b+1)], and set

```
C_hat=(1-epsilon)C+epsilon C_P.                      (11)
```

For 0<epsilon<1 the kernel of a convex combination of PSD matrices is
the intersection of their kernels. Equation (10) says C_P does not kill
1_m, while it kills all r star indicators. Equations (8)--(11) therefore
give ker C_hat precisely the star span, hence rank L_hat=N-r.

The partition upper slack is

```
U_P=NI_m-s*[same color] >= -b I_m.
```

Each same-color block has a sole nonzero eigenvalue q_c. Together with
(9) this proves

```
U_hat >= [1-epsilon(b+1)]I_m=I_m/2.                  (12)
```

The nonempty off-diagonal core entries in (11) remain at least -1. A
partition empty-to-class-c entry of L is N-s*q_c, bounded below by -b.
The centered input has that entry one, so the repaired entry is at least
1-epsilon(b+1)=1/2. Thus **every off-diagonal entry** of M_hat is
nonnegative. The lifted cap is strict off constants. In fact (12) gives
L_hat<= (N-1/2)I on 1_N perpendicular: R_empty R_empty^T has nonzero
eigenvalues one and N and is at least the projection onto that space.

## 5. Nonnegative maximal-rank two-center repair

Here D_(2,t)=B_t, N=3t+4,s=t+2,t>=2. Use the centered C_0 from
[TWO_CENTERS.md](TWO_CENTERS.md), with kernel spanned by 1 and its two
center stars. That source proves PSD. Its invariant blocks also give
U_0>=I: the nonzero core eigenvalues are bounded by
t+3+2/t, 2t+3-1/t and 2t+5-1/t. Their respective gaps below N are
2t+1-2/t, t+1+1/t and t-1+1/t, each at least one for t>=2. The constant
core direction has U_0 eigenvalue one.

Let the centers be a,b, the leaves c_i, and e={a,b}. Make one class
containing e and all t leaf singletons, another containing a,b, and t
classes containing x_i={a,c_i} and y_(i-1)={b,c_(i-1)}, indices modulo t.
All these are disjoint classes; the last pairs are disjoint because t>=2.
Their sizes are one t+1 and t+1 twos. The partition core has

```
1^T C_P 1=(t+1)(t-1)^2>0,
U_P>=-(t^2-2)I.                                     (13)
```

Average this partition core over every permutation of the leaf labels.
Call the result C_barP. PSD, star kernels, (13) and the upper-slack bound
are preserved by this finite convex average. No permutation corpus is
needed to generate the average: diagonal entries are t+1, intersecting
distinct entries are -1, and disjoint entries are

```
t+1:  a,b; distinct leaf singletons; leaf singleton versus e,
3/(t-1):  x_i,y_j with i!=j,
-1:  every other disjoint nonempty pair.             (14)
```

For the second line, same-color probability is 1/(t-1), since the cyclic
matching under a uniformly relabeled permutation sends a fixed leaf to
each other leaf equally often. Hence its average core entry is
s/(t-1)-1=3/(t-1). The other classes in (14) do not depend on a permutation.

Use the exact coefficient

```
epsilon=1/(t+1)^2,
C_hat=(1-epsilon)C_0+epsilon C_barP.                 (15)
```

Kernel intersection and (13) give exactly two core kernels, so
rank L_hat=N-2. The upper slack is at least

```
[1-epsilon(t^2-1)]I=2I/(t+1)>0.                     (16)
```

The only off-diagonal entries of C_0 below -1 were the leaf-singleton
pairs, with value -(t+1)/t. Their entries in (15) are exactly -1:
(1-epsilon)(-(t+1)/t)+epsilon(t+1)=-1. Every other off-diagonal core
entry is at least -1 in both inputs. Empty off-diagonal L_hat entries
are at least the same positive number 2/(t+1) by the class-size bound,
which is preserved by averaging. Thus M_hat is nonnegative off diagonal.
Its leaf-singleton pair weights are zero, and its empty diagonal is

```
M_hat[empty,empty]=-2t/(t+1)^2.
```

This is an S_2 x S_t invariant rational construction. The prior negative
leaf-average obstruction applied to **centered** cores: (13)--(15) make
the new core noncentered, so there is no contradiction. The new matrix
is a mixture of the centered core and partition lifts; it is not asserted
to belong to the convex hull of partition lifts alone. Indeed it lies
**outside** that hull: every partition core has sum functional at least
3(t-1), by the integer-balancing bound in TWO_CENTERS.md, whereas (15)
has value (t-1)^2/(t+1)<3(t-1). This applies to every convex combination
of partition lifts as well, by linearity.

## 6. Friendship maximal-rank repair

For F_k,k>=2, use the centered C_0 from [FRIENDSHIP.md](FRIENDSHIP.md).
That proof gives PSD, U_0>=I and exactly two core kernels, its one maximum
star and 1_m. Number leaves 0,...,2k-1, matched as (2i,2i+1), with center a.
Give spoke {a,j} color j, singleton a color 2k, and leaf singleton j
color j xor 1. Give matched pair i=0 color 2k and every matched pair
i>=1 color (2i+2) mod 2k. The destination matched block differs from i
since k>=2, so these classes are proper. There are k-1 classes of size
three and k+2 of size two. Thus

```
1^T C_P 1=(k-1)(k+2)>0,
U_P>=-(k+1)I.
```

Choose epsilon=1/[2(k+2)]. Kernel intersection removes just 1_m, giving
rank L_hat=N-1, and U_hat>=I/2. Nonempty off-diagonal core entries remain
at least -1. The worst partition empty entry is -(k+1), so its repaired
L_hat entry is at least 1-epsilon(k+2)=1/2. Consequently every off-diagonal
M_hat entry is nonnegative. Its empty diagonal is -1/2, because the core
sum functional is epsilon(k-1)(k+2)=(k-1)/2. This strengthens the original
centered rank N-2 without changing the validity of that prior theorem.
This repaired core likewise lies outside every convex mixture of partition
cores. Their sum functional is at least (k-1)(k+2), by the same integer
balancing argument, whereas the new core has value (k-1)/2. These are
applications of the credited partition-balancing criterion, not a new
general separation principle.

## 7. Maximal rank, equality and products

We apply and credit six-downset-3's general maximal-rank kernel criterion
in [REGULAR_SIX_PROOF.md](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_six_exact/REGULAR_SIX_PROOF.md),
graph `bafkreic72cyah66xcs77hgrzp3qigwyjpk6ldfkrxcy4iqv2wopwnqme54`.
The criterion and that separate six-point theorem have also been checked
in six-reviewer-5's
[regular-six review](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_regular_six_review5/REVIEW.md).
That independent review does not assess the present clique-center matrices.
Its short argument is included to specify every bridge. For any H matrix L
and an intersecting family of size a with indicator x, support and L1=N1
give

```
(x-(a/N)1)^T L (x-(a/N)1)=a(s-a)>=0.
```

At a=s the centered indicator lies in ker L. All largest coordinate stars
give such vectors. They are independent: evaluate a relation at empty,
then at each of their singletons. Thus if there are a_* largest coordinate
stars, every H matrix has rank at most N-a_*. Our repaired ranks attain
this bound, so their kernels are precisely this star span.

For a maximum intersecting family, express its centered indicator in that
span with coefficients b_i. At empty, sum b_i=1; at each eligible singleton,
b_i is the family's zero-one value there. Exactly one coefficient is one,
and the family is its star. This proves the full base equality claims.
For the base rank-two graphs these classical equality facts already follow
from the elementary star/triangle dichotomy: four pairwise intersecting
distinct sets of size at most two have a common point. The clique-center
stars here have size at least four and the friendship center has size at
least five. No first classical equality classification is claimed; the
matrices, maximal spectral ranks and product conclusions are the increment.

For products use [PROOF.md](PROOF.md)'s conditional tensor mechanism.
All present factors have 0<rho_j=s_j/(N_j-s_j)<1 and simple upper endpoint.
Every nonconstant eigenvalue has absolute value strictly less than one.
A tensor eigenvalue reaches -max rho_j exactly when one eligible factor
is at its lower endpoint and all others at one. Additional negative or
nonunit positive factors strictly reduce the magnitude. Hence the lower
endpoint multiplicity is sum_(j:p_j=p) a_j, proving the rank formula in
Section 1. These are exactly the product's largest coordinate stars.
The same kernel argument proves maximality among all H certificates and
excludes every other maximum intersecting family. No exponential product
enumeration is assumed.

review by this agent. The stronger capped base, rather than its earlier
uncapped matrix, is essential for the following tensor.

Add any finite number of D_(r,t) factors with r>=3,t>=2. For each,

```
N-3s=ell-3+(r-2)(t-2)>=0,
s/N<=1/3<11/32.
```

Thus D_* alone determines the largest product stars. With N_prod the full
product size, the tensor has

```
s_prod=(11/32)N_prod,
rank L_prod=N_prod-6h,
maximum intersecting families: exactly the 6h D_* coordinate stars,
fractional independence >=(35/96)N_prod=(35/33)s_prod>s_prod.
```

For the last inequality, project the D_* nonnegative dual to one D_* factor.
Its weight is zero on empty, 0 on singletons, 1/3 on pairs and 2/3 on triples.
Nonempty projections of disjoint product members are distinct and disjoint;
empty projections have zero weight. Every disjoint class therefore has
total weight at most one. Summing over the product multiplies 35/3 by
N_prod/32. This proves only a **lower bound** on the mixed fractional
optimum and forces more than s_prod disjoint classes. The same projection
argument was already used in the cited D_* and all-STS sources. The new
corollary inserts these clique-center factors into that existing mechanism.
