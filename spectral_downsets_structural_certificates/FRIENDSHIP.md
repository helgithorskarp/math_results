# Capped certificates for every friendship graph downset

Authoring agent: **six-downset-1**, role **researcher**, 2026-09-30.
Status: ordinary written infinite proof, with exact rational validation.
No independent review, formalization, or historical-priority claim is made.

## 1. Family and result

Let F_k be the downset generated by a friendship graph: k triangles sharing
one common vertex a and otherwise vertex-disjoint, with k>=1. It contains
the empty set, the center singleton, 2k leaf singletons, 2k center-leaf
pairs, and k matched leaf pairs. It contains no triples. Thus

```
N=5k+2,                 s=2k+1.
```

The center has the largest star; leaf stars have size three. For k=1 all
three stars have size three and the family is the uniform D_3.

For every k>=1 there is a rational symmetric H matrix M_k with M_k<=I.
For k>=2 the construction below has

```
M_k[empty,empty]=-2k/(3k+1),
M_k[empty,A]=1/(3k+1) for every nonempty A,
all other off-diagonal entries nonnegative,
rank((N-s)M_k+sI)=5k,
multiplicity(lambda_min(M_k))=2,
lambda_min(M_k)=-(2k+1)/(3k+1),
multiplicity(lambda_max(M_k)=1)=1.                         (1)
```

Consequently every finite product of these downsets and other capped
factors on disjoint supports satisfies H. For every fixed k>=2 and h>=1,
the h-fold power has

```
N_h=(5k+2)^h,        s_h=(2k+1)(5k+2)^(h-1),
rank((N_h-s_h)M_k^(tensor h)+s_h I)=N_h-2h.               (2)
```

This is a cap result beyond the partition construction: for k>=2,
N=2s+k, so N modulo s is k, neither zero nor one. By
[PROOF.md, section 6](PROOF.md), no simplex partition certificate with s
classes can have the cap for any F_k with k>=2. Ordinary H for rank-two
families already follows from the Vizing construction. The result here
supplies a different capped matrix for this infinite subclass.

## 2. A centered-core sufficient condition

Suppose a valid H core C has C1=0 and C[A,B]>=-1 for every distinct pair
of nonempty indices. Put m=N-1 and U=NI_m-J_m-C. Then every off-diagonal
entry of U is -(1+C[A,B])<=0 and U1=1. Therefore

```
U=I_m+L,
L=sum_{A<B} (1+C[A,B])(e_A-e_B)(e_A-e_B)^T >=0.           (3)
```

Hence U is positive definite. The empty lift gives
(N-s)(I-M)=EUE^T, so M<=I and equality has just the constant direction.
Equivalently, all off-diagonal entries of the lifted M are nonnegative,
and I-M is its weighted Laplacian. The empty loop may be negative.
This is elementary matrix theory; the sufficient condition is not claimed
as a new Laplacian principle in isolation.

## 3. The rational core

Write the leaves in k matched pairs. For pair j, denote its two leaf
singletons by c_{j,+}, c_{j,-}, its center-leaf pairs by b_{j,+}, b_{j,-},
and its leaf pair by d_j. Index the nonempty core by
a, all b, all c, all d. For k>=2 put f=1/(k-1).

Every diagonal of C equals 2k. Its off-diagonal entries are:

| Indices | Core entry |
| --- | ---: |
| a and any b, or two distinct b | -1 |
| a and any c or d | 0 |
| b and c or d from the same matched pair | -1 |
| b and c or d from different matched pairs | f |
| two c from the same matched pair | k-2 |
| two c from different matched pairs | -1 |
| any c and any d | -1 |
| distinct d | 0 |

These classes exhaust all distinct pairs. Every intersection has entry -1:
the center star; a spoke and its leaf singleton; a spoke and its matched
leaf pair; and a leaf singleton and its matched leaf pair. Additional
disjoint pairs may also have entry -1, which merely gives zero matrix
weight. No entry is less than -1.

The core is centered by direct row counting. On the center star a and b,
the diagonal 2k and 2k entries -1 sum to zero. A b row sums to zero on
the c indices because -2+2(k-1)f=0, and on the d indices because
-1+(k-1)f=0. An a row has no other contribution. A c row similarly
sums to zero on a and b; on the c level it sums to
2k+(k-2)-(2k-2)=k, canceled by the k entries -1 on d. A d row sums to
zero on a and b, and its 2k diagonal is canceled by the 2k c entries -1.
Thus C1=0.

It remains to prove C>=0. Once this holds, the lift in PROOF.md, section 2,
constructs H, while (3) supplies the cap. All displayed entries and the
lift are rational.

## 4. Complete orthogonal decomposition and positivity

First take differences within each matched leaf pair, separately on the b
and c levels. They decouple from all other levels and from one another.
Their eigenvalues are 2k+1 on b differences and k+2 on c differences,
each with multiplicity k. Both are positive.

Next take vectors constant within each leaf pair, and with coefficients
summing to zero across the k pairs. Normalize each two-coordinate b or c
pair direction by sqrt(2). For every unit coefficient vector perpendicular
to 1_k, the b,c,d directions have the symmetric matrix

```
B_k = [ 2k+1       -2k/(k-1)       -sqrt(2)k/(k-1) ]
      [ -2k/(k-1)      3k                    0     ]
      [ -sqrt(2)k/(k-1) 0                  2k     ].       (4)
```

There are k-1 mutually orthogonal copies of this block. For example,
the b-c pairwise-constant self-block is -2, its other-block value is 2f,
and their difference is -2-2f=-2k/(k-1). The other entries follow from
the same self-block minus other-block count.

The bottom diagonal block diag(3k,2k) is positive definite. Its Schur
complement in (4) is

```
2k+1 - 7k/[3(k-1)^2] > 0.                              (5)
```

Indeed (k-1)^2>=k/2 for integers k>=2, since
2(k-1)^2-k=(2k-1)(k-2)>=0. Therefore the subtracted quantity is at most
14/3 and 2k+1>=5, leaving at least 1/3. Thus each B_k is positive definite.

Finally consider the four level-constant directions a,b,c,d, normalized
by their level sizes. Their cross-block coupling vanishes by the row
counts above. The two remaining blocks are

```
on a,b: [ 2k       -sqrt(2k) ]       eigenvalues 0, 2k+1;
        [ -sqrt(2k)     1    ]

on c,d: [ k       -sqrt(2)k ]        eigenvalues 0, 3k.
        [ -sqrt(2)k    2k   ]
```

The dimensions are 2k for within-pair differences, 3(k-1) for centered
pairwise-constant directions, and four for the final constants. They
sum to 5k+1, the entire core dimension. Orthogonality follows from
within-pair averaging and the decomposition R^k=<1_k> direct_sum 1_k
perpendicular. This proves PSD with exactly two zero eigenvalues and
rank C=5k-1. No spectral-completeness bridge is left to an experiment.

Because C1=0, Q has zero empty row and column and otherwise equals C.
The all-ones direction is orthogonal to the range of Q, so
rank(Q+J)=rank(C)+1=5k. The two remaining zero eigenvalues of Q+J
give the lower endpoint and its multiplicity in (1). Equation (3) gives
the strict upper cap off the constant direction. The case k=1 uses the
uniform incidence projection for D_3, as proved in PROOF.md, section 9.

## 5. Product and template consequences

Every nonconstant eigenvalue of M_k for k>=2 has absolute value strictly
less than one: the upper endpoint one is simple, and the lower endpoint
has magnitude rho=(2k+1)/(3k+1)<1. The conditional tensor lemma therefore
proves every finite product claimed in section 1.

For a power, a tensor eigenvalue reaches -rho only when exactly one
factor is -rho and all others are one. Any other nonconstant factor
reduces its absolute value, and more than one negative factor also reduces
the magnitude. There are h choices of the negative factor, each with
multiplicity two. Thus the tensor Hoffman PSD matrix has nullity 2h,
proving (2). No optimal-rank statement among all possible certificates is
asserted.

The n=5 four-cycle deletion obstruction in [DELETIONS.md](DELETIONS.md)
retains the graph F_2. Its inherited uniform projection has maximum M
eigenvalue 29/21>1, and every partition certificate fails its cap by the
residue N=12 modulo s=5. The present core is a third, explicitly capped
construction for that same family. The other failed five-coordinate
template, deleting K4 minus an edge, has N=11,s=5 and already admits a
capped equitable partition because N=1 modulo s. Thus both small
restriction failures are construction limitations, not cap obstructions.

## 6. Reproduction and trust boundary

[friendship.py](friendship.py) generates the rational core and matrix.
[verify_friendship.py](verify_friendship.py) independently constructs M
from its explicit nonzero weights, compares every entry, and checks all
H requirements and both PSD bounds with exact Fraction LDL. It checks
centering and the predicted rank, the positive-core Schur remainder,
the cap by a weighted Laplacian identity, and the
same-family failure of the inherited and partition constructions at k=2.
It also checks the capped partition for the other five-coordinate
deletion template.

The finite run uses k=1,...,10, dimensions at most 52, and a mixed product
with a matching downset. Exact outputs are in
[friendship_expected.json](friendship_expected.json). The infinite
quantifiers and every-power rank formula follow from the written proof,
not extrapolation from these ten cases or enumeration of product matrices.

```bash
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 \
  python3 verify_friendship.py
```

Use CPython 3.11.2, standard library only, one process. No solver,
floating-point spectrum, imported certificate, private input, or large
omitted corpus is required. The trust boundary is the unformalized
orthogonal decomposition and ordinary linear algebra, together with
Python integer/Fraction semantics for the finite validation.
