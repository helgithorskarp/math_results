## 1. Statement and credited ingredients

Let B(u,v) be the rank-two downset generated by K_2 joined to K_(u,v),
where u,v>=2 are integers. Exchange parts to normalize 2<=u<=v.
Write h=u+v and label the centers a,b. Its nonempty levels are center
singletons A, center edge F={a,b}, center-to-left/right spokes P_L,P_R,
leaf singletons C_L,C_R, and all leaf edges E. Their sizes are
2,1,2u,2v,u,v,uv. Put

```
N=4+3h+uv,     m=N-1,     s=h+2,     rho=s/(N-s)<1.
```

The center stars S_a,S_b have size s. Leaf stars have sizes v+3,u+3<s.

**Theorem.** Every B(u,v) has an explicit rational symmetric M, constructed
below and in [two_center_bipartite.py](two_center_bipartite.py), with M1=1,
M[X,Y]=0 whenever X intersects Y, and

```
-rho I <= M <= I,
rank((N-s)M+sI)=N-2,       rank(I-M)=N-1.
```

The lower endpoint has multiplicity two, the upper endpoint is simple,
and N-2 is maximal among all real H matrices, even without the upper cap.
The only maximum intersecting families are S_a,S_b. The specified matrix
has nonnegative off-diagonal entries **if and only if v<=u^2**. Its empty
loop may have either sign. Failure of that sign condition is not an
obstruction to alternative matrices.

For any nonempty finite list of these factors on disjoint supports, put
p=max_j(s_j/N_j), N_P=product_j N_j, and let c count factors attaining p.
The tensor has largest-star size N_P*p, unrestricted maximal Hoffman PSD
rank N_P-2c, and exactly 2c maximum families, the eligible center-coordinate
stars. A k-th power has rank N^k-2k and exactly 2k maximum families.
Section 7 gives a conditional mixed-product consequence using D_*.

Ordinary H for every rank-two downset already follows from Vizing coloring,
as reproduced in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). The rank-two star/triangle

## 2. The complete invariant affine face

Consider S_2 times S_u times S_v invariant symmetric cores C_0 on nonempty
members, with diagonal h+1 and entry -1 at distinct intersecting pairs.
Part exchange is not imposed, even when u=v. The 24 disjoint orbits are:

| Disjoint pair | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| distinct center singletons | b_A |
| A,C_L or A,C_R | q_L or q_R |
| center singleton and a spoke at the other center | p_L or p_R |
| A,E | q_A |
| F,C_L or F,C_R | r_L or r_R |
| F,E | q_F |
| distinct C_L,C_L or C_R,C_R | b_L or b_R |
| C_L,C_R | b_T |
| C_L,P_L or C_R,P_R, nonincident | a_L or a_R |
| C_L,P_R or C_R,P_L | t_L or t_R |
| C_L,E or C_R,E, nonincident | c_L or c_R |
| opposite-center spokes within one part, different leaves | k_L or k_R |
| opposite-center spokes across parts | k_T |
| P_L,E or P_R,E, nonincident | w_L or w_R |
| disjoint E,E | z |

Every orbit occurs when u,v>=2. C_0 1=0 and
C_0 v_(S_a)=C_0 v_(S_b)=0 are equivalent to the following formulas, with
twelve free coordinates k_L,k_R,k_T,a_L,a_R,t_L,t_R,b_L,b_R,b_T,q_L,q_R:

```
p_L=2-(u-1)k_L-v*k_T,          p_R=2-(v-1)k_R-u*k_T,
b_A=1-u*p_L-v*p_R,
q_A=-(1+u*q_L+v*q_R)/(uv),
r_L=1-(u-1)a_L-v*t_L-q_L,     r_R=1-(v-1)a_R-u*t_R-q_R,
w_L=[v-(u-1)a_L-v*t_R]/[v(u-1)],
w_R=[u-(v-1)a_R-u*t_L]/[u(v-1)],
q_F=2-(u-1)w_L-(v-1)w_R-q_A,
c_L=-[u+q_L+(u-1)(a_L+b_L)+v(t_L+b_T)]/[v(u-1)],
c_R=-[v+q_R+(v-1)(a_R+b_R)+u(t_R+b_T)]/[u(v-1)],
z=[q_F-1-(u-1)c_L-(v-1)c_R]/[(u-1)(v-1)].             (1)
```

For necessity, a spoke at a has sum p_L-2+(u-1)k_L+v*k_T on the star
at b. A center-singleton row on the opposite star has sum
b_A-1+u*p_L+v*p_R. The C_L star row is
q_L+r_L-1+(u-1)a_L+v*t_L, and the E star row is
q_A+q_F-2+(u-1)w_L+(v-1)w_R. The right rows are analogous;
a member belonging to a center star has zero sum on that star directly.
These force p,b_A,r,q_F.

The total A row gives q_A. Subtracting its own zero star row from a
total P_L row gives -v+(u-1)a_L+v*t_R+v(u-1)w_L=0, and similarly on
the right, giving w_L,w_R. After using a star equation, the total C_L
row is u+q_L+(u-1)(a_L+b_L)+v(t_L+b_T)+v(u-1)c_L=0.
The E total row then gives
1-q_F+(u-1)c_L+(v-1)c_R+(u-1)(v-1)z=0. These force c_L,c_R,z.
Finally symmetry and the killed center-star sum give the row-sum
identity R_A+R_F+u*R_(P_L)+v*R_(P_R)=0, so the F total row follows.
The same counts prove sufficiency. The twelve free orbits are distinct
and occur, so the affine space has dimension twelve over rationals or
reals. This characterization does not assert PSD for arbitrary points.

## 5. Kernel repair, empty lift and exact sign boundary

Label the leaves 0,...,h-1 and centers h,h+1, so there are s coordinates.
Color {i} by 2i modulo s and {i,j} by i+j modulo s. Distinct incident
edges have distinct colors, different from their incident singleton.
Each center has all other s-1 coordinates as neighbors; its star hits
every color once. Thus this is a proper partition into s nonempty classes.

If all class sizes are equal, recolor the first left singleton to the
smallest color absent from its star. That star has v+3<s members, so
such a color exists. Properness is preserved, no member of either center
star changes color, and two class sizes change by -1,+1. Therefore the
deterministic partition always has nonconstant sizes q_c. Its core
C_P[X,Y]=s*1[color(X)=color(Y)]-1 equals Z(sI_s-J_s)Z^T>=0,
has the prescribed entries, kills both center stars, and has
1^T C_P 1=s*sum(q_c^2)-m^2>0.

Use the credited repair from [maxrank_mixtures.py](maxrank_mixtures.py):

```
beta=max(0,s*max_c(q_c)-N), epsilon=1/[2(beta+1)],
C_hat=(1-epsilon)C_0+epsilon*C_P.                       (12)
```

Both coefficients are positive. The intersection of the PSD kernels
in (8) removes precisely total: C_P kills both stars and has positive
quadratic form on total. Thus ker C_hat=span{v_(S_a),v_(S_b)} and
rank C_hat=N-3. Since N I-J-C_P=N I-sZZ^T>=-beta I, (11) gives

```
N I_m-J_m-C_hat >= [1-epsilon*(beta+1)]I_m=I_m/2.        (13)
```

With R=[-1^T;I_m], set

```
L=J_N+R C_hat R^T,      M=(L-sI_N)/(N-s).               (14)
```

R^T1=0 gives L1=N1 and M1=1. Intersecting nonempty entries, including
their diagonals, give M=0. Orthogonality of the PSD summands gives
rank L=1+rank C_hat=N-2. The identity
N I_N-L=R(N I_m-J_m-C_hat)R^T and (13) prove M<=I and
rank(I-M)=N-1. This proves both spectral bounds.

For v<=u^2 all off-diagonal C_0 entries are at least -1. In the tail
only b_L=-v/u^2 can violate this: c_L>=-1 since v(u-1)>=u+2;
c_R>=-1 since u(v-1)>=14/3-1/u-1/v; and
z>-1/[(u-1)(v-1)]. The other weights in (2)-(3) satisfy the bound directly.
The boundary cores have minimum off-diagonal -1, also checked exactly.
C_P obeys the same bound, so nonempty off-diagonal M>=0 in this regime.
Empty off-diagonal entries obey, for all parameters,

```
L[empty,X]=1-epsilon*(s*q_color(X)-m)>=1/2.              (15)
```

Conversely, v>u^2 lies in the tail. The first two left singletons start
with colors 0,2, distinct modulo s>=6. If recoloring occurs, the colors
present at the first leaf are exactly 0,u,u+1,...,s-1, since that leaf
is adjacent to all right leaves and both centers. The smallest absent
color is 1. Consequently the two left singleton colors remain distinct
in either case, so their partition entry is -1 and

```
M[{0},{1}]=(1-epsilon)(1-v/u^2)/(N-s)<0.               (16)
```

This proves the claimed sign equivalence for the specified construction,
without an infeasibility conclusion for any alternative matrix. No
nonnegative-entry claim is made about arbitrary tensor products.

## 6. Unrestricted rank, equality and products

For any intersecting family with indicator x and size t, support and row
sums give (x-(t/N)1)^T L(x-(t/N)1)=t(s-t). PSD implies t<=s.
For any real H matrix, the two independent centered star vectors
f_a=v_(S_a)-(s/N)1 and f_b=v_(S_b)-(s/N)1 have zero PSD quadratic form
and therefore belong to ker L. Thus rank L<=N-2 without an upper cap;
our rank is unrestricted maximal. At equality write the centered
indicator as alpha*f_a+beta*f_b. Empty gives alpha+beta=1; the individual
center singletons give alpha,beta in {0,1}. Exactly one equals one,
so the family is its center star. This proof uses no finite enumeration.

Each factor has eigenvalues in [-rho_j,1], 0<rho_j<1, with a
two-dimensional lower endpoint and simple upper endpoint. Tensor
eigenvalues are products. A negative product reaches -max_j rho_j only
with one eligible lower endpoint and every other factor at 1; further
negative factors or another nonunit factor strictly reduce its magnitude.
Thus the tensor lower eigenspace has dimension 2c. Support and row sums
are preserved; its star fraction is p=max_j s_j/N_j and rho=p/(1-p).
This proves capped H and rank N_P-2c.

The 2c eligible centered coordinate-star vectors are independent:
empty and the individual eligible center singleton coordinates separate
their coefficients. Every H matrix kills these vectors, proving the
unrestricted rank bound. For an equality indicator the coefficients sum
to one by empty and each is 0 or 1 by its individual singleton coordinate.
Exactly one is one, giving the full product classification.
