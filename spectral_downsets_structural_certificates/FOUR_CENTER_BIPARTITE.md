## 1. Quantified theorem

Let B_4(u,v) be the downset generated by the edges of K_4 joined to
K_(u,v), for integers **u,v>=2**. Normalize **2<=u<=v** by interchanging
the leaf parts. The downset consists of empty, the four center singletons,
their six center edges, all u+v leaf singletons, all4(u+v) center-to-leaf
spokes, and the uv leaf-to-leaf edges. Put

```
h=u+v,   N=11+5h+uv,   s=h+4,   rho=s/(N-s).
```

Exactly the four center stars have size s; the two leaf star sizes are
v+5 and u+5, strictly smaller. Also N-2s=uv+3h+3>0, so0<rho<1.

**Theorem.** There is an explicit rational symmetric matrix M on B_4(u,v)
with M1=1, M[A,B]=0 whenever A intersects B, and

```
-rho I <= M <= I,
rank L=N-4,   L=(N-s)M+sI,
rank(I-M)=N-1.
```

Rank N-4 is maximal among the lower slacks of **all real H matrices**
on this downset, even without the cap. The lower endpoint has multiplicity
four and the upper endpoint is simple. Exactly the four center-star
families are maximum intersecting families. Signed disjoint entries are
allowed; no sign classification is claimed.

For every finite nonempty product on disjoint ground supports of these
factors, let p=max_j s_j/N_j, N_P=product_j N_j, and c be the number of
factors attaining p. The tensor matrix is capped and has unrestricted
maximal lower-slack rank N_P-4c. Exactly4c maximum families occur, the
eligible coordinate stars. In particular a k-th power has rankN^k-4k.
For products of the credited one-, two-, three- and four-center
complete-bipartite factors, let r_j in{1,2,3,4} be their center counts and
d=sum_(j:s_j/N_j=p)r_j. The rank is N_P-d and exactlyd maximum coordinate
stars occur. Section7 supplies the proof and precise dependencies.

The increment is the signed disjoint-center-edge mechanism, its complete
four-center PSD/cap and rank proof, and the special matching-corner rank
repair. Ordinary H for rank-two downsets follows already from constructive
Vizing, as credited in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md); that baseline does not supply
the current extra cap and explicit unrestricted maximal rank.

## 7. Equality families and products

For an intersecting family I of size k, the support condition gives
1_I^T L1_I=s k and L1=N1, hence

```
(1_I-(k/N)1)^T L(1_I-(k/N)1)=k(s-k).
```

At k=s this centered indicator belongs to ker L, which is exactly the
span of the four centered star indicators. Thus

```
1_I=p(1-sum_i a_i)1+sum_i a_i S_i.
```

Evaluating empty, which no intersecting family can contain, gives
sum_i a_i=1. Evaluating each center singleton gives a_i in{0,1}.
Precisely one coefficient is1, so I is its center star. This is a
kernel proof of the equality classification; the classical base
star/triangle characterization is credited and not asserted new.

For a tensor product of capped factors with spectra in[-rho_j,1],
the least product eigenvalue is-max_j rho_j. A product of two or more
negative endpoints cannot give the least negative value, since every
rho_j<1; if a product is negative and involves further factors of
magnitude<1 its magnitude is strictly smaller. Each top eigenspace is
the one-dimensional constant space. Therefore the least eigenspace is
the direct sum of the endpoint spaces of greatest-fraction factors,
tensored with constants on the others. Its dimension is4c here, or
d=sum eligible r_j in the mixed one/two/three/four-center case.

The credited tensor/support identity in graph7578 supplies H and the cap,
with largest star s_P=N_P*p. The centered eligible coordinate-star
vectors are independent by empty and their singleton evaluations, so the
rank is N_P-d and is unrestricted maximal among all real H matrices.
The same evaluations in the indicator identity show that every equality
family is precisely one eligible coordinate star. No heuristic product
search supplies this all-order conclusion.
