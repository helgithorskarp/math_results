## 1. Scope, statement and prior ingredients

Let W(u,v) be the rank-two downset generated by K_1 joined to K_(u,v),
where u,v are integers at least two. Exchange the parts to normalize
2<=u<=v. Its members are the empty set, a center singleton a, leaf
singletons C_L,C_R, center spokes X_L,X_R, and all uv leaf edges E.
Set

```
h=u+v,     N=2+2h+uv,     m=N-1,     s=h+1,
rho=s/(N-s)<1.
```

The center star S={a} union X_L union X_R has size s. Leaf stars have
sizes v+2 and u+2, strictly smaller than s.

**Theorem.** Every W(u,v) has an explicit rational symmetric matrix M
with M1=1, M[A,B]=0 whenever A intersects B, and

```
-rho I <= M <= I,
rank((N-s)M+sI)=N-1,       rank(I-M)=N-1.
```

Both spectral endpoints are simple. The Hoffman PSD rank N-1 is maximal
among all real H matrices, including competitors without the upper cap.
The center star is the only maximum intersecting family. When v<=u^2 the
constructed matrix has nonnegative off-diagonal entries; for larger
imbalance signed entries are allowed. The empty loop remains allowed.

For any nonempty finite list of these factors on disjoint supports, write
p=max_j(s_j/N_j), N_P=product_j N_j, and let c count factors attaining p.
Their tensor has largest-star size N_P*p, unrestricted maximal Hoffman
PSD rank N_P-c, and exactly c maximum intersecting families, the eligible
center-coordinate stars. A k-th power has rank N^k-k and exactly k such
families. Section 7 gives a conditional mixed-product consequence using
the already published capped D_* certificate and its fractional dual.

## 2. The invariant seven-parameter affine face

Consider S_u times S_v invariant symmetric cores C on the nonempty
members, with diagonal h and entry -1 at distinct intersecting pairs.
Exchange of the two parts is not imposed, including when u=v.
The remaining disjoint entries have the following names:

| Pair of levels, always disjoint | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| a,C_L or a,C_R | p_L or p_R |
| a,E | q |
| C_L,C_L or C_R,C_R, distinct | b_L or b_R |
| C_L,C_R | b_T |
| C_L,X_L or C_R,X_R | a_L or a_R |
| C_L,X_R or C_R,X_L | t_L or t_R |
| X_L,E or X_R,E, nonincident | w_L or w_R |
| C_L,E or C_R,E, nonincident | c_L or c_R |
| E,E, disjoint | z |

There are no disjoint spoke-spoke pairs. Since both part sizes are at
least two, every listed orbit occurs. The equations C1=0 and Cv_S=0
are equivalent to the following forced values, with seven free rational
coordinates a_L,a_R,t_L,t_R,b_L,b_R,b_T:

```
p_L=1-(u-1)a_L-v*t_L,
p_R=1-(v-1)a_R-u*t_R,
w_L=[v+1-(u-1)a_L-v*t_R]/[v(u-1)],
w_R=[u+1-(v-1)a_R-u*t_L]/[u(v-1)],
q=2-(u-1)w_L-(v-1)w_R,
c_L=-[u+(u-1)b_L+v*b_T]/[v(u-1)],
c_R=-[v+(v-1)b_R+u*b_T]/[u(v-1)],
z=-c_L/(v-1)-c_R/(u-1).                              (1)
```

For example the star row at C_L is
p_L-1+(u-1)a_L+v*t_L=0. The other singleton is analogous.
The star equation at E is q-2+(u-1)w_L+(v-1)w_R=0.
The nonstar row at X_L is
-1+(u-1)a_L+v*t_R-v+v(u-1)w_L=0, and at C_L it is
u+(u-1)b_L+v*b_T+v(u-1)c_L=0. The edge nonstar row is
(u-1)c_L+(v-1)c_R+(u-1)(v-1)z=0.
The center nonstar row u*p_L+v*p_R+uv*q=0 then follows
automatically. These counts prove necessity and sufficiency of (1).
This is an affine support/kernel characterization, not a PSD assertion.

Choose

```
a_L=a_R=0,     t_L=1/v,     t_R=1/u,
b_L=-v/u^2,    b_R=-1,     b_T=(u-1)/u^2.               (2)
```

The resulting centered core C_0 has p_L=p_R=q=0 and

```
w_L=1/u+1/[v(u-1)],       w_R=1/v+1/[u(v-1)],
c_L=-u/[v(u-1)],          c_R=-(2u-1)/[u^2(v-1)],
z=[u^3+(2u-1)v]/[u^2*v*(u-1)*(v-1)]>0.                (3)
```

All denominators are positive. These formulas are implemented literally
in [bipartite_cones.py](bipartite_cones.py). Its public constructors
reject inexact part sizes, booleans, invalid shifts and floating-point
face coordinates. Custom affine face points have no PSD/cap guarantee.

## 3. Complete grid decomposition and positivity

Identify the edge space with R^u tensor R^v, split each leaf space as
span{1} orthogonal-sum 1-perpendicular, and use orthonormal bases of the
two perpendicular spaces.

**Double-centered grid.** On 1_u-perpendicular tensor 1_v-perpendicular,
the core acts by h+2+z>0, with no image in the other levels. The dimension
is (u-1)(v-1).

**Left leaf modes.** For a unit y perpendicular to 1_u, use y on X_L,
y on C_L, and y tensor 1_v on E. These three orthogonal vectors have
squared norms 1,1,v. Their unnormalized Gram matrix is

```
G_L=[ h+1          -1-a_L        -v(1+w_L)                ]
    [ -1-a_L      h-b_L         -v(1+c_L)                ]
    [ -v(1+w_L)   -v(1+c_L)     v[h+2+z-(1+z)v]          ]. (4)
```

This is the Gram matrix of the quadratic form, not the operator matrix;
the latter is diag(1,1,v)^(-1) G_L. There are u-1 mutually orthogonal
copies. All other levels have zero image by the zero sum of y.

**Right leaf modes.** Exchange (u,L) and (v,R) in (4), including their
different weights from (2)-(3). Their norms are 1,1,u and their Gram
matrix is G_R. There are v-1 copies. Left and right edge images are
orthogonal, as are their full invariant spaces.

**Constants.** Order the six constant vectors as
(a,X_L-sum,X_R-sum,C_L-sum,C_R-sum,E-sum). Their norms squared are
(1,u,v,u,v,uv). The constant Gram matrix is G=F K F^T, where

```
F=[ 1  0  0  0 ]          K=[ h   -u        0                  0          ]
  [ 0  1  0  0 ]            [ -u  u(v+1)   -u                 v          ]
  [-1 -1  0  0 ]            [ 0   -u       u*h-v(u-1)/u       v(u-1)/u   ]
  [ 0  0  1  0 ]            [ 0    v       v(u-1)/u           v(u+1)     ].
  [ 0  0  0  1 ]                                                        (5)
  [ 0  0 -1 -1 ]
```

The last diagonal entry of K is v(u+1). All entries can be obtained by
counting the literal level pairs; the factorization also follows from
the two kernel equations in Section 2. The exact checker independently
checks all six constant images against the full matrix.

The subspaces above are orthogonal, invariant, and exhaust the nonempty
space, because

```
(u-1)(v-1)+3(u-1)+3(v-1)+6=uv+2u+2v+1=m.              (6)
```

To prove G_L,G_R,K positive definite for all 2<=u<=v, multiply them
by the positive denominators

```
d_L=u^2(u-1),       d_R=u*v*(v-1),       d_K=u,
```

and set u=2+U,v=u+V with U,V nonnegative integers. Every leading
principal determinant of these polynomial matrices has strictly positive
integer coefficients and a positive constant coefficient. The complete
coefficients are [bipartite_cone_determinants.json](bipartite_cone_determinants.json),
rederived by [bipartite_cone_polynomials.py](bipartite_cone_polynomials.py).

| Matrix | Term counts of successive leading minors | Constants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| d_L G_L | 9,24,50 | 20,344,1584 |
| d_R G_R | 14,42,95 | 20,384,1456 |
| u K | 5,15,30,50 | 8,80,992,9280 |

The code constructs rational blocks from the seven row/star equations
independently of the hand-cleared polynomial matrices, proves each
clearing identity coefficient by coefficient, and computes determinants
by the full permutation definition. The coefficient list uses records
[exponent_of_U,exponent_of_V,integer_coefficient]. Equality of the
polynomial identity and positivity of every recorded coefficient are
checked; aggregate counts alone are not a certificate.

Sylvester's criterion now gives positivity on every nonconstant mode
and rank four on constants. In (5), ker F^T consists of the star relation
a+X_L-sum+X_R-sum and the nonstar relation C_L-sum+C_R-sum+E-sum.
Consequently

```
C_0 >=0,      ker C_0=span{v_S,1},      rank C_0=N-3.    (7)
```

## 4. A uniform upper slack, including arbitrary imbalance

Define U_0=N I_m-J_m-C_0. Centering gives U_0*1=1. We prove

```
U_0>=I_m,       ker(U_0-I_m)=span{1}.                  (8)
```

On nonconstant modes J vanishes. The corresponding Gram matrices for
U_0-I are

```
W_L=(N-1)diag(1,1,v)-G_L,
W_R=(N-1)diag(1,1,u)-G_R.
```

Their leading minors, cleared by d_L,d_R, have positive coefficients in
the same U,V variables. The double-centered scalar is
N-1-(h+2+z)=uv+h-1-z>0; its positive clearing denominator is
D=u^2*v*(u-1)*(v-1). The original edge positivity uses the same D.

| Polynomial or matrix | Term counts | Constants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| d_L W_L leading minors | 11,30,75 | 32,1072,83488 |
| d_R W_R leading minors | 18,56,125 | 32,1008,78624 |
| D(h+2+z) | 22 | 62 |
| D(uv+h-1-z) | 26 | 42 |

For constants, the trace of the normalized PSD operator is

```
T=sum_i G[i,i]/norm_i
 =4u+3v+5-v(u-1)/u^2+u/v+(2u-1)/u^2.                  (9)
```

Every eigenvalue is at most T. On the constant subspace perpendicular
to 1, it is enough that N-1>T. Its cleared numerator is

```
u^2*v*(N-1-T)
 =(u^2+1)(u-1)v^2-u^2(2u+4)v-u^3-(2u-1)v.             (10)
```

Four positive-coefficient regimes cover all but ten parameter pairs:

| Regime | Substitution | Terms | Constant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| u>=5, v>=u | u=5+U, v=u+V | 15 | 680 |
| u=2, v>=8 | v=8+U | 3 | 32 |
| u=3, v>=6 | v=6+U | 3 | 123 |
| u=4, v>=5 | v=5+U | 3 | 216 |

Here U,V>=0 and every coefficient in each complete list is positive.
The remaining pairs are (2,2),...,(2,7),(3,3),...,(3,5),(4,4).
For these ten pairs, compute the constant Gram matrix

```
H[i,j]=(N-1)*norm_i*delta[i,j]-norm_i*norm_j-G[i,j].     (11)
```

The fixture retains all six exact rational LDL pivots for every pair.
In the displayed constant order the first five pivots are strictly
positive, the sixth is zero, and its remaining row is zero. Thus H is
PSD of rank five. Centering supplies its one-dimensional kernel, the
coefficient vector (1,1,1,1,1,1), representing the total vector. This
proves (8) in all ten exceptions. The thresholds and normalization in
(9)-(11) prove completeness of the finite exception list.

In total the certificate has 721 positive coefficient terms and ten

## 5. Nonconstant partition repair and the empty lift

Label all h+1 coordinates by 0,...,h, with center h. Color a singleton
{i} by 2i modulo s, and an edge {i,j} by i+j modulo s. Edges incident
to the same coordinate have distinct colors, and none shares the
singleton's color at that coordinate. This is a proper partition of
the intersection graph into s classes. The center star hits every
class exactly once, because its colors are 2h and h+i for 0<=i<h.

Let q_c be the nonempty class sizes. If they are all equal, recolor the
singleton of the first left leaf to the smallest color unused at that
leaf. Its star has v+2<s members, so such a color exists. Properness is
preserved, no center-star member changes color, and two class sizes
change by -1,+1. Thus the deterministic partition used below always has
nonconstant sizes. This argument also covers a balanced initial
partition; it does not assume the cyclic class sizes are nonconstant.

On the nonempty members define

```
C_P[A,B]=s*1[color(A)=color(B)]-1.
```

Writing Z for the class-incidence matrix gives
C_P=Z(sI_s-J_s)Z^T>=0. It has the prescribed diagonal and intersecting
entries, kills v_S, and
1^T C_P 1=s*sum_c q_c^2-m^2>0. Set

```
beta=max(0,s*max_c(q_c)-N),
epsilon=1/[2(beta+1)],
C_hat=(1-epsilon)C_0+epsilon*C_P.                       (12)
```

Both mixing coefficients are positive. Intersecting the two PSD kernels
using (7) and the positive total functional gives
ker C_hat=span{v_S}, rank C_hat=N-2.
Also N I-J-C_P=N I-s*ZZ^T>=-beta I. Equation (8) then gives

```
N I_m-J_m-C_hat >= [1-epsilon*(beta+1)]I_m=I_m/2.        (13)
```

With R=[-1^T;I_m], form the established empty lift

```
L=J_N+R C_hat R^T,       M=(L-sI_N)/(N-s).              (14)
```

Since R^T1_N=0, L1=N1 and M1=1. At nonempty intersecting
indices its entries give M=0, including the required zero nonempty
diagonal. L>=0 follows from C_hat>=0. The identity
N I_N-L=R(N I_m-J_m-C_hat)R^T and (13) prove M<=I and
rank(I-M)=N-1. Orthogonality of the two summands in L gives
rank L=1+rank C_hat=N-1.

For v<=u^2 all off-diagonal core entries in (2)-(3) are at least -1:
the only potentially smaller type is b_L=-v/u^2. The partition core
also has off-diagonal entries at least -1. Thus all nonempty
off-diagonal entries of M are nonnegative in this regime. At an empty
off-diagonal entry,

```
L[empty,A]=1-epsilon*(s*q_color(A)-m)>=1/2,
```

so these entries are positive for every parameter pair. No sign condition
on the empty diagonal is claimed, nor an off-diagonal sign assertion for
arbitrary tensors.

For any intersecting family with indicator x and size t, the support and
row-sum identities give

```
(x-(t/N)1)^T L(x-(t/N)1)=t(s-t).
```

PSD forces t<=s. For any real H matrix, not necessarily capped, the centered largest-star
vector f_S=v_S-(s/N)1 has zero quadratic form in its PSD matrix L:
the support condition gives v_S^T M v_S=0 and the row sum gives
L1=N1. Hence Lf_S=0 and rank L<=N-1. Our rank is therefore
unrestricted maximal. The same equality calculation for an intersecting
family of size s puts its centered indicator in span{f_S}. Its empty
coordinate fixes the scalar to one, so it equals S. This also proves
the stated uniqueness without any enumeration premise.

## 6. Products and equality families

For each base factor the lower eigenvalue -rho_j is simple, the upper
eigenvalue 1 is simple with vector 1, and 0<rho_j<1. A tensor product
has eigenvalues that are products of factor eigenvalues. Every negative
product has magnitude at most max_j rho_j. Equality requires exactly
one lower-endpoint factor with rho_j=max rho and all other factors at
1: additional nonunit factors strictly decrease the magnitude. There
are exactly c such directions. The product has the required zero
support and row sums, and its star fraction is p=max_j s_j/N_j;
the identity rho=p/(1-p) gives H and rank L_P=N_P-c.

The c centered eligible coordinate-star vectors are independent: their
empty coordinate and their individual center-singleton coordinates
separate the coefficients. Every H matrix on the product must kill all
of them by the equality argument in Section 5. Thus no real H matrix
can have greater rank. If I is a maximum family, express its centered
indicator as their linear combination. The empty coordinate gives
sum_j alpha_j=1. Evaluating each eligible center singleton gives
alpha_j in {0,1}. Exactly one alpha is one, so I is that coordinate
star. This is the full classification claimed in Section 1.

## 7. Mixed D_* products and the fractional lower bound

This corollary depends on the companion [exact capped D_* source](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_six_exact/CAP_THEOREM.md)
and [regular-six equality proof](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_six_exact/REGULAR_SIX_PROOF.md),
graph `bafkreic72cyah66xcs77hgrzp3qigwyjpk6ldfkrxcy4iqv2wopwnqme54`.
The fractional dual is the earlier [six-point witness](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_six_exact/THEOREM.md),
graph `bafkreifi3266vp45bb5t4snhknbmsq4swce6sovjv3qzxthofv76sgnary`.
The [independent regular-six audit](https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/blob/main/spectral_downset_regular_six_review5/REVIEW.md),
graph `bafkreiconcodpfzi5h4q72dqvnwxkzb7x65mtiujqlqynbbtudkcthcz3q`,
reviews that input; it is not a review of our bipartite formula.

D_* has N_*=32,s_*=11, six maximum coordinate stars, a capped matrix
with a six-dimensional lower eigenspace and simple eigenvalue 1, and
a nonnegative fractional-stable-set dual of total 35/3. Its weights
are zero at empty/singletons, 1/3 at pairs, and 2/3 at triples.

Take k>=1 copies of D_* and any number of W(u,v) factors. In any
pairwise disjoint collection of product members, the nonempty projections
to one D_* factor are pairwise disjoint and cannot repeat. Repeated
empty projections have weight zero. Assigning the D_* dual weight to
each product member's projection therefore gives a feasible product
dual, of total (35/96)N_P. This lower-bounds fractional independence;
no exact optimum is asserted.
For every bipartite cone uv>=u+v and h>=4, so

```
s/N <=(h+1)/(3h+2)<=5/14.
```

The same upper bound holds for D_*, since 11/32<5/14. Thus the proved
fractional lower bound is at least 49/48 times the largest star in
every such product.

There is no density tie between a cone and D_*, because

```
11N-32s=11uv-10(u+v+1)
```

is negative at (2,2) and positive at every other normalized pair:
for u=2 it increases strictly from v=3, and for u>=3 its minimum
is attained at u=v=3 and remains positive. If q>=1 factors are
W(2,2), precisely their q centers are eligible, rank L_P=N_P-q,
and there are exactly q maximum families. If there is no W(2,2),
the D_* factors are eligible, rank L_P=N_P-6k, and exactly their
6k coordinate stars are maximum. The same empty/singleton coefficient
argument applies using the six distinct D_* coordinates. In the
latter case the fractional gap is at least 35/33 times the star.
