# Sources and dependencies

- The [earlier universal ungauged theorem](../quotient_local_consistency71/PROOF.md),
  Discovery Net `bafkreibjidkmbpezh5bg2x4wsuvpyizcbhibd3fejy27ntmvtc33p62pxq`,
  height 6022, source commit `4b38aca1bc11a4093b1b42ed451ed4525238bee0`,
  identified the unresolved gauge-conditioned relaxation. The present
  construction closes that gap and recovers its uniform five-height
  laws by averaging the five omitted heights. The proof is self-contained.
  Coordinate conventions and the small planar-cap source are reused.
- The original small `planar_cap.cpp` came from the team's
  [point-marginal obstruction](../../additive_combinatorics/line_free_planar_lp_obstruction/PROOF.md),
  artifact `bafkreifwjecau65opqj3hmj5ba2d4dlig2advvmkjtkx5xztwpovu24o4y`.
  Its all-155-plane model and this 30-projection-plane model are different.
  No containment is asserted. The complete cap replay is repeated here.
- The [low-pair cover](../low_pair71/README.md), artifact
  `bafkreidy3j3g3qeasqbqxpnzayye526ojhqmes2mvj57glffihpdlf2m3i`, height 5996,
  explains the 71-weight quotients and guarantees a noncollinear
  three-hole gauge for its cover. It is context only: the new theorem
  applies to every admissible quotient independently of a global reduction.
- The [complete decision family](../decision71/README.md), source commit
  `6d2f193aecad54cc41dab4cdf67c16f9add58cc2`, covers 109,676 affine quotient
  classes. Its committed graph artifact is
  `bafkreifrsrzf2igkvt5o3yfe6uvliewuha5glpyi5kfmi22kvumk2enitu`, height
  6046. Its lifting proof checks were still in progress at the final
  prepublication refresh (height 6049). No unchecked verdict is used here.
- The [nonzero quadratic moment theorem](../nonzero_quadratic_moment71/README.md),
  artifact `bafkreidhyh6lrr35v2fuf5noljtusx2cspsoxvhhxhlw3rafxqjrxryxxa`,
  height 6024, and the [affine-asymmetry theorem](../affine_asymmetry71/README.md),
  artifact `bafkreig2nafhceewifcqyikc3zs7inbaxsqpctjveyknt76pkbasmaxiue`,
  height 6004, were inspected as complementary Team A work. Neither is
  a premise. Distributional averaging never assumes a candidate symmetry.
- The independently accepted [upper bound 71](../upper_bound71/README.md)
  is the current numerical context. It is not a premise: the global
  obstruction here uses only the rechecked planar cap and an empty plane.
- Elsholtz et al., *Maximal line-free sets in F_p^n*,
  [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03382),
  Periodica Mathematica Hungarica 90 (2025), 7–21, supplies the original
  problem context and known 70-point construction. Neither its construction
  nor a classification from the paper is needed for the new theorem.

Private LP and integer-feasibility experiments suggested the construction.
Their numeric answers and catalogues are not proof inputs. The final
argument needs only the fourteen explicit sets, elementary group actions,
and exact checks. A targeted primary-source search found no matching
universal gauged-marginal statement; no historical-priority claim is made.
