counts. The standard-library verifier checks integer products, modular
coprimality using a separate Euclidean algorithm, exact rational Sturm
counts and every relevant actual edge. Shared routines in `exact.py` handle
basic exact operations and canonical hashing; the main producer/checker
norm expansions and their CAS/standard-library algorithms differ. The
physical fixture additionally has a separate distance formula and exact
degree-seven irreducibility test. No floating-point estimates or solver
verdicts enter the theorem.

independent implementation checks, but no independent reviewer-1 verdict
