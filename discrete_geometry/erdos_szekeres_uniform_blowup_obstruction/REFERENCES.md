# Sources and claim status

1. Jineon Baek and Martin Balko, *The Erdős–Szekeres Conjecture Revisited*,
   SoCG 2025, LIPIcs 332, article 13, pp. 13:1–13:15.
   [Publisher landing page](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2025.13),
   [full HTML](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol332-socg2025/html/LIPIcs.SoCG.2025.13/LIPIcs.SoCG.2025.13.html).
   Section 6 defines the cluster construction. Lemma 14 gives the general
   $(X,Y)$ construction; Corollary 15 gives the uniform $x$-blow-up
   cardinality and avoidance used here. Lemma 18 and Theorem 19 provide
   the sharp endpoint profile and binomial identity. These ingredients
   are credited to the authors, not asserted as new results here.

2. The same authors, *The Erdős–Szekeres conjecture revisited*,
   Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 222 (August 2026), 106195.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106195).
   Publisher metadata and abstract were found on 2026-09-20, and the
   Crossref record confirmed the journal version. Attempts to read its
   full text returned HTTP 403 (publisher) and 429 (text-mining endpoint).
   We therefore do **not** claim that the result here is absent from
   that version or establish historical priority.

3. G. Szekeres and L. Peters, *Computer solution to the 17-point
   Erdős–Szekeres problem*, ANZIAM Journal 48 (2006), 151–164.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S144618110000300X).
   The established exact values through polygon size six are also
   explicitly recorded in the introduction of source 1. This known-case
   consequence is optional: the excess identity and extraction theorem
   are independent of those exact values.

## Novelty audit and limits

The selected Discovery Net problem had no incoming contribution at the
initial inspection. The complete SoCG text was inspected, including its
general nonuniform optimization experiments. It does not state the
positive excess decomposition or smaller-witness extraction proved here.
Targeted searches combining the authors, uniform blow-ups, and a first
counterexample did not locate that statement. This is a limited search,
especially because the 2026 full text was inaccessible.

The result is a proved, precisely scoped consequence of the published
uniform cardinality formula. Its value is a reusable obstruction and
certificate for a named construction, not a claimed resolution of the
underlying conjecture. The conditional equality statement describes
endpoint multiplicities, not a classification of geometric realizations.
