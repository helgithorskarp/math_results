# Source audit and novelty boundary

Primary literature was checked live on 2026-09-21, after selection from
the Discovery Net Nagata degree-multiplicity neighborhood.

1. Kathlén Kohn and Kristian Ranestad, **Projective Geometry of
   Wachspress Coordinates**, *Foundations of Computational Mathematics*
   20 (2020), 1135–1173.
   [Published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10208-019-09441-z),
   [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.02123).
   Theorem 1 / Example 3 supplies the classical unique polygon adjoint;
   Theorem 7 gives the next graded-space dimension. Lemma 6 and the proof
   of Proposition 5 already force the line arrangement at multiplicity
   two and degree equal to the number of sides when there are more than
   six sides. We explicitly credit these results. Bezout forcing is a
   known method, not an innovation of this note. The published full text
   was inspected, including its fixed-component argument.

2. Joaquim Roé and Paola Supino, **Nagata type statements** (2017),
   [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.00583).
   Context for degree-multiplicity questions, Waldschmidt constants and
   special versus very general point configurations. No theorem from
   this survey is needed for our proof.

3. Carlos Galindo, Francisco Monserrat, Carlos-Jesús Moreno-Ávila and
   Julio-Jose Moyano-Fernández, **On the valuative Nagata conjecture**,
   *Research in the Mathematical Sciences* 12, article 18 (2025).
   [Published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40687-025-00500-2).
   Its introduction states the classical conjecture and distinguishes
   the known square case from the unresolved nonsquare cases. Current
   searches found no resolution changing that status. Our work does
   not settle any nonsquare very-general-points case.

Bounded searches combined “Wachspress”, “symbolic powers”, “Waldschmidt”,
“adjoint”, “initial degree”, “residual arrangement”, “fat points”, and
graph/line-configuration terms. We found the first-order and degree-`n`
double-point results above, but no identical sharp all-multiplicity
band statement or its disjoint-cycle minimum-space description.

The claimed increment is exactly: the full vector-space identities at
every multiplicity up to excess degree `n-7`; explicit failure at excess
`n-6`; and a component-adjoint basis, of dimension the number of missing
cycles, for the odd minimum spaces. It is an elementary extension of
known geometric mechanisms. Apparent novelty is relative to the sources
searched; no exhaustive historical-priority claim is made.

The line-product upper bound and the consequent failure of these special
configurations to attain a Nagata threshold are immediate and are not
presented as a breakthrough or as new evidence against Nagata's conjecture.
Ordinary-power containments, finite generation of the symbolic Rees
algebra, the complete higher-degree Hilbert functions, and arrangements
with triple concurrency are outside the claim.
