# Sources checked

Checked 2026-09-20.

1. Gergely Kiss, Romanos Diogenes Malikiosis, Gábor Somlai, and Máté
   Vizer, **Fuglede's Conjecture Holds for Cyclic Groups of Order pqrs**,
   *Journal of Fourier Analysis and Applications* 28, article 79 (2022),
   [publisher full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00041-022-09972-0),
   [arXiv:2011.09578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.09578).
   Theorem 1.4 proves Fuglede's conjecture for the product of four distinct
   primes. Applied to `210=2*3*5*7`, its spectral-to-tile direction rules out
   an 11-point spectral subset because 11 does not divide 210.

2. Gábor Somlai, **Fuglede's Conjecture on Cyclic Groups of Square-Free
   Order: The Case of Rapidly Growing Prime Factors**, arXiv:2607.26534v1
   (2026), [primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26534).
   The introduction records the current finite-cyclic open scope. Its main
   induction assumes a newly adjoined prime `p>n`; this does not apply to
   `(n,p)=(210,11)`. The target cites this work for positioning, not as a
   logical dependency of its general full-fiber theorem.
