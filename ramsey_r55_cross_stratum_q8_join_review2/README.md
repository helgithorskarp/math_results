## Verdict

**ACCEPT within the stated reduction-and-interface scope, with high
confidence.** The claim is a complete same-graph reduction, not an UNSAT
result: 2,188,168 exact original tasks route into the four unrestricted q8
bases, while 1,010 exact original parents remain. The 239 guards partition
all (2^{55}) eleven-core assignments, so 956 q8 units plus those parents
give 1,966 obligations. No new original task, good43, or Ramsey bound is
established.

The q>=8 proof is direct: retaining every red block preserves the whole
red-K4-free residual, while surplus blue blocks may enter the unrestricted
q8 core. For q7,r5/r6, the imported and proof-checked 19-vertex theorem
supplies two disjoint blue K4s in one blue block plus the old core. Root
column and equal-color block sorting are vertex relabelings. The remaining
q7r7, q9r9, q10r9, and q10r10 ranges are exactly the cases where this
fixed-r construction has no eighth block.

The receiver is appropriately conservative: it accepts only an exact
whole-cohort RUP object or byte-exact full-input DRAT proof, requires every
one of 239 cohorts before promoting an r-family, reruns the 640 local proofs
before admitting q7r5/q7r6, and leaves incomplete or extra-cube evidence
UNKNOWN. The r8 negative edge branch is a mixed-premise consequence of the
published (R(5,4)=25) theorem and is kept separate from propositional
proof checking. The historically defective order-5 automorphism certificate
is not a premise.

## Trust boundary and limitations

The verdict imports the already reviewed maximal-packing carrier and q8 base
semantics, completeness of McKay's Ramsey(4,4) catalogs, the 640 checked
local DRAT certificates, DRAT-trim, and (R(4,5)=25). I checked membership,
literal identities, hashes, routing arithmetic, the full guard partition,
the signed-branch argument, and receiver admission logic; I did not
independently regenerate McKay's catalogs or formalize the reductions in a
proof assistant. The saved local proofs are not published because of their
size, but all hashes and regeneration commands are public.

Literature checking confirms that [R(4,5)=25 is classical and now also has
an independent HOL4 formal proof](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2024.16).
The [McKay catalogue page](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/ramsey.html)
reports exactly 640, 546,356, 362, and 4 Ramsey(4,4) graphs at orders 15,
11, 7, and 3, respectively. The cross-stratum reuse of the broad q8 bases
appears graph-level new; no priority claim is justified from a targeted
search. This is rigorous and useful campaign infrastructure, but not by
itself a publishable improvement to the numerical bound on R(5,5), whose
[published upper bound remains 46](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.70029).

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

1. **Highest impact, feasible:** publish a small formal statement of the
   fixed-r transfer (including the unrestricted-core hypothesis) in a proof
   assistant. This would isolate the only non-enumerative bridge from the
   large Python/CNF trust base.
2. **High impact, feasible:** commit a compact signed-map Merkle manifest for
   all 640 q7 pullbacks. Reviewers could then bind every local source-core
   map to the public summary without retaining the 393 KB scratch receipt.
3. **Required for a Ramsey advance:** close an entire 239-cohort r-family or
   one of the 1,010 retained original parents with admitted proof evidence.
   Individual physical children and UNKNOWN solver runs cannot strengthen
   the theorem.
4. **Conjectural:** a color-symmetric augmentation theorem for q7r7 or a
   red-block compression for q9r9 could shrink the 1,010-parent complement.
   Such a claim needs a new exhaustive reduction/certificate; the present
   local 19-vertex theorem does not imply it.
