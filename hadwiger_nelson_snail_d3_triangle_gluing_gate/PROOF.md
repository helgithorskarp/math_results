# Certificate proof and exact scope

## 1. Frozen physical family

The parent Snail package defines 812 labelled supports `S(c,a)`, one for each
ordered pair of distinct vertices of the 29-point seed.  Each is the union of
the direct and reflected seed across the axis through `c,a`, together with
their rotations through 0, 120 and 240 degrees about `c`.  The independently
accepted parent certificate labels 655 of these `D3` supports four-chromatic.

The present source is `S(9,3)`.  This choice was frozen because its parent
certificate carries a 113-point non-three-colour witness, tied for the largest
stored witness.  The marked triangle is the parent certificate's
`seed_triangle` in the first direct embedded copy.  Each second support uses
the identically defined marked triangle.

A bijection between two equilateral unit triangles extends to exactly one
Euclidean isometry.  Three bijections preserve orientation and three reverse
it.  The producer and checker enumerate `itertools.permutations(range(3))` in
canonical order.  Therefore the 655 by 6 rows are exactly the declared
marked-triangle gluing family; no angle grid or numerical placement occurs.

## 2. Exact normalization and edge reconstruction

Both implementations work in the degree-16 field generated by

```text
a^2=-3, b^2=-11, c^2=5, e^2=-3320+632*a*b,
```

with `a,b,e` imaginary and `c` real.  The parent proof establishes the basis
independence and exact Snail coordinates.  The producer changes to
`w=(1+a)/2`; the checker retains the monomial `a` basis.

For an ordered source triangle `(p0,p1,p2)`, normalize every point `z` by

```text
(z-p0)/(p1-p0).
```

The denominator is inverted by exact 16-dimensional rational Gaussian
elimination and multiplied back to one as a control.  The third vertex is one
of the two equilateral completions.  If it differs from the fixed completion,
complex conjugation gives the unique reflected isometry.  The checker verifies
all three triangle images exactly.

The 174 formal addresses on each side are then concatenated.  Equal field
elements are one physical point.  For every unordered pair of distinct
physical points, the checker first evaluates the squared norm under a verified
homomorphism to the finite ring modulo 1,000,001,539.  A residue different from
one proves the exact norm is not one.  Every residue survivor is evaluated in
the full rational tower.  Hence the resulting edge set is the complete strict
unit-distance graph after collision merging.

An edge is counted as a new cross edge precisely when its two endpoints are
not both in the fixed support and not both in the moved support.  This avoids
double counting inherited edges and remains correct when more than the three
marked points collide.

## 3. Positive colour certificate

Each row stores two bits for each of the 348 formal addresses.  The checker
first requires all addresses representing the same physical point to carry
the same colour.  It then requires different colours on the endpoints of every
reconstructed unit edge.  Restricting the address word to physical points is
therefore a proper four-colouring of the complete strict graph.

This is a positive certificate theorem.  CaDiCaL generated the words but no
SAT, UNSAT or UNKNOWN verdict is trusted by the checker.  The producer itself
also reconstructs every edge and checks each decoded model before packing it.

## 4. Consequence and limitations

All 3,930 declared supports are four-colourable, including every one with at
most 508 vertices (in fact all have at most 319).  Consequently none is a
five-chromatic record candidate and no subset of one can be five-chromatic.

The conclusion is deliberately restricted to the fixed first support, its
fixed marked triangle, the fixed marked triangle in each second support, and
the six pointwise triangle bijections.  Other triangles or other interaction
architectures are not covered.  The result supplies no global vertex lower
bound.  Exact arithmetic, the pinned parent coordinates, Python's rational
arithmetic, the finite loops and ordinary hardware are the trust boundary;
the proof is not formally mechanized.
