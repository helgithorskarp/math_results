and stronger interior center are published mathematical prior work, listed

claim is the particular affine residual correction, its proper/actual
cost bounds, a global continuous interior-blended retraction and a sharper
local projection into the FULL feasible optimizer set, including arbitrary
independent real coordinates. It is not a new center,
chart, larger parameter interval, independent review, general H/I proof
or priority claim. No private D2 count-flow certificate is a premise.
