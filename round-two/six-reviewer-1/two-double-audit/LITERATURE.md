# Primary literature and priority boundary

six-reviewer-1 / independent mathematical reviewer. Candidate-specific live checks:2026-10-04.

[Cheung and Ng, Relationship between the zeros of two polynomials](https://www.math.hku.hk/imrwww/IMRPreprintSeries/2009/IMR2009-11.pdf), Theorem1.2 and the ordinary-derivative specialization, supplies classical companion/differentiator context. This review proves the needed orthogonal compression identity directly, including repeated originals. Newton identities and derivative companions are classical.

[Brüdern and Robert, A paucity estimate related to Newton sums of odd degree](https://perso.univ-st-etienne.fr/rool6510/OneThreeFive.pdf), Mathematika58(2012),225–235, discusses equal quartet odd sums of degrees1,3,5. Its introduction/reference3 credits Choudhry's four-parameter construction, Bull.Calcutta Math.Soc.83(1991),85–86. The original Choudhry paper was not obtained. Neither an integer paucity theorem nor that bibliographic lead proves this real spectral angular inequality.

[Melánová, Sturmfels and Winter, Recovery from Power Sums](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.13981) treats complex, real and positive power-sum fibers. The present collision and positivity stratum requires a separate actual-domain proof.

[Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang–Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126), Conjecture1.2, identifies the first-power endpoint as the strongest remaining assertion of that inverse-distance family; its proved quadratic case is distinct. [Tao's digestion of the ordinary Sendov proof](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/) also separates the ordinary conjecture from stronger inverse-distance questions. This real angular audit does not solve the complex first-power endpoint.

Bounded searches used same-sign two-double/angular/Sendov terminology, the sharp16 constant and the companion mechanism. No matching externally verified theorem was located. This does not establish historical priority. LEMMA10235 already supplied the committed classification and bound; this review adds independent full-parameter evidence and the explicit distance refinements. A paper should compare the exact angular statistic and multiplicity classification with earlier odd-sum results.
