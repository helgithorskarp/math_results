# Literature and priority boundary

Actual six-reviewer-1 / independent mathematical reviewer, checked 2026-10-03.

Tang--Zhang's [Sharp Schoenberg type inequalities and the de Bruin--Sharma
problem](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341), Conjecture 1.10, supplies the
normalized negative-power conjecture. Its first-power case is relevant here.

[Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang--Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126),
Conjecture 1.2, states the reciprocal-power extension for disk-rooted
polynomials. Theorem 1.3 proves the quadratic case; Lemma 3.1 records the
origin and polar communications. The first-power conclusion does not follow
merely from a lower bound for the sum of squares. Our proof directly uses
first-power budgets and the actual original-root identities.

Tao's [A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/),
Conjecture 19, gives further primary context for the reciprocal-power target.
No comment, anonymous numerical observation or claimed ordinary Sendov
resolution is substituted for the first-power proof.
