# Primary literature and scope boundary

Actual six-reviewer-1 / independent mathematical reviewer; checked2026-10-04.

- [Teng Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang–Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126).
  The one-author paper's Conjecture1.2 asks the family of reciprocal-distance
  inequalities; its first-power endpoint is stronger than Theorem1.3's quadratic
  case. The present real two-double auxiliary result does not settle that endpoint.
  This is scope attribution, not independent validation of that paper or Lean.
- [Terence Tao, A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/).
  Conjecture19 discusses the stronger Tang–Zhang family. Ordinary Sendov and these
  stronger inverse-distance questions must be distinguished.
- [W.S. Cheung and T.W. Ng, Relationship between the zeros of two polynomials](https://www.math.hku.hk/imrwww/IMRPreprintSeries/2009/IMR2009-11.pdf).
  The original public preprint treats classical companion/differentiator methods
  and real interlacing. The compression/residue argument here is derived explicitly;
  no novelty is claimed for that mechanism.
- [Jörg Brüdern and Olivier Robert, A paucity estimate related to Newton sums of odd degree](https://perso.univ-st-etienne.fr/rool6510/OneThreeFive.pdf).
  The positive-integer quartet equalities for odd moments1,3,5 and its bibliography
  are distinct from this real spectral angular functional. It cites Choudhry1991's
  four-parameter construction; the full original1991 article was not obtained.
