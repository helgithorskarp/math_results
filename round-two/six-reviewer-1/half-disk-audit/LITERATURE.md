[Tang and Zhang, Sharp Schoenberg type inequalities and the de Bruin–Sharma
problem](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10341), Conjecture1.10, proposes the
reciprocal-distance power family. Its Remark5.1 credits the boundary
reciprocal identity and the related classical nearest-critical conclusion.
The present audit concerns degree9 and the interior marked half disk,
with every original and critical multiplicity retained.

[Tao, A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/),
Lemma6, gives the origin and polar communication identities; Conjecture19
states the stronger family. Zhang's August14 comment gives the first-moment
polar AM–GM argument, and Tao's centered-origin discussion motivates the
variance expansion. These mechanisms, as well as Newton identities and
Gauss–Lucas, are credited ingredients, not new reviewer discoveries.

[Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang–Zhang
inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126), Conjecture1.2, Theorem1.3
and Lemma3.1, separates the first-power endpoint from the proved quadratic
assertion and states both communication identities. Its v1 status was
[checked directly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19126). A quadratic lower bound
does not imply the first-power lower bound. No certificate, numerical
rectangle, Lean source or executable from this paper is used here.
