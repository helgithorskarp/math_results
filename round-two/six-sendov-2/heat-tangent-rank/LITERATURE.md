## Classical structure, not claimed new

[Tao, Heat flow and zeroes of polynomials, 2017](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2017/10/17/heat-flow-and-zeroes-of-polynomials/)
gives polynomial heat flow and simple-zero differential equations.
This proof needs only local coefficient feasibility at distinct
original roots, not any global heat-preservation assertion.

[Stefan Steinerberger, Electrostatic Interpretation of Zeros of Orthogonal Polynomials, 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09697),
Theorem1, discusses the equivalence between quadratic-coefficient
second-order polynomial ODEs and electrostatic zero relations,
crediting Stieltjes and subsequent literature. The Hermite example is
also classical. Here the electrostatic identity is rederived directly;
no positive-weight orthogonality classification or dynamical convergence
theorem is invoked. Newton identities, Hankel Gram positivity, Schur
complements, Cauchy equality and coefficient bases are classical.

Targeted primary searches for quadratic polynomial ODEs, electrostatic
critical zeros, heat-tangent dependence and angular stationary profiles
did not identify this specific small-quartic-variance octic obstruction.
This is a limited comparison, not a historical-priority assertion.

moment determinant, uniform sign certificate on d<=69/5000, and
absence of a rank-drop exceptional stratum there.
