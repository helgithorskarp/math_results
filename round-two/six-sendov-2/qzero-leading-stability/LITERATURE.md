The new assertion beyond the inherited regularity is an unconditional q0 leading-vector
module unit, with explicit bounded-q stability and all-E secant bounds.
It was not asserted by9743, which proves nonvanishing only at full common
zeros with x*u!=0. Existing independent reviews cover their exact input
scopes and do not give a verdict on this new certificate.

The constant-pivot row identities, rational coefficient systems,
polynomial units and Hermitian norm arguments are classical mechanisms.
The contribution claims the explicit certificate and its stated scope,
not novelty of those general tools. Complex first power, collision limits,
full Jacobian rank and all-profile classification remain outside the proof.
