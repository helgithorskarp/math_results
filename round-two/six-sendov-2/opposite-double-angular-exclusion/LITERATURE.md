[Teng Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang--Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126),
Conjecture1.2, distinguishes the strongest first-power endpoint from its
proved quadratic case and lambda>=2 corollary. The paper has one author.
[Tao's primary digestion](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
also separates stronger inverse-distance questions from ordinary Sendov.
The present real angular collision result does not settle the full complex endpoint.

The mandated ordinary seeds
[degree-nine2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235) and
[Zhang2609.20256](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256) are historical/contextual.
The latter's current title states an explicit very-large-degree threshold;
it is not by itself evidence of the full ordinary resolution. The current
quadratic paper and Tao exposition report Mazur's ordinary proof. No
ordinary theorem is restated as this contribution's new result.

[Cheung and Ng, Relationship between the zeros of two polynomials](https://www.math.hku.hk/imrwww/IMRPreprintSeries/2009/IMR2009-11.pdf)
provides classical companion/differentiator context. Newton identities,
real derivative interlacing and Bernstein positivity are classical.
The needed compression/Schur and specialization arguments are given
explicitly in PROOF.md; no new claim of those classical mechanisms.

[Brudern and Robert, A paucity estimate related to Newton sums of odd degree](https://perso.univ-st-etienne.fr/rool6510/OneThreeFive.pdf),
Mathematika58(2012),225--235, discusses equal quartet odd sums1,3,5.
Its bibliography credits Choudhry's four-parameter construction,
Bull.Calcutta Math.Soc.83(1991),85--86. The original1991 full paper was
not obtained. Neither an integer paucity theorem nor that bibliography
proves the present real spectral angular inequality.
[Melanova, Sturmfels and Winter, Recovery from Power Sums](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.13981)
treats different power-sum recovery scopes; no injectivity or positivity
license is borrowed here.
