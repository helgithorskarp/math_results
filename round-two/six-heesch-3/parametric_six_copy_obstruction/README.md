# Six copies prevent two later surrounds

Actual author **six-heesch-3**, role **researcher**.
Author-checked exact computer-assisted local lemma, independently
unreviewed and unformalized; no historical priority claim.

The [proof](PROOF.md) gives five fixed T7 copies and either of two sixth
blockers whose horizontal position ranges over explicit real intervals:
R(L),16<L<120 except64/72, and F(X),-40<X<64 except16. A packing containing
the six copies cannot acquire two later complete strict surrounds under
arbitrary motions. Any common Euclidean isometry and additional initial
copies are permitted; the configuration may be disconnected. The
coincidence exceptions prevent rejecting an existing copy by self-overlap.

The real windows follow from exact unions of49 positive hexagon-overlap
intervals for each of three residual suppliers, with no real sampling.
Three first-stage unit forces retain18/18/48 motions, including50 odd
30-degree rotations in Q(sqrt(3)). Eleven of14 final suppliers are ruled
out by the second-stage license of the uniform two-copy theorem; the
remaining three collide with the sixth blocker. No prescribed-mate or
global-registration hypothesis is used.

Seven supplied disc fourths contain this motif. Six have74 copies and
one has81. Each has no two further surrounds under arbitrary motions.
The checker also preserves the actual129-copy T7 fifth and the known
169-copy T6 sixth. The target of a planar shape with rigorously finite
Heesch number at least seven remains open: these are fixed-prefix and
local-configuration upper obstructions, with no global T7 upper.

## Reproduce

Use CPython3.12.14, standard library only. From this directory run each
command separately, waiting for the previous process to exit:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 check.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -O check.py
```

Complete evidence must agree with stable SHA256
`30ff1464687d4d48a6b4a79a49375ae7c7df24757197ffe8ce5dde2e2a65bfdf`.
Expected output: seven literal fourth applications,13 damaged certificates
rejected, positive corona depths[6,5]. `--output PATH` writes the complete
evidence for entrywise comparison; `--build` regenerates the certificate
and compact expected hash. All arithmetic used for exclusions is exact.

`check.py` reconstructs local tangent sectors, every pinned supplier,
every blocker and pair image, all real interval unions and seven literal
applications. Damaged controls include omitted odd motions, a forged
local sector, a180-degree gap, a missing pair exclusion, forged hexagon
bands, unlicensed final-layer pruning and both same-copy exception lists.
The positive control's uncovered final fifth contains a forbidden pair
and remains admissible. Nesting retains every actual copy at the second
stage; the pair rule requires that second stage.
