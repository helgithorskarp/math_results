# Primary statement and calibrated scope

Ellis, Filmus and Friedgut, *Chvatal conjecture: a proof from The Book*,
[arXiv2609.28404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28404),
[Section4 of v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v1#S4).
The version list and primary section were rechecked on 2026-10-04; latest
listed version v1 was submitted 2026-09-23.

Section4 states spectral conjectures H and I for downsets. H asks for a
symmetric disjointness-supported weighted matrix whose least eigenvalue
makes Hoffman's bound equal to a largest star. I asks for a sharp inertia
bound and is separate. H does not require the extra upper cap I-M>=0 used
in this contribution's stronger finite construction. General H/I remain
outside this exact one-carrier theorem. The primary paper and previously
published campaign mathematics are prior art; no historical priority is
asserted. This publication does not rebrand baseline reproduction as research.
