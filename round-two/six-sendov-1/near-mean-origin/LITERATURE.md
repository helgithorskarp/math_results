# Literature, scope and dependencies

Actual author **six-sendov-1**, role **researcher**. Refreshed 2026-10-01.

The assigned family is the degree-nine complex first-power Tang--Zhang
inequality. Ordinary Sendov is reported solved in the current primary
literature. It is not the open target of this contribution.

## Primary sources

* Teng Zhang, [Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang--Zhang
  inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126), v1 dated 2026-09-16.
  Conjecture1.2 retains the lambda=1 endpoint; Theorem1.3 proves the
  lambda=2 assertion. Lemma3.1 gives the classical origin and polar
  identities. Section5 gives a centered origin interpolation under a
  second-moment bound, not the present first-moment coercive estimate.
* Terence Tao, [A digestion of the proof of Sendov's
  conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/).
  Lemma6 gives the communication identities and Conjecture19 states
  the stronger power-sum family. Centering at the reciprocal mean and
  origin communication are credited methods, not inventions here.

The supplied [effective-high-degree seed](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20256)
and [historical degree-nine seed](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07235) were
checked at the clean launch. They concern ordinary Sendov, not a
resolution of the current first-power target. No historical-priority
or external formalization claim is made.

No previous finite certificate is a logical input to the abstract theorem.
The source reconstructs its nine polynomials and all constants directly
from the defining origin integral. The corollary depends on the general
polar mean lemma and classical origin identity. All advertised deductions
are ordinary author proofs pending independent review.

The concrete novelty is explicit arbitrary-multiplicity origin coercivity
on the stated first-moment mean tube, with separate positive radial and
angular costs. A bounded comparison found no identical statement in the
primary and pertinent published campaign sources inspected. This is not
a comprehensive literature-priority claim. The unrestricted first-power
endpoint and the general origin inequality outside the tube remain open
in this work.
