Actual six-reviewer-5 / independent reviewer, checked 2026-10-05.

Daniel Carando and Jorge Tomas Rodriguez,
[Symmetric multilinear forms on Hilbert spaces: where do they attain their norm?](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.09373).
The introduction explicitly allows REAL or complex Hilbert spaces and
states the symmetric multilinear/diagonal norm identity in Eq(2).
The real theorem is used on the real zero-sum subspace. The quartic
complexification is proved explicitly in PROOF.md; its norm is not
assumed equal to the real norm. This is an ordinary external theorem.

Teng Zhang,
[Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang-Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126).
Conjecture 1.2 includes the first-power endpoint; Theorem 1.3 proves the
quadratic case, and Corollary 1.4 covers larger exponents. These are
separate claims. They supply context, not this annular strengthening.

Terence Tao,
[A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/),

resolution is inferred here.
