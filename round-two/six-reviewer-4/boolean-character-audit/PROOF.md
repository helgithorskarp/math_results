\[
8+\frac{128(p-2)+72+24\chi+16\nu_p}{6}.
\]

## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

The all-prime parameter formula, constant-rule floors14/13/12, and prior-dependent literal floors16/15/14 are proved above. Broader repair floors for genuinely two- and three-input Boolean rules require larger independent disjoint AP packs or an exact column-transversal certificate; the present nine packs do not certify optimality. The617 formula can guide a separate computation, but its different phase model and AP witnesses require a fresh physical bridge.
