## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

**Proved here:** the scale-indexed step50 positive certificates sharpen
the integer endpoints from \(408m+1,409m\) to \(402m+1,403m\), with
the same arbitrary phase rules and original free columns. The proof uses
ordinal-coordinate scale coverage, rather than pretending that a
normalized field pack keeps short steps under every integer lift.

**Further work, not proved:** a larger disjoint pack for every actual
scale and full truth word would increase the repair cut; it requires
new complete positive certificates, not a heuristic packing failure.
Alternatively a literal hitting-set certificate for all monochromatic
APs could yield a stronger edit lower bound than disjoint packing.
Neither an upper bound on packing number nor a five-edit feasible repair
follows from this evidence. The useful constructive next question is
whether any independently phased repair meets these cuts while obeying
the mixed-phase AP constraints. That demands an actual full coloring or
a separately complete impossibility certificate. Noncoprime moduli
require a new field/phase projection analysis because the inverse used
here fails. Formalizing the Boolean absorption, ordinal-coordinate lift
and certificate decoder would reduce the remaining ordinary-proof trust.
