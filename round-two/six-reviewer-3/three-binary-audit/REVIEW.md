## Strengthening and improvement opportunities

**Proved function-transfer corollary.** Let R be any standard thirteen-input
prefix with at least28 comparators, whose complete ordered Boolean output
function equals this literal Q on all8192 inputs. Then no size-at-most44 sorter
extending R can have the same zero-HIGH-singleton/exactly-three-HIGH-binary and
first-LOW-singleton/no-prior-LOW-binary route, with events counted after R.
Thresholding proves R and Q are identical on arbitrary ordered inputs. Replace
R by Q, retaining every later comparator. The total size does not increase.
For every original extreme-mark domain, the actual physical tag configuration
after each later gate is unchanged; hence binary, singleton and preparation
event types and their order are unchanged. Before the first LOW singleton all
other LOW gates are preparations, so they do not charge LOW histories; a
singleton strictly increases positive ordinary weight for either prefix.
The replacement therefore falls in the verified literal-Q exclusion.
**D/R histories inside R are not transferred.** All pruning charges used in the
conclusion are those freshly checked after replacement by Q. This corollary
requires whole ordered function equality and length at least28, not an output
image, a similar prefix or a guessed normal form.

Controls check both an initial disjoint-gate reorder and Q followed by the
whole-function identity `(0,11)`. The latter has length29 and exactly the same
whole Boolean function, but increases the original HIGH-pair D from9 to10.
It therefore explicitly demonstrates why whole-function transfer cannot be
implemented as transfer of an internal deletion history. This is a scoped
ordinary corollary, not a new global sorting-network bound or priority claim.

The next consequential extension is a complete treatment of four/five-binary
freed partners or genuine HIGH singleton separators. It needs a new complete
preparation reduction and actual-domain negatives; the three-port closure is
not a certificate for a four/five-port or singleton-interleaved domain.
Removing the imported10034 theorem from this review would require a fresh audit
of its all-order component argument and its balanced exceptional original cube,
in addition to any imported9982 exclusion used by that argument. Neither is
implied by the present freed-head witness census. Formalizing the general
pruning, event commutation and threshold replacement would reduce the remaining
ordinary-proof trust boundary more than another run of the same negative data.
