pairwise disjoint private pairs \(a_i,b_i\) outside them. Let \(D\)

and \(ya_Lb_L\). Then \(N=6h+10\), \(s=3h+2\); the point stars have

## Original principal deletion and repair

The old+marked rows have exactly the one relation
\[
g_x+g_{xy}+\sum_{i,a}V_{ia}=0.
\]
Deleting x leaves an independent basis of that full space, dimension
\(3h+5\). The W rows have exactly their sum relation; deleting the light
full private row leaves an independent W basis, dimension \(3h+2\).
The remaining original principal Gram \(A_*\), size \(N-3\), is positive
definite. Its projected P components cannot change that independence.

The deleted private row is the linear combination
\[
U_{L3}=-\sum_{(i,a)\ne(L,3)}U_{ia}
-\frac{3(h+1)}\ell(g_y+\sum_aV_{La}).
\]
Write its coefficients as \(z_*\), column \(b_*=A_*z_*\), and
actual diagonal \(w=z_*^TA_*z_*\). The perturbation vector \(r\) is1
at the three first-heavy private rows and0 elsewhere, so \(r^Tz_*=-3\).
Eliminating the complete old+marked positive definite block leaves exactly
the W principal matrix. The inverse energy is
\[
\kappa=r^TA_*^{-1}r=\frac{h-1}{h\nu}+\frac1{\mu_L}+\frac4{\beta_L}>0.
\]
For an explicit derivation, extend its right hand side by -3 on the deleted
light full row. In heavy means, the deviation of the first triple's value1
from its average1/h has squared length \((h-1)/h\), giving the first term.
The total heavy mean gives \(1/\mu_L\). The light triple's values
\((0,0,-3)\) decompose into mean -1 and even contrast \((1,1,-2)\),
giving \(4/\beta_L\); no odd contrast or old-block component remains.

Add \(\delta\) on the three disjoint pairs joining first-heavy private
vertices to the deleted light full vertex. The original principal block
stays fixed; its deleted column becomes \(b_*+\delta r\). Its exact Schur
complement is
\[
6\delta-\kappa\delta^2.
\]
The heavy-star relation survives because it has no private-vertex
coefficient. Reinsert x by that relation. For \(0<\delta<6/\kappa\),
this gives PSD core rank \(N-2\), whole lower rank \(N-1\), and the
unique centered heavy-star lower kernel.

The actual empty row must be recomputed. In full original coordinates the
whole perturbation is \(\delta(uv^T+vu^T)\),
\(u=\sum_{a=1}^3e_{1a}-3e_\emptyset\),
\(v=e_{L3}-e_\emptyset\). Both are centered, with
\(u^2=12,v^2=2,u\cdot v=3\). Its two nonzero eigenvalues are
\(\delta(3\pm2\sqrt6)\), so its norm is
\(C\delta\), \(C=3+2\sqrt6<8\). Therefore
\[
NP-Q_{\rm repaired}\succeq(1-C\delta)P.
\]
For the author's \(\delta=1/[4(8+\kappa)]\), this confirms its claimed
\(1-8\delta>3/4\) gap, full cap rank \(N-1\), and simple unit eigenvalue.

## All-real greatest rank and products

For every real ordinary H competitor, a size-s point star is an original
independent set and \(L[S,S]=sI\). Also \(L1=N1\). Thus its centered
indicator has zero L energy, and PSD forces it into the lower kernel.
The upper bound \(N-1\) holds without any cap or invariance assumptions.

For any finite nonempty product of factors on disjoint grounds, each with
simple unit and simple lower extreme \(-\rho_j=-s_j/(N_j-s_j)\),

maximize \(s_j/N_j\), the minimum eigenspace has exactly dimension k,
the unit eigenvalue is simple, and lower/cap ranks are \(N-k,N-1\).
The product star size is \(N\max_j(s_j/N_j)\). Its k centered maximal-star
indicators are mutually orthogonal under uniform product measure and are
forced lower-kernel vectors for every real ordinary competitor, proving
the universal greatest-rank claim. The product cap gap is positive; no
numerical floor independent of the factors is established.
