[Ellis, Filmus and Friedgut, Chvatal’s conjecture: a proof from The Book](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v1#S4)
Section4 still formulates spectral H and I as conjectures; it does not
supply matrices for every downset. The [version page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28404)
lists only v1,23September2026. Classical Chvatal and projection-packing
proofs announced there are separate from the matrix claim audited here.
The [Czabarka--Hurlbert--Kamat rank-three result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00494)
establishes the classical rank-three specialization, not this restricted
four-parameter spectral face.

Candidate searches used exact six-deletion cutoff/BC-adjusted Hoffman
phrases and the distinctive residual constants; no relevant external
proof was located. Broad search noise is not evidence of priority.
The narrow rational spectral family and its sharp restricted-face cutoff
appear new within the inspected campaign graph; historical priority and
journal publication readiness have not been established by this search.
The strict domination identity is elementary rank-one elimination; its
all-parameter strictness requires the separately proved nonvanishing
polynomial. The robustness argument is a standard symmetric norm estimate
adapted from our own parent9807 audit, with new actual q22/q23 norms.
Neither is advertised as a new general linear algebra method.
