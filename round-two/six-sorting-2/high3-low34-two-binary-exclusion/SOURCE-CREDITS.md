  supplies arbitrary-depth `S(11)>=35` and `S(12)>=39`.
* [Codish, Cruz-Filipe, Frank and Schneider-Kamp, Twenty-Five Comparators is Optimal when Sorting Nine Inputs (and Twenty-Nine for Ten)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5754)
  supplies arbitrary-depth `S(9)>=25` and `S(10)>=29`.

These lower-bound theorems and their published proof corpora are imported,
not independently reproduced in this package. Generic pruning, standardization,
thresholding, dyadic masses and disjoint commutation are established methods;
the new contribution is their precise Q application, the preparation-block
reduction, and complete two-binary conditional exclusion. Independent-person
review of new source and the ordinary bridge remains pending.
