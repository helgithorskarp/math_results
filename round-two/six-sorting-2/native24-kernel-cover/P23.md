Author and executing agent: **six-sorting-2, researcher**, 2026-10-01.

**Theorem.** Every standard thirteen-input sorter beginning with the
literal first **23** comparators of N13L46D9 in [fixture.json](fixture.json)
has at least 45 comparators. All suffix orders, repetitions, interleavings
and depths are allowed. The global thirteen-input interval remains 44..45.

any standard gate to this fixed P23 is excluded at size 44. This includes
all replacements of the native gate24 (2,4), even if their Boolean

No kernel cover for a changed prefix is assumed.

## Saturation forces the missing gate

For every original pair of input positions, place the two smallest
distinct ranks there and preserve every Boolean assignment on the eleven
other original inputs. Count marked touches D, once per comparator even
when both endpoints are marked. Group only these ordinary counts by the
current pair mask z and put d(z)=max D and W=sum_z 2^d(z).

Future marked positions and ordinary touch increments depend only on z.
A comparator has at most two preimages per marker pair; both are charged
in a double fibre. Thus W never decreases, since
2^(1+max(d1,d2)) >= 2^d1+2^d2.
No conditional free-input domains are identified to count redundancies:
R is not used in this lift.

In a full sorter with m comparators, deleting the two extreme histories
and standardizing the free network leaves an eleven-input sorter with
at most m-D gates. Therefore every terminal D is at most m-35.
There is one terminal pair, so any total-size-at-most-44 completion must
satisfy W<=512 at every prefix.

The complete original-family computation at P23 is

| Current low pair | Maximum D | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| {0,1} | 8 | 256 |
| {0,2} | 7 | 128 |
| {0,4} | 7 | 128 |

Hence W(P23)=512 exactly. Any standard comparator on 0 preserves each
of these masks and charges every history, doubling W. It is forbidden.
A comparator avoiding all of {0,1,2,4} preserves the whole ordinary
profile. A comparator touching exactly one of the live secondary-minimum
ports {1,2,4} doubles that class's weight without a merger and is forbidden.
A comparison between port1 and either port2 or port4 merges unequal
weights256/128 into weight512, increasing total W to640. It is forbidden.
The only allowed event touching the live set is the equal-weight merge
(2,4), which replaces its two128 leaves by one256 leaf.

This event must occur: otherwise an original pair whose second minimum
is at4 never reaches the required terminal pair {0,1}. Before its first
occurrence, every suffix gate therefore avoids {0,1,2,4}. These gates
are all disjoint from (2,4), so that comparison commutes to the front of
the suffix. The network becomes

    P23 ; (2,4) ; remaining suffix = P24 ; remaining suffix,

with its size and full sorting function preserved. This contradicts the
imported P24 exclusion. No bound on the number of preparation gates or
on depth was introduced. This proves the theorem.

The full 78-gate next-step classification in
[p23-certificate.json](p23-certificate.json) has 36 invisible preparation
gates, the one forced live event, and 41 gates exceeding the ceiling.
These exact transitions support the stated elementary classification;
completeness over arbitrary suffix length follows from the invariant
and first-event argument, not from a one-step search cutoff.
