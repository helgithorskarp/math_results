# Six histories exclude the native 21-comparator prefix at size 44

Author and executing agent: **six-sorting-2**, role **researcher**,
2026-10-02. Status: complete author proof with two distinct exact
computational representations; unformalized, without an external review
verdict.

Let P21 be the first 21 ordered comparators of the native N13L46D9 word
in [fixture.json](fixture.json). A standard comparator (a,b), a<b,
puts the smaller value on a. Explicitly,

~~~text
(0,11),(1,7),(2,4),(3,5),(8,9),(10,12),
(0,2),(3,6),(4,12),(5,7),(8,10),
(0,8),(1,3),(2,5),(4,9),(6,11),(7,12),
(0,1),(2,10),(3,8),(4,6).
~~~

**Theorem.** Every standard thirteen-input sorting network beginning
with P21 has at least 45 comparators, with arbitrary suffix order,
depth, repetitions and interleaving. Its minimum total size lies in
45..46, and its minimum suffix size in 24..25. The known native46 word
supplies the checked upper bound. A 45-comparator completion of P21 is
not supplied. In particular every one of the 78 next-gate extensions
P21;(a,b) is excluded at total size at most 44.

This strengthens the earlier [native P22 theorem](P22.md), graph9082,
by deleting its final hypothesis comparator (9,11). The proof imports
that theorem for one branch and closes the other branches with six
fixed original two-high histories. The unrestricted thirteen-input
minimum remains **44..45** in the
[primary table](https://bertdobbelaere.github.io/sorting_networks.html),
refreshed 2026-10-02. Other starting prefixes, including native P20,
are outside this result.

## 1. Ordinary transport for a fixed selected family

Place two distinct LOW ranks below every free value, or two distinct
HIGH ranks above every free value, at any fixed pair of original
inputs. The remaining eleven inputs range over their entire Boolean
cube. The marked pair's positions and touched gates are independent
of those free values. Let D count every gate meeting at least one
mark, once even for a stationary touch or a gate meeting both marks.

For any fixed selected subset T of original placements, at a current
marked configuration z put

~~~text
d_T(z) = max { D_f : f in T currently has configuration z },
W_T = sum_z 2^d_T(z).
~~~

Omit empty classes. A standard comparator's current-pair map has at
most two preimages for each new configuration. A double fibre
charges both old configurations; a single fibre has nonnegative
increment. Because

~~~text
2^(1+max(d1,d2)) >= 2^d1 + 2^d2,
~~~

W_T cannot decrease under an appended comparator. This proof applies
to a selected subset as well as to the full pair family. Each original
conditional free domain remains separate before class maxima are
formed; equality of marked configurations does not identify their
Boolean images.

At the end of a total-m sorter, all two-low placements have marks at
0/1 and all two-high placements at11/12. Removing the marked paths
leaves a generalized eleven-input sorter with at most m-D_f gates;
standardization adds no gates. The established S(11)>=35 therefore
gives D_f<=m-35, and

~~~text
W_T <= 2^(m-35)
~~~

at every prefix. For m<=44 the ceiling is at most512. No semantic
identity deletion R is needed in this new transport argument.

The pruning, route-tree and Huffman methods are established:
[Harder, Section3.2 and Theorem26](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.04400v3)
and the credited campaign
[pruning proof](../semantic-pruning/PROOF.md), graph8539.
The large S(11) lower-bound corpus is imported, not rerun.

## 3. Three ordinary anchors force a nine-word maximum cover

For any reachable unary-HIGH port p define the ordinary pair anchor

~~~text
M_p = sum_{two-high classes z containing p} 2^d(z).
~~~

If a gate touches the unary route at p, that route goes to the gate's
larger endpoint. Restricted to old pairs containing p, the pair map
is injective and every pair is charged. The next anchor is at least
2*M_p. If a gate avoids p, membership of p persists and the ordinary
fibre inequality gives nondecrease. Thus, if this unary route has t
future touches, including stationary touches,

~~~text
2^t M_p <= 2^(m-35).
~~~

This is the ordinary case of the credited
[anchored transport theorem](../semantic-pruning/ANCHORS.md), graph8604.
It needs only S(11)>=35, rather than the auxiliary semantic families.
The standalone checker audits all1014 port/gate combinations for the
restricted-pair membership and touched-map injection.

At P21 and at **each** P21;M_s, the thirteen unary-HIGH input routes
reach exactly9,11,12. Independently reconstructed complete two-high
profiles give

| Unary port p | Ordinary anchored mass M_p | Maximum future touches |
|---|---:|---:|
| 9 | 64 | 3 |
| 11 | 80 | 2 |
| 12 | 192 | 1 |

Every route must ultimately reach12. Three distinct routes require
two binary merges. The route at12 has one touch available, so it
must join only at the final merge, with no singleton touch.
The routes at9/11 must merge with each other first, then join12.
Port11's two mandatory merges exhaust its budget. Port9 can have
at most one extra singleton touch, occurring before its merger with11.

Let F be the arbitrary word before the first maximum event after
M_s. It avoids frozen0/1 and the current unary-HIGH ports9/11/12,
so it uses

~~~text

~~~

The first-event alternatives are exactly:

* The direct merge (9,11).
* One singleton g_r=(min(r,9),max(r,9)), for some r in U.

For the direct branch, (9,11) commutes left across F and M_s,

In a singleton branch the next maximum events must be

The necessary normal form is therefore


Crucially, g_r is kept **after F**: F may touch its free operand r.

## 5. The three shadows give an unavoidable contradiction

The shadow configurations initially are

~~~text
{5,12}:D5,  {7,12}:D5,  {8,12}:D5.
~~~

Under a standard F on2..8 their secondary HIGH ports move only
upwards, and remain in{5,6,7,8};12 is untouched. Write q_i and d_i
for the three secondary ports and costs after F.

Applied to this **fixed selected subset by itself**, Section1 gives
nondecreasing shadow class-max mass. Its initial value is
3*2^5=96. The fact that two shadow histories might later merge
does not invalidate that transport inequality.

There are exactly two possibilities:

* If any q_i differs from r, none of the first two K_r gates meets
  this secondary mark. Only the final gate on12 is charged. The
  resulting pair{q_i,12} has cost d_i+1>=6 and is different from
  all three baseline pairs, whose secondary ports are9/10/11.
  Its positive weight is additional to the baseline512, violating
  the ceiling.
* If all three q_i equal r, their single current shadow class has
  weight at least96. Its integer maximum cost is at least7, since
  2^6<96<=2^7. Necessarily r is in5..8. All three K_r gates then
  meet a mark: g_r moves the secondary HIGH from r to9;
  (9,11) moves it to11; (11,12) meets both HIGH marks and is
  counted once. This original history has cost at least
  7+3=10 after K_r, contradicting D<=44-35=9. Equivalently its single
  ordinary class has weight at least1024>512.

The checker audits all32 shadow K_r alternatives and all84

**every** F. No finite enumeration of preparation words, solver

Every minimum stem and maximum-event branch is now excluded.
Together with the checked native46 upper witness, this proves the
