Let P22 be the first 22 literal comparators of the 46-gate N13L46D9
word in [fixture.json](fixture.json). Standard (a,b), with a<b, writes
the minimum to a. The order of this prefix is part of the hypothesis.

**Theorem.** Every standard sorting network starting with P22 has at
least 45 comparators. Its minimum total size therefore lies in 45..46,
using the pinned 46-gate sorter as the upper witness. Its minimum suffix
size lies in 23..24. No 45-gate completion of this prefix is supplied.
This also excludes all 78 literal next-gate prefixes P22;(a,b), at
arbitrary subsequent order and depth.

The proof establishes a complete three-matching event cover for P22.
One matching gives the previously excluded native prefix. The other
two matchings give exactly 90 literal roots, all excluded by independently
reconstructed selected nested masses. In particular the two earlier
ten-wire construction targets of 146 and 147 states are excluded at
their 18-gate suffix budget. The unrestricted thirteen-input 44..45 gap
remains open. P21 and other prefixes are outside the theorem.

## 1. Ordinary saturation and the first maximum event

For each original placement of two minimum marks, keep its original full
eleven-free-input Boolean cube. Let D count a comparator meeting at
least one mark, once even when both endpoints are marked. At a current
pair configuration z put d(z)=max D and W=sum_z 2^d(z).

The future mark map and ordinary increment depend only on the current
configuration. A comparator has at most two preimages per new
configuration; a double fibre charges both. Thus

    2^(1+max(d1,d2)) >= 2^d1 + 2^d2

proves that W is nondecreasing. At a full m-gate sorter, pruning leaves
an eleven-input sorter with at most m-D comparators. All marks end on
the same two ports, so W <= 2^(m-35). Consequently a total-size-at-most-44
completion must have W<=512 at every prefix. The same proof holds for
two maximum marks. This ordinary argument uses no merged conditional
free-input domains to determine redundant gates.

At P22 the complete two-minimum envelope is

| Minimum ports | D | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| {0,1} | 7 | 128 |
| {0,2} | 7 | 128 |
| {0,3} | 7 | 128 |
| {0,4} | 7 | 128 |

Hence W=512. A standard comparator on port 0 preserves every pair
configuration and charges it, increasing W. No completion under the
budget can touch 0.

The semantic unary maximum anchors, independently obtained from all
original unary-maximum, two-maximum and mixed domains, are exactly

    port11: label43;     port12: label43.

The anchor theorem gives U_high=sum_p 2^label(p) <= 2^m at every prefix.
Here U_high=2^44. A touch on one reachable maximum port alone raises
its label by at least one; a binary merge has new label at least
1+max of the two old labels. Equality at the ceiling therefore permits
only a merge of equal labels. The first gate touching 11 or 12 is
(11,12). It must occur, since a unary maximum at 11 has to reach 12.
All preceding gates avoid its endpoints, so it commutes to the suffix
front. The resulting maximum label is at least 44, and no later gate
can touch 12.

## 2. Every minimum completion has one of three matchings

After moving (11,12) forward, the ordinary minimum envelope is unchanged:
its live second-minimum ports are 1,2,3,4, all of cost 7. With 0 forbidden,
a single-route touch increases W and a binary merge preserves W exactly
when both costs are equal. Thus every live event merges two equal costs,
removes the larger port and increases the surviving cost by one.

The first merge creates one cost-8 node and leaves two cost-7 leaves.
An unequal merge exceeds the ceiling, so the other two leaves merge next.
Finally the two cost-8 nodes merge. There are three perfect matchings:

| Name | Canonical minimum stem |
|---|---|
| A | (1,2);(3,4);(1,3) |
| B | (1,3);(2,4);(1,2) |
| C | (1,4);(2,3);(1,2) |

Every gate before the next live event avoids its currently live endpoints.
Move that event left across these disjoint preparation gates. Repeat
after each event; discarded preparation gates remain in the suffix,
preserving the complete sorting function and total size. This is valid
for any number of preparations and imposes no depth cutoff. Gates on
disjoint bottom pairs also commute, giving the table's canonical order.
The independent checker enumerates every equal event in every order:
all six complete words yield exactly these three stems.

The sole terminal minimum-pair configuration is {0,1}, D=9. Any later
comparator on 0 or 1 raises W, so both ports are frozen. Put

## 4. Every alternate root violates the nested mass ceiling

For a fixed original placement f of three lows and three highs, retain
its ENTIRE seven-free-input cube throughout the 32-gate prefix. Delete
marked touches D_f and free comparators that act identically on that
full original domain, R_f. Set C_f=D_f+R_f. Track the free carriers and
normalize the retained oriented seven-wire word Q_f by current physical
free-output order. Its initial carrier permutation preserves the full
Boolean cube.

Use the already proved integer extension bound

    B(Q) = max(16, low semantic anchor bound, high semantic anchor bound).

It is invariant under a global wire relabeling and nondecreasing when
any oriented comparator is appended. Put lambda_f=C_f+B(Q_f). For any
fixed finite selected family F, the universal nested-class theorem gives

    M_F(P) = sum_z 2^(max_{f in F at z} lambda_f) <= 2^m

for every total-m sorting extension. Its proof follows each original
domain separately, including when two current marker configurations
merge. An uncharged retained gate cannot decrease B; a deleted gate
raises C. A double fibre charges both old configurations and satisfies
the dyadic maximum inequality. At the sorted terminal configuration the
remaining pruned word sorts the whole free cube, forcing lambda<=m.
No conditional images are identified merely because their marks agree.

[p22-fixture.json](p22-fixture.json) chooses 479 original domains across
the 90 roots, with four to eight different current configurations per
root. The standalone checker independently reconstructs every D, R,
carrier map, retained Q, inner original-domain hash and anchor leaf.
It validates the entire pruning function on every original assignment.
Selected configurations within a root are distinct, so each selected
mass is simply the sum of the reconstructed 2^lambda terms.

The masses range from 33*2^39 to 40*2^39, all STRICTLY ABOVE
2^44=32*2^39. Every root has lower bound 45. The largest individual
lambda is only 43, so no selected single domain gives the exclusion:
the class aggregation is essential. Inner B counts are 18 at16,275 at17,
167 at18 and19 at19. [p22-nested.json](p22-nested.json) contains all
selected records and binds them to the complete cover by its raw hash
and every literal root hash. Hence all 90 alternate roots are excluded.
Together with matching B this proves the theorem.
