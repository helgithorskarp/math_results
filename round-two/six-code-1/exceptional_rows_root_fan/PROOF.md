## General colored graph lemma and unit-root fan

Let a finite SIMPLE undirected graph have edges of colors1/2 and vertex
partition A,V,C,B. The unit vertices are A union V; eligible vertices
are V union B. Assume all unit edges have color1, and no unit has an
eligible neighbor. Let R>=1 be the number of chosen roots in A, each
having radius at most two to EVERY vertex. Assume V union B nonempty.
In the packing application these chosen roots are precisely the k=0
units; no degree5 assumption is needed for this intermediate lemma.

Put d(v)=support degree, c_i(v)=number of color-i edges at v, and

    D_A=sum_{a in A}d(a), D_V=sum_{v in V}d(v),
    I=sum_{a in A}min(d(a),|A|-1), I_even=2 floor(I/2),
    C1=sum_{c in C}c_1(c), C2=sum_{c in C}c_2(c).

Every neighbor of V lies in C. Those D_V edges consume D_V distinct
color1 C endpoints. A root cannot reach any eligible point through
another unit or through an eligible nonunit neighbor. Hence every
root has a C neighbor, requiring R distinct A-C edges. Independently,
internal A edges contribute an even number of endpoints at most I,
so A-C has at least D_A-I_even edges. Therefore

    C1 >= D_V+max(R,D_A-I_even).                       (2)

Fix one root. Reaching each b in B within two edges requires a
distinct C-B edge. These |B| edges are disjoint from all A-C and
V-C edges. Each consumes one SUPPORT endpoint in C, including a
color2 edge. Therefore

    C1+C2 >= D_V+max(R,D_A-I_even)+|B|.                (3)

For the fan refinement, list all c in C with c_1(c)>0, sorted by
decreasing a_c=d(c)-1>=0. Let ell be the least number of largest
a_c whose sum is at least |V|+|B|. Each such eligible point must be
reached through one of a root's C neighbors. The edge to the root
uses color1, leaving at most a_c other support edges at c. If the
root had fewer than ell C neighbors, even the largest remaining
capacities could not cover all eligible points. Thus ell exists and
each root has at least ell distinct C neighbors. The R roots use
R ell distinct A-C edges. This proves

    C1 >= D_V+max(R ell,D_A-I_even),
    C1+C2 >= D_V+max(R ell,D_A-I_even)+|B|.            (4)

The second inequality includes B empty, when it follows from the first.
Ignoring target colors, collisions and capacity consumed by other roots
only enlarges the fan capacity estimate. An eligible unit's D_V endpoints
are distinct from all A-C root edges; counting them inside a max with R
would lose a necessary sum. When ell=1, (4) retains (2)/(3).
