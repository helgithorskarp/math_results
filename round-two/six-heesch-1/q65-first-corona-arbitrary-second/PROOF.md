# First-scaffold reduction

Actual author **six-heesch-1**, role **researcher**. Author-checked exact
computer-assisted lemma, independently unreviewed and unformalized. The
purpose is to eliminate a specified construction family, not to claim a
finite-number record or historical priority.

## Statement and physical conventions

Let Q be the literal65-cell Q65-P17 source in input.json. A cell coordinate
is the lower-left corner of a closed unit square. Let U be every integer
cell coordinate at Chebyshev distance at most one from a cell of Q; |U|=123.
Choose a nonempty S subset U whose union of closed squares is a topological
disc. The source is unmarked; all congruent copies are permitted in an
otherwise arbitrary surround.

The first seven physical maps, one identity root and six first copies, are
fixed by input.json BEFORE changing S. For each saved normalized pose
(o,x,y), act on all physical vertices of Q by its D4 matrix M, and let b be
the coordinatewise minimum of the image cells. The actual isometry is
z -> Mz+(x,y)-b. A changed source minimum or orientation ordering must not
alter this map. The checker recovers the maps from all eight distinct
normalized images of Q and transforms each candidate cell's four vertices.

Let P0=S and P1 be the seven-copy union at these maps. Assume:

1. These seven copies have pairwise disjoint interiors.
2. P1 is a topological disc and contains every Moore-neighbor cell of P0.
3. There is a finite collection of further congruent copies of S, pairwise
   interior-disjoint and interior-disjoint from P1, whose union with P1
   contains every point of P1 in its interior.

Hypothesis3 allows arbitrary real translations, rotations and reflections.
The resulting outer prefix may have holes. Requiring each new copy to meet
the old boundary only strengthens these premises. Two admissible coronas
with a disc first prefix at exactly these first maps imply the premises.

**Lemma.** The eight physical representatives in tiling.json, translated by
all integer combinations of u=(22,6),v=(-6,22), tile the entire plane by S.
Consequently |S|=65, and such a simple source is a plane tiler. A common
Euclidean isometry of all data preserves the result.

Nothing is claimed for another first arrangement or a source outside U.
Unlike predecessor9992, the new lemma does not fix any second-copy pose.
It adds source and first-prefix disc assumptions; it is not a generalization
of the predecessor's topology-free statement in every respect.

## Necessary first-prefix Boolean conditions

For p in sorted U, x_p selects the same source cell in every first map.
Packing clauses are generated by direct intersections of every unordered
copy pair: coincident image cells of p,q give not x_p or not x_q. Repeated
variables yield the appropriate unit. The root halo is reconstructed at
all nine neighboring coordinates of every possible root cell. For source
site p and target cell a, the clause is not x_p or OR(x_q), over ALL first
copies/source sites that could cover a. Tautologies are omitted.

At each possible vertex of P0 and P1, opposite occupied quadrants with
both other quadrants empty create a pinch. A topological disc cannot have
that local pattern. For each pair of possible opposite suppliers p,q add
not x_p or not x_q or OR(other-quadrant suppliers). This is necessary even
if the supplier variables coincide. No claim that these local clauses
alone ensure global disc topology is used.

The compact evidence adds99 necessary topological separation clauses.
The reader reconstructs each at level0 or1 from physical image suppliers:

* For any finite cell region C and supplied occupied cells inside and
  outside C, a side-connected prefix must occupy some cell of N4(C) outside
  C. The clause negates the two occupied endpoint suppliers and includes
  every possible supplier on that boundary. A cell-edge path proves it.
* For a finite cell region H containing a point-cell q, if every cell of
  N4(H) outside H is occupied, q must be occupied in a hole-free prefix.
  Otherwise q's empty component cannot reach infinity. The clause includes
  every supplier of q and negates one certified supplier for EVERY boundary
  cell. The whole region boundary and all supplier memberships are checked.

A disc polyomino has a connected cell-edge graph and no bounded component
of its empty cell-edge graph. Thus these clauses follow from the two disc
hypotheses. The regions need not equal observed components or be minimal.
No sampled mask or topology oracle is trusted as an axiom.

## Universal source guards from original90-degree sectors

There are two compact signed source guards G. A positive atom fixes a site
present; a negative atom fixes it absent. They use72 and60 atoms, leaving51
and63 syntactically unspecified bits. These numbers do not count admissible
polyominoes. guard.py verifies each guard universally, rather than checking
only its literal mask or trusting producer overlap witnesses.

At each saved step, the missing quadrant at an integer vertex v is forced
absent from ALL presently known physical copies by negative atoms. Its two
adjacent quadrants are occupied by positive atoms. A positive initial-first
cell incident to v licenses the obligation: v is an original P1 boundary
point, so hypothesis3 must cover a neighborhood of v. Vertices belonging
only to newly forced copies are never licensed.

Every boundary angle of a simple square polyomino is at least90 degrees.
In a packed proposed surround, the isolated90-degree sector at v therefore
requires one further copy with a90-degree corner at v. A straight boundary
or interior point would consume a larger sector and overlap one of the two
occupied adjacent quadrants. Two smaller-sector providers are impossible.
The required corner aligns both incident edges with the original square
axes. Hence this ONE required provider has a D4 linear part and an integer
physical translation: both source vertex and v are integer. Other proposed
copies are left unrestricted. This is a local registration deduction, not
a global lattice-registration assumption.

For each of eight D4 matrices and every one of123 potential source sites,
the reader constructs the unique translation placing that site in the
missing quadrant cell. This is a complete superset of registered corner
providers. Each alternative either has its anchor absent under G or
overlaps a mandatory positive cell of a known copy. One certified physical
map remains for seven successive steps; at the eighth no map remains.
Each retained map has a selected source anchor. If that forced copy fails
to pack for some other mask satisfying G, a proposed surround has already
failed. Under the assumed existence of a surround, it must be one of its
copies, so the induction is sound. Additional unknown copies cannot undo
the positive-cell overlaps used to exclude alternatives.

All7865 alternative frames are tested for each guard:7 times983 plus984.
The actual source variable can change; the fixed physical map cannot.
Thus any S satisfying G contradicts hypothesis3, and the necessary mask
clause is exactly the negation of G. Its direction is checked explicitly.
The two64-cell literal first-disc fixtures independently pass whole-cell
packing, contacts, halos and simple oriented boundary-cycle checks. They
are positive controls for the first-prefix hypotheses, not substitutes for
the universal guards or claims about alternative first coronas.

## Period failure, refutation and whole-plane conclusion

The period lattice has index det(u,v)=520. The reader uses the adjugate
membership test: a difference (dx,dy) is in the lattice exactly when both
22dx+6dy and -6dx+22dy are divisible by520. It verifies520 distinct quotient
representatives (x,y),0<=x<260,0<=y<2. For each representative class r,
C_r sums all selected cells of all eight physical period copies, retaining
integer multiplicities when one source variable contributes more than once.

Introduce z_r asserting C_r!=1. For each coefficient-one x_p in row r add
not z_r or not x_p or OR(other distinct variables in r). Coefficient>=2
terms have no such clause. With z_r true, a row therefore cannot contain
exactly one active coefficient-one variable. Zero, several active variables,
or a sole coefficient>=2 variable are precisely the allowed bad cases.
The disjunction of all520 z_r is an EXACT failure predicate for this period;
a nonempty-source disjunction is also included.

The independently reconstructed base has643 variables and3407 clauses,
SHA256 e5d9244e7ff0e4ab7669aa7d73a9326d366b7c22c9f0efcc173bda4487cfe62a.
Adding99 topology clauses and2 guard clauses gives3508 distinct clauses,
SHA256 56ea49ea29346ab1ce66593808c271853cbd827327f8df283ac1a96f7202f124.
The130-addition,3125-byte proof.rup ends with the empty clause. For each

valid reverse-unit-propagation consequence. No solver verdict is trusted.
The whole formula is unsatisfiable.

Any source satisfying the geometric hypotheses satisfies every necessary
clause. If its period failed, the exact predicate would extend its source

Therefore every C_r=1. Translating all eight representatives by the lattice
