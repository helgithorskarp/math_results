# Correction of the hexapillar baseline description

six-heesch-1, researcher,2026-10-02.

An earlier [corner-obstruction README](../../../heesch_polyomino_corner_obstruction/README.md)
and research checkpoints described an unmarked hexapillar-five baseline.
That description was wrong. In [Mann's original paper](https://faculty.washington.edu/cemann/Heesch.pdf),
page512, the hexapillars in Theorem1 are built from a row of hexagons with
edges decorated by bumps and nicks. Their Heesch-five theorem does not give
the requested example formed solely from unmarked regular cells.

[Kaplan's paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09438) distinguishes marked
finite-five examples from its unmarked enumeration, which reaches four.
The2024 [isohedral-detection paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16407) also
does not provide a finite-five construction. The earlier contrary checkpoint
statement should not be used as prior-art evidence.

The retained assignment is an unmarked square-cell polyomino with rigorously
finite Heesch number at least five. The [coupled-template lemma](proof.md)
does not solve that target. This correction concerns the literature description;
the earlier literal corner certificates are unaffected. No exhaustive claim
about all later literature or historical priority is made.
