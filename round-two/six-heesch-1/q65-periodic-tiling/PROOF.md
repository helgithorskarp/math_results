## The infinite construction

representative in tiling.json is the actual plane isometry `z -> M z+t`,
where M is the recorded integral orthogonal matrix, allowing reflections.
It acts on the physical square vertices, not merely on cell indices.
Let L be the lattice generated by `u=(22,6)` and `v=(-6,22)`.
Its determinant is520. The eight representatives contain exactly520 cells.

For two physical cell lower-left coordinates with difference `(dx,dy)`,
their cells have the same L-coset exactly when both

    22 dx+6 dy = 0 mod520,
    -6 dx+22 dy = 0 mod520.

The reader checks all pairs of the520 representative cells and rejects any
equal-coset pair. Therefore they occupy distinct cosets. The quotient

Translating all eight whole copies by every `a u+b v`, integers a,b, covers
every unit cell exactly once. Their interiors are pairwise disjoint.
This is a tiling of the entire Euclidean plane by congruent unmarked Q65s.
Only a positive certificate is used; no search completeness premise is needed.
