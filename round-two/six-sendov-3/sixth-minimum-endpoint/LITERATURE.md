[Teng Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang--Zhang inequality](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126),
Conjecture 1.2, Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.4 distinguish the strongest
first-power assertion from the proved quadratic and higher-power cases.
The present work is a local degree-nine boundary expansion and stability
result; it does not settle the full complex first-power conjecture.

[Terence Tao, A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/),
main-text Conjecture 19, supplies the power-family context. Blog comments
are not mathematical premises here. Ordinary Sendov is prior art.

[Michael J. Miller, Unexpected local extrema for the Sendov conjecture](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0505424v3),
Theorem 1, already uses an integrated repeated-real-critical/quadratic
polynomial, including degree nine, for the nearest-critical-distance
objective. This integrated template is credited prior art, not a new
construction mechanism claimed here.
