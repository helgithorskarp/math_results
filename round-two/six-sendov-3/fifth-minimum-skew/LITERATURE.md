# Primary literature and status, live checked 2026-10-05

[Teng Zhang, Beyond Sendov's conjecture: the quadratic Tang–Zhang inequality,
arXiv:2609.19126, Conjecture 1.2 and Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19126)
states the first-power endpoint as the strongest member of the reciprocal
power conjecture. The theorem establishes the quadratic case; the paper
does not supply the present first-power boundary expansion.

[Terence Tao, A digestion of the proof of Sendov's conjecture,
main Conjecture 19](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/a-digestion-of-the-proof-of-sendovs-conjecture/)
states the reciprocal-power family. Blog comments are not premises.

[Michael J. Miller, Unexpected local extrema for the Sendov conjecture,
Theorem 1, arXiv:math/0505424v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0505424v3)
already uses an integrated repeated-real-critical/quadratic derivative
template, including degree nine, for the nearest-critical-distance objective.
That template is prior art. No exclusive-priority claim is made.

Novelty here is the fifth scalar of the actual global FIRST boundary
minimum and full sharp skew/cost comparisons under precisely attributed
published global-entry and analytic-minimum premises. Unrestricted
interior complex FIRST, an effective collar, the exact fifth endpoint
and the next sharp central-skew coefficient remain unclaimed.
