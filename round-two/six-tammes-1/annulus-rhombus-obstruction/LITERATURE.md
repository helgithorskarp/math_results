- O.R.Musin and A.S.Tarasov,
  [The Tammes problem for N=14](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2536),
  *Experimental Mathematics*24(2015),460--468. This solves N14 through
  irreducible contact-graph enumeration. Its solved status is credited;
  it is not a proof of N15 optimizer coverage or the map cohort here.
- O.R.Musin and A.S.Tarasov,
  [Enumeration of irreducible contact graphs on the sphere](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5450),
  especially [Proposition 4.1(5)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.5450), records
  the adjacent half-angle cotangent identity for a spherical rhombus.
  Contact planarity, degree restrictions and convex-face filters also
  have classical precedents. Section 2 of the new proof establishes
  exactly the physical bridge used here under explicit hypotheses;
  those classical identities and principles are not claimed as new.
- K.Bezdek and S.Reid,
  [Contact graphs of unit sphere packings revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5756),
  [Lemmas 4--5 and Section 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.5756), already uses
  local quadrilateral/pentagon angle exclusions and nontriangle
  incidence arguments at contact distance \(\pi/3\), i.e. \(c=1/2\).
  In particular its Quadrilateral Lemma treats adjacent corners whose
  other sectors are triangles. This is relevant prior art for local
  face filters, including the earlier 9681 mechanism; it is not a proof
  input for our TQQ/shared-edge argument. No priority claim is made for
  classical special cases or the triangular-prism calibration.
