## Definition and theorem

Let H be the subgroup generated by 3^88 in F617*, of order seven. The element 3
is primitive, and 3^44 H = -H. Let c:F617*->{0,1} be H-invariant. Assume every
nonconstant seven-term field arithmetic progression avoiding zero is bichromatic.
Write y_i=c(3^i H), with i modulo 88, and f_i=y_i XOR y_(i+44), modulo 44.
Let K=sum(f_i). At K=11 the majority background is b=0; at K=33 it is b=1.
The eleven positions where f_i=1-b are the minority positions.

**Theorem.** At K in {11,33}, if the longest cyclic minority run has length six,
there are exactly six minority runs and six background runs. The ordered minority
profile, starting at the unique length-six run, is (6,1,1,1,1,1). Consequently
the other five minority positions are isolated.

There are 1,876 remaining necessary normalized phase words per background in
this conditional branch. These are not feasible-field-coloring counts. A length-six
run is not excluded, neither phase weight is excluded, and the whole H7 family
is not excluded. The earlier

## Lossless 120-case cover

The phase-eight premise bounds runs of each phase value by seven. Suppose the
longest minority run has length six. It is unique, since there are only eleven
minority positions. Five minority positions remain. The 33 background positions
require at least ceil(33/7)=5 runs, and cyclic runs of the two values alternate.
There can be at most 1+5=6 minority runs. Thus the run count is five or six.

At five runs, the other four positive minority-run lengths sum to five. Exactly
one is two and the other three are one. There are four ordered profiles
(6,2,1,1,1), (6,1,2,1,1), (6,1,1,2,1), (6,1,1,1,2).
The five background gaps g_j are positive, at most seven, and sum to 33.
Their deficits from seven sum to two: either one gap is five, or two gaps are six.
This gives 5+C(5,2)=15 ordered gap vectors. Both backgrounds are retained.
The resulting 4*15*2=120 fully fixed phase words are all distinct.

Multiplying all actual field points by a nonzero scalar preserves nonconstant
zero-avoiding field APs. A power of 3 rotates the unique six-position run to
indices 0..5. All ordered profiles and gap vectors remain in the cover. No
reflection, inversion, phase exchange, or identification of lower colors is used.
The 44 lower colors x_i=y_i are independent Boolean variables, and upper colors
are y_(i+44)=x_i XOR f_i. The sole gauge x_0=0 is justified by complementing
all 88 colors, which preserves f and bichromaticity. No color assignment is lost.

At six runs, the other five minority runs are singletons. The six background gaps
are positive, at most seven, and sum to 33. Their deficits from seven are six
integers in 0..6 summing to nine. The count is
C(14,5)-6*C(7,5)=1876. Two deficits cannot both exceed six, so inclusion-exclusion
has no further terms. This describes the remaining necessary branch.
