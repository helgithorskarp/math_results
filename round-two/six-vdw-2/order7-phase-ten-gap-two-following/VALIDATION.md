# Validation boundary

The public reconstruction regenerates the complete twelve-CNF cover,
compares every canonical hash and record to EXPECTED.csv, independently
reconstructs the full actual-field clause multiset, checks every exact-six
gate and substituted parent clause, and runs both strict RUP modes on
untrusted proof candidates. Recorded proof hashes and counts all reproduce.

Normal and optimized controls reject missing backgrounds, false counts or
premises, forced neighbors, damaged semantic constraints and counter gates,
removed/reversed bound-four clauses for both backgrounds, the proposed
stronger third cut, changed helpers before execution, unsupported empty
clauses and missing live hints. Each proof mode accepts a valid
contradictory-unit RUP control. Semantic damages repair CNF hashes and row
counts so rejection tests mathematical constraints rather than hashes alone.

VERIFICATION.json gives exact counts, runtime and memory. These checks plus
the written complete cover establish the restricted lemma. They do not
prove a bound-three lemma, an exact-ten endpoint exclusion, a new numerical
W bound, external independent review or formalization. The broader private
pilot's offset-6 native UNKNOWN is outside this proof's counterexample cover
and is neither a premise nor a fixture.
