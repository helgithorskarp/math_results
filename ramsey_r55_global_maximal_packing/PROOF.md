# A globally complete maximal-packing family for good43

Here red edges are graph edges. A good43 has neither a red nor a blue K5.
This is a complete search reduction, conditional only on the explicitly
imported standard Ramsey bounds and catalog completeness. It constructs no
good43 and decides no physical branch. The combinatorial normalization itself
uses no solver and does not depend on the catalog enumeration being checked by us.

## Global packing and residual normalization

Greedily remove red K4s until no red K4 remains. There are at least five:
the first five choices occur at orders 43,39,35,31,27, and R(4,5)<=25
forces a red K4 in each good residual. Let r be the resulting number.
Now greedily remove blue K4s until no blue K4 remains. Red-K4-freeness
persists under deletion. If q is the total number of removed blocks, the
remaining graph has no monochromatic K4, so its order is at most 17 by
R(4,4)<=18. Since 43-4q is positive and congruent to 3 modulo 4,

    q in {7,8,9,10}, r in {5,...,q}, n=43-4q in {15,11,7,3}.

There are 18 (q,r) macro classes. In particular q=7 remains present; no
uniform eight-K4 forcing or good19 augmentation assertion is used.

For each n, import the complete list of Ramsey(4,4,n) graphs up to
isomorphism from Brendan McKay's author catalog. Its counts are respectively
640,546356,362,4 at n=15,11,7,3. Relabel the whole residual to one catalog
representative. This is an ordinary vertex isomorphism, not a claim that the
author's vertex labels were generated by any specified canonical-labeling
algorithm. Every catalog record is included. No fixed residual alone
substitutes for a whole class.

Label the r red blocks first, followed by the q-r blue blocks, followed by
the residual. Vertices 0..3 are a red root K4. In each other four-block,
sort its four vertices in nonincreasing order of their four-bit red
adjacency signatures to the root, with root vertex i contributing bit i.
All permutations within a monochromatic K4 preserve its fixed internal
edges. These operations relabel every physical cross edge consistently.
They leave the residual's chosen labels unchanged. This is the previously
accepted h3835 root-column normalization, restricted to four-blocks.

The union of the blue four-blocks and the core, namely vertices 4r..42,
contains no red K4: it is exactly the residual after red exhaustion.
The core contains no blue K4 by blue exhaustion, and no red K4. Thus the
new multi-block maximality clauses apply to the entire union, not only to
pairs of blocks.

Conversely, a model of any of the complete formulas below is a good43.
Therefore the disjunction over every listed core of every (q,r) formula is
satisfiable if and only if a good43 exists, under catalog completeness.
The family is a new global labeling cover. It is not asserted to be a subset
of the older 39-branch labelings, nor can their solver verdicts be transferred
without an explicit transport proof.

## Exact physical carrier

Use the accepted h3835 K4-pair domains, whose matrices avoid monochromatic
fives in the union of the two fixed four-blocks. Ordinary same-color pairs
have 37823 states, opposite-color pairs 35714. With the root-column ordering,
a root-to-red block has 1998 states and a root-to-blue block has 1931 states.

Between one four-block and one core vertex, allow all four-bit columns except
the one completing a K5 in the block's color. There are exactly 15 states.
There are q*n such disjoint stars. Keep the whole core fixed; do not multiply
by n!, since its isomorphism was used to relabel it to the representative.
No division by a graph automorphism group is made.

All these matrix and star coordinates use disjoint physical edges. With
A=r-1 and B=q-r, a fixed-core task therefore has exactly

    T(q,r)=1998^A 1931^B 37823^(binom(A,2)+binom(B,2))
           *35714^(A*B) *15^(q*(43-4q))

physical carrier graphs. A code is a mixed-radix tuple in this product;
`family.py` implements both directions. The complete task registry contains
2,189,178 tasks and defines disjoint integer intervals without materializing
millions of JSON records. The whole carrier count is

    N=sum_{q=7}^{10} sum_{r=5}^{q} C(43-4q)*T(q,r),
    C(15)=640, C(11)=546356, C(7)=362, C(3)=4.

This also counts distinct labeled physical carrier graphs. For fixed q,r,
distinct catalog records have different fixed edge sets (literal uniqueness
is checked), and within a task the coordinate encoding is injective. Distinct
r impose opposite colors on some fixed four-block. Finally, if q'>q, the
first extra four-block of the q' representation lies entirely in the core
of the q representation. It is monochromatic, contradicting that core's
Ramsey(4,4) property. Thus different q cannot describe the same labeled graph.
There can still be many isomorphic graphs; no quotient by full-graph
isomorphism is claimed or needed.

`COMPARISON.json` contains N and the accepted h3863 count H as exact integers.
Independent multiplication over individual matrix/star coordinates establishes

    18767*N < H < 18768*N;  N < 2^770;  4096*N < H.

The predeclared whole-family reduction gate was the last inequality. This
compares two complete labeled search carriers, one of which (H) counts codes
with possible duplicates. It is not a fraction of target isomorphism classes
removed and not a measured solver speedup. No rank, block-symmetry, or other
unrelated denominator is multiplied into this count. Carrier graphs can
violate the red-maximality union or the remaining global five-set clauses.

## Complete physical formulas

For a task, fix the 6q internal clique edges and all binom(n,2) core edges.
Assign variables in lexicographic order to every other unordered physical
vertex pair: 903-6q-binom(n,2), respectively 756,800,828,840 variables.
Variable 1 is a forced true constant; physical edge variables start at 2.
Thus DIMACS variable totals are 757,801,829,841.

Add root-column order clauses forbidding every increasing adjacent pair of
four-bit signatures (360 clauses per nonroot K4). For each of all
binom(43,5)=962598 vertex sets and each color, require an opposite-color
edge; omit only clauses already satisfied by a fixed opposite-color edge.
Finally forbid every red K4 in the union 4r..42, again omitting only such
tautologies. This retains every physical target constraint, including all
sets meeting three, four, or five blocks. Pair domains and star restrictions
are implied by this complete layer; they are used for carrier indexing,
not as a replacement for the target constraints.

The independent auditor reconstructs fixed edges, every variable, and every
literal directly from physical vertex sets; it imports no producer module.
The generator also checks its actual clause count against a separate
polynomial count. If the core has e red edges, t red triangles, and u blue
triangles, its fixed monochromatic-subset polynomials are
(1,n,e,t,0,0) and (1,n,binom(n,2)-e,u,0,0). Multiply by (1+x)^4 for each
same-color four-block and by (1+4x) for each opposite-color four-block;
the coefficient of x^5 counts the non-tautological five-set clauses.
For B=q-r blue blocks the red maximality count is

    256*binom(B,4)+64*n*binom(B,3)+16*e*binom(B,2)+4*t*B.

The two physical catalog checkers agree entry by entry on all edges and
triangles, not only on aggregate totals. Catalog completeness remains an
external input; checking every listed graph does not prove that no graph was
omitted. No external automorphism verifier is used.

## Dependencies and limitations

The standard upper bounds and K4-pair/root domains are imported from the
accepted h3835 source and h3845 review. H is the h3863 carrier, independently
accepted at h3865. The exact source identities and references are in
`provenance.json`. The author catalog page is
https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/ramsey.html .

Trust includes these mathematical inputs, the stated completeness of the
four catalog lists, Python integer/file semantics, the two transcriptions,
the C++ compiler/runtime, SHA-256, and ordinary hardware. This is not a
formal proof-assistant certificate or external peer review of the new result.
The catalog-isomorphism existence step is mathematical: the interface can
verify a supplied 43-vertex transport certificate, but supplies no automatic
isomorphism finder over the entire catalog. Physical construction and
complete SAT/UNSAT decisions are still required and belong to team-r55-1.

Concurrent teammate h3869, source `b827c28fbc9edcaf0a01bdfd7639f4b92e2f7f12`,
canonicalizes an entire Ramsey(3,5,12) residual using selectors for nine of
the older branches. It shares the catalog-relabeling principle. Its single
300-second physical call returned UNKNOWN. That family and the present
maximal-K4/Ramsey(4,4) cover use different global labelings; no count or
solver verdict is transferred or multiplied. Its source is
https://github.com/helgithorskarp/math_results/tree/main/ramsey_r55_tail12_selector .
No historical novelty for residual isomorphism normalization is asserted.
