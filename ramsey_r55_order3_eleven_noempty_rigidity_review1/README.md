## Independent hand reduction

Assume no four-bit signature is empty. Let `U` contain indices whose
complementary three red triangles contain no blue triangle. Projecting onto
the complement of `i`, the accepted nine-signature bound gives `x_i>=1`.
At most two singleton-`i` vertices exist. If `i` is outside `U`, its
complement contains a blue triangle, so two such vertices would complete a
blue `K5`; hence `x_i=1`. If `i` is in `U`, the reviewed two-empty-anchor
theorem gives `x_i=2`.

For `i` outside `U`, equality in the three-triangle signature bound gives

```text
x_j + y_ij = 2,
y_jk + t_ijk = 1,
t_(complement of i) + q = 0.
```

Each complement indexed by `U` contains a literal red `K4`, so its triple
signature and the full signature are forbidden. The independent checker
reconstructs every core, every complementary triple, and every such red `K4`.
The 26 cases split as seven with `|U|=1`, eighteen with `|U|=2`, and core 194
with `|U|=4`.

If `U={a}`, the equations force the three pairs containing `a` to have
multiplicity zero and the three triples containing `a` to occur. Together
with the two singleton-`a` vertices, at least five fixed vertices are red to
`C_a`, contradicting the bound four. If `U={a,b}`, two projected equalities
force a complementary triple count first to one, while the red `K4` for the
other good index forces it to zero. These arguments close 25 cores directly.

For core 194 all four singleton counts are two. Triple and full signatures
are forbidden, so the final two fixed signatures must be distinct pairs,
giving exactly fifteen profiles. Pick either pair `{i,j}`. Its vertex and the
two singleton-`i` vertices are pairwise blue and blue to both remaining red
triangles. Those two triangles have a blue cross-edge—otherwise their union
would be a red `K6`—and this edge completes a blue `K5`. Thus no pair is
possible.

[`independent_check.py`](independent_check.py) implements this deduction
without importing the reviewed package and without repeating its 39,105-case
arithmetic enumerators. It also constructs the blue `K5` directly for all
fifteen core-194 profiles and matches the 45 published forced fixed edges.
The public compact certificate separately regenerated byte-for-byte and
passed under normal and optimized CPython, including all five corruptions.
