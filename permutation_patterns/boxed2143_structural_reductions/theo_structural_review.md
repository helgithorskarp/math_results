I checked all four written arguments, including hypotheses, both directions where claimed, multiplicities, boundary cases and the asymptotic bridge. I found no gap at the stated partial scope.

1. **Skew separation and unique factorization.** A classical 2143 selection crossing a skew cut would have some proper initial rank set equal to the largest ranks. Its three proper prefix rank sets fail that condition, so no such selection exists. For selections internal to a block, all points in the other block are horizontally outside the selected bounding rectangle. Every prefix containing exactly the largest ranks is an intrinsic cut; refining by all such cuts gives blocks with consecutive value intervals and unique finest skew-indecomposable factors. Taking a first factor and the remaining suffix therefore gives the displayed recurrence without double counting. The empty permutation supplies a_0=1 and has no nonempty indecomposable component.

2. **Growth equivalence.** Inclusion gives b_n<=a_n. If b_k<=D^k uniformly, each composition of n contributes at most D^n factor choices. Unique finest factorization and exactly 2^(n-1) positive compositions give a_n<=(2D)^n for n>=1. Increasing D to at least one is legitimate. The generating-function equation is only formal, as the packet correctly says. A fixed finite block alphabet has uniformly exponential indecomposable counts and cannot supply an unbounded-root answer merely by concatenation.

3. **Extended root limit.** Fixed-size skew sum is an injective map from the product of avoidance sets, so a_(m+n)>=a_m a_n. Counts are positive and a_0=1. For n=qm+r, the residual factor gives a_n>=a_m^q. Dividing the logarithm by n and taking liminf yields the fixed-m lower bound; taking its supremum establishes equality with limsup, including infinity. Exponentiation yields the extended root limit. This argument proves existence of that limit, not that it is infinite, and does not itself decide the target.

4. **Arbitrary positive increasing inflation.** Selected entries from the same increasing block would be consecutive in the selection. The descending adjacent pairs of 2143 exclude roles1/2 and3/4; roles2/3 would require both remaining selected values to lie strictly between them, impossible for other disjoint value blocks. Thus a selection projects to four distinct blocks. An original interior point supplies an interior point from its entire intermediate block, so boxed selections project to boxed originals. In the projected first and minimum blocks, selecting anything before the last entry leaves a later interior point; in the maximum and final blocks, selecting anything after the first leaves an earlier interior point. Those endpoints are therefore forced. Conversely the remaining selected-block points lie horizontally or vertically outside, and other blocks lie outside by the original boxed condition. Projection and the forced last,last,first,first lift are inverse maps. Positivity of every block size is essential and is stated; singleton blocks cause no exception. The all-size-two version is source-known; no priority conclusion follows for the general version.
