arXiv:2609.31811v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31811v1), supplies the
ordinary theorem boundary actually used in the current audit:

- Theorem3.3: exact rooted-score stackability criterion for arbitrary legal moves.
- Theorem1.1: the exact tree estimator with the least integer k>=2 convention.

The assembly uses the first two theorem imports through its classification
audit; it can then derive the lower by that classification's sufficiency.
It does not independently reprove arbitrary-move necessity or claim an
audit of the primary paper's reported mechanical verification.
