# Parts-pole inversions at every positive scale

Let P={p_0,...,p_508} be the hash-pinned Parts point set specified in
`model.py`. Coordinates are actual points of the Euclidean plane. For each
a in {0,...,508} and s>0, form

    Q(a,s) = {s (p_i-p_a)/|p_i-p_a|^2 : i != a}.

We prove that every strict unit-distance graph on Q(a,s) is four-colourable.
Moreover, its number of unit edges is at most 486, and this maximum is
attained by Q(0,2/3). Every support has exactly 508 distinct points.

The conclusion is restricted to this source and these poles. It does not
classify inversions at arbitrary other plane points, arbitrary deleted
subsets after a free-pole inversion, or general geometric deformations.

## 1. The geometric reduction

Write I_a(x)=(x-p_a)/|x-p_a|^2 away from p_a. Inversion is an involution
when the pole is translated to zero, hence it is injective. Removing the
one pole leaves508 points; rescaling by s>0 preserves distinctness. All
coordinates remain real algebraic for every event scale used below.

The elementary identity

    |I_a(p_i)-I_a(p_j)|^2
      = |p_i-p_j|^2/(|p_i-p_a|^2 |p_j-p_a|^2) = r(a;i,j)

follows by expanding the squared norm. All these r are positive. A unit
edge after scaling occurs precisely when r(a;i,j)=1/s^2. If a scaled graph
has any edge, its scale is therefore represented by one of the
binomial(508,2)=128778 pairs not using the pole. Across509 poles there are
65,548,002 labelled representatives. Repeated exact values need not be
separated to establish coverage. Scales producing no unit edge are trivial.

This also covers, up to similarities and reflection, every nonconstant
complex Möbius transformation with pole equal to one of the source points,
after omitting that point. Indeed,

    (A z+B)/(C z+D) = A/C + (BC-AD)/(C^2 (z+D/C)),  C !=0,

and z ->1/(z-a) is the reflection of I_a. Complex multiplication gives a
positive scale and a rotation. The excluded pole is not treated as a plane
vertex at infinity. No source unit edge is assumed preserved.

## 2. Exact arithmetic and a sufficient residue partition

The source has coordinates (x,sqrt(3)y), x,y in
F=Q(sqrt(5),sqrt(33)). In the basis (1,sqrt5,sqrt33,sqrt165), the metric is

    N(x,y)=x^2+3y^2.

The supplied Cartesian eight-radical rows are checked to have the requisite
parities before this representation is used. Linear independence of the
multiquadratic basis makes coefficient equality an exact equality test.

Use the prime

    p=1000000271,  sqrt5 ->838613109,  sqrt33 ->767568613.

Trial division verifies primality and modular squares verify the radical
relations. Every rational source denominator is invertible modulo p. The
complete509-by509 source-distance calculation verifies that all 509*508
ordered off-diagonal squared distances have nonzero residues.

Consequently the residue map extends from the ring generated by the source
coordinates and radicals to its localization at all source squared
distances. All r(a;i,j) belong to this ring. This is a homomorphism from a
localized ring, not a homomorphism from the entire characteristic-zero field
to a finite field. No modular square root of the positive rescaling is needed.

For a fixed pole a and residue t, define B(a,t) on the 508 non-pole labels
by including precisely those pairs whose r has residue t. If the physical
scale corresponds to an exact value r0, every actual unit pair has r=r0
and hence lies in B(a,image(r0)). Thus this is a supergraph of the complete
physical graph, including all accidental contacts. Different exact values
may merge in a bucket. This can only add edges, so positive colourings and
edge-count upper bounds transfer in the required direction. No negative
residue-graph answer is used to infer a physical chromatic lower bound.

## 3. Complete finite certificate

Sorting all pair values for every pole gives 56,554,414 nonempty residue
buckets. This is a modular count, not a count of distinct real scales or
nonisomorphic graphs.

Any simple graph having a nonempty 4-core has at least 5 core vertices and
at least 10 edges, by minimum degree 4. Buckets with fewer than10 edges are
therefore3-degenerate. Every remaining 118,103 bucket is explicitly peeled
by repeatedly removing a vertex of degree at most 3. Successful peelings
are reversed to produce and check positive four-colour words.

Exactly 17 buckets have a nonempty 4-core. Their positive words are stored
in `certificate.json`; each has 508 symbols from0,1,2,3. All retained edge
inequalities are checked. A 4-core is not evidence of non-four-colourability;
these 17 positive words close the exceptions. In total 56,554,397 buckets
are 3-degenerate and 17 use stored words. Every pole and every scale is
therefore covered, proving the four-colouring theorem. The empty graphs
omitted from the nonempty bucket count are also four-colourable.

The largest bucket has486 edges. This bounds the number of unit edges in
every physical image. The exact check in the next section attains486,
so the edge maximum is sharp within the stated family.

## 4. Separate geometric checks

The producer computes modular pair values from the norm-ratio formula.
The C++ verifier instead constructs each inverted point explicitly:

    (X,Y) -> (X-X_a,Y-Y_a)/N(X-X_a,Y-Y_a),

then subtracts inverted coordinates and computes every squared distance.
An independent source reader evaluates all eight Cartesian radical
coefficients first, using a checked square root of3, before converting
Y/sqrt3. Its coordinates agree entrywise with the four-basis producer.
All65,548,002 native pair values agree entrywise with the ratio route.
The canonical little-endian uint32 stream has SHA256

    956fdb8478b527ac69c858e05a1a6430592684c9b6698c14fec9ab85645443a7.

This checks the complete finite-field reconstruction. It is not an assertion
that65million separate algebraic real-distance equalities were calculated.

Separately, `fixture.py` imports neither the four-basis arithmetic nor the
modular geometry. It reads the Cartesian eight-radical source, performs
field inversion using the product of the seven nontrivial Galois conjugates,
and constructs the 508 actual points Q(0,2/3). It checks all 128,778 point
pairs using exact integer numerators and per-point denominators. The 508
points are distinct and have exactly 486 unit edges, agreeing entrywise with
the largest residue graph. The same positive word is proper on this graph.
No claim of chromatic number exactly4 is needed or made for this fixture.
Its canonical edge-list SHA256 is

    ce567d8e826bf849985d121ae847350e028b6f95ab055dc5bdf0690e7dd708f9.

Controls reject three malformed field projections and four malformed colour
words. K4 peels, K5 does not, and K4,4 has a 4-core but an explicit two-colouring.
The exact four-point source

    (0,0), (1,0), (1/2,0), (1/2,sqrt3/6)

inverts at (0,0) to the three points (1,0),(2,0),(3/2,sqrt3/2), giving a unit
triangle. This positive control verifies the count-changing construction
and newly created unit contacts on a hand-checkable example.

## 5. Arithmetic bounds and trust

In NumPy, coordinate differences have magnitude at most p-1. Computing
x^2+3y^2 uses magnitude at most4(p-1)^2<2^63. Every subsequent product is
reduced modulo p before another multiplication, so each product is below
p^2<2^63. The C++ code reduces squares separately, bounds the prime and
input coordinates, and uses products below p^2. The generated binary stream
uses explicit little-endian bytes, without native-endian assumptions.
Both full scans were also run with undefined-behaviour sanitization.

Python's exact fixture uses arbitrary-precision integer and rational
arithmetic. It needs no floating-point distance threshold or root isolation.
SAT is used only to produce the 17 positive words. At-least-one,
at-most-one and same-colour edge exclusion clauses encode four-colouring;
decoded words are checked directly. No SAT UNSAT result, source deletion
certificate, or imported five-chromaticity certificate is a proof premise.

Trust lies in the hash-pinned source coordinate rows, the written inversion,
localization and graph-colouring reductions, the arithmetic implementations,
Python/NumPy/C++ execution and compiler semantics. The distinct representations
and algorithms are author-side cross-checks, not independent-author review
or formal proof. No historical priority is claimed for inversion or modular
supergraph methods.

## 6. Construction decision

The intended record test was an actual508-point five-chromatic graph. The
complete continuous-scale family instead has positive four-colour evidence,
and even its sharp maximum edge count is486. The named pole-inversion gate
is retired. No larger pole set or unconstrained conformal search is implied.

This result does not meet the campaign's next-boundary requirement of a
useful composable colour relation or a physical non-four signal. Subsequent
source selection must establish such a premise before family expansion.
